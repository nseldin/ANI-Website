Nclex Essential

true

Major Risks

  • Allergic reactions

  • Rash

  • GI upset

  • Potential immune stimulation

Interactions

  • Immunosuppressants

  • Hepatotoxic medications

Contraindications

  • Ragweed/daisy family allergy

  • Autoimmune disease without provider review

  • Transplant or immunosuppression without provider review

Nursing Assessment

  • Ask about asthma/allergies, autoimmune disease, transplant meds, and immunosuppressant use

Teaching

  • Stop and report wheeze, swelling, hives, or severe rash

  • Do not use long term for immune stimulation unless provider-directed

Nclex Traps

  • Immune support sounds positive until the client has autoimmune disease or takes immunosuppressants.

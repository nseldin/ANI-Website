Used To Treat

Product- and age-specific moderate-to-severe allergic asthma with perennial aeroallergen sensitization and inadequate inhaled-corticosteroid control, chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps, IgE-mediated food allergy to reduce reactions from accidental exposure while continuing avoidance, and chronic spontaneous urticaria. It does not treat acute bronchospasm or an acute allergic reaction.

Mechanism

Omalizumab is a humanized IgG1 kappa antibody that binds the C-epsilon-3 domain of circulating IgE. This sterically prevents IgE from binding Fc-epsilon-RI on mast cells, basophils, and dendritic cells. Reduced free IgE causes Fc-epsilon-RI expression to fall, decreasing the probability that allergen will cross-link enough surface IgE to trigger calcium signaling, granule exocytosis, histamine, tryptase, prostaglandin, leukotriene, IL-4, IL-5, and IL-13 release. Total measured IgE rises after treatment because omalizumab-IgE complexes clear more slowly than free IgE; that rise does not mean allergic activation has worsened. Asthma and nasal-polyp dosing uses pretreatment total IgE plus body weight, while chronic spontaneous urticaria dosing does not use IgE level.

Boxed Warning

BOXED WARNING: Life-threatening anaphylaxis with bronchospasm, hypotension, syncope, urticaria, and throat or tongue angioedema can occur after the first dose or beyond one year of regular treatment. Initiate in a healthcare setting, observe for an appropriate period, maintain immediate anaphylaxis treatment capability, teach delayed symptoms, and select self-administration only with label-defined risk mitigation. Xolair for food allergy reduces accidental-exposure reactions but is not emergency treatment and does not replace epinephrine or avoidance.

Contraindications

  • Severe hypersensitivity to omalizumab or an excipient

  • Not for acute bronchospasm, status asthmaticus, or emergency treatment of anaphylaxis or food reaction

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify indication-specific dose logic, pretreatment IgE and weight where required, dose/vial combination, injection sites, observation plan, and epinephrine readiness.

  • Teach that anaphylaxis may be delayed after leaving. Assess wheeze, throat tightness, tongue swelling, dizziness, syncope, hives, or hypotension urgently.

Interactions

  • No formal classic CYP interactions are expected, but corticosteroids must not be abruptly withdrawn after response.

  • Do not combine asthma biologics without specialist evidence; consider helminth exposure and live treatment plans individually.

Key Labs

  • Pretreatment total IgE and body weight for asthma, CRSwNP, and food-allergy dosing; symptoms, exacerbations, epinephrine use, and injection reactions

  • Do not use repeat total IgE during treatment or for up to a year after discontinuation to recalculate dose without label guidance because complexes elevate the result

Nclex Traps

  • Total IgE rises during omalizumab even while free IgE falls.

  • Omalizumab can reduce future food-allergy reaction risk, but IM epinephrine remains first-line for an acute reaction.

Description

Omalizumab is a subcutaneous monoclonal antibody that binds free IgE before IgE can occupy the high-affinity Fc-epsilon-RI receptor on mast cells, basophils, and dendritic cells. It targets the C-epsilon-3 region of free IgE and does not meaningfully bind IgE already attached to Fc-epsilon-RI, avoiding direct receptor cross-linking and massive degranulation. As free IgE falls, cells down-regulate Fc-epsilon-RI and become less easily triggered over time. This preventive effect can reduce allergic asthma attacks, food-allergy reaction risk from accidental exposure, or urticaria activity, but it never replaces allergen avoidance, epinephrine, or acute asthma rescue. Delayed anaphylaxis can occur even after more than a year of tolerated doses.

Adverse Effects

  • Injection-site reaction, headache, arthralgia, dizziness, upper abdominal pain, fever, or product/indication-specific common effects

  • Anaphylaxis, serum-sickness-like fever-arthralgia-rash, eosinophilic conditions, helminth concern, malignancy signal uncertainty, or severe hypersensitivity

Category

Pancreatic labs

Name

Lipase

Range

Often about 10-140 U/L, facility dependent.

Why

Lipase rises with pancreatic inflammation and is more specific for pancreatitis than amylase.

Aliases

  • serum lipase

High Causes

  • Acute pancreatitis

  • Pancreatic obstruction

  • Some renal disease

  • Gallstone disease

Low Causes

  • Low lipase is usually not the acute safety problem.

Critical Concerns

  • Severe epigastric pain radiating to the back plus elevated lipase suggests pancreatitis.

Nursing Considerations

  • Monitor pain, N/V, hydration, calcium, glucose, respiratory status, and shock signs.

Nclex Traps

  • Low calcium with pancreatitis can signal severity.

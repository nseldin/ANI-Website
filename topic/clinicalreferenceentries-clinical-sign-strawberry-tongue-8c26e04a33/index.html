Definition

Strawberry tongue is an observed oral sign. It is a clue to toxin-mediated or inflammatory illness. A positive finding means a white coating may peel to reveal a red tongue with prominent papillae.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Strawberry tongue means a white coating may peel to reveal a red tongue with prominent papillae.

Why It Matters

The observed Strawberry tongue finding can signal toxin-mediated or inflammatory illness. Mucosal inflammation and desquamation expose hyperemic enlarged fungiform papillae. Strawberry tongue cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • toxin-mediated or inflammatory illness

Assessment Method

Inspect tongue color and papillae.

Positive Finding

A white coating may peel to reveal a red tongue with prominent papillae.

Mechanism

Mucosal inflammation and desquamation expose hyperemic enlarged fungiform papillae.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Strawberry tongue finding raises concern for toxin-mediated or inflammatory illness because mucosal inflammation and desquamation expose hyperemic enlarged fungiform papillae. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Strawberry tongue does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Strawberry tongue to focus evaluation of toxin-mediated or inflammatory illness and the reviewed alternatives: stomatitis, vitamin deficiency, food dye. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect tongue color and papillae. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Strawberry tongue as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Several diseases and dehydration produce similar redness. Therefore, Strawberry tongue cannot confirm or exclude toxin-mediated or inflammatory illness by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • stomatitis

  • vitamin deficiency

  • food dye

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as stomatitis, vitamin deficiency, food dye.

False Negative Situations

Strawberry tongue may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of toxin-mediated or inflammatory illness.

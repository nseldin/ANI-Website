Summary

Radioactive iodine uptake is a high-yield Endocrine diagnostic reference. Measures iodine uptake to help distinguish causes of hyperthyroidism.

Quick Answer

Radioactive iodine uptake: Measures iodine uptake to help distinguish causes of hyperthyroidism. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Low uptake

    Meaning

    Thyroid hormone is high but the gland is not actively trapping iodine, suggesting thyroiditis, exogenous hormone, or iodine load patterns.

  • Label

    High diffuse uptake

    Meaning

    The gland is actively making hormone, classically Graves disease when clinical features fit.

  • Label

    Focal uptake

    Meaning

    Hot or cold nodules require thyroid-specific interpretation; cold nodules may need ultrasound/FNA risk assessment.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Measures iodine uptake to help distinguish causes of hyperthyroidism.

    • Before / preparation

    • Screen pregnancy/breastfeeding status, allergies, kidney concerns if contrast is paired, and ability to remain still for scan timing.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Teach that a small tracer may be used, encourage ordered hydration after some studies, and monitor for delayed or unexpected symptoms.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Unstable symptoms during testing, severe pain, allergic symptoms, or results suggesting embolus, obstruction, ischemia, or metastatic disease.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Nuclear studies often show function, not just anatomy. Match the test to the clinical question.

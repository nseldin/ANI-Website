  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Used To Treat

Interpreting lithium concentrations with dose timing, renal function, sodium/fluid status, interactions, and toxicity symptoms.

Mechanism

A lithium level measures serum exposure to a renally cleared mood stabilizer with a narrow therapeutic index. Interpret it with dose timing, hydration, sodium balance, kidney function, NSAIDs, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, diuretics, tremor, GI symptoms, ataxia, confusion, seizures, and rhythm concerns.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Lithium level monitoring in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Lithium level monitoring is not a separate drug; it is a therapeutic drug monitoring reference that measures serum lithium exposure for a renally cleared mood stabilizer with a narrow therapeutic index. Use it to connect the number with dose timing, hydration, sodium balance, kidney function, NSAIDs, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, diuretics, tremor, GI symptoms, ataxia, confusion, seizures, and rhythm changes.

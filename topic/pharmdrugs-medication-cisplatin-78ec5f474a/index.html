Mechanism

Cisplatin works by creating platinum-DNA crosslinks that block replication and transcription, triggering cancer-cell death. Cisplatin is classically nephrotoxic and ototoxic, oxaliplatin causes cold-triggered neuropathy, and carboplatin is more marrow/platelet-heavy; nausea, hypersensitivity, renal dosing, and CBC trends matter.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for cisplatin in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Cisplatin is a platinum antineoplastic: it works by creating platinum-DNA crosslinks that block replication and transcription, triggering cancer-cell death. It is used for cancer treatment or immune/autoimmune protocols depending on the agent, regimen, tumor biology, and dose schedule.

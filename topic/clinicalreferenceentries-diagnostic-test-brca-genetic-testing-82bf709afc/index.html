Summary

BRCA genetic testing is a high-yield Oncology diagnostic reference. Germline genetic testing for BRCA1/BRCA2 variants associated with hereditary breast/ovarian and related cancer risk.

Quick Answer

BRCA genetic testing: Germline genetic testing for BRCA1/BRCA2 variants associated with hereditary breast/ovarian and related cancer risk. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Negative

    Meaning

    No tested pathogenic BRCA variant is found; hereditary risk may still exist from untested genes or family patterns.

  • Label

    Pathogenic variant

    Meaning

    A disease-associated BRCA1/BRCA2 variant is found, changing cancer-risk management and family counseling.

  • Label

    Variant of uncertain significance

    Meaning

    A DNA change is found but its meaning is unclear; it should not be treated like a pathogenic mutation.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Germline genetic testing for BRCA1/BRCA2 variants associated with hereditary breast/ovarian and related cancer risk.

    • Before / preparation

    • Genetic counseling and informed consent are key because results affect family risk, screening, and prevention decisions.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Teach that screening estimates risk; it does not always diagnose. Promote follow-up and reduce fear with clear next steps.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Positive screening with symptoms, high-risk history, severe abnormality, or failure to follow up on a positive result.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • A genetic test result can affect relatives, not only the individual client.

Used To Treat

Relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis to reduce relapse activity and inflammatory lesion formation when therapy is appropriate.

Mechanism

Interferon-beta binds type I interferon receptors and alters transcription of immune-regulatory genes. The net effect is reduced inflammatory CNS demyelination activity through changes in cytokines, T-cell activation, and leukocyte trafficking.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for interferon-beta in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Interferon-beta is an immune-modulating MS medication, not a general immunosuppressant. It changes cytokine signaling, antigen presentation, and leukocyte migration across the blood-brain barrier, so flu-like symptoms, depression screening, liver enzymes, CBC, injection reactions, and relapse trends matter.

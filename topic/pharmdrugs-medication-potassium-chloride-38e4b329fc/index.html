Mechanism

Potassium chloride works by replacing potassium needed for normal cardiac conduction and skeletal/smooth muscle function. The safety issue is route/rate/concentration because rapid potassium can be fatal.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warning for some concentrated injectable potassium products: must be diluted before IV use; rapid/undiluted IV potassium can be fatal.

Contraindications

  • Hyperkalemia.

  • Severe renal failure/anuria without dialysis plan.

  • Never give IV push.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Never IV push potassium.

  • Verify renal function and urine output.

  • Use pump for IV replacement and monitor cardiac rhythm when indicated.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • ACE inhibitors, ARBs, spironolactone, triamterene, salt substitutes increase hyperkalemia risk.

  • Digoxin toxicity risk rises with low potassium.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • potassium

  • creatinine/eGFR

  • urine output

  • ECG

  • Use medication-specific labs when ordered. For high-risk drugs, connect renal function, liver function, electrolytes, CBC, coagulation studies, drug levels, ECG, and vital-sign trends to safety.

Nclex Traps

  • Potassium is a fatal-error medication if pushed IV.

  • The NCLEX trap is memorizing the name without connecting class, contraindication, assessment, and priority adverse effect.

Used To Treat

renal, electrolyte, dialysis, urinary, or fluid-balance indications depending on the agent.

Description

Potassium chloride is an electrolyte replacement: it works by replacing potassium needed for normal cardiac conduction and skeletal/smooth muscle function. It is used for treatment or prevention of hypokalemia.

Summary

Fractional exhaled nitric oxide (FeNO) is a high-yield Respiratory diagnostic reference. Measures exhaled nitric oxide as a marker of type 2/eosinophilic airway inflammation in asthma assessment.

Quick Answer

Fractional exhaled nitric oxide (FeNO): Measures exhaled nitric oxide as a marker of type 2/eosinophilic airway inflammation in asthma assessment. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

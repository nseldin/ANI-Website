Used To Treat

Hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative advanced or metastatic breast cancer in combination endocrine regimens when ordered.

Boxed Warning

High-alert warning: ribociclib can cause neutropenia, hepatobiliary toxicity, QT prolongation, interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis, and embryo-fetal harm.

Mechanism

Ribociclib inhibits CDK4/6, reducing cell-cycle progression through the G1/S checkpoint. QT prolongation and hepatotoxicity monitoring distinguish it from some classmates.

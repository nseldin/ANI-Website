Used To Treat

Prevention or treatment of venous/arterial clots and stroke prevention in selected atrial fibrillation clients.

Mechanism

Warfarin INR monitoring works by blocking vitamin K recycling, which decreases functional clotting factors II, VII, IX, and X plus proteins C and S. The effect is delayed because existing clotting factors must clear first.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warning: major or fatal bleeding. Warfarin also carries fetal-harm risk and is generally avoided in pregnancy.

Contraindications

  • Active major bleeding, severe uncontrolled bleeding risk, recent high-risk procedures, and unsafe neuraxial timing for applicable agents.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • Bleeding assessment, CBC/hemoglobin/hematocrit, platelets, renal function. INR for warfarin; aPTT/anti-Xa per protocol for heparins when ordered.

Nclex Traps

  • The priority danger is bleeding. New neuro change, black stools, severe headache, hypotension, or falling hemoglobin requires escalation.

Description

Warfarin INR monitoring is a vitamin K antagonist anticoagulant: it works by blocking vitamin K recycling, which decreases functional clotting factors II, VII, IX, and X plus proteins C and S. It is used for prevention or treatment of venous/arterial clots and stroke prevention in selected atrial fibrillation clients.

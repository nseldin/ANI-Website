Used To Treat

Boxed Warning

No universal boxed warning; major high-yield warnings include tooth/bone effects in children and fetal risk.

Mechanism

Demeclocycline works by reversibly binding the 30S ribosomal subunit and preventing aminoacyl-tRNA attachment, which halts bacterial protein elongation. It is usually bacteriostatic; teeth/bone effects, pregnancy and young-child cautions, photosensitivity, esophagitis, and calcium/iron/magnesium binding matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Separate from dairy, iron, calcium, antacids.

  • Photosensitivity teaching.

Description

Demeclocycline is a tetracycline antibiotic: it works by reversibly binding the 30S ribosomal subunit and preventing aminoacyl-tRNA attachment, which halts bacterial protein elongation. It is used for acne, tick-borne illness, atypical respiratory infections, chlamydia, MRSA skin infections, malaria prophylaxis/treatment, and other susceptible infections.

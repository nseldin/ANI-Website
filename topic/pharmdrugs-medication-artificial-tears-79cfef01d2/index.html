Used To Treat

Dry eye symptoms, ocular surface lubrication, exposure-related dryness, and irritation from tear-film instability.

Mechanism

Artificial tears works by supplementing the tear film with lubricating polymers, electrolytes, osmoprotectants, or lipid-layer support depending on the product. The goal is to reduce ocular-surface friction, evaporation, hyperosmolar irritation, and epithelial desiccation rather than to create a systemic receptor effect.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Artificial tears in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Artificial tears is an ophthalmic lubricant / tear-film replacement: it works by supplementing the tear film with lubricating polymers, electrolytes, osmoprotectants, or lipid-layer support depending on the product. It is used for dry eye symptoms, ocular surface lubrication, exposure-related dryness, and irritation from tear-film instability.

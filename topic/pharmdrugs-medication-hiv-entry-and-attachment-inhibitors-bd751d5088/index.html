Used To Treat

Combination antiretroviral therapy using a stage-specific entry inhibitor, particularly salvage treatment for heavily treatment-experienced patients or CCR5-tropic infection when the exact product indication is met.

Description

HIV entry inhibitors interrupt different sequential steps rather than forming one interchangeable class. Fostemsavir's active drug temsavir binds viral gp120 before CD4 attachment; ibalizumab binds CD4 domain 2 after attachment and prevents entry-related conformational change; maraviroc occupies host CCR5 and requires tropism testing; enfuvirtide binds viral gp41 and prevents membrane fusion.

Mechanism

Entry begins when gp120 engages CD4, then a coreceptor, followed by gp41-mediated membrane fusion. Blocking one step does not guarantee activity when the virus uses another coreceptor or carries target-specific resistance. Ibalizumab binds a CD4 surface opposite the MHC-II interaction site and does not prevent gp120 attachment, which explains the term post-attachment inhibitor and its lack of conventional CD4 immunosuppression.

Boxed Warning

Warning profiles differ: maraviroc carries a boxed hepatotoxicity warning with possible allergic features; fostemsavir and ibalizumab currently do not. All must be used with an active background regimen. Monitor viral load because functional monotherapy, missed infusions, strong-inducer interactions, or target resistance can rapidly select failure.

Nursing Essentials

  • Identify the entry step, viral tropism when relevant, resistance history, active companion drugs, schedule, interaction burden, hepatitis coinfection, liver function, CD4 count, and HIV RNA.

  • A fall in viral load requires the entire regimen; never describe a salvage entry agent as adequate monotherapy.

Key Labs

  • HIV RNA, CD4 count, genotype/phenotype and tropism data when applicable, liver tests, hepatitis B/C status, ECG risk for fostemsavir, and infusion observation for ibalizumab.

Nclex Traps

  • Ibalizumab is post-attachment even though it binds host CD4; fostemsavir is pre-attachment because temsavir binds viral gp120.

  • Maraviroc requires CCR5-tropic virus, whereas ibalizumab has activity against CCR5- and CXCR4-tropic strains.

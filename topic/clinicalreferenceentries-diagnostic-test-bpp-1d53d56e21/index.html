Summary

BPP (biophysical profile) is a high-yield Obstetrics diagnostic reference. Biophysical profile combines fetal movement, tone, breathing, fluid, and often NST.

Quick Answer

BPP (biophysical profile): Biophysical profile combines fetal movement, tone, breathing, fluid, and often NST. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Biophysical profile combines fetal movement, tone, breathing, fluid, and often NST.

    • Before / preparation

    • Confirm gestational age, fetal/maternal baseline status, Rh status when invasive testing is involved, and whether the client is having bleeding, contractions, or ruptured membranes.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Monitor maternal vital signs, fetal status when indicated, uterine activity, bleeding, fluid leakage, infection cues, and decreased fetal movement.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Decreased fetal movement, nonreassuring fetal tracing, bleeding, leaking fluid, fever, severe abdominal pain, contractions after an invasive procedure, or hypertensive warning signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Amniotic fluid volume is a placental/oxygenation clue. Do not focus only on fetal movement.

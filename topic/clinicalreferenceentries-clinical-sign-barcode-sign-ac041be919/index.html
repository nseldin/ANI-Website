Definition

Barcode sign is an ultrasound imaging sign. It is a clue to absent pleural sliding, sometimes pneumothorax. A positive finding means parallel horizontal lines continue below the pleura instead of a granular pattern.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Barcode sign means parallel horizontal lines continue below the pleura instead of a granular pattern.

Why It Matters

The Barcode sign imaging pattern can signal absent pleural sliding, sometimes pneumothorax. Without visceral-parietal sliding, tissue motion does not create the normal sandy M-mode appearance. Barcode sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • absent pleural sliding, sometimes pneumothorax

Assessment Method

Review M-mode across the pleural line.

Positive Finding

Parallel horizontal lines continue below the pleura instead of a granular pattern.

Mechanism

Without visceral-parietal sliding, tissue motion does not create the normal sandy M-mode appearance.

Clinical Interpretation

The Barcode sign imaging pattern raises concern for absent pleural sliding, sometimes pneumothorax because without visceral-parietal sliding, tissue motion does not create the normal sandy M-mode appearance. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Barcode sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Barcode sign to focus evaluation of absent pleural sliding, sometimes pneumothorax and the reviewed alternatives: pleurodesis, ARDS, esophageal intubation. Evidence Limitations

Absent sliding has many causes and does not alone diagnose pneumothorax. Therefore, Barcode sign cannot confirm or exclude absent pleural sliding, sometimes pneumothorax by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

A false-positive interpretation can result from acquisition artifact, an inappropriate imaging phase or plane, or overlap with pleurodesis, ARDS, esophageal intubation.

False Negative Situations

Barcode sign may be missed when the associated process is early or subtle, the relevant region is not included, or acquisition timing and resolution are inadequate.

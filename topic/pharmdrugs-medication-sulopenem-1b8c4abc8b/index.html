Used To Treat

Susceptible bacterial infections such as respiratory, urinary, skin/soft tissue, intra-abdominal, bloodstream, surgical prophylaxis, or CNS infections when the chosen beta-lactam covers the likely organism.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; serious hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis and C. difficile-associated diarrhea are major class concerns.

Mechanism

Sulopenem works by binding multiple penicillin-binding proteins and broadly blocking cell-wall synthesis. Carbapenems are high-power beta-lactams for severe resistant or polymicrobial infections; seizure risk, renal dosing, allergy history, culture de-escalation, and ertapenem's lack of Pseudomonas coverage matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Check allergy history and reaction type.

  • Obtain cultures before antibiotics when possible without delaying emergency treatment.

  • Monitor diarrhea, rash, renal function for selected agents.

Contraindications

  • Serious beta-lactam hypersensitivity

Interactions

  • Warfarin effect may increase with many antibiotics.

  • Methotrexate interactions with penicillins can occur.

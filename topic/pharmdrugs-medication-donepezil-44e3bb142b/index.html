Used To Treat

Symptomatic treatment of Alzheimer disease and selected dementia-related cognitive symptoms.

Mechanism

Donepezil inhibits acetylcholinesterase, increasing acetylcholine signaling in cortical/cholinergic pathways. Benefits are modest cognitive/function support; risks include bradycardia, syncope, nausea/diarrhea, weight loss, and sleep disturbance.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for donepezil in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Donepezil is an acetylcholinesterase inhibitor for dementia symptoms: it inhibits acetylcholinesterase, increasing acetylcholine signaling in cortical/cholinergic pathways. It is used for symptomatic treatment of Alzheimer disease and selected dementia-related cognitive symptoms.

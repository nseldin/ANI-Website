Definition

Sarcoptes scabiei is a clinically important ectoparasite associated with scabies, crusted scabies, and secondary bacterial skin infection. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

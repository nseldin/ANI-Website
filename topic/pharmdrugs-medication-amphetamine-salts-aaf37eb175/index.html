Used To Treat

ADHD and narcolepsy; dosing and monitoring focus on CNS stimulation, appetite/weight, sleep, blood pressure, heart rate, misuse risk, and psychiatric symptoms.

Mechanism

Amphetamine salts works by increasing synaptic norepinephrine and dopamine by promoting presynaptic monoamine release and reducing reuptake through transporter effects. That stimulant mechanism explains improved attention/wakefulness as well as tachycardia, BP elevation, insomnia, appetite suppression, anxiety, and misuse risk.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Amphetamine salts in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Amphetamine salts is a psychiatric medication: it works by increasing synaptic norepinephrine and dopamine by promoting presynaptic monoamine release and reducing reuptake through transporter effects. It is used for ADHD and narcolepsy; dosing and monitoring focus on CNS stimulation, appetite/weight, sleep, blood pressure, heart rate, misuse risk, and psychiatric symptoms.

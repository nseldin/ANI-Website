Used To Treat

Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, with tofersen restricted to adults with a confirmed SOD1 mutation and edaravone/riluzole used without that genotype requirement.

Description

ALS disease-modifying therapies are mechanistically distinct treatments that slow selected pathways of motor-neuron injury rather than restoring motor neurons already lost. The group includes glutamatergic and ion-channel modulator riluzole, free-radical-scavenging antioxidant edaravone, and SOD1 mRNA-degrading antisense oligonucleotide tofersen for mutation-confirmed SOD1-ALS. Their benefits, evidence, route, monitoring, and eligible populations cannot be inferred from the shared disease name.

Mechanism

ALS motor-neuron loss arises through interacting excitotoxic, proteostatic, oxidative, mitochondrial, inflammatory, axonal, and genetic processes. Tofersen directly lowers one pathogenic protein and received accelerated approval using plasma neurofilament light reduction as a surrogate reasonably likely to predict benefit. Edaravone and riluzole do not repair dead motor neurons, so respiratory, nutritional, communication, mobility, and palliative support remain central.

Boxed Warning

These agents currently have no class-wide boxed warning. Tofersen can cause myelitis, radiculitis, papilledema/increased intracranial pressure, and aseptic meningitis after lumbar administration. Edaravone contains sodium bisulfite and can cause anaphylaxis or severe asthma-like reactions; riluzole requires hepatic and neutropenia awareness.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm genotype when considering tofersen, exact formulation and cycle for edaravone, swallowing/feeding access, ALSFRS-R and respiratory trend, bulbar function, weight, falls, communication needs, and goals of care.

  • Escalate new dyspnea, weak cough, morning headache, orthopnea, aspiration, rapid weight loss, or inability to manage secretions; medication does not substitute for timely ventilatory and nutritional support.

Key Labs

  • Drug-specific liver tests, CBC when indicated, CSF/procedure assessment for intrathecal tofersen, and longitudinal FVC or other respiratory measures, weight, ALSFRS-R, bulbar and functional status.

Nclex Traps

  • Tofersen is not for all ALS; it requires a pathogenic SOD1 mutation.

  • Do not state that edaravone's antioxidant chemistry proves the mechanism of its clinical ALS effect; the label says that mechanism is unknown.

Used To Treat

Mild-to-moderate COVID-19 in adults at high risk for progression to severe disease, started within the authorized early-treatment window; it is not pre- or post-exposure prophylaxis.

Description

Nirmatrelvir/ritonavir is an early oral COVID-19 antiviral: nirmatrelvir blocks the viral main protease needed to process replication proteins, while ritonavir strongly inhibits CYP3A so nirmatrelvir remains at therapeutic concentrations. The antiviral concept is simple; the medication-reconciliation burden is not, because ritonavir can make common drugs dangerously accumulate.

Mechanism

Nirmatrelvir inhibits SARS-CoV-2 Mpro/3CLpro, preventing cleavage of viral polyproteins into functional replication proteins. Ritonavir contributes little direct SARS-CoV-2 effect at this dose; it inhibits CYP3A-mediated nirmatrelvir metabolism and boosts exposure.

Boxed Warning

Significant drug interactions: ritonavir is a strong CYP3A inhibitor that can cause severe, life-threatening, or fatal toxicity from interacting medications. Review every prescription, OTC, herbal, and recently stopped drug; determine which must be held, adjusted, substituted, or monitored before the first dose.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify symptom onset, high-risk status, renal-dose pack, severe hepatic disease, interacting drugs, and whether temporary interruption is safe.

  • Teach the complete course, dysgeusia/diarrhea, hypersensitivity, hepatic symptoms, and that recurrent symptoms after treatment require reassessment rather than self-extending therapy.

Key Labs

  • eGFR for dose selection, liver function when indicated, medication-specific monitoring for interactions, and oxygenation/clinical COVID trajectory.

Nclex Traps

  • Ritonavir is the booster and the interaction engine. Never approve Paxlovid from a partial medication list.

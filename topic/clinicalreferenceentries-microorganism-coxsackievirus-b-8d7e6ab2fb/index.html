Definition

Coxsackievirus B is a clinically important virus associated with myocarditis or pericarditis, aseptic meningitis, pleurodynia, and neonatal sepsis-like disease. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

    Pathogenesis

  • Content

    Coxsackievirus B is associated with myocarditis or pericarditis, aseptic meningitis, pleurodynia, and neonatal sepsis-like disease. Severity and presentation vary by age, immune status, pregnancy, anatomic site, inoculum, and comorbidity. Urgent escalation is based on the syndrome—such as shock, respiratory compromise, rapidly progressive neurologic findings, severe dehydration, or invasive infection—not merely on a positive organism label.

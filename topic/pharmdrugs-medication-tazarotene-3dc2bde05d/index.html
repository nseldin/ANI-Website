Class

Topical acetylenic retinoid prodrug; RAR-beta/gamma agonist

Used To Treat

Plaque psoriasis and acne vulgaris by product/strength; a separate 0.1% cream is an adjunct for selected facial photoaging changes with comprehensive sun avoidance and skin care.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: CONTRAINDICATED IN PREGNANCY because retinoids can cause fetal harm. Obtain a pregnancy test within 2 weeks before starting, begin during a normal menstrual period, and use effective contraception. Avoid sun/UV exposure, sunburn, eczema and simultaneous irritating products; reduce frequency or stop for severe dermatitis.

Mechanism

Cutaneous esterases hydrolyze tazarotene to tazarotenic acid. RAR-beta/gamma activation changes retinoid response-element transcription, downregulating abnormal keratinization and proliferation while modifying inflammatory mediators. In psoriasis this helps flatten thick plaques; in acne it prevents follicular plugging. The same receptor biology that remodels epithelium produces irritation and makes embryofetal exposure unacceptable despite limited topical absorption.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify negative pregnancy test, contraception plan, indication, strength and treated BSA before initiation.

  • Apply a thin layer only to involved/acne-prone skin after it is dry; surrounding normal skin receives irritation without added benefit.

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy

  • Known tazarotene or vehicle hypersensitivity

  • Avoid eczematous, sunburned or abraded skin and clarify other photosensitizers

Interactions

  • Other retinoids, strong keratolytics, alcohol/astringents and drying cosmetics add dermatitis.

  • Photosensitizing drugs such as thiazides, tetracyclines and fluoroquinolones increase sun-reaction risk.

  • Avoid unplanned UV phototherapy or tanning.

Key Labs

  • Pregnancy test within 2 weeks before initiation and repeat as clinically indicated

  • No routine serum tazarotene level

  • Skin response, irritation, BSA and photoprotection

Nclex Traps

  • Tazarotene is a prodrug; tazarotenic acid is the active RAR ligand.

  • A topical route does not remove its pregnancy contraindication.

  • The photoaging product palliates fine wrinkling/pigment changes but does not reverse all sun damage or prevent skin cancer.

Description

Tazarotene is a potent topical retinoid prodrug rapidly converted in skin to tazarotenic acid. The active metabolite binds RAR-beta and RAR-gamma and changes transcription that normalizes keratinocyte differentiation, reduces hyperproliferation, and suppresses inflammatory features of acne and plaque psoriasis. Its defining safety distinction is teratogenic potential: it is contraindicated in pregnancy and requires pregnancy testing shortly before initiation in patients who can become pregnant.

Adverse Effects

  • Pruritus, burning/stinging, erythema, peeling, dryness and pain

  • Photosensitivity and sunburn

  • Worsening psoriasis irritation or retinoid dermatitis

  • Embryofetal harm if used during pregnancy

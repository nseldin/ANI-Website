Class

Iodine-131 radiopharmaceutical thyroid ablation therapy

Used To Treat

Hyperthyroidism or differentiated thyroid-cancer remnant/iodine-avid tissue ablation when ordered by nuclear medicine/endocrinology.

Mechanism

Radioactive iodine is taken up by thyroid follicular cells through normal iodine trapping and iodine-131 emits beta radiation that locally damages or ablates overactive or iodine-avid thyroid tissue rather than blocking thyroid peroxidase like methimazole/PTU.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify pregnancy and breastfeeding status before therapy and follow radiation-safety instructions exactly.

  • Teach temporary distance/hygiene precautions, hydration/voiding guidance, and to report neck swelling, severe throat pain, fever, or worsening thyrotoxic symptoms.

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding are major contraindications. Clarify severe uncontrolled thyrotoxicosis, inability to follow radiation precautions, or recent iodine/contrast exposure per protocol.

Interactions

  • Iodine exposure, antithyroid drugs, and thyroid hormone therapy can affect uptake or timing; follow nuclear medicine/endocrinology instructions.

Key Labs

  • Pregnancy test when applicable.

  • TSH/free T4/T3 trend.

  • Radioiodine uptake/scan context when ordered.

Nclex Traps

  • Radioactive iodine destroys thyroid tissue by thyroid uptake plus beta radiation. It is not the same mechanism as methimazole or PTU, and pregnancy/breastfeeding safety screening is non-negotiable.

Description

Radioactive iodine is an iodine-131 radiopharmaceutical thyroid ablation therapy: it is taken up by thyroid follicular cells through normal iodine trapping and iodine-131 emits beta radiation that locally damages or ablates overactive or iodine-avid thyroid tissue rather than blocking thyroid peroxidase like methimazole/PTU. It is used for hyperthyroidism or differentiated thyroid-cancer remnant/iodine-avid tissue ablation when ordered by nuclear medicine/endocrinology.

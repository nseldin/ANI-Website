Definition

Hyporeflexia means muscle-stretch reflexes are weaker than expected. Areflexia means the reflex is absent; the terms should not be treated as identical.

Plain Language

A tendon tap produces a smaller-than-expected jerk, or no clear jerk despite correct technique.

Why It Matters

Hyporeflexia can point to a peripheral nerve, nerve-root, motor-neuron, neuromuscular, muscle, metabolic, or medication problem. Rapidly spreading weakness with reduced reflexes, breathing difficulty, weak cough, swallowing trouble, or unstable heart rate or blood pressure can signal an acute neuromuscular emergency such as Guillain-Barre syndrome.

Assessment Method

Ensure the muscle is relaxed and the tendon is correctly positioned, use a consistent reflex hammer strike and reinforcement when appropriate, compare both sides and multiple levels, then assess strength, tone, sensation, atrophy, fasciculations, gait, and autonomic or respiratory findings.

Positive Finding

A normally expected tendon response is reproducibly smaller than the patient's other reflexes or absent despite correct technique and appropriate reinforcement.

Mechanism

Damage or impaired conduction in the sensory input, spinal synapse, motor output, neuromuscular junction, or muscle weakens the two-neuron reflex response.

Evidence Limitations

Poor positioning, failure to relax, weak strike, obesity, edema, pain, age, medication, and baseline variation can reduce the observed response; one diminished reflex does not identify the lesion. Therefore, Hyporeflexia cannot confirm or exclude diminished reflex-arc activity from peripheral nerve, nerve-root, anterior-horn, neuromuscular, muscle, metabolic, medication, or selected acute spinal-cord dysfunction by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

A false-positive maneuver can result from incorrect position or force, pain from normal variation, radiculopathy, peripheral neuropathy, medication effect, guarding, or calling an incompletely defined response positive.

False Negative Situations

Hyporeflexia may be missed with incomplete technique, guarding, early or partial disease, limited cooperation, or a response too subtle for the examiner.

Modern Status

Current localization sign; hyporeflexia means diminished reflexes, while areflexia means absent reflexes, and symmetric reduction can be normal in some people.

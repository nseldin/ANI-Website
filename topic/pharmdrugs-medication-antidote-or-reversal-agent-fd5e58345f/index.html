Description

Antidote/reversal agents counter a specific toxic exposure or dangerous medication effect.

Used To Treat

Toxicity, overdose, reversal, or rescue situations for a defined poison, medication, electrolyte, or receptor effect.

Mechanism

The mechanism is antidote-specific: receptor displacement, cofactor repletion, chelation, immune binding, physiologic stabilization, or enhanced elimination.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify the exposure, timing, dose, airway/breathing/circulation status, labs, rebound toxicity risk, and poison-control/provider protocol.

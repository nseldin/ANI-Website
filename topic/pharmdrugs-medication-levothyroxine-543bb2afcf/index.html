Mechanism

Levothyroxine works by replacing thyroid hormone. T4 products provide thyroxine that is converted to active T3, while liothyronine provides T3 directly; metabolic effects build through nuclear thyroid-hormone receptor signaling, so over-replacement causes tachycardia, heat intolerance, weight loss, anxiety, and bone/cardiac strain.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warning: not for weight loss; large doses can cause serious or life-threatening toxicity.

Contraindications

  • Untreated thyrotoxicosis.

  • Uncorrected adrenal insufficiency.

  • Do not use for weight loss.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Take on empty stomach consistently.

  • Monitor TSH and symptoms.

  • Overreplacement can cause tachycardia, palpitations, heat intolerance, bone loss.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Calcium, iron, antacids, bile acid sequestrants reduce absorption.

  • Warfarin effect may increase with thyroid hormone changes.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • TSH

  • free T4

  • Use medication-specific labs when ordered. For high-risk drugs, connect renal function, liver function, electrolytes, CBC, coagulation studies, drug levels, ECG, and vital-sign trends to safety.

Nclex Traps

  • It takes weeks to see full effect; do not expect same-day symptom correction.

  • The NCLEX trap is memorizing the name without connecting class, contraindication, assessment, and priority adverse effect.

Used To Treat

Hypothyroidism replacement and selected TSH-suppression protocols.

Description

Levothyroxine is a thyroid hormone replacement: it works by replacing thyroid hormone. It is used for hypothyroidism replacement and selected TSH-suppression protocols.

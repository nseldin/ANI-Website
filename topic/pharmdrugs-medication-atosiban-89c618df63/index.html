Description

Atosiban is an oxytocin receptor antagonist tocolytic: it competitively blocks oxytocin receptors on myometrium, reducing phospholipase C/IP3 calcium signaling so uterine contraction strength and frequency fall. It is used for short-term delay of preterm labor in settings where atosiban is available and ordered. Availability varies by country and it is not a standard U.S. bedside medication.

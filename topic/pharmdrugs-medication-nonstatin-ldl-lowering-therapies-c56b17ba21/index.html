Used To Treat

Product-specific LDL lowering and cardiovascular-risk reduction when diet, statin tolerance, familial disease, baseline risk, and indication support the selected pathway.

Description

These nonstatin therapies lower LDL through different hepatic bottlenecks. Oral bempedoic acid inhibits ATP-citrate lyase upstream of HMG-CoA reductase; twice-yearly maintenance inclisiran uses GalNAc-siRNA to destroy PCSK9 mRNA; monthly IV evinacumab neutralizes ANGPTL3 and can lower LDL even when LDL receptors are largely absent in homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia.

Mechanism

Bempedoic acid requires liver-enriched ACSVL1 activation, decreases cholesterol synthesis, and upregulates LDL receptors. Inclisiran loads PCSK9-directed siRNA into RISC, allowing catalytic mRNA cleavage and more LDL-receptor recycling. Evinacumab releases lipoprotein and endothelial lipases from ANGPTL3 inhibition and promotes VLDL processing upstream of LDL formation, making its effect relatively LDL-receptor independent.

Boxed Warning

None currently carries a boxed warning. Bempedoic acid can cause hyperuricemia, gout, tendon rupture, and statin exposure interactions. Inclisiran mainly requires hypersensitivity and injection-site surveillance. Evinacumab can cause serious hypersensitivity and embryo-fetal harm. Match monitoring to pathway rather than treating all nonstatins alike.

Nursing Essentials

  • Document the lipid phenotype, ASCVD risk, familial diagnosis, prior therapy and intolerance, pregnancy potential, uric acid/tendon history, dosing interval, and adherence before selecting or evaluating treatment.

  • Trend percentage LDL reduction and clinical risk, not merely whether a drug was administered; investigate secondary causes and reinforce diet and other indicated risk-reduction therapy.

Key Labs

  • Fasting or nonfasting lipid panel as appropriate, LDL-C response, liver tests when indicated, uric acid and renal status for bempedoic acid, and pregnancy assessment for agents with fetal risk.

Nclex Traps

  • Inclisiran silences PCSK9 production; monoclonal PCSK9 inhibitors bind circulating PCSK9. Both preserve LDL receptors but use different molecules and schedules.

  • Evinacumab is especially useful in HoFH because its LDL lowering does not require normal LDL-receptor function.

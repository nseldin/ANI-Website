Description

Beta-1 selective beta blockers preferentially reduce cardiac beta-1 stimulation at usual doses.

Used To Treat

Hypertension, angina, rate control, post-MI protection, dysrhythmia control, heart failure for selected agents, and perioperative/critical-care rate control for selected IV agents.

Mechanism

They block beta-1 receptors in the heart and juxtaglomerular kidney cells, lowering heart rate, AV conduction, contractility, renin release, and myocardial oxygen demand. Selectivity is dose-dependent and can be lost at higher doses.

Nursing Essentials

  • Check heart rate, blood pressure, rhythm/heart block, dizziness, fatigue, acute decompensated heart failure, and masked hypoglycemia. Esmolol is ultra-short acting IV.

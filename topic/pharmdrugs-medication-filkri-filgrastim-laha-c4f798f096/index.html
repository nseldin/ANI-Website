Used To Treat

Multiple labeled neutropenia and progenitor-cell indications and increased survival after myelosuppressive radiation exposure. It is a product-specific filgrastim biosimilar for adults and children; it does not replace infection treatment, transfusion support, or radionuclide decorporation.

Description

Filkri (filgrastim-laha) is a short-acting G-CSF biosimilar that binds the G-CSF receptor and expands neutrophil precursors to reduce the depth and duration of neutropenia. Because nonpegylated filgrastim clears faster than pegfilgrastim, H-ARS treatment uses daily weight-based dosing until the label-defined ANC recovery endpoint rather than two separated doses. FDA approved on January 15, 2026 and biosimilar to Neupogen, Filkri is supplied only in 300 microgram/0.5 mL and 480 microgram/0.8 mL prefilled syringes, not a vial. Because product-specific presentation and dosing differ, a search for Filkri must not open the generic class card or silently substitute another suffix. Therapy begins as soon as possible after a suspected or confirmed myelosuppressive exposure greater than 2 Gy. A baseline CBC helps define injury, but lack of immediate laboratory access should not delay treatment after a credible exposure.

Mechanism

Filgrastim-laha binds the G-CSF receptor and activates signaling that expands and matures neutrophil precursors, then promotes their release from marrow. Because nonpegylated filgrastim is cleared more quickly than pegfilgrastim, repeated daily dosing is needed until the label-defined ANC recovery endpoint. Starting before the nadir gives surviving progenitors time to respond, which reduces the duration and depth of neutropenia. The drug increases host-cell supply; it does not neutralize radiation or act as an antibiotic.

Administration Timing

  • For H-ARS, give 10 micrograms/kg subcutaneously once daily as soon as possible after suspected or confirmed exposure above 2 Gy, using the current label and incident protocol.

  • Obtain a baseline CBC, then monitor CBC approximately every three days. Do not delay the first dose solely because a CBC is unavailable after a credible exposure.

  • Continue daily until ANC is greater than 1,000/mm3 for three consecutive CBCs or greater than 10,000/mm3 after a radiation-induced nadir. Do not copy the two-dose pegfilgrastim schedule into H-ARS.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify FILKRI and the -laha suffix, indication, weight, one of the two prefilled-syringe strengths, and schedule because there is no FILKRI vial and a tenfold microgram error can be dangerous.

  • Direct administration of a dose below 0.3 mL, equivalent to 180 micrograms, from a FILKRI prefilled syringe is not recommended because of dosing-error risk; follow the exact label and pharmacy process rather than estimating a small syringe volume.

  • Ask about latex allergy because the prefilled-syringe needle cap contains dry natural rubber, which is derived from latex. Trend ANC with clinical infection because improved counts do not prove infection control.

Key Labs

  • Check CBC with differential and platelets at baseline and approximately every three days through the label-defined ANC recovery endpoint because the trend identifies the radiation nadir and sustained marrow response. Avoid interpreting transient leukocytosis without that trend because one elevated count does not establish durable recovery or infection control.

  • Creatinine, urinalysis, cultures, oxygenation, and inflammatory or imaging studies when renal, infectious, pulmonary, or aortic complications are suspected.

Adverse Effects

  • Bone pain, musculoskeletal discomfort, leukocytosis, and injection-site reactions reflect or accompany accelerated marrow activity.

  • Splenic rupture, ARDS, anaphylaxis, sickle-cell crisis, glomerulonephritis, capillary leak, thrombocytopenia, and aortitis are uncommon but high-consequence risks.

Contraindications

  • Serious hypersensitivity to filgrastim, pegfilgrastim, or formulation components is a labeled contraindication.

  • Filkri is not an internal-contamination agent and must not displace Prussian blue, DTPA, potassium iodide, decontamination, or organ-specific radiation care because G-CSF stimulates marrow but does not remove radionuclides or treat nonhematopoietic injury.

Escalation Recurrence

  • Hypoxemia, lung infiltrates, shock, left-upper-quadrant pain, facial swelling, hematuria, oliguria, or suspected aortitis requires urgent evaluation and product review.

  • Persistent neutropenic fever, bleeding, pancytopenia, or multi-organ deterioration requires incident-command and specialist escalation despite an improving ANC.

Interactions

  • Do not import routine oncology coadministration rules into H-ARS; in oncology, cytotoxic therapy close to filgrastim can injure stimulated progenitors.

  • Lithium or other growth factors can add to leukocyte stimulation, while antibiotics, antifungals, transfusions, and source control remain independently necessary.

Population Risks

  • Population

    Neonates and children

    Note

    Use current weight-based label dosing, but do not directly administer less than 0.3 mL or 180 micrograms from a prefilled syringe; exact pharmacy preparation is critical for small doses.

  • Population

    Latex allergy

    Note

    Because the prefilled-syringe needle cap contains dry natural rubber derived from latex, assess reaction history and select the incident plan accordingly to prevent a serious hypersensitivity exposure.

  • Population

    Sickle-cell disorders, pregnancy, older age, or marrow disease

    Note

    Severe sickling and lower physiologic reserve complicate treatment, while direct human H-ARS efficacy data are necessarily limited.

Evidence Limitations

  • The H-ARS survival indication relies on the FDA Animal Rule rather than randomized human radiation exposure trials.

  • A biosimilar determination supports similarity to the reference product but does not erase exact-label, presentation, or suffix requirements.

Nclex Traps

  • Filkri is short-acting filgrastim-laha and uses daily H-ARS dosing.

  • Do not delay credible-exposure treatment while waiting for the nadir.

  • A rising ANC is not proof that infection has cleared.

Regulatory Status

FDA approved January 15, 2026 for its labeled filgrastim indications, including increased survival after myelosuppressive radiation exposure under the Animal Rule.

Boxed Warning

PREFILLED-SYRINGE WARNING: FILKRI has no vial; direct administration below 0.3 mL or 180 micrograms is not recommended because small-volume measurement can produce a dosing error, and the needle cap contains dry natural rubber derived from latex that can provoke hypersensitivity. Verify the exact ANC stopping endpoint and monitor for serious G-CSF toxicity because a transient count rise can coexist with infection or evolving organ complications.

Boxed Warning Specificity

The warning states the product-specific contraindication, medication-error hazard, or emergency-response limit together with the action needed to reduce harm.

Definition

Stellwag sign is an observed ocular sign. It is a clue to thyroid eye disease. A positive finding means blinking is infrequent or incomplete.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Stellwag sign means blinking is infrequent or incomplete.

Why It Matters

The observed Stellwag sign finding can signal thyroid eye disease. Lid retraction, sympathetic activation, and ocular-surface discomfort disrupt normal blinking. Stellwag sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • thyroid eye disease

Assessment Method

Observe spontaneous blink rate and completeness.

Positive Finding

Blinking is infrequent or incomplete.

Mechanism

Lid retraction, sympathetic activation, and ocular-surface discomfort disrupt normal blinking.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Stellwag sign finding raises concern for thyroid eye disease because lid retraction, sympathetic activation, and ocular-surface discomfort disrupt normal blinking. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Stellwag sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Stellwag sign to focus evaluation of thyroid eye disease and the reviewed alternatives: dry eye, parkinsonism, concentration. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Observe spontaneous blink rate and completeness. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Stellwag sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Blink rate varies with attention, environment, and neurologic state. Therefore, Stellwag sign cannot confirm or exclude thyroid eye disease by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • dry eye

  • parkinsonism

  • concentration

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as dry eye, parkinsonism, concentration.

False Negative Situations

Stellwag sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of thyroid eye disease.

Modern Status

Historical adjunct with low specificity. Stellwag sign remains searchable because learners still encounter the term, but it cannot confirm or exclude thyroid eye disease. Apply the reviewed limitation for Stellwag sign: Blink rate varies with attention, environment, and neurologic state.

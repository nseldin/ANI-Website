Definition

McDonald sign of pregnancy is a bedside uterine-flexibility sign. It is a clue to softened uterine isthmus in early pregnancy. A positive finding means the uterine body flexes unusually easily.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive McDonald sign of pregnancy means the uterine body flexes unusually easily.

Why It Matters

The observed McDonald sign of pregnancy finding can signal softened uterine isthmus in early pregnancy. Hormonal softening of the isthmus increases mobility between cervix and corpus. McDonald sign of pregnancy cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • softened uterine isthmus in early pregnancy

Assessment Method

During an indicated bimanual examination, assess gentle flexion of the uterine body against the cervix.

Positive Finding

The uterine body flexes unusually easily.

Mechanism

Hormonal softening of the isthmus increases mobility between cervix and corpus.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed McDonald sign of pregnancy finding raises concern for softened uterine isthmus in early pregnancy because hormonal softening of the isthmus increases mobility between cervix and corpus. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; McDonald sign of pregnancy does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use McDonald sign of pregnancy to focus evaluation of softened uterine isthmus in early pregnancy and the reviewed alternatives: uterine anomaly, prolapse, exam artifact. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: During an indicated bimanual examination, assess gentle flexion of the uterine body against the cervix. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating McDonald sign of pregnancy as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Definitions and technique are poorly standardized. Therefore, McDonald sign of pregnancy cannot confirm or exclude softened uterine isthmus in early pregnancy by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • uterine anomaly

  • prolapse

  • exam artifact

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as uterine anomaly, prolapse, exam artifact.

False Negative Situations

McDonald sign of pregnancy may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of softened uterine isthmus in early pregnancy.

Modern Status

Historical presumptive sign; obsolete for diagnosis. McDonald sign of pregnancy remains searchable because learners still encounter the term, but it cannot confirm or exclude softened uterine isthmus in early pregnancy. Apply the reviewed limitation for McDonald sign of pregnancy: Definitions and technique are poorly standardized.

The reviewed precaution for this finding is: This is distinct from McDonald cerclage and should not be deliberately provoked.

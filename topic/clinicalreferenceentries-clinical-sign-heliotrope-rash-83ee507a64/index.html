Definition

Heliotrope rash is an observed periocular sign. It is a clue to dermatomyositis. A positive finding means violaceous eyelid discoloration often accompanies edema.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Heliotrope rash means violaceous eyelid discoloration often accompanies edema.

Why It Matters

The observed Heliotrope rash finding can signal dermatomyositis. Interface inflammation and vascular injury affect thin periocular skin. Heliotrope rash cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • dermatomyositis

Assessment Method

Inspect eyelids and periorbital tissues.

Positive Finding

Violaceous eyelid discoloration often accompanies edema.

Mechanism

Interface inflammation and vascular injury affect thin periocular skin.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Heliotrope rash finding raises concern for dermatomyositis because interface inflammation and vascular injury affect thin periocular skin. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Heliotrope rash does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Heliotrope rash to focus evaluation of dermatomyositis and the reviewed alternatives: angioedema, contact dermatitis, orbital cellulitis. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect eyelids and periorbital tissues. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Heliotrope rash as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Pigment, cosmetics, and edema can alter appearance. Therefore, Heliotrope rash cannot confirm or exclude dermatomyositis by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • angioedema

  • contact dermatitis

  • orbital cellulitis

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as angioedema, contact dermatitis, orbital cellulitis.

False Negative Situations

Heliotrope rash may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of dermatomyositis.

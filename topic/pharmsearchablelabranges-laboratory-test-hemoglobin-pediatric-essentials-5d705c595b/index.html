Name

Hemoglobin

Range

0-1 mo 13.4-19.9 g/dL; 1-5 yr 10.9-15.0; 5-11 yr 11.9-15.0; 11-18 yr female 11.9-15.0, male 12.7-17.7

Why

Newborn hemoglobin is higher, then falls during infancy. Do not grade pediatric anemia from adult memory.

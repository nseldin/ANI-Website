Used To Treat

Type 2 diabetes glucose management; selected agents also reduce heart failure, kidney, weight, or cardiovascular risk.

Mechanism

Rosiglitazone works by activating nuclear PPAR-gamma receptors, changing transcription in adipose and insulin-sensitive tissues to improve insulin sensitivity. The same fluid-retention and adipose effects explain edema, heart-failure worsening, weight gain, fracture risk, and delayed onset.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Rosiglitazone in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Rosiglitazone is a thiazolidinedione PPAR-gamma agonist antidiabetic: it works by activating nuclear PPAR-gamma receptors, changing transcription in adipose and insulin-sensitive tissues to improve insulin sensitivity. It is used for type 2 diabetes glucose management; selected agents also reduce heart failure, kidney, weight, or cardiovascular risk.

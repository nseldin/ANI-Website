Used To Treat

Toxicity, overdose, reversal, or rescue situations for a specific medication or poison exposure.

Mechanism

Clopidogrel works by blocking the platelet P2Y12 ADP receptor, reducing GP IIb/IIIa activation and platelet aggregation. This prevents arterial thrombosis after ACS/stents but increases bleeding; adherence after stent placement is a major safety point.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warning: reduced effectiveness in CYP2C19 poor metabolizers.

Contraindications

  • Active pathological bleeding such as intracranial bleed or active GI bleed.

  • Severe hypersensitivity.

  • Active bleeding, suspected intracranial hemorrhage, recent major surgery/trauma, severe uncontrolled hypertension, and other protocol-specific exclusions.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess bleeding and bruising.

  • Often used after stent; do not stop without prescriber direction.

  • Report black stools, hematuria, severe headache, neurologic changes.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

  • Subclass specificity: Clopidogrel is categorized here as P2Y12 receptor inhibitor antiplatelet. Pathway: Hematology medication > Antiplatelet > P2Y12 receptor inhibitor. This subtype shapes the expected mechanism, contraindications, boxed/high-alert warnings, monitoring, interactions, and patient teaching.

Interactions

  • Anticoagulants, NSAIDs, aspirin, SSRIs/SNRIs may increase bleeding risk.

  • Omeprazole/esomeprazole can reduce activation.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • CBC

  • hemoglobin/hematocrit

  • Airway, breathing, circulation, mental status, ECG, glucose, electrolytes, renal/liver function, coagulation studies, drug levels when available, poison-control guidance, and recurrence after short-acting antidotes.

Nclex Traps

  • A client after stent stopping clopidogrel can thrombose the stent.

  • Antidote does not replace ABCs. Stabilize airway/breathing/circulation and monitor for recurrent toxicity.

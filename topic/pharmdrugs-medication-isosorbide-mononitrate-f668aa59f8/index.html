Used To Treat

Long-acting angina prevention.

Mechanism

Isosorbide mononitrate works by donating nitric-oxide-related vasodilator activity, reducing preload and/or afterload so myocardial oxygen demand falls.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess blood pressure, orthostasis, headache, chest pain pattern, and recent PDE-5 inhibitor use.

  • Teach that this prevents angina and is not the fastest rescue medication for acute chest pain unless specifically ordered.

Contraindications

  • Do not combine with PDE-5 inhibitors or riociguat because severe hypotension can occur.

Interactions

  • PDE-5 inhibitors, riociguat, alcohol, and other antihypertensives increase hypotension risk.

Key Labs

  • No routine serum drug level. Monitor blood pressure and angina frequency.

Nclex Traps

  • Long-acting nitrate prevention is different from immediate rescue logic. Hypotension and PDE-5 inhibitors remain priority safety checks.

Description

Isosorbide mononitrate is a nitrate antianginal: it works by donating nitric-oxide-related vasodilator activity, reducing preload and/or afterload so myocardial oxygen demand falls. It is used for long-acting angina prevention.

Used To Treat

Nausea and vomiting due to surgery/anesthesia, chemotherapy, migraine, vestibular disorders, pregnancy, gastroparesis, or GI illness depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Psyllium works by absorbing water as soluble fiber to form a gel that increases stool bulk and can modestly improve LDL/glucose patterns. It must be taken with enough fluid and separated from some medications.

Description

Psyllium is a bulk-forming soluble fiber laxative: it works by absorbing water as soluble fiber to form a gel that increases stool bulk and can modestly improve LDL/glucose patterns. It is used for bowel symptom control, constipation relief, bowel preparation, secretion thinning, or diarrhea management depending on the specific agent and contraindications.

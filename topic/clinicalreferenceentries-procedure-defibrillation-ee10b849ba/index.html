Summary

Defibrillation is a high-yield Cardiovascular diagnostic reference. Delivers unsynchronized electrical energy for pulseless ventricular tachycardia or ventricular fibrillation.

Quick Answer

Defibrillation: Delivers unsynchronized electrical energy for pulseless ventricular tachycardia or ventricular fibrillation. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Delivers unsynchronized electrical energy for pulseless ventricular tachycardia or ventricular fibrillation.

    • Before / preparation

    • For pulseless ventricular tachycardia or ventricular fibrillation, prioritize CPR, defibrillator readiness, pad placement, clear communication, and rapid shock per algorithm.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Resume CPR immediately after shock per resuscitation algorithm, reassess rhythm/pulse at appropriate intervals, and continue airway, IV/IO, medication, and reversible-cause priorities.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Persistent pulseless rhythm, delayed CPR, unsafe contact during shock, failure to clear, or rhythm misidentification.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Defibrillation is unsynchronized for pulseless VT/VF. Do not delay shock for lengthy assessment when the rhythm and pulseless status are clear.

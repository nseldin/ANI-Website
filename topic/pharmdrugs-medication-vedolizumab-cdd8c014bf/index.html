Used To Treat

Targeted treatment for autoimmune/inflammatory disease, cancer, asthma/allergy, migraine prevention, osteoporosis, infections, or transplant-related indications depending on the antibody.

Mechanism

Vedolizumab works by binding a specific immune, inflammatory, cancer, or receptor target. The nursing lens is infection risk, infusion reactions, screening needs, and target-specific toxicity.

Boxed Warning

Warnings are product-specific; serious infection, malignancy risk, infusion reactions, hypersensitivity, and immunization restrictions are common high-yield themes.

Contraindications

  • Serious hypersensitivity

  • active serious infection for many immunosuppressive biologics

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Screen for infection/TB/hepatitis when indicated.

  • Monitor infusion/injection reactions.

  • Avoid live vaccines during significant immunosuppression unless directed.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Other immunosuppressants increase infection risk.

  • Live vaccines may be unsafe.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • CBC

  • LFTs

  • TB/hepatitis screening when indicated

  • infection assessment

  • QT risk, sedation, EPS, anticholinergic effects, bowel obstruction concern, hydration, and serotonin syndrome risk when relevant.

Nclex Traps

  • Fever on a biologic can be a serious infection cue, not just a nuisance side effect.

  • Different antiemetics have different dangers: ondansetron/QT, metoclopramide/EPS-tardive dyskinesia, promethazine/sedation-tissue injury.

Description

Vedolizumab is a gut-selective alpha4beta7 integrin antagonist humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody: it works by binding a specific immune, inflammatory, cancer, or receptor target. It is used for targeted treatment for autoimmune/inflammatory disease, cancer, asthma/allergy, migraine prevention, osteoporosis, infections, or transplant-related indications depending on the antibody.

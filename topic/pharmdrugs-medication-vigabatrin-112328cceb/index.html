Used To Treat

Seizure prevention/treatment; selected agents are also used for bipolar disorder, neuropathic pain, migraine prevention, or essential tremor.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary by drug; many antiseizure drugs carry suicidality warnings and serious rash, blood, liver, or pregnancy risks for the specific agent.

Mechanism

Vigabatrin works by stabilizing neuronal firing through sodium channels, calcium channels, GABA, glutamate, or synaptic-vesicle targets depending on the agent. The nurse connects this to CNS toxicity, rash risk, pregnancy risk, and drug levels when ordered.

Nursing Essentials

  • Do not stop abruptly.

  • Monitor seizure frequency, CNS depression, rash, suicidality, pregnancy risk, and drug-specific labs.

  • Maintain safety/seizure precautions.

Contraindications

  • Serious hypersensitivity/rash history to related drug

  • pregnancy risk requires clarification for teratogenic agents

Interactions

  • CNS depressants increase sedation.

  • Enzyme-inducing agents affect contraceptives, warfarin, and many drugs.

Key Labs

  • LFTs

  • sodium for carbamazepine/oxcarbazepine

Nclex Traps

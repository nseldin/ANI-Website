Summary

Toe-Brachial Index (TBI) is a high-yield Cardiovascular diagnostic reference. Compares toe and brachial pressures when ABI may be falsely elevated, especially with calcified vessels in diabetes or kidney disease.

Quick Answer

Toe-Brachial Index (TBI): Compares toe and brachial pressures when ABI may be falsely elevated, especially with calcified vessels in diabetes or kidney disease. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Mechanism

Rizatriptan stimulates 5-HT1B/1D receptors, causing cranial vasoconstriction and reduced trigeminal neuropeptide release. Screen vascular disease, uncontrolled hypertension, serotonin-risk combinations, and medication-overuse headache.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for rizatriptan in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Rizatriptan is a triptan and selective 5-HT1B/1D receptor agonist: it stimulates 5-HT1B/1D receptors, causing cranial vasoconstriction and reduced trigeminal neuropeptide release. It is used for acute migraine treatment after migraine has started; not prevention.

Class

ACE inhibitor antihypertensive

Used To Treat

Hypertension, heart failure, post-MI cardiac protection, diabetic kidney protection/proteinuria reduction, and chronic kidney disease risk reduction when appropriate.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warning: fetal toxicity; discontinue as soon as pregnancy is detected.

Mechanism

Accupril works by reducing angiotensin II and aldosterone. That lowers vasoconstriction and fluid retention but raises risks for hypotension, hyperkalemia, renal perfusion changes, cough, and angioedema.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor BP, potassium, creatinine, cough, angioedema.

  • Avoid in pregnancy.

  • Clarify potassium supplements/salt substitutes.

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy

  • history of ACE-inhibitor angioedema

  • aliskiren use in diabetes for many agents

Interactions

  • Potassium supplements/salt substitutes, spironolactone, ARBs, NSAIDs, lithium.

Description

Accupril is an ACE inhibitor antihypertensive: it works by reducing angiotensin II and aldosterone. It is used for hypertension, heart failure, post-MI cardiac protection, diabetic kidney protection/proteinuria reduction, and chronic kidney disease risk reduction when appropriate.

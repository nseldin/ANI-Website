Used To Treat

Mechanism

Isosorbide dinitrate works by donating nitric-oxide-related vasodilator activity, reducing preload and/or afterload so myocardial oxygen demand falls.

Nursing Essentials

  • Check blood pressure, headache, dizziness, syncope risk, and recent phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor use.

  • Teach nitrate-free interval if prescribed to reduce tolerance.

Contraindications

  • Do not give with recent sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil, or riociguat because severe hypotension can occur.

Interactions

  • PDE-5 inhibitors and riociguat can cause life-threatening hypotension with nitrates.

  • Alcohol and other vasodilators can increase hypotension.

Key Labs

  • No routine serum drug level. Monitor blood pressure, chest pain pattern, and orthostatic symptoms.

Nclex Traps

  • Nitrates relieve/prevent ischemic chest pain by lowering oxygen demand, but hypotension and PDE-5 inhibitor use are stop-and-question cues.

Description

Isosorbide dinitrate is a nitrate antianginal: it works by donating nitric-oxide-related vasodilator activity, reducing preload and/or afterload so myocardial oxygen demand falls. It is used for angina prevention and selected heart failure regimens when ordered.

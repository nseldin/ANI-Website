Class

Artemisinin antimalarial

Used To Treat

Uncomplicated malaria due to susceptible Plasmodium species when paired with lumefantrine.

Mechanism

Artemether works by using agent-specific parasite targets: artemisinins create peroxide-mediated blood-stage injury, quinine/chloroquine impair heme detoxification, 8-aminoquinolines target liver forms, antifolates impair folate/DNA pathways, and atovaquone targets mitochondrial electron transport. Species, resistance, G6PD status, QT risk, retinal monitoring, food-dependent absorption, and adherence determine safety.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess fever pattern, travel exposure, pregnancy status, ECG/QT risk, and ability to take doses with food.

  • Teach completion of the full course even when fever improves.

Contraindications

  • Clarify severe malaria, significant QT prolongation risk, and interacting QT-prolonging medications.

Interactions

  • QT-prolonging drugs, strong CYP3A4 modifiers, and some antiretrovirals may require clarification.

Key Labs

  • Parasitemia if ordered, glucose in severe illness, electrolytes if QT risk, and clinical fever trend.

Nclex Traps

  • Rapid symptom improvement is not permission to stop therapy early.

Description

Artemether is an artemisinin antimalarial: it works by using agent-specific parasite targets: artemisinins create peroxide-mediated blood-stage injury, quinine/chloroquine impair heme detoxification, 8-aminoquinolines target liver forms, antifolates impair folate/DNA pathways, and atovaquone targets mitochondrial electron transport. It is used for uncomplicated malaria due to susceptible Plasmodium species when paired with lumefantrine.

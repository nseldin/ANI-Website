Used To Treat

Inflammation, asthma/COPD exacerbations, allergic reactions, autoimmune disease, adrenal insufficiency replacement, cerebral edema, and transplant/oncology protocols.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; major concerns include immunosuppression, adrenal suppression, hyperglycemia, GI bleeding, mood changes, osteoporosis, and infection masking.

Mechanism

Triamcinolone works by diffusing into cells and activating cytosolic glucocorticoid receptors that move to the nucleus, change transcription, suppress phospholipase A2/COX-2/cytokine signaling, and reduce inflammatory cell trafficking. Benefit is less inflammation/immune activation; risks are hyperglycemia, infection masking, adrenal suppression, mood changes, myopathy, bone loss, skin thinning, and route-specific effects such as oral thrush or ocular pressure.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor glucose, infection, GI bleeding, mood, BP, fluid retention.

  • Do not stop chronic therapy abruptly.

  • Taper if ordered.

Contraindications

  • Systemic fungal infection for many systemic steroids

  • live vaccines with immunosuppressive doses

Interactions

  • NSAIDs increase GI bleeding.

  • Diabetes meds may need adjustment.

  • Warfarin effect can change.

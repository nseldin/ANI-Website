Definition

Ballotable kidney sign is a bimanual palpation sign. It is a clue to enlarged or unusually mobile kidney. A positive finding means a renal mass moves or rebounds between the hands.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Ballotable kidney sign means a renal mass moves or rebounds between the hands.

Why It Matters

A positive Ballotable kidney sign response can signal enlarged or unusually mobile kidney. A retroperitoneal mobile kidney can be displaced anteriorly against the examining hand. Ballotable kidney sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • enlarged or unusually mobile kidney

Assessment Method

Place one hand posteriorly below the rib and the other anteriorly, then gently displace the organ between hands.

Positive Finding

A renal mass moves or rebounds between the hands.

Mechanism

A retroperitoneal mobile kidney can be displaced anteriorly against the examining hand.

Clinical Interpretation

A positive Ballotable kidney sign response raises concern for enlarged or unusually mobile kidney because a retroperitoneal mobile kidney can be displaced anteriorly against the examining hand. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Ballotable kidney sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Ballotable kidney sign to focus evaluation of enlarged or unusually mobile kidney and the reviewed alternatives: splenomegaly, liver mass, colon mass. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Place one hand posteriorly below the rib and the other anteriorly, then gently displace the organ between hands. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Ballotable kidney sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Obesity, guarding, and other abdominal masses limit localization. Therefore, Ballotable kidney sign cannot confirm or exclude enlarged or unusually mobile kidney by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • splenomegaly

  • liver mass

  • colon mass

False Positive Situations

A false-positive maneuver can result from incorrect position or force, pain from splenomegaly, liver mass, colon mass, guarding, or calling an incompletely defined response positive.

False Negative Situations

Ballotable kidney sign may be missed with incomplete technique, guarding, early or partial disease, limited cooperation, or a response too subtle for the examiner.

Modern Status

Historical adjunct; imaging defines masses. Ballotable kidney sign remains searchable because learners still encounter the term, but it cannot confirm or exclude enlarged or unusually mobile kidney. Apply the reviewed limitation for Ballotable kidney sign: Obesity, guarding, and other abdominal masses limit localization.

Safety Precautions

Before assessing Ballotable kidney sign, explain the method, obtain consent, preserve privacy, and use only the technique described here: Place one hand posteriorly below the rib and the other anteriorly, then gently displace the organ between hands. Stop if that technique becomes unsafe. The reviewed precaution for this maneuver is: Do not deeply ballot a painful, traumatized, transplant, or aneurysmal abdomen.

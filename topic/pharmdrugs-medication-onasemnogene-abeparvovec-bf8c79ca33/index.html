Class

Single-dose AAV9 vector-mediated SMN gene-replacement therapy

Used To Treat

ZOLGENSMA: IV treatment of children younger than 2 years with SMA and biallelic SMN1 mutation. ITVISMA: intrathecal treatment of adult and pediatric patients age 2 years and older with a confirmed SMN1 mutation; product eligibility is not interchangeable.

Boxed Warning

Serious liver injury: both products can cause acute serious liver injury or liver failure, especially with preexisting hepatic abnormalities. Assess hepatic function, administer systemic corticosteroid before and after the single dose, and monitor AST, ALT, bilirubin, PT/INR and clinical liver status for at least 3 months according to the exact product. Immediate escalation is required for jaundice, vomiting, lethargy, coagulopathy or rapidly rising enzymes.

Mechanism

A replication-incompetent self-complementary AAV9 capsid enters target cells and releases DNA encoding human SMN under a constitutive promoter. The double-stranded-ready construct supports rapid transcription without integrating predictably into chromosomes. Nondividing neurons may maintain episomes, while expression can dilute in dividing tissues; neutralizing immunity also makes repeat AAV exposure unsafe or ineffective.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm exact suffix/product, age, weight if IV, genotype, prior AAV9 gene therapy, anti-AAV9 testing when required, liver status, infection, vaccination timing, platelets, troponin, renal status and corticosteroid plan. Patients previously treated with ZOLGENSMA should not receive ITVISMA.

  • Monitor thrombocytopenia, thrombotic microangiopathy, cardiac injury/troponin, systemic immune response, sensory neurologic toxicity after intrathecal delivery, infection and theoretical vector-integration tumor risk; use biohazard handling for shedding as instructed.

Key Labs

  • AST, ALT, total/direct bilirubin, PT/INR, albumin, platelet count, creatinine/urinalysis, troponin I and product-specific anti-AAV9 or other baseline testing; serial motor, respiratory and bulbar outcomes.

Nclex Traps

  • ZOLGENSMA and ITVISMA contain related SMN gene-transfer platforms but differ in suffix, route, age group and dose; never treat them as interchangeable formulations.

  • A one-time dose still requires months of monitoring and lifelong awareness of prior AAV exposure.

Description

Onasemnogene abeparvovec is an AAV9 vector biologic and gene therapy that delivers functional human SMN DNA so motor neurons and other transduced cells can produce survival motor neuron protein despite defective SMN1. The generic concept now spans two distinct U.S. products: weight-based IV ZOLGENSMA for selected children under 2 and fixed-dose intrathecal ITVISMA for patients age 2 and older. It is not CRISPR editing, and the vector DNA mainly persists as episomes rather than repairing the native gene.

Definition

Toxocara canis is a clinically important nematode (roundworm) associated with visceral larva migrans, ocular toxocariasis, and covert toxocariasis. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

Summary

Fetal fibronectin is a high-yield Obstetrics/Gynecology diagnostic reference. Vaginal/cervical secretion test used in selected clients to help assess short-term preterm birth risk.

Quick Answer

Fetal fibronectin: Vaginal/cervical secretion test used in selected clients to help assess short-term preterm birth risk. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Negative

    Meaning

    Short-term preterm birth risk is low in the appropriate symptomatic client and gestational-age window.

  • Label

    Positive

    Meaning

    Fetal fibronectin is present and preterm birth risk is higher, but the result is not a diagnosis of imminent delivery.

  • Label

    Clinical significance

    Meaning

    Use with gestational age, cervical length, contractions, rupture of membranes, bleeding, infection, and provider plan.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Vaginal/cervical secretion test used in selected clients to help assess short-term preterm birth risk.

    • Before / preparation

    • Avoid factors that can distort results per protocol, such as recent intercourse, cervical exam, bleeding, or ruptured membranes depending instructions.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Monitor maternal vital signs, fetal status when indicated, uterine activity, bleeding, fluid leakage, infection cues, and decreased fetal movement.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Decreased fetal movement, nonreassuring fetal tracing, bleeding, leaking fluid, fever, severe abdominal pain, contractions after an invasive procedure, or hypertensive warning signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • A negative fetal fibronectin is most useful for low short-term preterm-birth risk; a positive result is less specific.

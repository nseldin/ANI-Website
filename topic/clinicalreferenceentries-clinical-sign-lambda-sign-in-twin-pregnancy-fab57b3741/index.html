Definition

Lambda sign in twin pregnancy is an ultrasound imaging sign. It is a clue to dichorionic diamniotic twin placentation. A positive finding means a triangular wedge of chorion extends between amnion layers.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Lambda sign in twin pregnancy means a triangular wedge of chorion extends between amnion layers.

Why It Matters

The Lambda sign in twin pregnancy imaging pattern can signal dichorionic diamniotic twin placentation. Two chorionic sacs meet and create a thick chorionic projection at placental insertion. Lambda sign in twin pregnancy cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

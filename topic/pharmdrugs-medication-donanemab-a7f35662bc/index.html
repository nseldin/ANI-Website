Used To Treat

Alzheimer disease in patients with mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia stage and confirmed amyloid pathology, with product-specific MRI surveillance and the option to consider stopping after amyloid is reduced to the labeled PET threshold.

Boxed Warning

Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities: serious and fatal ARIA and intracerebral hemorrhage can occur. Confirm amyloid, obtain baseline MRI and MRIs before the label-specified early infusions, and discuss ApoE epsilon4 testing because homozygotes have greater symptomatic/severe risk. Use special caution with antithrombotic or thrombolytic exposure and hold/escalate compatible neurologic symptoms.

Mechanism

Donanemab recognizes plaque-associated pyroglutamate amyloid-beta and promotes microglial removal of deposited amyloid. Rapid plaque and vascular-amyloid change can destabilize small vessels, producing edema or hemorrhage. This links the mechanism directly to baseline and pre-infusion MRI surveillance and to urgent assessment of headache, confusion, vision or gait change, focal deficit, nausea or seizure.

Description

Donanemab is a disease-modifying humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody that binds an N-terminal pyroglutamate amyloid-beta epitope concentrated in deposited plaque, recruiting immune clearance in early symptomatic Alzheimer disease. It does not reverse established neuronal loss. Its defining risk is ARIA-E edema/effusion and ARIA-H microhemorrhage or siderosis, especially in ApoE epsilon4 homozygotes.

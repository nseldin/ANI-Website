Summary

OGTT (oral glucose tolerance test) is a high-yield Endocrine diagnostic reference. Oral glucose tolerance test evaluates glucose handling, including gestational diabetes workup.

Quick Answer

OGTT (oral glucose tolerance test): Oral glucose tolerance test evaluates glucose handling, including gestational diabetes workup. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Normal glucose handling

    Meaning

    Glucose rises after the oral load and returns within expected limits.

  • Label

    Abnormal / elevated

    Meaning

    Glucose remains too high after the load, supporting impaired glucose tolerance, diabetes, or gestational diabetes depending protocol and pregnancy status.

  • Label

    Clinical significance

    Meaning

    Correct fasting, timing, and full sample sequence matter; vomiting or missed timing can invalidate the test.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Oral glucose tolerance test evaluates glucose handling, including gestational diabetes workup.

    • Before / preparation

    • Check timing, fasting status, current endocrine medications, steroid use, pregnancy status when relevant, and whether dynamic testing requires serial specimens.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Connect the result to symptoms, glucose safety, thyroid/adrenal crisis risk, and medication teaching.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Severe hypoglycemia/hyperglycemia, thyroid storm/myxedema cues, adrenal crisis, altered mental status, dehydration, or unstable vital signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Endocrine tests often require correct timing. Wrong timing can make the result misleading.

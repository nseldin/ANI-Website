Category

Liver labs

Name

ALT

Range

Often about 7-56 U/L, facility dependent.

Why

ALT is more liver-specific than AST and rises with hepatocellular injury.

Aliases

  • alanine aminotransferase

  • SGPT

High Causes

  • Viral hepatitis

  • Medication toxicity

  • Fatty liver disease

  • Ischemic hepatitis

  • Alcohol-associated liver injury

Low Causes

  • Low ALT is usually not the safety problem.

Critical Concerns

  • ALT elevation plus jaundice, RUQ pain, coagulopathy, or altered mental status signals high concern.

Nursing Considerations

  • Check acetaminophen exposure, alcohol use, viral risk, and hepatotoxic meds.

Nclex Traps

  • A falling ALT is not always improvement if liver failure is worsening and cells can no longer leak enzymes.

Definition

Müller sign is an observed pulsation sign. It is a clue to severe aortic regurgitation. A positive finding means the uvula pulsates in synchrony with the heartbeat.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Müller sign means the uvula pulsates in synchrony with the heartbeat.

Why It Matters

The observed Müller sign finding can signal severe aortic regurgitation. Wide pulse-pressure waves transmit visible movement to highly vascular soft tissue. Müller sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • severe aortic regurgitation

Assessment Method

Observe the uvula in good light without touching it.

Positive Finding

The uvula pulsates in synchrony with the heartbeat.

Mechanism

Wide pulse-pressure waves transmit visible movement to highly vascular soft tissue.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Müller sign finding raises concern for severe aortic regurgitation because wide pulse-pressure waves transmit visible movement to highly vascular soft tissue. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Müller sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Müller sign to focus evaluation of severe aortic regurgitation and the reviewed alternatives: gag movement, palatal tremor, anxiety. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Observe the uvula in good light without touching it. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Müller sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Visualization and normal vascular motion limit reproducibility. Therefore, Müller sign cannot confirm or exclude severe aortic regurgitation by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • gag movement

  • palatal tremor

  • anxiety

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as gag movement, palatal tremor, anxiety.

False Negative Situations

Müller sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of severe aortic regurgitation.

Modern Status

Historical adjunct; very low practical value. Müller sign remains searchable because learners still encounter the term, but it cannot confirm or exclude severe aortic regurgitation. Apply the reviewed limitation for Müller sign: Visualization and normal vascular motion limit reproducibility.

Safety Precautions

Observe Müller sign with consent, privacy, adequate exposure, and only the manipulation required by this assessment: Observe the uvula in good light without touching it. The reviewed precaution for this finding is: Do not provoke gagging merely to seek this historical sign.

Definition

Calcinosis cutis sign is an observed and palpated deposit sign. It is a clue to dystrophic calcium deposition in connective-tissue disease. A positive finding means hard white or yellow nodules may extrude chalk-like material.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Calcinosis cutis sign means hard white or yellow nodules may extrude chalk-like material.

Why It Matters

The observed Calcinosis cutis sign finding can signal dystrophic calcium deposition in connective-tissue disease. Damaged tissue nucleates calcium-phosphate deposition despite often normal serum calcium. Calcinosis cutis sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • dystrophic calcium deposition in connective-tissue disease

Assessment Method

Inspect and palpate firm subcutaneous nodules, especially at pressure sites.

Positive Finding

Hard white or yellow nodules may extrude chalk-like material.

Mechanism

Damaged tissue nucleates calcium-phosphate deposition despite often normal serum calcium.

The observed Calcinosis cutis sign finding raises concern for dystrophic calcium deposition in connective-tissue disease because damaged tissue nucleates calcium-phosphate deposition despite often normal serum calcium. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Calcinosis cutis sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Use Calcinosis cutis sign to focus evaluation of dystrophic calcium deposition in connective-tissue disease and the reviewed alternatives: tophaceous gout, osteoma cutis, infected cyst. Evidence Limitations

Gouty tophi and foreign bodies can look similar. Therefore, Calcinosis cutis sign cannot confirm or exclude dystrophic calcium deposition in connective-tissue disease by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as tophaceous gout, osteoma cutis, infected cyst.

False Negative Situations

Calcinosis cutis sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of dystrophic calcium deposition in connective-tissue disease.

Summary

Fern test is a high-yield Obstetrics diagnostic reference. Microscopic ferning pattern supports rupture of membranes.

Quick Answer

Fern test: Microscopic ferning pattern supports rupture of membranes. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Negative

    Meaning

    No ferning pattern is seen; rupture of membranes is less supported but not fully excluded.

  • Label

    Positive

    Meaning

    A ferning/crystallization pattern supports amniotic fluid and rupture of membranes.

  • Label

    Clinical significance

    Meaning

    Use with pooling, nitrazine, speculum exam, gestational age, infection signs, and fetal/maternal status.

Result Meaning Provenance

reviewed-canonical-profile

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Microscopic ferning pattern supports rupture of membranes.

    • Before / preparation

    • Confirm gestational age, fetal/maternal baseline status, Rh status when invasive testing is involved, and whether the client is having bleeding, contractions, or ruptured membranes.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Monitor maternal vital signs, fetal status when indicated, uterine activity, bleeding, fluid leakage, infection cues, and decreased fetal movement.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Decreased fetal movement, nonreassuring fetal tracing, bleeding, leaking fluid, fever, severe abdominal pain, contractions after an invasive procedure, or hypertensive warning signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Ferning supports ROM, but the clinical picture and provider evaluation still matter.

Description

Benzodiazepines are CNS depressants that amplify GABA-A chloride-channel inhibition, quickly reducing anxiety, seizures, alcohol-withdrawal hyperexcitability, muscle spasm, or procedural distress. The same mechanism creates sedation, falls, delirium, dependence, withdrawal seizures, and respiratory-depression danger when combined with opioids, alcohol, or other depressants.

Mechanism

They bind an allosteric site on GABA-A receptors and increase the frequency of chloride-channel opening when GABA is present, hyperpolarizing neurons and reducing CNS excitability.

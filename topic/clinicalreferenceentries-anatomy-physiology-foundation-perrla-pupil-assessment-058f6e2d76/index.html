PERRLA pupil assessment

Quick Answer

Document measured pupil size and the observed response; do not chart PERRLA automatically when a component was not tested or when baseline asymmetry exists.

Sections

  • Label

    Technique

    Text

    Compare pupils in ambient light, measure size, inspect shape, assess direct and consensual light response, and test near response only when appropriate. Consider lighting, eye disease, surgery, medicines, trauma, and baseline photographs or records.

  • Label

    Why findings change

    Text

    Pupil size and reactivity reflect retinal input, cranial nerves II and III, autonomic pathways, iris function, and drug effects. An abnormal finding therefore localizes a pathway imperfectly and must be interpreted with the full neurologic and eye examination.

  • Label

    Urgent change

    Text

    New unequal or fixed pupils with headache, trauma, ptosis, eye-movement abnormality, altered consciousness, weakness, or severe eye pain require urgent evaluation.

  • Label

    Documentation pitfall

    Text

    A normal acronym can hide meaningful measurements. Record right and left size, reactivity, timing, symptoms, and changes from baseline.

Source Note

Summary

PERRLA is documentation shorthand for pupil size and symmetry, shape, reaction to light, and accommodation when accommodation is actually assessed.

    Expansion
    • Cue

      P

      Meaning

      Pupils. The observation concerns the right and left pupils and their measured behavior.

    • Cue

      E

      Meaning

      Equal. Symmetry can be compared, while baseline physiologic anisocoria must be considered.

    • Cue

      R

      Meaning

      Round. Shape may change with injury, surgery, iris disease, or congenital variation.

    • Cue

      R2

      Meaning

      Reactive. Constriction demonstrates an intact response pathway but must be graded and timed.

    • Cue

      L

      Meaning

      Light. Direct and consensual light responses test afferent and efferent pathways.

    • Cue

      A

      Meaning

      Accommodation. Near response is a distinct assessment and should be charted only when actually tested.

    Limitations

    • The acronym can hide measured size and response quality.

    • Do not chart accommodation when it was not tested.

    • Normal pupils do not exclude serious neurologic disease.

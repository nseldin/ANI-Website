Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity to salmon calcitonin or formulation

  • Correct hypocalcemia and mineral deficiency

  • Use alternatives when better-established osteoporosis treatment is appropriate

Nursing Essentials

  • For hypercalcemia, trend calcium and recognize tachyphylaxis; treat the cause and arrange definitive therapy.

  • Inspect nasal mucosa periodically for nasal products and reassess ongoing benefit versus malignancy risk.

Interactions

  • Can increase renal lithium clearance and lower lithium concentration.

  • Other calcium-lowering therapies compound hypocalcemia.

  • Antibody formation can reduce calcitonin response.

Key Labs

  • Calcium, phosphorus, magnesium and vitamin D

  • Alkaline phosphatase and clinical Paget response

  • Nasal examination by route and lithium concentration when relevant

Nclex Traps

  • Calcitonin can lower calcium quickly but tachyphylaxis limits durability.

  • Nasal and injectable indications are not identical.

  • Fracture reduction has not been demonstrated for every labeled osteoporosis formulation/context.

Used To Treat

Injection treats selected symptomatic Paget disease when alternatives are unsuitable, hypercalcemia, and selected postmenopausal osteoporosis when alternatives are unsuitable; nasal products have narrower osteoporosis labeling. Fracture-efficacy limitations and malignancy risk constrain long-term use.

Mechanism

Calcitonin receptor activation couples mainly to Gs-cAMP/PKA and disrupts osteoclast ruffled-border motility, proton secretion and attachment. Renal calcium/phosphate excretion can also increase. Receptor internalization/downregulation and downstream desensitization reduce response after repeated exposure, explaining why an initial calcium fall can fade within days and why calcitonin is not definitive treatment for many hypercalcemic states.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Serious or fatal hypersensitivity can occur, particularly with salmon-derived peptide; consider skin testing when allergy is suspected. Correct hypocalcemia and vitamin-D deficiency first. A meta-analysis found higher malignancy incidence than placebo, so periodically re-evaluate continued treatment.

Description

Calcitonin is a peptide hormone that binds calcitonin receptors on osteoclasts and rapidly suppresses bone resorption, lowering calcium flux from bone. It can help acute hypercalcemia or Paget-related high turnover, but receptor desensitization produces tachyphylaxis and weakens sustained calcium lowering. Long-term osteoporosis use is limited by weaker fracture evidence and a malignancy signal.

Adverse Effects

  • Nausea, vomiting, flushing and injection-site inflammation

  • Hypocalcemia with cramps, tetany or seizure

  • Nasal irritation, epistaxis, ulceration and septal injury by intranasal route

  • Hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis and possible malignancy risk

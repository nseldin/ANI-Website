Used To Treat

Pain, fever, and inflammation. Selected NSAIDs are also used for arthritis, dysmenorrhea, gout flares, or patent ductus arteriosus closure.

Mechanism

Meloxicam works by inhibiting cyclooxygenase-mediated prostaglandin production. That lowers pain/inflammation but can reduce renal blood flow and gastric protection while increasing bleeding/CV risk.

Boxed Warning

Class boxed warnings commonly include increased risk of serious cardiovascular thrombotic events and serious GI bleeding/ulceration/perforation.

Contraindications

  • Perioperative CABG pain

  • active serious GI bleeding

  • serious NSAID hypersensitivity

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess GI bleed risk, kidney function, BP/heart failure, anticoagulant use, and allergy/asthma history.

  • Use cautiously in renal disease, older adults, heart failure, and anticoagulated clients.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Anticoagulants/antiplatelets/SSRIs increase bleeding risk.

  • ACE/ARB/diuretic combination increases kidney injury risk.

  • Lithium and methotrexate toxicity risk can increase.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • creatinine/eGFR

  • hemoglobin/hematocrit if bleeding risk

  • blood pressure

  • Bleeding, GI pain, renal function, blood pressure, edema, anticoagulant/antiplatelet use, and asthma sensitivity.

Nclex Traps

  • NSAIDs can worsen kidney perfusion and heart failure; they are not benign OTC meds.

  • NSAIDs can worsen kidney function, GI bleeding, heart failure fluid retention, and bleeding risk.

Description

Meloxicam is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID): it works by inhibiting cyclooxygenase-mediated prostaglandin production. It is used for pain, fever, and inflammation. Selected NSAIDs are also used for arthritis, dysmenorrhea, gout flares, or patent ductus arteriosus closure.

Name

ABCDE primary assessment

Nclex Essential

true

Quick Answer

Assess and treat life threats as they are found, call for help early, and restart the sequence after interventions or any deterioration.

Sections

  • Label

    Treat while assessing

    Text

    The primary survey is not a passive checklist. Open or protect an airway, support oxygenation and ventilation, control catastrophic bleeding, support perfusion, correct immediately reversible neurologic threats, and prevent heat loss within role and protocol.

  • Label

    Repeated sequence

    Text

    An intervention can change the next finding, and a previously stable domain can worsen. Repeat ABCDE after treatment, transfer, procedure, medication, or clinical change.

  • Label

    Context

    Text

    Trauma, medical illness, pregnancy, pediatrics, toxic exposure, and resuscitation require setting-specific modifications. Catastrophic external hemorrhage may be addressed before airway in protocols that use C-ABCDE.

  • Label

    Escalation

    Text

    Activate emergency response for threatened airway, severe respiratory distress, shock, major neurologic change, or uncontrolled exposure. Do not let documentation delay treatment.

Summary

ABCDE is a repeated primary survey that identifies and treats immediate threats to airway, breathing, circulation, neurologic function, and whole-body exposure or environmental control.

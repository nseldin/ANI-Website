Used To Treat

Selected ovarian, fallopian-tube, peritoneal, breast, pancreatic, and prostate cancers based on product-specific BRCA, homologous-recombination-repair, prior-treatment, and maintenance criteria.

Description

PARP inhibitors block repair of single-strand DNA damage and can trap PARP enzymes on DNA, turning ordinary replication into lethal double-strand injury. Tumors deficient in homologous recombination, especially BRCA1/2-deficient tumors, cannot accurately repair that damage, creating synthetic lethality; the same DNA-repair pressure explains marrow suppression, MDS/AML risk, and embryo-fetal toxicity.

Mechanism

PARP1/2 detect DNA breaks, add poly-ADP-ribose chains, and recruit repair proteins. Inhibition prevents catalytic repair and stabilizes PARP-DNA complexes; stalled replication forks collapse into double-strand breaks. Normal cells can use BRCA-dependent homologous recombination, but HRR-deficient tumor cells accumulate catastrophic genomic damage and undergo apoptosis.

Boxed Warning

High-yield class safety context: no single boxed warning applies to every PARP inhibitor. Monitor CBC for anemia, neutropenia, and thrombocytopenia; persistent cytopenia can signal therapy-related MDS/AML. Product-specific risks include olaparib pneumonitis/VTE, niraparib hypertension/PRES, and renal or transporter interaction differences.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify the exact biomarker, cancer, maintenance versus treatment setting, renal/hepatic dose context, and interacting drugs.

  • Trend CBC and symptoms of MDS/AML, pregnancy risk, fatigue, nausea, dyspnea, thrombosis, blood pressure, and neurologic changes according to the selected agent.

Key Labs

  • CBC at baseline and periodically, creatinine/eGFR, liver tests, pregnancy testing when applicable, tumor biomarker/HRR status, and blood pressure for niraparib.

Nclex Traps

  • PARP inhibition and BRCA deficiency are complementary defects; the drug does not repair BRCA.

  • Do not group all four agents under one toxicity checklist: niraparib's hypertension/PRES and olaparib's pneumonitis/VTE are differentiating clues.

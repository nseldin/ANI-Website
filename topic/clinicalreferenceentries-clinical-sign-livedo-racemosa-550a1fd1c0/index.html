Definition

Livedo racemosa is an observed vascular skin sign. It is a clue to pathologic medium-vessel or thrombotic disease. A positive finding means large incomplete irregular segments extend beyond a uniform net.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Livedo racemosa means large incomplete irregular segments extend beyond a uniform net.

Why It Matters

The observed Livedo racemosa finding can signal pathologic medium-vessel or thrombotic disease. Irregular obstruction of medium dermal arteries produces patchy downstream deoxygenation. Livedo racemosa cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • pathologic medium-vessel or thrombotic disease

Assessment Method

Inspect for broken irregular branching violaceous rings.

Positive Finding

Large incomplete irregular segments extend beyond a uniform net.

Mechanism

Irregular obstruction of medium dermal arteries produces patchy downstream deoxygenation.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Livedo racemosa finding raises concern for pathologic medium-vessel or thrombotic disease because irregular obstruction of medium dermal arteries produces patchy downstream deoxygenation. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Livedo racemosa does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Livedo racemosa to focus evaluation of pathologic medium-vessel or thrombotic disease and the reviewed alternatives: cholesterol emboli, cryoglobulinemia, vasculopathy. Evidence Limitations

Distinction from reticularis can be subjective. Therefore, Livedo racemosa cannot confirm or exclude pathologic medium-vessel or thrombotic disease by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as cholesterol emboli, cryoglobulinemia, vasculopathy.

False Negative Situations

Livedo racemosa may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of pathologic medium-vessel or thrombotic disease.

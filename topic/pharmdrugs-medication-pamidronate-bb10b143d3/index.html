Used To Treat

Osteoporosis treatment/prevention, glucocorticoid-induced bone loss, Paget disease, and malignancy-related bone disease/hypercalcemia.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; esophageal irritation, osteonecrosis of the jaw, atypical femur fracture, hypocalcemia, and renal restrictions matter.

Mechanism

Pamidronate works by binding hydroxyapatite avidly at active bone-remodeling surfaces, then inhibiting osteoclast-mediated bone resorption. Nitrogen-containing agents disrupt osteoclast mevalonate-pathway prenylation, causing osteoclast dysfunction/apoptosis; esophagitis precautions, hypocalcemia, renal function, osteonecrosis of the jaw, and atypical femur fracture matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Take oral doses with plain water; remain upright 30-60 minutes per product.

  • Assess dental issues, calcium/vitamin D, renal function.

Contraindications

  • Esophageal abnormalities delaying emptying for oral agents

  • inability to sit/stand upright

  • hypocalcemia

  • severe renal impairment for many agents

Interactions

  • Calcium/iron/antacids reduce absorption.

  • NSAIDs can worsen GI irritation.

Nclex Traps

  • Do not lie down after oral bisphosphonate; esophagitis risk is the board trap.

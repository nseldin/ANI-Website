Definition

Nephrotic edema sign pattern is an observed fluid-distribution sign. It is a clue to severe proteinuria with hypoalbuminemia. A positive finding means periorbital edema is often prominent in the morning with dependent pitting later.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Nephrotic edema sign pattern means periorbital edema is often prominent in the morning with dependent pitting later.

Why It Matters

The observed Nephrotic edema sign pattern finding can signal severe proteinuria with hypoalbuminemia. Low plasma oncotic pressure plus renal sodium retention shifts fluid into compliant interstitial spaces. Nephrotic edema sign pattern cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • severe proteinuria with hypoalbuminemia

Assessment Method

Inspect periorbital tissues and dependent areas and assess pitting, weight, and urine changes.

Positive Finding

Periorbital edema is often prominent in the morning with dependent pitting later.

Mechanism

Low plasma oncotic pressure plus renal sodium retention shifts fluid into compliant interstitial spaces.

Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Nephrotic edema sign pattern does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Use Nephrotic edema sign pattern to focus evaluation of severe proteinuria with hypoalbuminemia and the reviewed alternatives: angioedema, hypothyroidism, venous edema. Evidence Limitations

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as angioedema, hypothyroidism, venous edema.

False Negative Situations

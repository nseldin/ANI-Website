Used To Treat

Bronchospasm in asthma or COPD; short-acting agents are rescue therapy and long-acting agents support maintenance regimens.

Mechanism

Arformoterol works by stimulating beta-2 receptors on bronchial smooth muscle for prolonged bronchodilation. LABAs are maintenance bronchodilators, not rescue monotherapy for acute bronchospasm, and tachycardia/tremor can still occur.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; overuse/paradoxical bronchospasm and LABA safety in asthma without anti-inflammatory therapy are key concerns.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity

  • tachydysrhythmia risk may require clarification

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess work of breathing, lung sounds, SpO2, HR, tremor, potassium with high-dose therapy.

  • Rescue inhaler overuse signals poor control.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Beta blockers may blunt effect.

  • Other sympathomimetics increase tachycardia.

  • Diuretics/steroids can worsen hypokalemia.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • heart rate

  • potassium with high-dose/nebulized therapy

  • SpO2/peak flow

  • Respiratory rate, work of breathing, breath sounds, oxygenation, peak flow if used, heart rate, tremor, and potassium for high-dose beta agonists.

Nclex Traps

  • Albuterol can lower potassium and increase HR; worsening breathing after inhaler can be paradoxical bronchospasm.

  • Rescue inhalers relieve acute bronchospasm. Long-acting agents are not used alone for sudden respiratory distress.

Description

Arformoterol is a long-acting beta-2 agonist bronchodilator: it works by stimulating beta-2 receptors on bronchial smooth muscle for prolonged bronchodilation. It is used for bronchospasm in asthma or COPD. Short-acting agents are rescue therapy and long-acting agents support maintenance regimens.

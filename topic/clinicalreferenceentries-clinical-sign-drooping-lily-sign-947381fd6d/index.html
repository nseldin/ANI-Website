Definition

Drooping lily sign is an imaging sign. It is a clue to duplex collecting system with an obstructed upper moiety. A positive finding means the functioning lower moiety is displaced downward and outward like a drooping flower.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Drooping lily sign means the functioning lower moiety is displaced downward and outward like a drooping flower.

Why It Matters

The Drooping lily sign imaging pattern can signal duplex collecting system with an obstructed upper moiety. A dilated nonfunctioning upper moiety compresses and displaces the lower collecting system. Drooping lily sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • duplex collecting system with an obstructed upper moiety

Assessment Method

Review excretory urography or cross-sectional imaging of a duplicated kidney.

Positive Finding

The functioning lower moiety is displaced downward and outward like a drooping flower.

Mechanism

A dilated nonfunctioning upper moiety compresses and displaces the lower collecting system.

Clinical Interpretation

The Drooping lily sign imaging pattern raises concern for duplex collecting system with an obstructed upper moiety because a dilated nonfunctioning upper moiety compresses and displaces the lower collecting system. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Drooping lily sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Drooping lily sign to focus evaluation of duplex collecting system with an obstructed upper moiety and the reviewed alternatives: renal mass, hydronephrosis, malrotation. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Review excretory urography or cross-sectional imaging of a duplicated kidney. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Drooping lily sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

It is not visible when anatomy or function differs and modern modalities show the cause directly. Therefore, Drooping lily sign cannot confirm or exclude duplex collecting system with an obstructed upper moiety by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

A false-positive interpretation can result from acquisition artifact, an inappropriate imaging phase or plane, or overlap with renal mass, hydronephrosis, malrotation.

False Negative Situations

Drooping lily sign may be missed when the associated process is early or subtle, the relevant region is not included, or acquisition timing and resolution are inadequate.

Irreversible mutant-selective KRAS G12C inhibitor

Used To Treat

Adults with biomarker-confirmed KRAS G12C-mutated locally advanced or metastatic non-small-cell lung cancer after prior systemic therapy, and with cetuximab for selected previously treated colorectal cancer.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: no boxed warning is listed. Severe GI reactions, QTc prolongation, hepatotoxicity, and ILD/pneumonitis require active monitoring. Hold for QTc above 500 ms or an increase above 60 ms; permanently discontinue for torsades/serious ventricular arrhythmia or confirmed ILD.

Mechanism

Adagrasib covalently binds cysteine-12 in the switch-II pocket of GDP-bound KRAS G12C and prevents conversion back to the active GTP-bound conformation. This suppresses RAF-MEK-ERK and related signaling without directly inhibiting wild-type KRAS; cetuximab helps blunt EGFR-mediated feedback in colorectal tumors.

Nursing Essentials

  • Obtain medication reconciliation for strong CYP3A inducers/inhibitors, sensitive CYP3A/CYP2C9/CYP2D6/P-gp substrates, and QT-prolonging drugs.

  • Trend stool/vomiting losses, hydration, weight, ECG/electrolytes, liver injury, and respiratory symptoms; use ordered antiemetic and antidiarrheal support early.

Key Labs

  • KRAS G12C result, ECG/QTc, potassium/magnesium/calcium, ALT/AST/bilirubin, renal function/hydration, and pulmonary evaluation for new respiratory symptoms.

Nclex Traps

  • Adagrasib and sotorasib share a target but not an identical toxicity/interaction profile; QT and severe GI effects point more strongly to adagrasib.

  • Correcting hypokalemia or hypomagnesemia is part of QT-risk control, not a separate issue.

Description

Adagrasib is an oral irreversible KRAS G12C inhibitor that traps mutant KRAS in its inactive state and blocks downstream growth signaling. Its distinguishing safety pattern is substantial nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea plus concentration-dependent QT prolongation, hepatotoxicity, and ILD/pneumonitis; broad CYP and transporter interactions can amplify toxicity or alter other drugs.

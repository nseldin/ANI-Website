Summary

24-hour urine is a high-yield Renal/Urologic diagnostic reference. Timed urine collection used for protein, creatinine clearance, hormones, electrolytes, or other quantified excretion.

Quick Answer

24-hour urine: Timed urine collection used for protein, creatinine clearance, hormones, electrolytes, or other quantified excretion. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Timed urine collection used for protein, creatinine clearance, hormones, electrolytes, or other quantified excretion.

    • Before / preparation

    • Teach collection timing exactly: discard first void, collect all urine for 24 hours, keep as instructed, and include the final void.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Trend the result with symptoms, vital signs, assessment findings, medications, and baseline history. Escalate critical or rapidly changing values.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Critical values, sharp trend changes, results that do not fit the client's condition, specimen contamination, or a result that requires immediate treatment.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Missing urine during a timed collection can invalidate the result.

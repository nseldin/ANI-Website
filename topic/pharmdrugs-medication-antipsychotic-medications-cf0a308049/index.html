Description

Antipsychotic medications are medications grouped by a shared clinical action: reduce psychosis mainly through dopamine and serotonin receptor effects. Individual drugs differ in EPS, metabolic, sedation, QT, and agranulocytosis risks. Common uses include schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, agitation or psychosis, and selected adjunct psychiatric uses. Use the linked examples to compare drug-specific warnings, contraindications, labs, and nursing care.

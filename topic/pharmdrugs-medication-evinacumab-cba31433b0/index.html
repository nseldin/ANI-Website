Used To Treat

As an adjunct to diet, exercise and other LDL-lowering therapies, homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia in adults and children age 1 year and older.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current EVKEEZA labeling has no boxed warning. Serious hypersensitivity, including anaphylaxis, can occur during infusion; slow, interrupt or stop and treat based on severity. Animal findings support embryo-fetal harm, so verify pregnancy context and use effective contraception during treatment and for 5 months after the last dose.

Mechanism

Hepatic ANGPTL3 normally inhibits lipoprotein lipase and endothelial lipase. Evinacumab binds circulating ANGPTL3, restoring LPL-mediated triglyceride-rich-lipoprotein lipolysis and EL activity. Enhanced VLDL-remnant processing and clearance reduces substrate that would become LDL. This explains LDL-receptor-independent benefit and concurrent reductions in triglycerides and HDL-C.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm HoFH rather than routine hypercholesterolemia, document genotype/phenotype, LDL apheresis and all background therapy, pregnancy status, body weight and prior infusion reactions.

  • Administer 15 mg/kg IV over 60 minutes every four weeks through the required filter, do not co-infuse other drugs in the line, and monitor continuously for hypersensitivity.

Key Labs

  • Lipid panel as early as two weeks and periodically, pregnancy testing, infusion vital signs and hypersensitivity assessment; continue monitoring liver/renal status and apheresis plan as clinically indicated.

Nclex Traps

  • Evinacumab lowers LDL independently of LDL receptors; it is not a PCSK9 inhibitor.

  • A fall in HDL-C reflects endothelial-lipase release and does not mean the antibody failed to lower atherogenic lipoproteins.

Description

Evinacumab-dgnb is a monthly IV monoclonal antibody that neutralizes angiopoietin-like protein 3 for homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia. By releasing lipoprotein and endothelial lipases from ANGPTL3 inhibition, it accelerates VLDL processing and lowers LDL upstream of LDL formation, so it can work even when LDL-receptor function is profoundly impaired.

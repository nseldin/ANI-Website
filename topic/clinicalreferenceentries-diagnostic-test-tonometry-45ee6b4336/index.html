Summary

Tonometry is a high-yield Eye & Ear diagnostic reference. Measures intraocular pressure, important in glaucoma evaluation.

Quick Answer

Tonometry: Measures intraocular pressure, important in glaucoma evaluation. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Measures intraocular pressure, important in glaucoma evaluation.

    • Before / preparation

    • Check baseline vision/hearing concerns, pain, drainage, injury, diabetes/hypertension history, and whether drops or equipment affect testing.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Protect safety, explain simple cooperation steps, and connect abnormal findings to falls, infection, pressure, or neuro concerns.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Sudden vision/hearing loss, eye pain, trauma, very high intraocular pressure, neurologic deficits, or infection signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Eye pain plus high pressure can be urgent glaucoma, not routine eye strain.

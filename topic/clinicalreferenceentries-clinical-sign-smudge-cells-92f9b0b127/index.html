Definition

Smudge cells is a microscopic blood-film sign. It is a clue to fragile lymphocytes, often in chronic lymphocytic leukemia. A positive finding means bare nuclear chromatin smears without intact cytoplasm are frequent.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Smudge cells means bare nuclear chromatin smears without intact cytoplasm are frequent.

Why It Matters

The Smudge cells laboratory finding can signal fragile lymphocytes, often in chronic lymphocytic leukemia. Structurally fragile lymphocytes rupture under mechanical shear while the blood film is spread, leaving smeared nuclear chromatin without an intact cell membrane. Smudge cells cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

False Negative Situations

Used To Treat

Product-specific rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, axial spondyloarthritis, juvenile arthritis, ulcerative colitis, Crohn disease, atopic dermatitis, alopecia areata, and other labeled inflammatory conditions after required prior therapy. Tofacitinib, baricitinib, and upadacitinib are not dose-equivalent or indication-interchangeable.

Description

Many cytokine receptors have no intrinsic kinase. Instead, they recruit paired Janus kinases that phosphorylate STAT transcription factors when a cytokine binds. Oral JAK inhibitors occupy the kinase ATP pocket and reduce signaling from multiple cytokines at once. Tofacitinib functionally emphasizes JAK1/JAK3, baricitinib JAK1/JAK2, and upadacitinib JAK1 at labeled exposures, but selectivity is relative and changes with concentration. Because one JAK participates in several receptor pairs, inhibiting a preferred isoform still changes multiple cytokine programs. Broad pathway reach can produce rapid anti-inflammatory benefit, yet also disrupt antiviral defense, hematopoiesis, lipid regulation, and vascular or malignancy risk. That is why an oral tablet can require screening as serious as an injectable biologic.

Mechanism

Cytokine binding juxtaposes receptor-associated JAK1, JAK2, JAK3, or TYK2. The kinases trans-phosphorylate each other and receptor tails, STAT proteins dock and are phosphorylated, then STAT dimers enter the nucleus and change transcription. Common-gamma-chain cytokines use JAK1/JAK3; interferons and several interleukins use JAK1/TYK2; erythropoietin, thrombopoietin, and GM-CSF-family signals use JAK2 combinations. Tofacitinib, baricitinib, and upadacitinib reduce overlapping but nonidentical networks. Because ATP-pocket selectivity is incomplete, higher exposure from renal/hepatic dysfunction or CYP/OAT interactions can widen pathway suppression and toxicity.

Boxed Warning

All three carry boxed warnings for serious infections, mortality, malignancy, major adverse cardiovascular events, and thrombosis. The signal was defined against TNF blockers in higher-risk rheumatoid arthritis patients and informs class labeling. Screen TB and viral hepatitis, update vaccines, avoid live vaccines, and assess age, current or past smoking, prior cancer, atherosclerotic disease, venous thromboembolism, immobility, estrogen exposure, and infection risk before choosing therapy. Stop and urgently evaluate symptoms of myocardial infarction, stroke, pulmonary embolism, deep-vein thrombosis, or serious infection.

Adverse Effects

  • Herpes zoster and other serious infection, cytopenias, liver-test elevation, lipid elevation, gastrointestinal perforation, and malignancy

  • MACE, arterial or venous thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, mortality signal, hypersensitivity, and product-specific renal/hepatic exposure problems

Contraindications

  • Do not combine with another JAK inhibitor, biologic DMARD, or potent immunosuppressant such as azathioprine or cyclosporine unless the exact label and specialist plan support it.

  • Active serious infection, unresolved TB/hepatitis, severe cytopenias, high thrombotic or cardiovascular risk, malignancy context, pregnancy, and organ dysfunction require product-specific assessment.

Nursing Essentials

  • Teach clot, stroke, MI, infection, shingles, perforation, and cancer warning symptoms in plain language; oral route does not mean low risk.

  • Verify formulation and indication dose, renal/hepatic adjustment, CBC/liver/lipid schedule, vaccine history, and current or past smoking.

Interactions

  • Strong CYP3A4 modifiers substantially affect tofacitinib or upadacitinib exposure; baricitinib exposure rises with strong OAT3 inhibition such as probenecid.

  • Other immunosuppressants add infection and marrow risk; live vaccines are avoided during therapy.

Key Labs

  • TB and hepatitis assessment, CBC with differential, hemoglobin, liver tests, lipids, renal function, pregnancy context, and infection screening

  • Cardiovascular and venous-thromboembolism risk, smoking history, malignancy/skin surveillance, bowel symptoms, and disease activity

Nclex Traps

  • JAK selectivity is relative, not absolute; a JAK1-preferential label does not erase shared boxed risks.

  • The tablet acts inside cells downstream of many receptors, so it is a targeted synthetic DMARD rather than a biologic antibody.

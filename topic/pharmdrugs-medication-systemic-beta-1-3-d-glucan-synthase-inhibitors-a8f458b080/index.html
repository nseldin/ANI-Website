Used To Treat

Product-specific Candida infections: invasive candidiasis or candidemia for IV echinocandins, and vulvovaginal candidiasis or recurrent-vulvovaginal-candidiasis reduction for oral ibrexafungerp.

Description

Beta-1,3-D-glucan synthase inhibitors weaken a fungal cell-wall polymer that human cells do not make. Caspofungin, micafungin, anidulafungin, and rezafungin are IV echinocandins; rezafungin's long half-life permits once-weekly dosing. Ibrexafungerp is an oral triterpenoid that inhibits the same enzyme complex at a partly different binding region, so route, indication, pregnancy risk, and resistance behavior differ substantially.

Mechanism

Inhibiting the Fks-containing glucan synthase complex reduces beta-1,3-D-glucan construction, producing a fragile, osmotically unstable fungal wall. Activity is generally fungicidal against Candida but fungistatic against Aspergillus. FKS hot-spot mutations reduce susceptibility. This is a cell-wall mechanism, not azole-like ergosterol synthesis inhibition and not polyene membrane pore formation.

Boxed Warning

Ibrexafungerp carries a boxed embryo-fetal-toxicity warning and is contraindicated in pregnancy; verify pregnancy before treatment and before each monthly recurrent-VVC dose. Rezafungin and the echinocandins have no boxed warning but can cause anaphylaxis, infusion reactions, and hepatic abnormalities. Match warning surveillance to the exact agent and route.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm species, infection depth, susceptibility, pregnancy status, liver function, prior azole/echinocandin exposure, route, and whether source control or device removal is needed.

  • Do not infer that once-weekly rezafungin has established evidence for Candida meningitis, endocarditis, osteomyelitis, or every deep site; the label states those sites were not studied.

Key Labs

  • Blood or sterile-site cultures, species/susceptibility, CBC, liver tests, potassium/magnesium/phosphate in severe illness, pregnancy testing for ibrexafungerp, and clearance cultures for candidemia.

Nclex Traps

  • Ibrexafungerp is not an azole despite being oral, and echinocandins do not inhibit ergosterol synthesis.

  • A long half-life changes dosing convenience; it does not erase the need for cultures, source control, and site-specific penetration reasoning.

Used To Treat

Adults with chronic lymphocytic leukemia or small lymphocytic lymphoma, and with azacitidine, decitabine, or low-dose cytarabine for selected newly diagnosed older or intensive-chemotherapy-ineligible acute myeloid leukemia.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: no boxed warning is listed, but tumor lysis syndrome can cause renal failure, dysrhythmia, seizure, and death. Assess tumor burden and renal risk, provide hydration and anti-hyperuricemics, follow the exact ramp-up, and obtain timed potassium, phosphorus, calcium, uric acid, and creatinine checks.

Mechanism

BCL-2 normally binds and sequesters pro-apoptotic BH3-only proteins. Venetoclax occupies BCL-2's BH3-binding groove, displaces BIM, permits BAX/BAK activation and mitochondrial outer-membrane permeabilization, releases cytochrome c, and activates the caspase cascade. BCL-2 blockade frees BH3-only proteins such as BIM, which activate BAX/BAK and create mitochondrial outer-membrane pores. Cytochrome c then activates caspases, leading to rapid programmed cell death. Because a large sensitive tumor burden can die within hours, intracellular potassium, phosphate, and nucleic acids enter blood faster than the kidneys can clear them; therefore ramp-up dosing, hydration, urate control, interaction review, and timed chemistry checks are direct consequences of the mechanism rather than administrative extras.

Nursing Essentials

  • Never start or restart after a significant interruption without reassessing TLS risk and the required ramp-up level.

  • Strong CYP3A inhibitors are contraindicated during CLL/SLL initiation and ramp-up; review CYP3A/P-gp interactions, neutropenia, fever/infection, and live-vaccine avoidance.

Key Labs

  • CBC, potassium, phosphorus, calcium, uric acid, creatinine/eGFR, LDH/tumor burden, liver context, and timed pre/post-dose TLS panels according to risk.

Nclex Traps

  • Venetoclax restores apoptosis; the faster it works in bulky disease, the greater the TLS danger.

  • A missed-dose restart is not automatically the same dose if the interruption is long enough to lose ramp-up tolerance.

Description

Venetoclax is an oral selective BCL-2 inhibitor that removes an anti-apoptotic brake from malignant cells, freeing BIM and related proteins to permeabilize mitochondria and activate caspases. Tumor lysis syndrome can occur within hours as susceptible cells die, so risk stratification, ramp-up dosing, hydration, urate control, interaction management, and timed chemistry checks are core parts of the mechanism.

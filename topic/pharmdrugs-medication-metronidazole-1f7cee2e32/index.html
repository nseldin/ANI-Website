Used To Treat

Fungal infections or prophylaxis, ranging from topical candidiasis/dermatophytes to serious systemic mycoses depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Metronidazole works by entering anaerobic organisms and being reduced into reactive nitro radicals that damage DNA and inhibit nucleic-acid synthesis. Human aerobic cells do not reduce it the same way, which is why the drug is especially useful for anaerobes and protozoa; nausea, metallic taste, neuropathy with prolonged exposure, and alcohol/disulfiram-like counseling matter.

Boxed Warning

Some products include carcinogenicity warnings from animal data; neurotoxicity and alcohol reaction concerns are high yield.

Contraindications

  • Alcohol use during therapy and immediate post-therapy window.

  • Disulfiram use within recent period.

  • First trimester pregnancy requires indication-specific review.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Avoid alcohol during therapy and for the recommended period after.

  • Report numbness/tingling or seizures.

  • Metallic taste and GI upset can occur.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

  • Subclass specificity: Metronidazole is categorized here as Nitroimidazole anaerobic/antiprotozoal antimicrobial. Pathway: Anti-infective medication > Anaerobic antimicrobial > Nitroimidazole DNA-damaging antimicrobial. This subtype shapes the expected mechanism, contraindications, boxed/high-alert warnings, monitoring, interactions, and patient teaching.

Interactions

  • Alcohol can cause disulfiram-like reaction.

  • Warfarin effect can increase.

  • Lithium levels may increase.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • INR if on warfarin

  • LFTs if prolonged

  • neurologic assessment

  • Liver function for azoles, renal function/electrolytes for amphotericin B, infusion reactions, drug interactions, and pregnancy risk.

Nclex Traps

  • Alcohol avoidance is not optional teaching with metronidazole.

  • Azole antifungals have many CYP interactions. Amphotericin B is associated with kidney injury and electrolyte loss.

Description

Metronidazole is a nitroimidazole anaerobic/antiprotozoal antimicrobial: it works by entering anaerobic organisms and being reduced into reactive nitro radicals that damage DNA and inhibit nucleic-acid synthesis. It is used for anaerobic bacterial infections, bacterial vaginosis, trichomoniasis, Giardia/Entamoeba, or C. difficile-adjacent regimens depending on organism and protocol.

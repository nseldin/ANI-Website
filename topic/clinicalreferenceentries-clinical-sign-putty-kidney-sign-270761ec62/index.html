Definition

Putty kidney sign is an imaging sign. It is a clue to end-stage renal tuberculosis. A positive finding means dense lobar or diffuse calcification replaces much of a shrunken nonfunctioning kidney.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Putty kidney sign means dense lobar or diffuse calcification replaces much of a shrunken nonfunctioning kidney.

Why It Matters

The Putty kidney sign imaging pattern can signal end-stage renal tuberculosis. Chronic granulomatous destruction, caseation, fibrosis, and dystrophic calcification obliterate parenchyma. Putty kidney sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • end-stage renal tuberculosis

Assessment Method

Review radiography or CT for diffuse renal calcification and loss of function.

Positive Finding

Dense lobar or diffuse calcification replaces much of a shrunken nonfunctioning kidney.

Mechanism

Chronic granulomatous destruction, caseation, fibrosis, and dystrophic calcification obliterate parenchyma.

Clinical Interpretation

The Putty kidney sign imaging pattern raises concern for end-stage renal tuberculosis because chronic granulomatous destruction, caseation, fibrosis, and dystrophic calcification obliterate parenchyma. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Putty kidney sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Putty kidney sign to focus evaluation of end-stage renal tuberculosis and the reviewed alternatives: xanthogranulomatous pyelonephritis, calcified tumor, old hematoma. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Review radiography or CT for diffuse renal calcification and loss of function. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Putty kidney sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

The sign indicates late damage and is not specific without microbiologic context. Therefore, Putty kidney sign cannot confirm or exclude end-stage renal tuberculosis by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • xanthogranulomatous pyelonephritis

  • calcified tumor

  • old hematoma

False Positive Situations

A false-positive interpretation can result from acquisition artifact, an inappropriate imaging phase or plane, or overlap with xanthogranulomatous pyelonephritis, calcified tumor, old hematoma.

False Negative Situations

Putty kidney sign may be missed when the associated process is early or subtle, the relevant region is not included, or acquisition timing and resolution are inadequate.

Modern Status

Historical radiologic term for advanced disease. Putty kidney sign remains searchable because learners still encounter the term, but it cannot confirm or exclude end-stage renal tuberculosis. Apply the reviewed limitation for Putty kidney sign: The sign indicates late damage and is not specific without microbiologic context.

For Putty kidney sign, verify patient identity, the indication, acquisition quality, timing, and the relevant imaging plane or electrodiagnostic leads. Apply the reviewed precaution for this pattern: Use infection precautions and full urinary-tract evaluation; the image alone does not prove active TB.

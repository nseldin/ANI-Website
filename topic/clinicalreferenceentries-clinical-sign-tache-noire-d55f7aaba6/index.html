Definition

Tache noire is an observed inoculation-eschar sign. It is a clue to spotted-fever group rickettsiosis. A positive finding means a black crusted eschar with an erythematous halo marks the inoculation site.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Tache noire means a black crusted eschar with an erythematous halo marks the inoculation site.

Why It Matters

The observed Tache noire finding can signal spotted-fever group rickettsiosis. Local endothelial infection causes vascular injury, necrosis, and eschar formation. Tache noire cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • spotted-fever group rickettsiosis

Inspect hidden folds and scalp for a tick-bite lesion.

A black crusted eschar with an erythematous halo marks the inoculation site.

Mechanism

Local endothelial infection causes vascular injury, necrosis, and eschar formation.

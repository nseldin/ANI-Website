Summary

Insulin/C-peptide levels is a high-yield Endocrine diagnostic reference. Paired insulin and C-peptide testing helps evaluate endogenous insulin production, hypoglycemia causes, and diabetes classification.

Quick Answer

Insulin/C-peptide levels: Paired insulin and C-peptide testing helps evaluate endogenous insulin production, hypoglycemia causes, and diabetes classification. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Paired insulin and C-peptide testing helps evaluate endogenous insulin production, hypoglycemia causes, and diabetes classification.

    • Before / preparation

    • Check timing, fasting status, current endocrine medications, steroid use, pregnancy status when relevant, and whether dynamic testing requires serial specimens.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Connect the result to symptoms, glucose safety, thyroid/adrenal crisis risk, and medication teaching.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Severe hypoglycemia/hyperglycemia, thyroid storm/myxedema cues, adrenal crisis, altered mental status, dehydration, or unstable vital signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Endocrine tests often require correct timing. Wrong timing can make the result misleading.

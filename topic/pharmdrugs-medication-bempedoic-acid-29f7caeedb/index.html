Class

Oral liver-activated ATP-citrate-lyase cholesterol-synthesis inhibitor

Used To Treat

Reduce major cardiovascular events in statin-intolerant adults at increased risk, and lower LDL-C in adults with hypercholesterolemia including heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia, according to current indication and background therapy.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current NEXLETOL labeling has no boxed warning. Hyperuricemia and gout can begin within weeks; assess uric acid and symptoms. Tendinitis and tendon rupture can involve Achilles, biceps or rotator cuff and are more likely with age over 60, corticosteroids, fluoroquinolones, renal failure or prior tendon disease; stop for rupture and rest/evaluate tendinitis. Avoid simvastatin above 20 mg and pravastatin above 40 mg because exposure and myopathy risk rise.

Mechanism

ATP-citrate lyase converts cytosolic citrate to acetyl-CoA, supplying substrate for cholesterol and fatty-acid synthesis. ACSVL1 converts bempedoic acid and its active metabolite to ETC-1002-CoA/ESP15228-CoA, which inhibit ACL. The liver senses reduced cholesterol, activates SREBP2 and increases LDL-receptor expression. The OAT2 effect likely contributes to small creatinine and uric-acid elevations.

Nursing Essentials

  • Document statin tolerance, ASCVD risk, baseline LDL, gout/uric acid, tendon history, renal function, pregnancy and simvastatin/pravastatin dose before therapy.

  • Teach sudden tendon pain, swelling, snap or loss of function as a stop-and-call event; do not dismiss new ankle or shoulder symptoms as routine exercise soreness.

Key Labs

  • Lipid panel 8 to 12 weeks after initiation and periodically, uric acid when indicated, creatinine/BUN, liver tests, signs of gout, tendon examination and CK only when muscle symptoms suggest injury.

Nclex Traps

  • Bempedoic acid inhibits ACL upstream of statins and requires liver-enriched activation; it is not a PCSK9 inhibitor.

  • Less skeletal-muscle activation does not mean zero tendon or muscle risk.

Description

Bempedoic acid is an oral prodrug-like ATP-citrate-lyase inhibitor that lowers hepatic cholesterol synthesis one enzymatic step upstream of HMG-CoA reductase. Liver-enriched ACSVL1 activates it to a CoA thioester, so skeletal muscle has less active drug formation than liver. Lower intracellular cholesterol upregulates LDL receptors and increases plasma LDL clearance.

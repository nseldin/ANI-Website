Name

Combined hormonal contraceptive danger signs

Nclex Essential

true

Quick Answer

ACHES is a warning-sign cue, not a diagnosis. New severe symptoms require assessment for thromboembolism, stroke, cardiac disease, liver or gallbladder disease, pregnancy complications, or another cause.

Sections

  • Label

    Why estrogen changes risk

    Text

    Estrogen-containing contraception shifts hepatic coagulation proteins toward clot formation. Absolute risk remains low for many eligible users but rises with factors such as smoking at older age, prior thrombosis, thrombophilia, migraine with aura, severe hypertension, postpartum status, immobility, and selected diseases.

  • Label

    Assessment

    Text

    Clarify the exact product, start date, adherence, pregnancy possibility, headache phenotype, chest and breathing symptoms, abdominal location, visual change, leg asymmetry, risk factors, and interacting medicines.

  • Label

    Urgent action

    Text

    Call emergency services for stroke findings, severe breathing difficulty, syncope, or severe chest pain. Obtain urgent evaluation for new unilateral swelling or pain, severe neurologic symptoms, sudden vision loss, or severe abdominal pain.

  • Label

    Teaching limitation

    Text

    The cue does not list every adverse effect or every emergency, and it does not apply identically to progestin-only and nonhormonal methods. Current eligibility guidance and the specific label govern use.

Summary

Certain new abdominal, chest, neurologic, visual, or unilateral leg symptoms during combined hormonal contraceptive use can signal uncommon but serious complications requiring urgent evaluation.

    Expansion
    • Cue

      A

      Meaning

      Severe Abdominal Pain. This can reflect hepatobiliary disease, thrombosis, pregnancy complication, or another acute abdominal process.

    • Cue

      C

      Meaning

      Chest Pain or Shortness of Breath. Pulmonary embolism, cardiac ischemia, or another cardiopulmonary emergency must be considered.

    • Cue

      H

      Meaning

      New Severe Headache. Sudden, focal, or aura-associated symptoms can signal stroke or another neurologic emergency.

    • Cue

      E

      Meaning

      Eye or Vision Problems. Sudden visual loss or disturbance can accompany retinal, vascular, migraine, or neurologic disease.

    • Cue

      S

      Meaning

      Severe Unilateral Leg Pain or Swelling. Asymmetric symptoms can indicate deep-vein thrombosis.

Definition

Sonographic Murphy sign is an imaging-provoked sign. It is a clue to acute cholecystitis. A positive finding means maximal focal tenderness occurs over the gallbladder.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Sonographic Murphy sign means maximal focal tenderness occurs over the gallbladder.

Why It Matters

The Sonographic Murphy sign imaging pattern can signal acute cholecystitis. Probe pressure displaces an inflamed gallbladder against sensitive parietal tissues. Sonographic Murphy sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • acute cholecystitis

Assessment Method

During ultrasound, press the transducer directly over the visualized gallbladder.

Positive Finding

Maximal focal tenderness occurs over the gallbladder.

Mechanism

Probe pressure displaces an inflamed gallbladder against sensitive parietal tissues.

Clinical Interpretation

The Sonographic Murphy sign imaging pattern raises concern for acute cholecystitis because probe pressure displaces an inflamed gallbladder against sensitive parietal tissues. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Sonographic Murphy sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Sonographic Murphy sign to focus evaluation of acute cholecystitis and the reviewed alternatives: hepatitis, abdominal wall pain, right pleurisy. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: During ultrasound, press the transducer directly over the visualized gallbladder. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Sonographic Murphy sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Analgesia and altered sensorium reduce sensitivity, and tenderness is not specific. Therefore, Sonographic Murphy sign cannot confirm or exclude acute cholecystitis by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • hepatitis

  • abdominal wall pain

  • right pleurisy

False Positive Situations

A false-positive interpretation can result from acquisition artifact, an inappropriate imaging phase or plane, or overlap with hepatitis, abdominal wall pain, right pleurisy.

False Negative Situations

Sonographic Murphy sign may be missed when the associated process is early or subtle, the relevant region is not included, or acquisition timing and resolution are inadequate.

Modern Status

Current imaging adjunct; distinct from bedside Murphy sign. When performed as described, Sonographic Murphy sign can adjust concern for acute cholecystitis, but it is not a stand-alone diagnosis. Apply the reviewed limitation for Sonographic Murphy sign: Analgesia and altered sensorium reduce sensitivity, and tenderness is not specific.

Safety Precautions

For Sonographic Murphy sign, verify patient identity, the indication, acquisition quality, timing, and the relevant imaging plane or electrodiagnostic leads. Apply the reviewed precaution for this pattern: Document that the gallbladder was visualized rather than calling any RUQ tenderness sonographic Murphy.

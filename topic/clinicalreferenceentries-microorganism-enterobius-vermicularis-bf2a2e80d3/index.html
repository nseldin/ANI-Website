Definition

Enterobius vermicularis is a clinically important nematode (roundworm) associated with enterobiasis, nocturnal perianal pruritus, and sleep disturbance. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

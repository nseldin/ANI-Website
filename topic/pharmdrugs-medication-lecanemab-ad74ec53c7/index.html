Class

Aggregated amyloid-beta protofibril-directed humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody

Used To Treat

Alzheimer disease in patients with mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia stage and confirmed amyloid-beta pathology; IV initiation and product-specific maintenance options require scheduled MRI surveillance.

Boxed Warning

Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities: ARIA-E and ARIA-H can be serious or fatal, and intracerebral hemorrhage has occurred. Confirm amyloid pathology and obtain baseline plus label-scheduled MRIs; discuss ApoE epsilon4 testing because homozygotes have more symptomatic and severe ARIA. Use special caution with antithrombotics and thrombolysis, hold treatment and urgently evaluate compatible neurologic symptoms.

Mechanism

By binding aggregated amyloid-beta species, lecanemab marks them for microglial phagocytosis and other clearance pathways, lowering brain amyloid burden. Clearance around amyloid-laden small vessels can transiently increase vascular permeability or bleeding, explaining the required baseline and early surveillance MRIs and why new headache, confusion, visual change, dizziness, gait change, focal deficit or seizure cannot be dismissed.

Description

Lecanemab is a disease-modifying humanized IgG1 monoclonal antibody that preferentially binds soluble aggregated amyloid-beta protofibrils and also recognizes fibrils, promoting amyloid clearance in early symptomatic Alzheimer disease. It does not restore neurons already lost. The central safety concept is ARIA: vasogenic edema/effusion (ARIA-E) and microhemorrhage or superficial siderosis (ARIA-H), with higher serious risk in ApoE epsilon4 homozygotes.

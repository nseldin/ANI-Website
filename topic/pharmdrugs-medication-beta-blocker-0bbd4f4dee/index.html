Class

Beta-adrenergic receptor antagonist drug class

Description

Beta blockers antagonize beta-adrenergic receptors so sympathetic stimulation cannot drive the heart, kidney renin release, bronchi, or metabolic responses as strongly. Subclass matters immediately: beta-1 selective drugs focus on heart/renin effects, nonselective beta blockers also block beta-2 bronchodilation/metabolic signaling, and alpha/beta blockers add vasodilation.

Mechanism

Beta-1 blockade slows SA rate, AV conduction, contractility, and renin release; beta-2 blockade can worsen bronchospasm and blunt tremor/glycogenolysis cues; alpha-1 blockade with carvedilol/labetalol lowers vascular tone.

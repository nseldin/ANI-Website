Definition

Landolfi sign is an observed pupillary pulse sign. It is a clue to severe aortic regurgitation. A positive finding means pupils alternately constrict and dilate with the cardiac cycle.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Landolfi sign means pupils alternately constrict and dilate with the cardiac cycle.

Why It Matters

The observed Landolfi sign finding can signal severe aortic regurgitation. Large pulse-pressure oscillations are proposed to modulate iris perfusion or autonomic tone. Landolfi sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • severe aortic regurgitation

Assessment Method

Observe both pupils under steady illumination.

Positive Finding

Pupils alternately constrict and dilate with the cardiac cycle.

Mechanism

Large pulse-pressure oscillations are proposed to modulate iris perfusion or autonomic tone.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Landolfi sign finding raises concern for severe aortic regurgitation because large pulse-pressure oscillations are proposed to modulate iris perfusion or autonomic tone. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Landolfi sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Landolfi sign to focus evaluation of severe aortic regurgitation and the reviewed alternatives: physiologic hippus, drug effect, variable lighting. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Observe both pupils under steady illumination. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Landolfi sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

The mechanism and diagnostic performance are poorly established. Therefore, Landolfi sign cannot confirm or exclude severe aortic regurgitation by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • physiologic hippus

  • drug effect

  • variable lighting

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as physiologic hippus, drug effect, variable lighting.

False Negative Situations

Landolfi sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of severe aortic regurgitation.

Modern Status

Historical, rare, and unreliable. Landolfi sign remains searchable because learners still encounter the term, but it cannot confirm or exclude severe aortic regurgitation. Apply the reviewed limitation for Landolfi sign: The mechanism and diagnostic performance are poorly established.

Safety Precautions

Observe Landolfi sign with consent, privacy, adequate exposure, and only the manipulation required by this assessment: Observe both pupils under steady illumination. The reviewed precaution for this finding is: Do not mistake normal pupillary unrest for a cardiac sign.

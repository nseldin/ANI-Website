Definition

Kaposi-Stemmer sign is a bedside skin-fold sign. It is a clue to lymphedema with dermal fibrosis. A positive finding means the skin cannot be lifted into a fold.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Kaposi-Stemmer sign means the skin cannot be lifted into a fold.

Why It Matters

The observed Kaposi-Stemmer sign finding can signal lymphedema with dermal fibrosis. Protein-rich lymph stasis causes dermal thickening and fibrosis that tether skin. Kaposi-Stemmer sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • lymphedema with dermal fibrosis

Assessment Method

Try gently to pinch a skin fold at the base of the second toe or finger.

Positive Finding

The skin cannot be lifted into a fold.

Mechanism

Protein-rich lymph stasis causes dermal thickening and fibrosis that tether skin.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Kaposi-Stemmer sign finding raises concern for lymphedema with dermal fibrosis because protein-rich lymph stasis causes dermal thickening and fibrosis that tether skin. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Kaposi-Stemmer sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Kaposi-Stemmer sign to focus evaluation of lymphedema with dermal fibrosis and the reviewed alternatives: lipedema, venous edema, systemic edema. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Try gently to pinch a skin fold at the base of the second toe or finger. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Kaposi-Stemmer sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Early lymphedema can be negative and obesity may make pinching difficult. Therefore, Kaposi-Stemmer sign cannot confirm or exclude lymphedema with dermal fibrosis by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • lipedema

  • venous edema

  • systemic edema

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as lipedema, venous edema, systemic edema.

False Negative Situations

Kaposi-Stemmer sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of lymphedema with dermal fibrosis.

Modern Status

Current supportive lymphedema sign. Interpret Kaposi-Stemmer sign in the assessment context defined for this card because its reviewed limitation is topic-specific: Early lymphedema can be negative and obesity may make pinching difficult.

Safety Precautions

Observe Kaposi-Stemmer sign with consent, privacy, adequate exposure, and only the manipulation required by this assessment: Try gently to pinch a skin fold at the base of the second toe or finger. The reviewed precaution for this finding is: Do not pinch fragile, infected, ulcerated, or painful skin.

Nursing Relevance

Record the exact Kaposi-Stemmer sign observation, side or distribution, timing, technique, patient response, and change from baseline; the reviewed positive finding is: The skin cannot be lifted into a fold. Continue the focused evaluation by following the reviewed method and its limits: Try gently to pinch a skin fold at the base of the second toe or finger. Communicate the finding with its suspected association, lymphedema with dermal fibrosis, when it is new, worsening, or clinically important.

Documentation Guidance

Document the exact finding, assessment method, side or distribution, timing, patient response, associated symptoms and vital signs, and change from baseline. Record the observed result instead of writing only "positive" or "negative."

For Kaposi-Stemmer sign, apply this exact authored caution when deciding whom to notify and how quickly: Do not pinch fragile, infected, ulcerated, or painful skin. Base escalation for Kaposi-Stemmer sign on the patient's current condition, associated findings, and the cause under evaluation; this card does not assign a universal urgency level.

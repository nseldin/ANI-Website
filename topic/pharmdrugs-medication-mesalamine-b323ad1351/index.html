Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for mesalamine in this nursing reference. Important warnings include renal impairment or interstitial nephritis, acute intolerance syndrome that can mimic a colitis flare, myocarditis or pericarditis, hepatotoxicity, and severe skin reactions.

Class

5-aminosalicylate anti-inflammatory

Used To Treat

Ulcerative colitis maintenance and mild to moderate active ulcerative colitis depending on the ordered formulation and route.

Mechanism

Acts mostly locally in intestinal mucosa to reduce inflammatory prostaglandin and leukotriene activity. The nursing link is calming colon inflammation without treating systemic infection or an unstable abdomen.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor stool frequency, blood in stool, abdominal pain, hydration, weight trend, and signs that symptoms are worsening instead of improving.

  • Check renal function before and during therapy when ordered. Kidney injury can be silent.

Contraindications

  • Clarify salicylate or aminosalicylate hypersensitivity, significant renal impairment, severe hepatic impairment, or prior severe reaction to mesalamine.

Interactions

  • Other nephrotoxic drugs can increase renal risk.

  • Use caution with anticoagulants or bleeding-risk drugs based on the clinical picture.

Key Labs

  • Creatinine and eGFR for renal safety.

  • CBC and liver tests may be monitored depending on formulation, symptoms, and facility protocol.

Nclex Traps

  • Mesalamine is a colon anti-inflammatory for ulcerative colitis. It is not the priority answer for toxic megacolon, perforation signs, shock, or severe acute instability.

  • Worsening cramping, bloody diarrhea, fever, rash, or chest pain after starting therapy should be reported, not brushed off as the disease.

Description

Mesalamine is a 5-aminosalicylate anti-inflammatory: it acts mostly locally in intestinal mucosa to reduce inflammatory prostaglandin and leukotriene activity. It is used for ulcerative colitis maintenance and mild to moderate active ulcerative colitis depending on the ordered formulation and route.

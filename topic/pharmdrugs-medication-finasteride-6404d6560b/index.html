Mechanism

Finasteride works by inhibiting conversion of testosterone to dihydrotestosterone. Lower DHT gradually shrinks androgen-sensitive prostate tissue over months, so symptom benefit is delayed and pregnancy handling precautions matter because DHT signaling is important for male fetal genital development.

Boxed Warning

No boxed warning commonly emphasized; fetal male genital development risk is the major pregnancy handling concern.

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy exposure; pregnant clients should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

  • Hypersensitivity.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Pregnant clients should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

  • Monitor urinary symptom response or hair-loss indication.

  • Teach that sexual side effects can occur.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • No classic NCLEX interaction; review other BPH therapies for hypotension/falls.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • pregnancy exposure risk

  • PSA interpretation if prostate monitoring

  • Use medication-specific labs when ordered. For high-risk drugs, connect renal function, liver function, electrolytes, CBC, coagulation studies, drug levels, ECG, and vital-sign trends to safety.

Nclex Traps

  • The handling warning matters even if the pregnant person is not the prescribed patient.

  • The NCLEX trap is memorizing the name without connecting class, contraindication, assessment, and priority adverse effect.

Description

Finasteride is a 5-alpha-reductase inhibitor: it works by inhibiting conversion of testosterone to dihydrotestosterone. It is used for benign prostatic hyperplasia symptom reduction over months and male-pattern hair-loss treatment in selected clients; it is not rapid relief for acute urinary retention.

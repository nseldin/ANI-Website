Definition

Uremic fetor is an observed breath-odor sign. It is a clue to advanced kidney failure. A positive finding means breath has an ammonia-like or urine-like odor.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Uremic fetor means breath has an ammonia-like or urine-like odor.

Why It Matters

The observed Uremic fetor finding can signal advanced kidney failure. Very high circulating urea diffuses into saliva, where bacterial urease converts it to volatile ammonia that is released in exhaled breath. Uremic fetor cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • advanced kidney failure

Assessment Method

Notice characteristic breath odor during routine close assessment without asking the patient to exhale into the face.

Positive Finding

Breath has an ammonia-like or urine-like odor.

Mechanism

Very high circulating urea diffuses into saliva, where bacterial urease converts it to volatile ammonia that is released in exhaled breath.

Odor perception is subjective and cannot estimate kidney function. Therefore, Uremic fetor cannot confirm or exclude advanced kidney failure by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Negative Situations

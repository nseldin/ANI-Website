Summary

Nitrazine is a high-yield Obstetrics diagnostic reference. pH paper test supporting rupture of membranes when amniotic fluid alkalinity is present.

Quick Answer

Nitrazine: pH paper test supporting rupture of membranes when amniotic fluid alkalinity is present. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Negative

    Meaning

    Vaginal fluid is not alkaline by this screen, making rupture of membranes less supported.

  • Label

    Positive

    Meaning

    Alkaline fluid supports possible rupture of membranes, but blood, semen, infection, urine contamination, or antiseptics can cause false positives.

  • Label

    Clinical significance

    Meaning

    Interpret with pooling, ferning, ultrasound fluid, gestational age, contractions, fever, and fetal status.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • pH paper test supporting rupture of membranes when amniotic fluid alkalinity is present.

    • Before / preparation

    • Confirm gestational age, fetal/maternal baseline status, Rh status when invasive testing is involved, and whether the client is having bleeding, contractions, or ruptured membranes.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Monitor maternal vital signs, fetal status when indicated, uterine activity, bleeding, fluid leakage, infection cues, and decreased fetal movement.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Decreased fetal movement, nonreassuring fetal tracing, bleeding, leaking fluid, fever, severe abdominal pain, contractions after an invasive procedure, or hypertensive warning signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • False positives can occur with blood, semen, infection, or alkaline contaminants.

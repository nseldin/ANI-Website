Quick Answer

Use AMPLE after or alongside stabilization to find information that changes immediate treatment, imaging, anesthesia, antidotes, and disposition.

Sections

  • Label

    Decision-changing details

    Text

    Anticoagulants, insulin, sedatives, seizure medicines, pregnancy, severe allergies, implanted devices, recent surgery, last intake, and mechanism of injury can alter urgent care even when the history must be brief.

  • Label

    Sources of history

    Text

    When the patient cannot answer, use family, witnesses, medication containers, pharmacy data, emergency records, medical identification, and first-responder observations while protecting privacy.

  • Label

    Do not delay stabilization

    Text

    An AMPLE history never comes before immediate airway, breathing, circulation, hemorrhage, or neurologic interventions.

  • Label

    Relationship to SAMPLE

    Text

    AMPLE emphasizes trauma and emergency context; SAMPLE explicitly begins with signs and symptoms. Institutions may teach either, and ANI links both without merging them.

Summary

AMPLE organizes a rapid emergency or trauma history: allergies, medications, pertinent medical history or pregnancy, last meal, and events or environment surrounding the problem.

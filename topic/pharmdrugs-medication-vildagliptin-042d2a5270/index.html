Boxed Warning

Mechanism

Vildagliptin works by inhibiting dipeptidyl peptidase-4, the enzyme that breaks down endogenous incretin hormones. More intact GLP-1 and GIP increases glucose-dependent insulin release and reduces glucagon with low hypoglycemia risk unless combined with insulin or sulfonylureas.

Description

Vildagliptin is a DPP-4 inhibitor antidiabetic: it works by inhibiting dipeptidyl peptidase-4, the enzyme that breaks down endogenous incretin hormones. It is used for type 2 diabetes as an oral incretin-based glucose-lowering therapy.

Used To Treat

Type 2 diabetes when pancreatic insulin secretion can still be stimulated.

Mechanism

Glimepiride works by closing ATP-sensitive potassium channels on pancreatic beta cells, which depolarizes the cell, opens voltage-gated calcium channels, and triggers insulin release whether or not the meal arrives. That mechanism is why missed meals, older age, and kidney impairment can turn benefit into hypoglycemia.

Boxed Warning

No universal boxed warning; severe/prolonged hypoglycemia is the major nursing danger.

Contraindications

  • Diabetic ketoacidosis

  • serious hypersensitivity

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Give with meals as ordered.

  • Monitor hypoglycemia, especially older adults/renal impairment.

  • Teach consistent meals and glucose rescue.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Alcohol, many antibiotics, warfarin, beta blockers, and other diabetes meds can increase hypoglycemia risk or mask symptoms.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • blood glucose

  • A1c

  • renal function

  • Use medication-specific labs when ordered. For high-risk drugs, connect renal function, liver function, electrolytes, CBC, coagulation studies, drug levels, ECG, and vital-sign trends to safety.

Nclex Traps

  • Skipping a meal after sulfonylurea can cause dangerous hypoglycemia.

  • The NCLEX trap is memorizing the name without connecting class, contraindication, assessment, and priority adverse effect.

Summary

Fractional Flow Reserve (FFR) is a high-yield Cardiovascular diagnostic reference. Invasive pressure-wire measurement during coronary angiography that estimates whether a coronary stenosis limits blood flow.

Quick Answer

Fractional Flow Reserve (FFR): Invasive pressure-wire measurement during coronary angiography that estimates whether a coronary stenosis limits blood flow. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

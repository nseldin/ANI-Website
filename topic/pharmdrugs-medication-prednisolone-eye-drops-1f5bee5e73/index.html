Mechanism

Prednisolone eye drops works by diffusing into cells and activating cytosolic glucocorticoid receptors that move to the nucleus, change transcription, suppress phospholipase A2/COX-2/cytokine signaling, and reduce inflammatory cell trafficking. Benefit is less inflammation/immune activation; risks are hyperglycemia, infection masking, adrenal suppression, mood changes, myopathy, bone loss, skin thinning, and route-specific effects such as oral thrush or ocular pressure.

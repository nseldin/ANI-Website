Used To Treat

Relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis as disease-modifying therapy.

Mechanism

Glatiramer acetate shifts immune responses away from myelin-directed inflammatory T-cell activity. Teach injection rotation, local reactions, and transient post-injection flushing, chest tightness, palpitations, anxiety, or dyspnea.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Glatiramer acetate in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Glatiramer acetate is a multiple sclerosis immunomodulator and myelin basic protein analog mixture: it shifts immune responses away from myelin-directed inflammatory T-cell activity. It is used for relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis as disease-modifying therapy.

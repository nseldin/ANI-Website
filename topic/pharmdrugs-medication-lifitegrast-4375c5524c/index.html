Class

Topical LFA-1/ICAM-1 T-cell adhesion antagonist for dry-eye disease

Used To Treat

Signs and symptoms of dry-eye disease with twice-daily maintenance dosing according to the product label.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Hypersensitivity, including swelling, wheeze and anaphylaxis-like reactions, has been reported. Discard the single-use container immediately after dosing and do not save residual solution. Remove contact lenses before dosing and wait the labeled interval before reinsertion.

Mechanism

Activated T cells use integrin LFA-1 to bind ICAM-1 on vascular and epithelial surfaces, stabilizing immunologic contact and promoting migration and cytokine release. Lifitegrast occupies LFA-1 and reduces this binding, interrupting the self-amplifying inflammatory loop that destabilizes tears and injures epithelium. Punctal occlusion can reduce throat/nasal exposure and taste disturbance.

Nursing Essentials

  • Use one single-dose container for both eyes as directed, then discard it. Teach that taste disturbance reflects drainage and that consistent twice-daily use is needed to judge benefit.

  • Verify the exact eye, formulation, concentration, frequency, contact-lens instructions, and whether other drops must be separated by at least 5 minutes. Never let the bottle tip touch the eye, lashes, fingers, or another surface.

  • After the drop, gently close the eyelid and press the nasolacrimal duct for 1 to 2 minutes when appropriate. This reduces drainage into the nose and systemic exposure; wiping excess from the skin also limits local irritation.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity to lifitegrast or formulation components. Clarify active infection, unexplained corneal pain and contact-lens use.

Interactions

  • No major systemic interactions are expected at routine exposure. Separate lubricants and other topical agents by the prescribed interval; punctal occlusion can reduce dysgeusia.

Key Labs

  • Dry-eye symptom and ocular-surface examination

  • Visual acuity and corneal assessment when pain or persistent blur occurs

  • No routine serum monitoring

Nclex Traps

  • Lifitegrast blocks LFA-1/ICAM-1 adhesion; it is not a calcineurin inhibitor.

  • Dysgeusia is a classic clue to nasolacrimal drainage.

  • Do not recap and save a single-use container.

Description

Lifitegrast is a small-molecule eye drop that blocks lymphocyte function-associated antigen-1 (LFA-1) from binding ICAM-1. That interrupts T-cell adhesion, migration and activation across the inflamed ocular surface. It treats inflammatory dry-eye biology rather than simply adding fluid. Instillation irritation, transient blur and a distinctive unpleasant taste are common; dysgeusia occurs when drug drains through the nasolacrimal system.

Adverse Effects

  • Instillation irritation/pain, dysgeusia, transient reduced or blurred vision, hyperemia, tearing, discharge, headache and hypersensitivity.

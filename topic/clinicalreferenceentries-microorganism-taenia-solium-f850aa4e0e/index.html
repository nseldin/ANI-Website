Definition

Taenia solium is a clinically important cestode (tapeworm) associated with intestinal taeniasis, cysticercosis, and neurocysticercosis. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

Name

Platelets

Range

Adult female 150,000-400,000/mm3; pregnant female usually remains >=150,000/mm3, but mild gestational thrombocytopenia is usually 100,000-149,000/mm3 and often asymptomatic; <100,000/mm3 is not typical benign gestational thrombocytopenia and is a preeclampsia severe-feature threshold when paired with hypertension; HELLP platelet classes: 100,000-150,000 class III, 50,000-100,000 class II, <50,000 class I

Why

Use numbers to separate benign from dangerous. Example: 132,000/mm3 with normal BP/LFTs may fit mild gestational thrombocytopenia; 92,000/mm3 plus BP 160/110, RUQ pain, headache, or AST/ALT elevation points toward preeclampsia/HELLP.

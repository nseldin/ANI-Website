Definition

T sign in twin pregnancy is an ultrasound imaging sign. It is a clue to monochorionic diamniotic twin placentation. A positive finding means the membrane meets the placenta at a right angle without a chorionic wedge.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive T sign in twin pregnancy means the membrane meets the placenta at a right angle without a chorionic wedge.

Why It Matters

The T sign in twin pregnancy imaging pattern can signal monochorionic diamniotic twin placentation. A shared chorion leaves only two amnion layers at the placental junction. T sign in twin pregnancy cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

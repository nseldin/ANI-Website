Definition

Horner sign constellation is an ocular-autonomic constellation. It is a clue to interruption of the oculosympathetic pathway. A positive finding means mild ptosis and miosis occur, with anisocoria greater in darkness and sometimes anhidrosis.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Horner sign constellation means mild ptosis and miosis occur, with anisocoria greater in darkness and sometimes anhidrosis.

Why It Matters

The Horner sign constellation pattern can signal interruption of the oculosympathetic pathway. Loss of sympathetic input weakens Müller's lid muscle and iris dilator and may impair sweating. Horner sign constellation cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • interruption of the oculosympathetic pathway

Assessment Method

Inspect pupils in light and dark, lids, and facial sweating; assess dilation lag.

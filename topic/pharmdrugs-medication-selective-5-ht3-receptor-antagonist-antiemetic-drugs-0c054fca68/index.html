Medication class reference

Description

5-HT3 antagonists are antiemetic drugs that block serotonin type 3 signaling from the gut and chemoreceptor trigger zone. They are especially important when nausea is driven by chemotherapy, anesthesia, gastroenteritis, migraine, or protocol-directed pregnancy care.

Used To Treat

Nausea and vomiting from chemotherapy, postoperative states, gastroenteritis, migraine, pregnancy protocols, or other ordered indications.

Mechanism

They block serotonin 5-HT3 receptors on vagal afferents and in the chemoreceptor trigger zone, reducing emetic signaling from gut irritation or chemotherapy.

Nursing Essentials

  • Watch QT risk, serotonin-risk stacking, constipation, headache, electrolytes when vulnerable, and ECG context for high-risk clients.

Summary

Slit-lamp examination is a high-yield Eyes/Ears diagnostic reference. Magnified eye exam of cornea, anterior chamber, lens, and other structures for injury, infection, inflammation, or foreign body.

Quick Answer

Slit-lamp examination: Magnified eye exam of cornea, anterior chamber, lens, and other structures for injury, infection, inflammation, or foreign body. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Magnified eye exam of cornea, anterior chamber, lens, and other structures for injury, infection, inflammation, or foreign body.

    • Before / preparation

    • Check baseline vision/hearing concerns, pain, drainage, injury, diabetes/hypertension history, and whether drops or equipment affect testing.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Protect safety, explain simple cooperation steps, and connect abnormal findings to falls, infection, pressure, or neuro concerns.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Sudden vision/hearing loss, eye pain, trauma, very high intraocular pressure, neurologic deficits, or infection signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Eye and ear findings can be neurologic or safety problems, not just comfort issues.

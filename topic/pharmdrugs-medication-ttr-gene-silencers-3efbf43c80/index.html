Used To Treat

Product-specific hereditary ATTR polyneuropathy and, for vutrisiran, wild-type or hereditary ATTR cardiomyopathy.

Description

TTR gene silencers reduce synthesis of both mutant and wild-type transthyretin in hepatocytes, lowering the circulating precursor available to dissociate, misfold, and deposit as amyloid. Patisiran and vutrisiran are small interfering RNAs that recruit the RNA-induced silencing complex; inotersen and eplontersen are antisense oligonucleotides that recruit RNase H1. Delivery chemistry determines route, schedule, and toxicity rather than changing the final TTR-mRNA target.

Mechanism

Patisiran uses a lipid nanoparticle for IV hepatocyte delivery; vutrisiran couples siRNA to GalNAc for subcutaneous uptake through the asialoglycoprotein receptor. Inotersen is an unconjugated antisense oligonucleotide, while eplontersen is GalNAc-conjugated for hepatic targeting. All lower retinol-binding-protein transport because TTR carries that complex, so vitamin A levels fall even when tissue vitamin status is not directly measured by the serum number.

Boxed Warning

Inotersen uniquely carries boxed sudden, potentially fatal thrombocytopenia and glomerulonephritis/dialysis warnings and a REMS. The other three have no boxed warning; patisiran needs premedication and infusion-reaction surveillance, while all require product-specific vitamin A counseling. Never transfer inotersen's monitoring schedule or indication assumptions to another silencer.

Nursing Essentials

  • Identify molecule, RNA platform, route, interval, indication, renal/hepatic status, vitamin A plan, pregnancy context, and whether infusion premedication or REMS laboratory authorization is required.

  • Give only the recommended daily allowance of vitamin A unless a specialist directs otherwise; serum normalization with high doses may cause toxicity and does not prove tissue repletion.

Key Labs

  • TTR is a pharmacodynamic marker, not a stand-alone clinical outcome. Monitor neurologic or cardiac response, vitamin A-related ocular symptoms, and product-specific platelets, creatinine/eGFR, urinalysis/UPCR, liver tests, or infusion observations.

Nclex Traps

  • Silencers reduce new precursor production but do not directly dissolve every established amyloid deposit.

  • Patisiran and vutrisiran are siRNA; inotersen and eplontersen are antisense oligonucleotides.

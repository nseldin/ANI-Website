Definition

Plummer nails is an observed nail sign. It is a clue to thyrotoxicosis. A positive finding means the distal nail separates from the bed, often beginning at the fourth finger.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Plummer nails means the distal nail separates from the bed, often beginning at the fourth finger.

Why It Matters

The observed Plummer nails finding can signal thyrotoxicosis. Accelerated nail growth and altered keratin attachment promote distal onycholysis. Plummer nails cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • thyrotoxicosis

Assessment Method

Inspect the distal nail plate and bed.

Positive Finding

The distal nail separates from the bed, often beginning at the fourth finger.

Mechanism

Accelerated nail growth and altered keratin attachment promote distal onycholysis.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Plummer nails finding raises concern for thyrotoxicosis because accelerated nail growth and altered keratin attachment promote distal onycholysis. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Plummer nails does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Plummer nails to focus evaluation of thyrotoxicosis and the reviewed alternatives: trauma, onychomycosis, medication reaction. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect the distal nail plate and bed. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Plummer nails as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Onycholysis is common and rarely identifies thyroid disease by itself. Therefore, Plummer nails cannot confirm or exclude thyrotoxicosis by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • trauma

  • onychomycosis

  • medication reaction

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as trauma, onychomycosis, medication reaction.

False Negative Situations

Plummer nails may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of thyrotoxicosis.

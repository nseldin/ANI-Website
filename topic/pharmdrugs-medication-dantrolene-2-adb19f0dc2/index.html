Boxed Warning

Boxed warning for oral dantrolene: potentially fatal hepatotoxicity has occurred, especially with higher doses, longer duration, older age, and female sex. In malignant hyperthermia, IV dantrolene is life-saving and should not be delayed.

Class

Skeletal muscle relaxant; ryanodine receptor antagonist

Used To Treat

Malignant hyperthermia emergency, selected severe spasticity, and sometimes neuroleptic malignant syndrome when ordered.

Mechanism

Blocks calcium release from the skeletal muscle sarcoplasmic reticulum through ryanodine receptor antagonism. In malignant hyperthermia, this stops uncontrolled muscle contraction, heat production, acidosis, and rhabdomyolysis progression.

Nursing Essentials

  • For malignant hyperthermia: stop triggering anesthetic or succinylcholine, call for help, give IV dantrolene, cool the client, treat hyperkalemia and acidosis, and monitor for recurrence.

  • Monitor temperature, end-tidal CO2 if available, rigidity, heart rhythm, potassium, CK, urine color/output, renal function, and acid-base status.

Contraindications

  • Clarify active liver disease for chronic/oral use.

  • Use caution with significant muscle weakness, respiratory impairment, or cardiac disease. Emergency malignant hyperthermia treatment remains priority.

Interactions

  • Calcium channel blockers, especially verapamil or diltiazem, can increase serious cardiovascular and hyperkalemia risk in the malignant hyperthermia setting.

  • Alcohol and other CNS depressants can worsen sedation and weakness.

Key Labs

  • Potassium can rise in malignant hyperthermia.

  • CK and myoglobin can rise with rhabdomyolysis.

  • Liver function tests matter for chronic/oral therapy.

Nclex Traps

  • Dantrolene is the antidote-style medication for malignant hyperthermia. Fever can be late; rising end-tidal CO2 and muscle rigidity are early danger cues.

  • Do not confuse dantrolene with benzodiazepines. Benzodiazepines may calm seizures or agitation, but dantrolene treats the skeletal muscle calcium problem.

Description

Dantrolene is a skeletal muscle relaxant and ryanodine receptor antagonist: it blocks calcium release from the skeletal muscle sarcoplasmic reticulum through ryanodine receptor antagonism. It is used for malignant hyperthermia emergency, selected severe spasticity, and sometimes neuroleptic malignant syndrome when ordered.

Definition

Seashore sign is an ultrasound imaging sign. It is a clue to normal pleural sliding. A positive finding means horizontal chest-wall lines lie above a granular sandy pattern below the pleura.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Seashore sign means horizontal chest-wall lines lie above a granular sandy pattern below the pleura.

Why It Matters

The Seashore sign imaging pattern can signal normal pleural sliding. Moving aerated lung creates speckled motion beneath a stationary chest wall. Seashore sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • normal pleural sliding

Evidence Limitations

It excludes pneumothorax only at that location and motion artifact can mimic it. Therefore, Seashore sign cannot confirm or exclude normal pleural sliding by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

A false-positive interpretation can result from acquisition artifact, an inappropriate imaging phase or plane, or overlap with lung pulse, cardiac motion, poor probe contact.

False Negative Situations

Seashore sign may be missed when the associated process is early or subtle, the relevant region is not included, or acquisition timing and resolution are inadequate.

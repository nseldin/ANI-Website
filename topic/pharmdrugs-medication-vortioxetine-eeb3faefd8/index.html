Used To Treat

Major depressive disorder in adults.

Boxed Warning

Suicidal thoughts and behaviors: antidepressants increase risk in pediatric and young adult patients during short-term treatment. Monitor all clients for clinical worsening, agitation, or emergent suicidality, especially after starting or changing dose.

Mechanism

Vortioxetine inhibits the serotonin transporter and directly modulates several 5-HT receptor subtypes. The net effect increases serotonergic transmission and alters downstream neurotransmitter release, but the receptor-specific contribution to antidepressant efficacy has not been established.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess suicidality, bipolar/mania history, serotonin-drug combinations, bleeding-risk drugs, sodium/fall risk, nausea, sexual effects, and adherence.

  • Respect MAOI washout; monitor clonus, hyperreflexia, fever, agitation, diarrhea, and autonomic instability for serotonin syndrome.

Key Labs

  • Sodium when at risk, pregnancy context, bleeding/CBC when clinically indicated, and symptom/suicide assessment.

Nclex Traps

  • Multimodal does not mean non-serotonergic: vortioxetine can still cause serotonin syndrome and requires MAOI separation.

Description

Vortioxetine is a serotonin-reuptake inhibitor with additional receptor actions: 5-HT3/5-HT7/5-HT1D antagonism, 5-HT1B partial agonism, and 5-HT1A agonism. These actions modulate serotonergic networks, but the exact contribution beyond transporter blockade is uncertain; nausea, sexual dysfunction, serotonin toxicity, bleeding, hyponatremia, and manic activation remain central.

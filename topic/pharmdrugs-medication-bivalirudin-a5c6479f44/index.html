Description

Bivalirudin is a direct thrombin inhibitor anticoagulant: it directly binds thrombin's catalytic site and exosite, inhibiting free and clot-bound thrombin so fibrin formation and thrombin-driven platelet activation decrease. It is used for anticoagulation during PCI and selected heparin-induced thrombocytopenia or heparin-avoidance contexts by specialist/protocol order.

Susceptible infections based on organism, infection site, cultures, allergies, renal function, and local resistance patterns.

Mechanism

Ciprofloxacin ophthalmic works by inhibiting bacterial DNA gyrase/topoisomerase II and topoisomerase IV, blocking DNA supercoiling, replication, and chromosome separation. This bactericidal effect is useful but high-risk: tendon rupture, peripheral neuropathy, CNS effects, dysglycemia, QT prolongation, aortic warnings, myasthenia gravis worsening, and stewardship matter.

Boxed Warning

Fluoroquinolones carry boxed warnings for disabling tendon, muscle, joint, nerve, and CNS adverse effects. They also carry serious warnings for tendon rupture, peripheral neuropathy, CNS effects, worsening myasthenia gravis, and aortic aneurysm/dissection risk in vulnerable clients.

Contraindications

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

  • Clarify myasthenia gravis, tendon disorder, significant QT risk, pregnancy/pediatric concerns, and aortic aneurysm risk factors.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • Allergies, cultures before first dose when ordered, renal function for renally cleared drugs, liver function for hepatotoxic drugs, CBC, diarrhea/C. difficile cues, and therapeutic levels for aminoglycosides/vancomycin when ordered.

Nclex Traps

  • Do not delay urgent antibiotics for sepsis, but obtain cultures first if ordered and it does not delay care.

Description

Ciprofloxacin ophthalmic is a fluoroquinolone antibiotic: it works by inhibiting bacterial DNA gyrase/topoisomerase II and topoisomerase IV, blocking DNA supercoiling, replication, and chromosome separation. It is used for glaucoma or ocular hypertension treatment when the formulation and ocular indication match.

Name

Generic

flurazepam

Class

Benzodiazepine anxiolytic/sedative/anticonvulsant

Used To Treat

Anxiety, acute agitation, alcohol withdrawal, procedural sedation, muscle spasm, seizure rescue/status epilepticus, or insomnia depending on the agent.

Boxed Warning

Class boxed warning: concomitant opioid use can cause profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death; dependence/withdrawal risks also matter.

Mechanism

Flurazepam works by enhancing GABA-A activity, increasing inhibitory CNS tone. That is why sedation, falls, respiratory depression with other depressants, and withdrawal seizures matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor sedation, respiratory status, fall risk, paradoxical agitation, and withdrawal risk.

  • Use seizure precautions when given for seizures/withdrawal.

  • Flumazenil can reverse but may precipitate seizures in dependent clients.

Contraindications

  • Severe respiratory insufficiency unless monitored

  • myasthenia gravis or sleep apnea risk may require clarification

  • acute narrow-angle glaucoma for selected agents

Interactions

  • Opioids, alcohol, sedatives, antipsychotics, antihistamines increase CNS/respiratory depression.

Key Labs

  • respiratory assessment

  • liver function for longer-acting agents

  • CIWA/seizure assessment when relevant

Nclex Traps

  • Older adults are high-risk for delirium and falls. Do not mix with opioids casually.

Description

Flurazepam is a benzodiazepine anxiolytic/sedative/anticonvulsant: it works by enhancing GABA-A activity, increasing inhibitory CNS tone. It is used for anxiety, acute agitation, alcohol withdrawal, seizure rescue/status epilepticus support, procedural sedation, muscle spasm, or insomnia depending on the specific medication and order.

Sections

  • Content

    Droplet Precautions are transmission-based measures added to Standard Precautions when respiratory secretions carried over short-range respiratory exposure are an important route and baseline practices alone are insufficient.

    Id

    scope

    Title

    Scope

  • Content

    Use them for diseases and syndromes specified by current guidance, including seasonal influenza and suspected or confirmed invasive meningococcal disease. Because early presentations overlap with other infections, precautions may begin from the syndrome before the organism is known.

    Id

    indications

    Title

    Indications

  • Content

    A medical mask worn on room entry and patient source control reduce close-range exposure to infectious respiratory secretions. Standard Precautions add eye or face protection when splashes or sprays are anticipated, while patient placement, hand hygiene, equipment management, and transport planning reduce opportunities for secretions to reach others.

    Id

    mechanism

    Title

    Mechanism

  • Content

    Place and cohort patients according to policy, post clear instructions, perform hand hygiene, don required mask and other task-appropriate PPE before exposure, use patient source control during necessary transport, contain respiratory secretions, clean shared equipment, remove PPE safely, and perform hand hygiene again.

    Id

    procedure

    Title

    Procedure

  • Content

    Droplet Precautions do not replace Standard Precautions, ventilation, source control, or disease-specific airborne controls when aerosol transmission or an aerosol-generating procedure changes the risk. Exact PPE and duration can evolve and must follow current facility and public-health guidance.

    Id

    limitations

    Title

    Limitations

  • Content

    Do not delay emergency antibiotics, airway care, or resuscitation to complete routine room setup. Preserve monitoring and communication, identify exposed close contacts when the disease requires prophylaxis, and use the organism-specific discontinuation criteria rather than a generic fixed time.

  • Content

    Reviewed against current CDC isolation-precautions guidance and its condition-specific appendix.

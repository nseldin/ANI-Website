Used To Treat

Nausea and vomiting due to surgery/anesthesia, chemotherapy, migraine, vestibular disorders, pregnancy, gastroparesis, or GI illness depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Loperamide activates peripheral intestinal mu-opioid receptors, slowing peristalsis and increasing fluid absorption. Avoid in red-flag diarrhea; overdose can cause serious QT/QRS dysrhythmias.

Description

Loperamide is a peripheral mu-opioid receptor agonist antidiarrheal: it activates peripheral intestinal mu-opioid receptors, slowing peristalsis and increasing fluid absorption. It is used for short-term symptomatic diarrhea control when invasive infection, C. difficile, toxic megacolon, or severe inflammatory diarrhea is not suspected.

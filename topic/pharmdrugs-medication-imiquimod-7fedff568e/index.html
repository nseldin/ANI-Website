Class

Topical Toll-like receptor 7 agonist immune-response modifier

Used To Treat

Product/regimen-specific treatment of actinic keratoses, biopsy-confirmed primary superficial basal-cell carcinoma when surgery is less appropriate, and external genital/perianal warts in age-approved immunocompetent patients.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Intense local inflammation can cause weeping, erosion, ulceration, edema or phimosis and may require a rest period. Systemic influenza-like symptoms, hypotension and cytopenias are possible. Do not use on unhealed surgical skin or presume an undiagnosed pigmented, indurated or ulcerated lesion is eligible superficial BCC.

Mechanism

TLR7 activation recruits MyD88, IRAK kinases and TRAF6, activating NF-kappa-B and interferon-regulatory factors. Plasmacytoid dendritic cells and other innate cells produce type-I interferons and pro-inflammatory cytokines, enhancing antigen presentation, natural-killer activity and cytotoxic T-cell recruitment. In HPV warts this supports infected-cell clearance; in dysplasia/tumor fields it attacks abnormal keratinocytes. Imiquimod does not directly eradicate HPV from the body or guarantee cancer cure.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify strength, indication, nights per week, contact time and total course; each regimen differs.

  • Prepare the patient for inflammation, define hold/call thresholds, and arrange follow-up examination or biopsy to confirm clearance.

Contraindications

  • No label-listed absolute contraindication; avoid serious hypersensitivity

  • Do not apply internally to vagina, urethra, cervix, rectum, eyes or mouth

  • Clarify immune status, biopsy diagnosis, lesion subtype/location, unhealed skin and pregnancy

Interactions

  • Other irritants, retinoids, peels and UV exposure intensify skin injury.

  • Avoid simultaneous application to the same field unless specifically prescribed.

  • Systemic immunosuppression may reduce efficacy and changes risk interpretation.

Key Labs

  • Biopsy before superficial-BCC treatment and repeat evaluation of residual disease

  • CBC only when systemic symptoms/cytopenia are suspected

  • Serial lesion map, local reaction, lymph nodes and pregnancy assessment

Nclex Traps

  • Imiquimod activates TLR7; it does not directly poison DNA synthesis like fluorouracil.

  • External wart treatment does not eradicate HPV or prevent transmission.

  • Inflammation is expected, but severe erosion, systemic symptoms or urinary obstruction requires reassessment.

Description

Imiquimod is a topical innate-immune activator, not a direct cytotoxic chemotherapy. It stimulates Toll-like receptor 7 in endosomal immune cells, triggering interferon-alpha, TNF-alpha and other cytokines that recruit antiviral and antitumor immunity. The visible erythema, crusting and erosion reflect immune activation, but eligible lesion type and location are strict: superficial basal-cell carcinoma must be biopsy confirmed, small and appropriately located, and nodular or morpheaform tumors are not covered by the indication.

Adverse Effects

  • Erythema, erosion, crusting, flaking, edema, pain, burning and pigment change

  • Headache, fatigue, fever, myalgia, nausea and influenza-like syndrome

  • Lymphadenopathy, cytopenias and rare severe skin reactions

  • Genital swelling, erosion or phimosis

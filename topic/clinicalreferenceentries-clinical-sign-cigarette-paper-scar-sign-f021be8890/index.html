Definition

Cigarette-paper scar sign is an observed scar sign. It is a clue to dermal fragility in connective-tissue disease. A positive finding means scars are thin, widened, wrinkled, and papery.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Cigarette-paper scar sign means scars are thin, widened, wrinkled, and papery.

Why It Matters

The observed Cigarette-paper scar sign finding can signal dermal fragility in connective-tissue disease. Defective collagen reduces tensile strength and produces atrophic spreading during healing. Cigarette-paper scar sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • dermal fragility in connective-tissue disease

Assessment Method

Inspect old scars over knees, elbows, forehead, or shins.

Positive Finding

Scars are thin, widened, wrinkled, and papery.

Mechanism

Defective collagen reduces tensile strength and produces atrophic spreading during healing.

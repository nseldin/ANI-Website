Boxed Warning

Drug-specific boxed warning/class IC warning: increased mortality and nonfatal cardiac arrest were seen after myocardial infarction when used for asymptomatic ventricular ectopy. Avoid use for non-life-threatening ventricular dysrhythmias and clarify structural heart disease risk.

Contraindications

  • Cardiogenic shock.

  • Second- or third-degree AV block, sick sinus syndrome, or bundle-branch/bifascicular block without a pacemaker.

  • Clarify prior MI, structural heart disease, heart failure, significant QRS widening, or severe electrolyte abnormality before giving.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor ECG, PR interval, QRS duration, heart rate, dizziness, syncope, chest pain, dyspnea, and new/worse dysrhythmias.

  • Keep potassium and magnesium normal to lower proarrhythmia risk.

  • Question therapy if the client has structural heart disease, post-MI history, new heart failure symptoms, or marked QRS widening.

Nclex Traps

  • Flecainide is not a casual palpitations medication. The NCLEX safety clue is Class IC proarrhythmia, especially after MI or with structural heart disease.

  • Widening QRS, syncope, or new ventricular dysrhythmia means hold/question and notify the provider.

Class

Class IC antiarrhythmic; sodium-channel blocker

Used To Treat

Rhythm control for selected supraventricular dysrhythmias such as paroxysmal atrial fibrillation/flutter or PSVT, and selected serious ventricular dysrhythmias when benefits outweigh risks.

Mechanism

Strongly blocks fast cardiac sodium channels, slowing conduction through atrial, ventricular, and His-Purkinje tissue. ECG safety focus is QRS widening and proarrhythmia.

Interactions

  • Amiodarone and CYP2D6 inhibitors such as fluoxetine, paroxetine, and quinidine can increase flecainide levels.

  • Other antiarrhythmics, beta blockers, digoxin, and AV-node blockers can increase conduction slowing or bradycardia risk.

Key Labs

  • ECG/QRS and PR interval.

  • Potassium and magnesium.

  • Renal and hepatic function for dosing and accumulation risk.

  • Flecainide level when ordered.

Description

Flecainide is a class IC antiarrhythmic and sodium-channel blocker: it strongly blocks fast cardiac sodium channels, slowing conduction through atrial, ventricular, and His-Purkinje tissue. It is used for rhythm control for selected supraventricular dysrhythmias such as paroxysmal atrial fibrillation/flutter or PSVT, and selected serious ventricular dysrhythmias when benefits outweigh risks.

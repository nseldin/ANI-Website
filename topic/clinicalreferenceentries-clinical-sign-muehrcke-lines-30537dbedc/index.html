Definition

Muehrcke lines is an observed nail-bed sign. It is a clue to hypoalbuminemia or systemic illness. A positive finding means paired white bands blanch and do not move with nail growth.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Muehrcke lines means paired white bands blanch and do not move with nail growth.

Why It Matters

The observed Muehrcke lines finding can signal hypoalbuminemia or systemic illness. Nail-bed vascular edema from low albumin changes visible perfusion while the nail plate remains intact. Muehrcke lines cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • hypoalbuminemia or systemic illness

Assessment Method

Inspect paired transverse white lines and blanch the nail bed.

Positive Finding

Paired white bands blanch and do not move with nail growth.

Mechanism

Nail-bed vascular edema from low albumin changes visible perfusion while the nail plate remains intact.

False Negative Situations

Used To Treat

Chronic urate lowering for gout prevention and uric-acid control in selected tumor-lysis or hyperuricemia contexts; it does not treat an acute gout flare rapidly.

Mechanism

Allopurinol tumor lysis works by inhibiting xanthine oxidase, the enzyme that converts hypoxanthine and xanthine into uric acid. Lower uric-acid production prevents crystal burden over time, but mobilizing urate can precipitate flares early and hypersensitivity can be severe.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Allopurinol tumor lysis in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Allopurinol tumor lysis is a xanthine oxidase inhibitor: it works by inhibiting xanthine oxidase, the enzyme that converts hypoxanthine and xanthine into uric acid. It is used for chronic urate lowering for gout prevention and uric-acid control in selected tumor-lysis or hyperuricemia contexts; it does not treat an acute gout flare rapidly.

Contraindications

  • Initiation with calcium below the lower limit of normal

  • Serious hypersensitivity

  • Use caution with seizure disorder, QT risk, heart failure and moderate/severe hepatic impairment

Nursing Essentials

  • Give with food or shortly after a meal and swallow whole; food increases absorption and consistency reduces variability.

  • Measure calcium soon after initiation/dose changes and titrate from both calcium and intact PTH.

Interactions

  • Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors can raise cinacalcet exposure.

  • Cinacalcet strongly inhibits CYP2D6 and can raise flecainide, propafenone, tricyclic and other substrate exposure.

  • Other calcium-lowering or QT-prolonging drugs compound risk.

Key Labs

  • Corrected or ionized calcium

  • Phosphorus and intact PTH

  • ECG/electrolytes and hepatic function when risk warrants

Nclex Traps

  • Cinacalcet mimics a high-calcium signal; it does not contain calcium.

  • Very low PTH can mean adynamic bone, not perfect control.

  • Its CYP2D6 inhibition can be clinically important even though the main therapeutic target is parathyroid CaSR.

Used To Treat

Secondary hyperparathyroidism in adults with CKD on dialysis; hypercalcemia in parathyroid carcinoma; and severe hypercalcemia in primary hyperparathyroidism when parathyroidectomy is indicated but cannot be performed.

Mechanism

Positive allosteric binding shifts the CaSR calcium-response curve left. Enhanced Gq/Gi signaling lowers PTH release within hours, and reduced PTH decreases osteoclast-coupling and renal calcium conservation. Cinacalcet is metabolized through CYP3A4/1A2/2D6 and strongly inhibits CYP2D6, so it can alter both its own exposure and narrow-index substrate exposure.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Do not initiate if corrected calcium is below normal. Life-threatening/fatal hypocalcemia has occurred and can prolong QT, cause arrhythmia, lower seizure threshold, worsen hypotension or heart failure, and produce tetany. Cinacalcet is not indicated for secondary HPT in CKD patients not on dialysis because hypocalcemia risk is increased.

Description

Cinacalcet is an oral calcimimetic that positively modulates the parathyroid calcium-sensing receptor, increasing its sensitivity to extracellular calcium and suppressing PTH secretion. Chief cells interpret the same calcium concentration as higher, which reduces calcium and phosphate release from bone. The mechanism can overshoot into hypocalcemia with QT prolongation, seizure and arrhythmia, and overly low PTH can produce adynamic bone.

Adverse Effects

  • Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea and reduced appetite

  • Hypocalcemia, paresthesia, cramps and tetany

  • QT prolongation, arrhythmia, hypotension or seizure

  • Adynamic bone disease when intact PTH is oversuppressed

Mechanism

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess fall risk, sedation, driving safety, anticholinergic burden, glaucoma, urinary retention/BPH, constipation, and older-adult confusion risk.

Contraindications

  • Clarify narrow-angle glaucoma, urinary retention, severe BPH, significant CNS depressant use, or hypersensitivity.

Interactions

  • Alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines, sleep medicines, and other anticholinergics increase sedation/confusion risk.

Key Labs

  • No routine lab; safety is clinical sedation, balance, and anticholinergic assessment.

Nclex Traps

  • Meclizine fits vestibular/motion nausea better than chemotherapy serotonin-release nausea. Sedation and anticholinergic effects are the nursing catch.

Description

Meclizine is a first-generation H1 antihistamine with anticholinergic vestibular antiemetic action: it blocks H1 histamine signaling and has anticholinergic effects in vestibular nuclei/vomiting pathways, reducing abnormal inner-ear motion signals that trigger nausea and dizziness. It is used for motion sickness and vertigo-associated nausea when vestibular input is driving symptoms.

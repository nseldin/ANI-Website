Definition

Gottron sign is an observed skin sign. It is a clue to dermatomyositis. A positive finding means symmetric violaceous erythema or plaques occur over extensor joints.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Gottron sign means symmetric violaceous erythema or plaques occur over extensor joints.

Why It Matters

The observed Gottron sign finding can signal dermatomyositis. The same interface and microvascular inflammatory process extends beyond hand papules. Gottron sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • dermatomyositis

Assessment Method

Inspect extensor elbows, knees, or malleoli.

Positive Finding

Symmetric violaceous erythema or plaques occur over extensor joints.

Mechanism

The same interface and microvascular inflammatory process extends beyond hand papules.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Gottron sign finding raises concern for dermatomyositis because the same interface and microvascular inflammatory process extends beyond hand papules. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Gottron sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Gottron sign to focus evaluation of dermatomyositis and the reviewed alternatives: lupus, eczema, pressure erythema. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect extensor elbows, knees, or malleoli. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Gottron sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

It is less specific than classic papules and may be subtle. Therefore, Gottron sign cannot confirm or exclude dermatomyositis by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • lupus

  • eczema

  • pressure erythema

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as lupus, eczema, pressure erythema.

False Negative Situations

Gottron sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of dermatomyositis.

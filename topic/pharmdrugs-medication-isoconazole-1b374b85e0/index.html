Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; hepatotoxicity, QT effects, embryo-fetal risk for selected agents, and major CYP interactions are high-yield.

Mechanism

Isoconazole works by disrupting fungal ergosterol synthesis or fungal membrane function depending on class. Liver injury, QT risk, and CYP interactions are common safety anchors.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor liver symptoms/labs, QT risk, pregnancy status, and drug interactions.

  • Teach to report jaundice, dark urine, severe rash.

Description

Isoconazole is an antifungal: it works by disrupting fungal ergosterol synthesis or fungal membrane function depending on class. It is used for fungal infections or prophylaxis, including candidiasis, dermatophyte infections, systemic mycoses, or opportunistic infections depending the agent.

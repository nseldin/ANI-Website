Class

Oral nonpeptide pituitary GnRH receptor antagonist androgen-deprivation therapy

Used To Treat

Advanced prostate cancer in adults as single-agent ORGOVYX; relugolix also appears in the separate combination product MYFEMBREE for selected gynecologic indications.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current ORGOVYX labeling has no boxed warning. Androgen deprivation can prolong QT, so correct potassium/magnesium abnormalities and consider ECG monitoring with congenital long QT, heart failure or other QT-prolonging drugs. Serious angioedema/hypersensitivity and embryo-fetal toxicity also matter. Avoid oral P-gp inhibitors or separate by at least 6 hours when unavoidable; combined P-gp/strong CYP3A inducers can require a supervised dose change.

Mechanism

Relugolix competitively occupies pituitary GnRH receptors, reducing gonadotropin secretion and downstream Leydig-cell testosterone synthesis. A loading dose produces rapid suppression, daily dosing maintains it, and testosterone can recover after discontinuation. The drug does not directly poison prostate cancer cells; it removes androgen-receptor stimulation on which many tumors depend.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify 360-mg day-1 loading then 120 mg daily, exact product, adherence and interruptions: more than 7 days off requires reloading. Review P-gp/CYP3A drugs, QT and cardiovascular risk, electrolytes, partner contraception and hypersensitivity.

  • Trend testosterone and PSA with clinical/radiographic response; monitor hot flashes, metabolic change, anemia, sexual effects, mood, bone health, musculoskeletal symptoms and cardiovascular status.

Key Labs

  • Testosterone and PSA, CBC/hemoglobin, glucose/A1c, lipids, liver tests, potassium/magnesium, ECG when indicated and bone-health assessment during prolonged androgen deprivation.

Nclex Traps

  • GnRH antagonism avoids the initial testosterone flare of agonists, but missing more than 7 days requires the loading regimen again.

  • ORGOVYX is not MYFEMBREE; the latter's estrogen/progestin components change indication and boxed-warning risk.

Description

Relugolix is an oral competitive GnRH receptor antagonist that rapidly suppresses pituitary LH and FSH and lowers testicular testosterone to castrate concentrations for advanced prostate cancer without the initial androgen flare of a GnRH agonist. ORGOVYX is a P-gp substrate and relugolix alone; it must not be confused with MYFEMBREE, which combines relugolix with estradiol and norethindrone and has different indications and warnings.

Class

Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID)

Used To Treat

Pain, fever, and inflammation. Selected NSAIDs are also used for arthritis, dysmenorrhea, gout flares, or patent ductus arteriosus closure.

Boxed Warning

Class boxed warnings commonly include increased risk of serious cardiovascular thrombotic events and serious GI bleeding/ulceration/perforation.

Mechanism

Etodolac works by inhibiting cyclooxygenase-mediated prostaglandin production. That lowers pain/inflammation but can reduce renal blood flow and gastric protection while increasing bleeding/CV risk.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess GI bleed risk, kidney function, BP/heart failure, anticoagulant use, and allergy/asthma history.

  • Use cautiously in renal disease, older adults, heart failure, and anticoagulated clients.

Contraindications

  • Perioperative CABG pain

  • active serious GI bleeding

  • serious NSAID hypersensitivity

Interactions

  • Anticoagulants/antiplatelets/SSRIs increase bleeding risk.

  • ACE/ARB/diuretic combination increases kidney injury risk.

  • Lithium and methotrexate toxicity risk can increase.

Key Labs

  • creatinine/eGFR

  • hemoglobin/hematocrit if bleeding risk

  • blood pressure

Nclex Traps

  • NSAIDs can worsen kidney perfusion and heart failure; they are not benign OTC meds.

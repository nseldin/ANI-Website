Boxed Warning

High-alert warning focus: pretomanid must be used only in an appropriate multidrug resistant-TB regimen. Monitor for hepatotoxicity, treatment failure/resistance, QT-risk regimen partners, and companion-drug toxicities such as linezolid-related myelosuppression and neuropathy.

Class

Nitroimidazooxazine antitubercular; redox-activated antimycobacterial

Used To Treat

Drug-resistant pulmonary tuberculosis as part of specific combination regimens such as bedaquiline plus pretomanid plus linezolid, with or without moxifloxacin depending on protocol.

Description

Pretomanid is a nitroimidazooxazine TB drug that mycobacteria activate through the F420/Ddn redox system, generating reactive nitrogen species and disrupting mycolic-acid cell-wall lipid plus respiratory survival pathways. Nurses should immediately connect it to resistant-TB combination regimens, hepatotoxicity, neuropathy/myelosuppression context from companion drugs, QT/interaction checks, and strict adherence.

Mechanism

Pretomanid is a prodrug activated inside Mycobacterium tuberculosis by deazaflavin-dependent nitroreductase pathways. Activated metabolites can release nitric oxide under anaerobic conditions and interfere with mycolic-acid/arabinogalactan cell-wall lipid construction and respiratory survival, which helps target persistent drug-resistant TB populations.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm the exact resistant-TB regimen; pretomanid is not a stand-alone TB drug.

  • Monitor liver symptoms, CBC/neuropathy cues when linezolid is included, ECG/electrolytes when QT-risk partners are used, and adherence to the full course.

  • Escalate visual changes, numbness/tingling, unexplained bruising, severe nausea, jaundice, or persistent fever/cough.

Contraindications

  • Clarify hypersensitivity, pregnancy/lactation questions, severe hepatic disease, and whether the full required regimen can be safely used.

Interactions

  • Interaction risk is regimen-based: linezolid, bedaquiline, moxifloxacin, QT-prolonging drugs, serotonergic drugs, and hepatotoxic medications may all matter.

Key Labs

  • Liver function tests.

  • CBC if linezolid is included.

  • ECG/QTc and electrolytes when regimen requires.

  • TB culture/susceptibility and symptom trend.

Nclex Traps

  • Pretomanid is not ordinary metronidazole for TB; it is a redox-activated resistant-TB drug used only in defined combinations.

  • When BPaL/BPaLM is used, linezolid toxicity monitoring is part of the nursing picture even though pretomanid is the card name.

Mechanism

Mycophenolate mofetil works by inhibiting inosine monophosphate dehydrogenase, blocking de novo guanine synthesis that activated T and B lymphocytes depend on. That protects transplants and treats immune disease but explains infection risk, cytopenias, GI toxicity, teratogenicity, and interaction-heavy immunosuppression plans.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Mycophenolate mofetil in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Mycophenolate mofetil is an antimetabolite immunosuppressant and IMPDH inhibitor: it works by inhibiting inosine monophosphate dehydrogenase, blocking de novo guanine synthesis that activated T and B lymphocytes depend on. It is used for transplant rejection prevention, autoimmune disease control, or immune-modifying protocols depending on the agent.

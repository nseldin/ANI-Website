Summary

Compartment pressure measurement is a high-yield Musculoskeletal diagnostic reference. Direct pressure measurement in a muscle compartment when acute compartment syndrome is suspected.

Quick Answer

Compartment pressure measurement: Direct pressure measurement in a muscle compartment when acute compartment syndrome is suspected. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Direct pressure measurement in a muscle compartment when acute compartment syndrome is suspected.

    • Before / preparation

    • Treat suspected acute compartment syndrome as urgent. Assess pain out of proportion, pain with passive stretch, paresthesia, pallor, paralysis, and pulses.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Prepare the client, maintain safety/sterility, monitor the correct body system afterward, and report complications early.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Bleeding, infection, severe pain, respiratory distress, neuro change, perforation signs, abnormal drainage, fever, or unstable vital signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Do not wait for absent pulses. Pulses can remain present until late.

Used To Treat

Nausea and vomiting due to surgery/anesthesia, chemotherapy, migraine, vestibular disorders, pregnancy, gastroparesis, or GI illness depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Famotidine works by blocking histamine H2 receptors on parietal cells, lowering gastric acid. Confusion risk in older adults and renal dosing are classic nursing angles.

Boxed Warning

No boxed warning commonly emphasized; confusion can occur especially in older adults or renal impairment.

Contraindications

  • Known hypersensitivity to H2 blockers.

  • Renal impairment requires dosing review.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor symptom relief and GI bleeding signs.

  • Dose adjustment may be needed in renal impairment.

  • Assess confusion in older adults.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Acid-reducing effects can alter absorption of pH-dependent drugs.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • creatinine/eGFR

  • hemoglobin/occult blood if GI bleed concern

  • QT risk, sedation, EPS, anticholinergic effects, bowel obstruction concern, hydration, and serotonin syndrome risk when relevant.

Nclex Traps

  • New confusion in an older adult after an H2 blocker is worth assessing.

  • Different antiemetics have different dangers: ondansetron/QT, metoclopramide/EPS-tardive dyskinesia, promethazine/sedation-tissue injury.

Description

Famotidine is a histamine-2 receptor antagonist: it works by blocking histamine H2 receptors on parietal cells, lowering gastric acid. It is used for bowel symptom control, constipation relief, bowel preparation, secretion thinning, or diarrhea management depending on the specific agent and contraindications.

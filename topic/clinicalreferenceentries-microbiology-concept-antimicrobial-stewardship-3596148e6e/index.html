    Antimicrobial stewardship is a coordinated approach to selecting and monitoring antimicrobial therapy so patients receive effective treatment while avoidable toxicity, resistance pressure, and unnecessary exposure are reduced.

    Id

    definition

    Title

    Definition

  • Content

    Accurate diagnosis, timely cultures when indicated, appropriate empiric coverage, source control, review of results, dose optimization, de-escalation, route reassessment, and appropriate duration each reduce mismatch between therapy and the actual infection.

    Id

    mechanism

    Title

    Mechanism

  • Content

    Core practices include prospective review and feedback, facility-specific guidance, antibiotic time-outs, tracking, education, and leadership accountability.

    Id

    examples

    Title

    Examples

  • Content

    Stewardship does not mean withholding urgent therapy from a critically ill patient. It means starting appropriately, reassessing deliberately, and stopping or narrowing when evidence supports it.

    Id

    clinical-significance

    Title

    Clinical significance

  • Content

    No universal drug or duration fits every syndrome, organism, site, or patient. Local resistance data and patient-specific renal, hepatic, allergy, pregnancy, and interaction factors remain essential.

    CDC Core Elements define hospital stewardship as a systems approach to better antibiotic use, while WHO describes responsible use as part of resistance control.

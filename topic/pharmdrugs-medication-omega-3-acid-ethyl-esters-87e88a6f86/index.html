Contraindications

  • Serious hypersensitivity to components is a contraindication. Fish or shellfish allergy requires caution because the residual risk from purified marine oils is uncertain.

  • Do not use the cardiovascular indication or evidence of icosapent ethyl to justify this mixed product.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify product composition, total grams per day, capsule count, meal schedule, baseline LDL and triglycerides, AF history, antithrombotics, hepatic impairment, and allergy.

  • Swallow capsules whole. Repeat the full lipid panel because a strong triglyceride response can coexist with an undesirable LDL rise.

Interactions

  • Anticoagulants and antiplatelets can add bleeding risk; monitor clinically and with drug-specific testing when appropriate.

  • Continue or optimize LDL-directed therapy when indicated because this product is a triglyceride treatment, not a statin replacement.

Key Labs

  • Triglycerides, LDL-C, non-HDL-C, ApoB selectively, liver enzymes in hepatic impairment, rhythm assessment, and bleeding surveillance.

  • At very high triglycerides, calculated LDL may be unreliable; reassess LDL and ApoB burden as triglycerides improve.

Nclex Traps

  • Lovaza-type products contain EPA plus DHA and may raise LDL-C. Vascepa is purified EPA and has a distinct outcome indication.

  • Prescription strength does not prove pancreatitis prevention; the label says that benefit has not been established.

Used To Treat

Adjunct to diet to reduce triglycerides in adults with severe hypertriglyceridemia at least 500 mg/dL. Current labeling does not establish cardiovascular-event or pancreatitis-risk reduction.

Mechanism

Digestive lipases release EPA and DHA from their ethyl esters. Both reduce hepatic triglyceride synthesis, increase beta oxidation, reduce substrate for diacylglycerol acyltransferase, and decrease VLDL assembly or secretion while increasing lipoprotein-lipase clearance. DHA can increase conversion of VLDL remnants to LDL and change LDL particle composition, which explains why LDL-C may rise even as triglycerides fall. Omega-3 incorporation into cardiac membranes and ion-channel or autonomic signaling may affect atrial electrophysiology, explaining recurrence warnings in patients with paroxysmal or persistent AF. Effects on platelet aggregation and thromboxane-related pathways can lengthen bleeding time, especially with antithrombotics.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. FDA boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Mixed EPA-DHA prescription products can raise LDL-C, increase atrial fibrillation or flutter recurrence, and add bleeding risk. Monitor the complete lipid panel, rhythm symptoms, and antithrombotic context rather than focusing only on falling triglycerides.

Class Pathway

  • Hydrolysis to EPA plus DHA

  • Reduced hepatic triglyceride synthesis and VLDL secretion

  • Triglyceride lowering with possible LDL-C increase

Description

Omega-3-acid ethyl esters, historically branded Lovaza, are a prescription EPA-DHA mixture that lowers severe triglycerides by reducing hepatic triglyceride synthesis and VLDL secretion and increasing triglyceride-rich-particle clearance, although its DHA component can raise LDL-C in some patients. The total dose is 4 g/day, taken once daily or in divided doses according to the product. They are not equivalent to purified EPA icosapent ethyl, and the cardiovascular outcome evidence for Vascepa does not transfer to a mixed product. They are also not interchangeable with OTC fish oil, whose EPA-DHA dose, oxidation, purity, and capsule burden vary. The label is for severe triglyceride lowering; it states that effects on cardiovascular mortality and morbidity and on pancreatitis risk have not been established. Atrial fibrillation or flutter recurrence, bleeding, fish allergy, and liver monitoring in hepatic impairment remain clinically important.

Adverse Effects

  • Eructation, dyspepsia, taste change, nausea, GI discomfort, and infection-like complaints are commonly reported.

  • LDL-C elevation, atrial fibrillation or flutter recurrence, bleeding, liver-enzyme elevation, and serious hypersensitivity can occur.

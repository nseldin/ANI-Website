Used To Treat

Add-on prevention in selected severe or uncontrolled asthma after diagnosis, adherence, inhaler technique, triggers, and comorbidities have been addressed. Selection follows phenotype, biomarkers, allergic sensitization, eosinophils, exacerbations, oral-steroid burden, comorbid disease, age, and payer criteria; these drugs do not rescue acute bronchospasm.

Description

The four antibodies interrupt different levels of the type 2 inflammatory network. Omalizumab binds free IgE, preventing mast-cell and basophil Fc-epsilon-RI loading. Mepolizumab binds the IL-5 ligand, depriving eosinophils of a major survival signal. Benralizumab binds IL-5 receptor alpha and recruits natural killer cells to deplete eosinophils through antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity. Tezepelumab blocks epithelial TSLP upstream of several cytokine pathways, so it can reduce multiple biomarkers and does not require the same narrowly defined allergic or eosinophilic phenotype. The target explains both who may benefit and what laboratory change should be expected.

Mechanism

Allergen exposure can cross-link IgE on Fc-epsilon-RI, releasing histamine, leukotrienes, and cytokines. Omalizumab intercepts free IgE and down-regulates Fc-epsilon-RI over time. IL-5 drives eosinophil differentiation, recruitment, activation, and survival through IL-5 receptor alpha plus the common beta chain; mepolizumab neutralizes IL-5 before receptor binding. Benralizumab occupies IL-5 receptor alpha and its afucosylated Fc binds Fc-gamma-RIIIa strongly on natural killer cells, causing near-complete eosinophil ADCC depletion. TSLP is an epithelial alarmin released near the top of the cascade; tezepelumab blocks TSLP-receptor interaction and reduces eosinophils, FeNO, IL-5, IL-13, and IgE. These are preventive immune effects measured over weeks, not immediate smooth-muscle relaxation.

Boxed Warning

Omalizumab has a boxed warning for life-threatening anaphylaxis that may occur after the first dose or after more than a year of therapy; initiation, observation, emergency readiness, and self-administration selection are deliberate. The other listed biologics have serious hypersensitivity risks without a boxed warning. Treat relevant helminth infection before IL-5/IL-5R/TSLP pathway therapy, avoid abrupt corticosteroid withdrawal, and preserve a separate acute-asthma plan.

Adverse Effects

  • Injection-site reactions, headache, hypersensitivity or anaphylaxis, and product-specific infection or immune effects

  • Failure when the selected target does not match the dominant phenotype, adherence problem, exposure, or comorbidity

Contraindications

  • Known severe hypersensitivity to the product; active untreated helminth infection changes suitability for selected biologics

  • Not for status asthmaticus, acute bronchospasm, or unsupervised replacement of corticosteroids

Nursing Essentials

  • Before initiation, document exacerbations, steroid courses, eosinophils, total IgE and allergen sensitization where relevant, FeNO if available, spirometry, adherence, and technique.

  • Track exacerbation rate and steroid burden rather than expecting an immediate subjective response; maintain anaphylaxis readiness and product-specific observation.

Interactions

  • Abrupt corticosteroid reduction can unmask adrenal insufficiency or eosinophilic disease; taper gradually.

  • Tezepelumab labeling advises avoiding live attenuated vaccines; biologics should not be casually combined without specialist evidence.

Key Labs

  • Absolute eosinophil count, total IgE before omalizumab dosing, allergen sensitization, FeNO, spirometry, and exacerbation history

  • Total IgE rises during omalizumab because drug-IgE complexes clear slowly, so repeat total IgE is not interpreted as free-IgE failure

Nclex Traps

  • Mepolizumab neutralizes IL-5; benralizumab binds IL-5 receptor alpha and depletes eosinophils.

  • A biologic can prevent future attacks but cannot replace epinephrine for anaphylaxis or a rapid bronchodilator during acute bronchospasm.

Used To Treat

Insomnia treatment when short-term or carefully monitored hypnotic therapy is appropriate.

Mechanism

Eszopiclone binds the benzodiazepine site on GABA-A receptors, increasing inhibitory chloride-channel signaling and sedation. Additive CNS depressants and unsafe nighttime behaviors matter more than simply whether the client slept.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for eszopiclone in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Eszopiclone is a Z-drug hypnotic that enhances GABA-A inhibitory signaling to promote sleep in insomnia. It is not a benign sleep vitamin: next-day impairment, falls, CNS depression, dependence, and complex sleep behaviors are the core nursing concerns.

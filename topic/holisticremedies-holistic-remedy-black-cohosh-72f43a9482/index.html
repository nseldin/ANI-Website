Aliases

  • actaea racemosa

  • cimicifuga racemosa

Used For

  • Marketed for menopausal hot flashes, night sweats, and menstrual symptoms

Nclex Essential

true

Major Risks

  • Possible liver toxicity

  • GI upset

  • Rash

  • Headache

Interactions

  • Hepatotoxic medications

  • Hormone-related therapies without provider review

Contraindications

  • Liver disease

  • Unexplained vaginal bleeding without evaluation

  • Hormone-sensitive cancer history without oncology/provider review

Nursing Assessment

  • Assess liver history, jaundice, dark urine, RUQ pain, and estrogen-sensitive cancer history

Teaching

  • Report jaundice, dark urine, severe fatigue, RUQ pain, or pale stools immediately

Nclex Traps

  • The key nursing teaching is liver warning signs, not just hot flash symptom relief.

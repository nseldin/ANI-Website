Used To Treat

Boxed Warning

No universal boxed warning; nitrate interaction can cause severe hypotension.

Mechanism

Riociguat works by inhibiting phosphodiesterase-5, preserving cGMP signaling so nitric-oxide-mediated smooth-muscle relaxation lasts longer. That supports erectile function or pulmonary vasodilation but makes nitrates and riociguat dangerous because profound hypotension can occur.

Nursing Essentials

  • Do not combine with nitrates.

  • Assess BP, chest pain meds, alpha blockers, and priapism/vision/hearing warning symptoms.

Contraindications

Interactions

  • Alpha blockers and antihypertensives increase hypotension risk.

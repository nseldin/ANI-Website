Class

Central melanocortin-4-receptor agonist for defined hypothalamic/monogenic obesity

Used To Treat

Long-term excess-weight reduction in patients age 4 years and older with acquired hypothalamic obesity, and age 2 years and older with Bardet-Biedl syndrome or genetically confirmed POMC, PCSK1, or LEPR deficiency; not general polygenic obesity.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current IMCIVREE labeling has no boxed warning. Monitor spontaneous sexual arousal, prolonged erections/priapism, depression and suicidal ideation, serious hypersensitivity, diffuse hyperpigmentation and changing/new nevi; perform baseline and periodic full-skin examination. In acquired hypothalamic obesity, monitor adrenal insufficiency and sodium imbalance, especially with central diabetes insipidus. Stop if meaningful early weight response is absent per label.

Mechanism

Leptin signaling through POMC neurons generates alpha-MSH, which activates hypothalamic MC4 receptors to reduce food intake and increase energy expenditure. POMC, PCSK1 or LEPR defects and selected hypothalamic injury reduce pathway activation. Setmelanotide directly agonizes MC4R downstream of those lesions. Its lower-level MC1 activity stimulates melanocyte melanin production, explaining generalized hyperpigmentation and darkening/new nevi.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm the qualifying diagnosis and age, genetic report when required, acquired-HO endocrine deficits, renal function, mood/suicide history, skin examination and sexual adverse-effect counseling.

  • Trend hunger and weight response, rotate injection sites and monitor sodium, fluid balance and adrenal-replacement adequacy in hypothalamic patients. Do not generalize its indication to common obesity.

Key Labs

  • Weight/BMI trajectory and hunger score, renal function, sodium/osmolality and adrenal-axis replacement in acquired HO, glucose/A1c and lipids as clinically indicated, plus full skin examination.

Nclex Traps

  • MC4 agonism only bypasses defects upstream of MC4R; the drug is not a universal appetite suppressant.

  • Skin darkening follows off-target/less-selective MC1 activation and requires lesion surveillance, not simply cosmetic reassurance.

Description

Setmelanotide is a daily subcutaneous MC4-receptor agonist that restores signaling in a defined central satiety pathway when the defect lies upstream of an intact MC4 receptor. It can reduce pathologic hunger in acquired hypothalamic obesity, Bardet-Biedl syndrome, and selected POMC/PCSK1/LEPR deficiencies. It is not expected to work for ordinary obesity or defects that abolish MC4-receptor function itself.

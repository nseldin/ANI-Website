Definition

Scratch test for liver span is an auscultatory percussion maneuver. It is a clue to estimated liver border. A positive finding means sound intensity changes near the proposed liver border.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Scratch test for liver span means sound intensity changes near the proposed liver border.

Why It Matters

A positive Scratch test for liver span response can signal estimated liver border. Solid liver transmits scratching vibrations differently from air-filled bowel. Scratch test for liver span cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Definition

Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign is an imaging sign. It is a clue to oncocytic or hypervascular renal mass. A positive finding means vessels or enhancing septa radiate from a central scar.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign means vessels or enhancing septa radiate from a central scar.

Why It Matters

The Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign imaging pattern can signal oncocytic or hypervascular renal mass. A central fibrous scar and radially arranged vascular stroma create the pattern. Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • oncocytic or hypervascular renal mass

Assessment Method

Review contrast CT or angiography for radial enhancement.

Positive Finding

Vessels or enhancing septa radiate from a central scar.

Mechanism

A central fibrous scar and radially arranged vascular stroma create the pattern.

Clinical Interpretation

The Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign imaging pattern raises concern for oncocytic or hypervascular renal mass because a central fibrous scar and radially arranged vascular stroma create the pattern. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign to focus evaluation of oncocytic or hypervascular renal mass and the reviewed alternatives: focal nodular hyperplasia metastasis, angiomyolipoma, RCC. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Review contrast CT or angiography for radial enhancement. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Substantial overlap prevents reliable benign diagnosis. Therefore, Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign cannot confirm or exclude oncocytic or hypervascular renal mass by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • focal nodular hyperplasia metastasis

  • angiomyolipoma

  • RCC

False Positive Situations

A false-positive interpretation can result from acquisition artifact, an inappropriate imaging phase or plane, or overlap with focal nodular hyperplasia metastasis, angiomyolipoma, RCC.

False Negative Situations

Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign may be missed when the associated process is early or subtle, the relevant region is not included, or acquisition timing and resolution are inadequate.

Modern Status

Limited imaging clue; not specific enough to classify a mass. When performed as described, Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign can adjust concern for oncocytic or hypervascular renal mass, but it is not a stand-alone diagnosis. Apply the reviewed limitation for Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign: Substantial overlap prevents reliable benign diagnosis.

For Spoke-wheel renal tumor sign, verify patient identity, the indication, acquisition quality, timing, and the relevant imaging plane or electrodiagnostic leads. Apply the reviewed precaution for this pattern: Do not reassure that a renal mass is benign solely from spoke-wheel enhancement.

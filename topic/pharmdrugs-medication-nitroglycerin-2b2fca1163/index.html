Mechanism

Nitroglycerin works by donating nitric-oxide-related vasodilator activity, reducing preload and/or afterload so myocardial oxygen demand falls.

Boxed Warning

No boxed warning commonly emphasized; life-threatening hypotension can occur with PDE-5 inhibitors.

Contraindications

  • Recent PDE-5 inhibitor use such as sildenafil, tadalafil, or vardenafil.

  • Severe hypotension.

  • Severe anemia or increased intracranial pressure requires clarification.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Check blood pressure before use.

  • Teach headache is common; severe hypotension/syncope is not okay.

  • Store sublingual tablets properly and replace when expired.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil and riociguat can cause severe hypotension.

  • Alcohol and antihypertensives can worsen hypotension.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • No routine lab; assess BP, pain, ECG changes

  • Use medication-specific labs when ordered. For high-risk drugs, connect renal function, liver function, electrolytes, CBC, coagulation studies, drug levels, ECG, and vital-sign trends to safety.

Nclex Traps

  • Recent erectile dysfunction medication use is the classic hold/clarify cue.

  • The NCLEX trap is memorizing the name without connecting class, contraindication, assessment, and priority adverse effect.

Used To Treat

Angina/chest pain from myocardial ischemia, ACS support, and selected acute heart failure/pulmonary edema BP reduction.

Description

Nitroglycerin is a nitrate antianginal: it works by donating nitric-oxide-related vasodilator activity, reducing preload and/or afterload so myocardial oxygen demand falls. It is used for angina/chest pain from myocardial ischemia, ACS support, and selected acute heart failure/pulmonary edema BP reduction.

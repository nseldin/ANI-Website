Used To Treat

Susceptible infections based on organism, infection site, cultures, allergies, renal function, and local resistance patterns.

Mechanism

Linezolid works by binding the 50S ribosomal subunit near 23S rRNA and preventing formation of the initiation complex for bacterial protein synthesis. It covers resistant gram-positive organisms, but thrombocytopenia, serotonin syndrome risk, lactic acidosis, optic/peripheral neuropathy with prolonged use, and CBC monitoring matter.

Contraindications

  • Known serious hypersensitivity to the medication/class.

  • Clarify before giving if pregnancy, severe renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vitals, or high-risk interactions are present.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Description

Linezolid is an oxazolidinone antibiotic: it works by binding the 50S ribosomal subunit near 23S rRNA and preventing formation of the initiation complex for bacterial protein synthesis. It is used for resistant gram-positive infections such as MRSA or VRE pneumonia/skin/bloodstream-context infections when protocol and susceptibility fit.

Definition

Tracheal deviation sign is an observed and palpated emergency sign. It is a clue to major intrathoracic pressure or volume shift. A positive finding means the trachea is displaced from midline.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Tracheal deviation sign means the trachea is displaced from midline.

Why It Matters

The observed Tracheal deviation sign finding can signal major intrathoracic pressure or volume shift. Pressure pushes the mediastinum away, while major volume loss can pull it toward the affected side. Tracheal deviation sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • major intrathoracic pressure or volume shift

Assessment Method

Inspect and gently palpate tracheal position at the suprasternal notch.

Positive Finding

The trachea is displaced from midline.

Mechanism

Pressure pushes the mediastinum away, while major volume loss can pull it toward the affected side.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Tracheal deviation sign finding raises concern for major intrathoracic pressure or volume shift because pressure pushes the mediastinum away, while major volume loss can pull it toward the affected side. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Tracheal deviation sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Tracheal deviation sign to focus evaluation of major intrathoracic pressure or volume shift and the reviewed alternatives: neck mass, rotated positioning, prior surgery. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect and gently palpate tracheal position at the suprasternal notch. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Tracheal deviation sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Normal asymmetry, goiter, and late timing limit value. Therefore, Tracheal deviation sign cannot confirm or exclude major intrathoracic pressure or volume shift by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • neck mass

  • rotated positioning

  • prior surgery

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as neck mass, rotated positioning, prior surgery.

False Negative Situations

Tracheal deviation sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of major intrathoracic pressure or volume shift.

The reviewed precaution for this finding is: Do not wait for deviation before treating obstructive shock and respiratory collapse.

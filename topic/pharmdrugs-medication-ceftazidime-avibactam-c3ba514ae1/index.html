Class

IV antipseudomonal third-generation cephalosporin plus class A/C and selected D beta-lactamase inhibitor

Used To Treat

Susceptible complicated urinary, complicated intra-abdominal with metronidazole, and hospital-acquired/ventilator-associated bacterial pneumonia in adults and indicated pediatric ages. It is a key option for selected KPC- or OXA-48-like-producing Enterobacterales and some resistant Pseudomonas, but not MBL infection as monotherapy.

Description

Ceftazidime/avibactam, branded Avycaz, combines an antipseudomonal cephalosporin that binds PBPs with a diazabicyclooctane inhibitor that protects it from many ESBL, AmpC, KPC, and some OXA-48-like serine beta-lactamases. Ceftazidime supplies bactericidal cell-wall activity; avibactam has no direct antibacterial effect. The combination does not inhibit class B metallo-beta-lactamases such as NDM, VIM, or IMP. For selected MBL-producing Enterobacterales, aztreonam can evade the MBL while avibactam protects aztreonam from co-produced serine enzymes, which explains an aztreonam-plus-Avycaz strategy rather than Avycaz alone.

Mechanism

Ceftazidime enters through porins and acylates essential PBPs, while avibactam reversibly carbamylates susceptible beta-lactamases. Efficacy depends on ceftazidime fT>MIC and avibactam time above a protective threshold. Renal clearance of both components means rapidly changing kidney function can produce either underexposure or ceftazidime neurotoxicity. Resistance can emerge through KPC or AmpC mutation, enzyme overexpression, porin loss, efflux, and PBP change, so repeat cultures and source control matter during persistent high-burden infection.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify species, MIC, carbapenemase result, renal function, dose, infusion, and source control before administration because enzyme match and component exposure determine whether the combination is active.

  • For persistent bacteremia or clinical decline, repeat cultures and contact infectious disease/microbiology because emergent resistance or an unrecognized MBL can make continuation harmful.

Key Labs

  • Culture/MIC, carbapenemase gene/phenotype, creatinine/eGFR at baseline and during change, CBC, liver tests, mental status, and serial cultures when clearance matters.

  • When used with aztreonam for an MBL, coordinate overlapping administration under specialist protocol because avibactam must protect aztreonam during exposure.

Nclex Traps

  • Avycaz covers KPC and some OXA-48-like enzymes, not NDM/VIM/IMP.

  • For complicated intra-abdominal infection, Avycaz is used with metronidazole because ceftazidime/avibactam does not supply complete anaerobic coverage.

Ceftazidime Avibactam

Adverse Effects

  • Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, headache, liver-test elevation, positive direct Coombs test, phlebitis, candidiasis, and CDI.

  • Anaphylaxis, severe cutaneous reaction, cytopenias, interstitial nephritis, confusion, myoclonus, nonconvulsive status, or seizure with ceftazidime accumulation.

Contraindications

  • Serious hypersensitivity to ceftazidime, avibactam, cephalosporins, or relevant beta-lactams is a contraindication.

  • Do not use Avycaz alone for known NDM, VIM, or IMP because avibactam cannot inhibit the zinc-dependent enzyme.

Interactions

  • Probenecid and potent OAT inhibitors may reduce avibactam renal elimination; apply current product guidance.

  • Nephrotoxic co-therapy can reduce clearance and amplify ceftazidime neurologic toxicity.

    Pediatric caution

    Note

    Pediatric indication, meningitis role, formulation, dose, interval, renal maturation, and maximum dose differ by agent and age. Use the exact label or pediatric infectious-disease protocol because beta-lactams are not safely scaled from an adult regimen by intuition.

  • Type

    geriatric

    Label

    Older adult caution

    Note

    Older adults can have sharply reduced renal clearance despite an unremarkable serum creatinine. Estimate kidney function, reassess it during acute illness, and connect new confusion, myoclonus, or seizure to possible beta-lactam accumulation rather than assuming delirium is unrelated.

  • Type

    pregnancy

    Label

    Pregnancy and lactation

    Note

    Many beta-lactams have substantial pregnancy and lactation experience, but infection severity, allergy history, maternal organ function, placental exposure, neonatal bilirubin handling, and the exact agent still matter. Treat serious infection promptly with an evidence-supported regimen rather than applying one class-wide pregnancy rule.

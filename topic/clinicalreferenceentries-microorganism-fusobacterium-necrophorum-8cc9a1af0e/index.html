Definition

Fusobacterium necrophorum is a clinically important bacterium associated with severe pharyngitis, peritonsillar infection, Lemierre syndrome, and septic pulmonary emboli. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

  • Content

    Fusobacterium necrophorum is a Gram-negative bacillus with a obligate anaerobe oxygen profile. It is not acid-fast, not known to form spores, nonmotile, and extracellular in its reviewed clinical behavior. These traits help narrow an early laboratory differential, but no trait pattern alone proves identity or susceptibility.

Mechanism

Velagliflozin works by blocking proximal-tubule sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 (SGLT2) so glucose and sodium are excreted in urine. The same osmotic diuresis and glucosuria explain blood-pressure/heart-failure/kidney benefits, genital mycotic infections, volume depletion, and euglycemic ketoacidosis risk.

Summary

Colposcopy is a high-yield Obstetrics/Gynecology diagnostic reference. Magnified cervical/vaginal exam after abnormal Pap/HPV results to identify lesions and guide biopsy.

Quick Answer

Colposcopy: Magnified cervical/vaginal exam after abnormal Pap/HPV results to identify lesions and guide biopsy. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Magnified cervical/vaginal exam after abnormal Pap/HPV results to identify lesions and guide biopsy.

    • Before / preparation

    • Verify consent when required, allergies, NPO/sedation needs, anticoagulant/bleeding risks, baseline vitals, and what monitoring is needed after the procedure.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Teach expected mild spotting/discharge if biopsy is done and report heavy bleeding, fever, foul drainage, or severe pelvic pain.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Bleeding, infection, severe pain, respiratory distress, neuro change, perforation signs, abnormal drainage, fever, or unstable vital signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Colposcopy follows abnormal screening; it is not the same thing as a Pap smear.

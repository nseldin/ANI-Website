Class

Thyroid hormone replacement

Used To Treat

Hypothyroidism, thyroid hormone replacement after thyroidectomy, and selected TSH-suppression protocols.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warning: not for obesity/weight loss; excessive doses can cause serious or life-threatening toxicity.

Mechanism

Renthyroid works by replacing thyroid hormone. T4 products provide thyroxine that is converted to active T3, while liothyronine provides T3 directly; metabolic effects build through nuclear thyroid-hormone receptor signaling, so over-replacement causes tachycardia, heat intolerance, weight loss, anxiety, and bone/cardiac strain.

Nursing Essentials

  • Give consistently, often morning empty stomach.

  • Monitor TSH/free T4, HR, chest pain, hyperthyroid symptoms.

  • Older/cardiac clients need careful titration.

Contraindications

  • Untreated adrenal insufficiency

  • thyrotoxicosis unless specifically managed

Interactions

  • Calcium/iron/antacids reduce absorption.

  • Warfarin effect may increase.

  • Sympathomimetics increase cardiac risk.

Key Labs

  • TSH

  • free T4

  • heart rate

Nclex Traps

  • Palpitations/chest pain after dose increase may mean overtreatment.

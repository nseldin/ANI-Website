Used To Treat

IBS-C in adults and age-approved pediatric patients, adult chronic idiopathic constipation, and age-approved pediatric functional constipation according to the current label.

Mechanism

Linaclotide and its active metabolite bind GC-C, raising intracellular cGMP and activating protein kinase G II. PKG II phosphorylates CFTR, increasing chloride and bicarbonate secretion; sodium and water follow into the lumen. Extracellular cGMP reduces firing of colonic afferent pain fibers, helping IBS-C abdominal pain. Peptide digestion and negligible absorption keep action local.

Contraindications

  • Age younger than 2 years

  • Known or suspected mechanical GI obstruction

  • Clarify severe active diarrhea, dehydration and unexplained acute abdominal symptoms

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm the current age-approved indication and strength; pediatric approvals and doses differ from adult IBS-C/CIC dosing.

  • Give on an empty stomach at least 30 minutes before a meal as labeled; swallow whole or use the approved bead-administration method without crushing/chewing.

Interactions

  • Other laxatives add diarrhea/dehydration.

  • Minimal systemic absorption makes CYP interactions unlikely.

  • Severe diarrhea can reduce absorption of concurrent oral drugs, especially those with narrow therapeutic windows.

Key Labs

  • Stool frequency/consistency, abdominal pain and hydration

  • Blood pressure, sodium, potassium, bicarbonate and renal function if diarrhea is severe

  • Age, weight and obstruction assessment

Nclex Traps

  • The boxed warning is for children younger than 2; current labeling includes older pediatric constipation indications.

  • GC-C activation raises cGMP; this is not a stimulant-laxative nerve effect.

  • Take before food, unlike lubiprostone, which is taken with food and water.

Boxed Warning

BOXED WARNING: Risk of serious dehydration in pediatric patients younger than 2 years. Linaclotide is contraindicated below age 2 because neonatal-animal GC-C hyper-responsiveness caused rapid fluid secretion, severe dehydration and death. It is also contraindicated in known or suspected mechanical GI obstruction. Stop and rehydrate/evaluate severe diarrhea.

Description

Linaclotide is a minimally absorbed 14-amino-acid guanylate-cyclase-C receptor agonist peptide that raises epithelial cGMP to drive chloride, bicarbonate and water secretion into the intestinal lumen. The resulting fluid accelerates transit, while extracellular cGMP dampens visceral pain signaling; the drug carries a pediatric dehydration boxed warning.

Adverse Effects

  • Diarrhea, abdominal pain, flatulence, distention and headache

  • Severe diarrhea with dizziness, syncope, hypotension, electrolyte abnormality or dehydration

  • Fecal urgency or incontinence

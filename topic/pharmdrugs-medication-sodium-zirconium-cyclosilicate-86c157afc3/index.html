Mechanism

Sodium zirconium cyclosilicate selectively captures potassium in the GI tract in exchange for sodium and hydrogen, increasing fecal potassium removal. Monitor potassium response, edema/sodium load risk, medication timing, and emergency hyperkalemia needs separately.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Sodium zirconium cyclosilicate in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Sodium zirconium cyclosilicate is a nonabsorbed potassium binder and inorganic cation exchanger: it selectively captures potassium in the GI tract in exchange for sodium and hydrogen, increasing fecal potassium removal. It is used for hyperkalemia management when GI potassium binding is appropriate.

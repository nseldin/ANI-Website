Class

Peripherally acting mu-opioid receptor antagonist (PAMORA)

Used To Treat

Opioid-induced constipation in adults with chronic non-cancer pain, including stable pain related to prior cancer or its treatment.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for naldemedine in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Mechanism

Naldemedine antagonizes peripheral mu, delta, and kappa opioid receptors, with the clinical constipation effect driven mainly by enteric mu blockade. Its side chain increases molecular size and polarity, reducing blood-brain barrier penetration.

Nursing Essentials

  • Exclude obstruction/recurrent obstruction and evaluate diverticulitis, GI malignancy, surgery, radiation, Crohn disease, or other perforation risk.

  • Monitor abdominal pain, diarrhea, nausea, severe/persistent pain suggesting perforation, sweating/chills/rhinorrhea or reduced analgesia suggesting withdrawal, and CYP3A/P-gp interactions.

Key Labs

  • Hepatic context, hydration/electrolytes if diarrhea is significant, pregnancy status, and bowel/withdrawal assessment.

Nclex Traps

  • Stop naldemedine when chronic opioid therapy stops; without the opioid cause, continued peripheral antagonism adds harm without purpose.

Description

Naldemedine is a naltrexone-derived peripherally acting mu-opioid receptor antagonist (PAMORA) that blocks enteric opioid receptors, restoring GI secretion and motility while its bulky polar side chain limits CNS entry and usually preserves analgesia. Bowel obstruction, weakened bowel wall, opioid withdrawal, CYP3A/P-gp interactions, and fetal withdrawal are key risks.

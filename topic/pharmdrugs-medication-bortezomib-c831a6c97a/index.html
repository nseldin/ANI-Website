Class

Reversible 26S proteasome beta-5 inhibitor

Used To Treat

Multiple myeloma and mantle-cell lymphoma in regimen-specific induction, consolidation, or relapsed-disease settings.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: no boxed warning is listed. Monitor sensory and motor neuropathy before every dose and adjust early; assess orthostasis, ileus/constipation, cytopenias, infection, cardiac/pulmonary symptoms, and PRES. Intrathecal administration is fatal and absolutely prohibited.

Mechanism

Reversible inhibition of the 20S core prevents degradation of ubiquitinated proteins and disrupts survival signaling, cell-cycle regulation, and protein-quality control. Immunoglobulin-producing myeloma cells have unusually high endoplasmic-reticulum stress, so further protein accumulation activates the unfolded-protein response and apoptosis.

Nursing Essentials

  • Subcutaneous administration generally reduces neuropathy compared with IV; rotate sites and never administer intrathecally.

  • Use antiviral prophylaxis when ordered, trend bowel function and orthostasis, and evaluate new burning/numbness, weakness, syncope, dyspnea, confusion, seizure, or visual change.

Key Labs

  • CBC with platelets each cycle, CMP/liver function, orthostatic vitals, tumor-lysis labs when indicated, and structured neuropathy assessment.

Nclex Traps

  • Bortezomib neuropathy can include autonomic dysfunction, so constipation and orthostatic hypotension may share the same toxic mechanism.

  • Platelets often fall cyclically and recover during the rest period; trend the regimen pattern rather than one isolated count.

Description

Bortezomib is a reversible proteasome inhibitor that blocks the beta-5 chymotrypsin-like activity of the 26S proteasome, making ubiquitinated and misfolded proteins accumulate until plasma cells undergo lethal stress and apoptosis. Peripheral and autonomic neuropathy, orthostatic hypotension, cyclic thrombocytopenia, herpes-zoster reactivation, GI toxicity, tumor lysis, PRES, and pulmonary/cardiac toxicity define nursing surveillance.

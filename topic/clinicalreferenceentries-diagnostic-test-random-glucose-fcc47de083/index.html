Summary

Random glucose is a high-yield Endocrine diagnostic reference. Measures glucose at any time, useful when symptoms suggest hyperglycemia or hypoglycemia.

Quick Answer

Random glucose: Measures glucose at any time, useful when symptoms suggest hyperglycemia or hypoglycemia. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Low

    Meaning

    Hypoglycemia is immediately clinically important if symptomatic or severe.

  • Label

    Normal

    Meaning

    Random glucose is not elevated at the time checked.

  • Label

    High

    Meaning

    Marked random hyperglycemia with classic symptoms can support diabetes diagnosis and may signal DKA/HHS risk when very high or symptomatic.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Measures glucose at any time, useful when symptoms suggest hyperglycemia or hypoglycemia.

    • Before / preparation

    • Check timing, fasting status, current endocrine medications, steroid use, pregnancy status when relevant, and whether dynamic testing requires serial specimens.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Connect the result to symptoms, glucose safety, thyroid/adrenal crisis risk, and medication teaching.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Severe hypoglycemia/hyperglycemia, thyroid storm/myxedema cues, adrenal crisis, altered mental status, dehydration, or unstable vital signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Endocrine tests often require correct timing. Wrong timing can make the result misleading.

Summary

PD-L1 testing (programmed death-ligand 1) is a high-yield Oncology diagnostic reference. Tumor immune-marker testing that can guide checkpoint inhibitor therapy decisions.

Quick Answer

PD-L1 testing (programmed death-ligand 1): Tumor immune-marker testing that can guide checkpoint inhibitor therapy decisions. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Low / negative expression

    Meaning

    PD-L1 expression is low or absent by the assay, which may reduce likelihood of benefit from selected checkpoint inhibitor strategies depending tumor type.

  • Label

    Positive / high expression

    Meaning

    PD-L1 expression is present at or above a cutoff and may support immunotherapy eligibility depending tumor type, assay, and treatment line.

  • Label

    Clinical significance

    Meaning

    PD-L1 is predictive context, not a cancer diagnosis; scoring systems differ by tumor and assay.

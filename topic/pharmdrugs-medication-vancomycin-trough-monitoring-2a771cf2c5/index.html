Used To Treat

Susceptible infections based on organism, infection site, cultures, allergies, renal function, and local resistance patterns.

Mechanism

Vancomycin trough monitoring works by measuring vancomycin exposure so serious gram-positive therapy stays effective without excess kidney injury. The level is interpreted with dose timing, renal function, infection severity, AUC/trough protocol, nephrotoxins, and clinical response.

Contraindications

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

Interactions

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

Description

Vancomycin trough monitoring is a glycopeptide/lipoglycopeptide antibiotic: it works by measuring vancomycin exposure so serious gram-positive therapy stays effective without excess kidney injury. It is used for vancomycin exposure monitoring to keep serious gram-positive therapy effective while reducing nephrotoxicity risk.

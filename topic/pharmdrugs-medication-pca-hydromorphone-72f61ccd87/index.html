Used To Treat

Moderate to severe pain, anesthesia/analgesia support, palliative dyspnea, cough for selected products, or opioid use disorder for selected agents.

Mechanism

Stimulates opioid receptors, altering pain perception and CNS response. The priority mechanism-to-risk link is respiratory depression.

Boxed Warning

Opioids carry boxed warnings for addiction, abuse, misuse, life-threatening respiratory depression, accidental ingestion, neonatal opioid withdrawal, and dangerous CNS-depressant combinations.

Contraindications

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • Respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, sedation scale, blood pressure, bowel function, pain response, and naloxone availability.

Nclex Traps

  • The priority assessment is respiratory status before another opioid dose.

Description

PCA hydromorphone is an opioid analgesic or opioid-use-disorder medication: it stimulates opioid receptors, altering pain perception and CNS response. It is used for moderate to severe pain, anesthesia/analgesia support, palliative dyspnea, cough for selected products, or opioid use disorder for selected agents.

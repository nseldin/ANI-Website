Definition

Heberden nodes is an observed and palpated joint sign. It is a clue to distal interphalangeal osteoarthritis. A positive finding means hard bony enlargements affect one or more DIP joints.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Heberden nodes means hard bony enlargements affect one or more DIP joints.

Why It Matters

The observed Heberden nodes finding can signal distal interphalangeal osteoarthritis. Osteophyte formation and joint remodeling enlarge the distal joint margins. Heberden nodes cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • distal interphalangeal osteoarthritis

Assessment Method

Inspect and palpate DIP joints.

Positive Finding

Hard bony enlargements affect one or more DIP joints.

Mechanism

Osteophyte formation and joint remodeling enlarge the distal joint margins.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Heberden nodes finding raises concern for distal interphalangeal osteoarthritis because osteophyte formation and joint remodeling enlarge the distal joint margins. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Heberden nodes does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Heberden nodes to focus evaluation of distal interphalangeal osteoarthritis and the reviewed alternatives: gouty tophus, psoriatic DIP disease, trauma. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect and palpate DIP joints. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Heberden nodes as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Inflammation can coexist and nodal appearance does not measure pain. Therefore, Heberden nodes cannot confirm or exclude distal interphalangeal osteoarthritis by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • gouty tophus

  • psoriatic DIP disease

  • trauma

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as gouty tophus, psoriatic DIP disease, trauma.

False Negative Situations

Heberden nodes may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of distal interphalangeal osteoarthritis.

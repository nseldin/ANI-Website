Boxed Warning

Class boxed warning: increased mortality in elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis.

Mechanism

Brexpiprazole works by modulating dopamine pathways, often through D2 blockade, with many agents also affecting serotonin, histamine, alpha, or muscarinic receptors. That is why EPS, NMS, QT, metabolic, and sedation monitoring differ by drug.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor EPS, tardive dyskinesia, neuroleptic malignant syndrome, metabolic effects, orthostasis, QT prolongation.

  • NMS: fever, rigidity, altered mental status, autonomic instability.

Description

Brexpiprazole is a second-generation atypical antipsychotic and dopamine-serotonin partial agonist: it works by modulating dopamine pathways, often through D2 blockade, with many agents also affecting serotonin, histamine, alpha, or muscarinic receptors. It is used for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, acute agitation/psychosis, and selected adjunct mood or nausea/tic indications depending on the specific medication.

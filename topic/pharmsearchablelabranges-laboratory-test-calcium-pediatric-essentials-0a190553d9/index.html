Name

Calcium

Range

Children generally about 8.8-10.8 mg/dL, facility dependent

Why

Low calcium can cause tetany, seizure risk, laryngospasm, and prolonged QT; high calcium can cause weakness, constipation, dehydration, and rhythm concerns.

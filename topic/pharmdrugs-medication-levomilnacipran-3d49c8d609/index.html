Used To Treat

Boxed Warning

Antidepressant boxed warning: increased risk of suicidal thoughts/behaviors in pediatric and young adult patients.

Mechanism

Levomilnacipran works by blocking serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake. That can improve mood/pain pathways but may raise blood pressure or worsen serotonin-toxicity risk.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor BP, mood/suicidality, serotonin syndrome, withdrawal if abruptly stopped.

Contraindications

Interactions

Description

Levomilnacipran is a serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI) antidepressant: it works by blocking serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake. It is used for depression, anxiety disorders, neuropathic pain, fibromyalgia, chronic musculoskeletal pain, or vasomotor symptoms depending on the medication.

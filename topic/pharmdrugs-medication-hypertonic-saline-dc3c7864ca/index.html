Mechanism

Hypertonic saline works by entering the brain and being converted to dopamine, restoring dopamine signaling in Parkinson motor circuits. Carbidopa limits peripheral conversion, reducing nausea and allowing more levodopa to reach the CNS. Wearing off, dyskinesias, hallucinations, orthostasis, and protein timing matter.

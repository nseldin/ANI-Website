Definition

Clonorchis sinensis is a clinically important trematode (fluke) associated with clonorchiasis, cholangitis or biliary obstruction, and cholangiocarcinoma risk. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

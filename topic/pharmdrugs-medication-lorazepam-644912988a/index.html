Used To Treat

Anxiety, acute agitation, alcohol withdrawal, procedural sedation, seizure rescue/status epilepticus, muscle spasm, or insomnia depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Lorazepam works by enhancing GABA-A activity, increasing inhibitory CNS tone. That is why sedation, falls, respiratory depression with other depressants, and withdrawal seizures matter.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warnings include abuse/misuse/addiction, dependence/withdrawal, and profound sedation/respiratory depression with opioids.

Contraindications

  • Severe respiratory insufficiency or sleep apnea without close monitoring.

  • Acute narrow-angle glaucoma.

  • Concomitant opioids/CNS depressants require strong caution.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor sedation, respiratory status, fall risk.

  • Use for acute anxiety, seizures, alcohol withdrawal per orders.

  • Do not stop chronic therapy abruptly.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Opioids, alcohol, sedatives increase respiratory depression.

  • Other CNS depressants increase falls/sedation.

  • Opioids, alcohol, sleep medications, antipsychotics, antihistamines, and other CNS depressants.

Key Labs

  • No routine lab; assess RR, sedation, CIWA if withdrawal context

  • Respiratory rate, oxygenation, sedation level, blood pressure, fall risk, alcohol/opioid use, and baseline mental status.

Nclex Traps

  • Benzodiazepine plus opioid is a respiratory safety red flag.

  • Older adults can develop confusion, falls, paradoxical agitation, and respiratory compromise. Flumazenil may trigger seizures in dependent clients.

Description

Lorazepam is a benzodiazepine: it works by enhancing GABA-A activity, increasing inhibitory CNS tone. It is used for anxiety, acute agitation, alcohol withdrawal, seizure rescue/status epilepticus support, procedural sedation, muscle spasm, or insomnia depending on the specific medication and order.

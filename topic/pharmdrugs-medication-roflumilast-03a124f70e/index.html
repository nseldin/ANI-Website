Contraindications

  • Moderate or severe liver impairment, Child-Pugh B or C

  • Avoid or use only after careful benefit-risk review in active depression, suicidality, marked underweight, or inability to monitor toxicity

Nursing Essentials

  • Record weight and BMI before treatment and trend weight, stool frequency, hydration, appetite, sleep, mood, anxiety, depression, and suicidal thinking.

  • Confirm severe chronic-bronchitis COPD with recurrent exacerbations and optimized inhaled therapy; teach that the tablet prevents future events rather than opening the airway now.

Interactions

  • Rifampin, phenobarbital, carbamazepine, phenytoin, and other strong CYP inducers reduce exposure and are not recommended.

  • CYP3A4 inhibitors or dual CYP3A4/1A2 inhibitors such as erythromycin, ketoconazole, fluvoxamine, or enoxacin can increase total exposure and adverse effects.

Key Labs

  • Weight/BMI, appetite, hydration, mood, sleep, exacerbation frequency, spirometry, and inhaler adherence

  • Liver history and tests when clinically indicated; routine roflumilast levels are not useful

Nclex Traps

  • Roflumilast is a COPD anti-inflammatory add-on, not a bronchodilator.

  • The 250-microgram tablet is a tolerability starter in labeled regimens, not the therapeutic maintenance dose.

Class

Oral selective phosphodiesterase-4 (PDE4) inhibitor for COPD exacerbation-risk reduction, not a bronchodilator

Used To Treat

Reduction of COPD exacerbation risk in patients with severe COPD associated with chronic bronchitis and a history of exacerbations. It is not a direct bronchodilator and is not indicated to relieve acute bronchospasm.

Mechanism

Roflumilast and its active N-oxide selectively inhibit PDE4, the major cAMP-hydrolyzing phosphodiesterase in neutrophils, eosinophils, macrophages, lymphocytes, airway epithelium, and other inflammatory cells. Less cAMP breakdown sustains protein kinase A and EPAC signaling, reducing inflammatory mediator release, oxidative activity, chemotaxis, and cellular trafficking. The exact pathway responsible for clinical COPD benefit is not completely defined. Unlike beta2 agonists, roflumilast does not directly stimulate airway smooth-muscle cAMP through a surface receptor and therefore does not produce rapid bronchodilation. CYP3A4 and CYP1A2 generate the active N-oxide; combined parent-plus-metabolite exposure explains why strong inducers reduce efficacy and inhibitors can increase adverse effects.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Psychiatric adverse reactions include insomnia, anxiety, depression, and suicidal ideation or behavior, including completed suicide; weigh benefit carefully in patients with depression or prior suicidal behavior and reassess any new mood or sleep change. Clinically significant weight loss can occur, so record baseline weight and monitor regularly. Moderate or severe hepatic impairment is contraindicated, and roflumilast must not delay acute COPD treatment.

Description

Roflumilast is an oral, once-daily PDE4 inhibitor used as a phenotype-specific COPD add-on, not as an inhaler and not as rescue therapy. PDE4 breaks down cyclic AMP inside inflammatory cells. Inhibiting it raises cAMP and dampens inflammatory-cell activation, which can reduce future exacerbations in severe chronic-bronchitis COPD. Because the effect is anti-inflammatory rather than direct smooth-muscle relaxation, the patient should not expect immediate relief after a tablet. Diarrhea, nausea, appetite loss, and weight loss are common enough to threaten adherence, while insomnia, anxiety, depression, and suicidal thoughts or behavior make baseline and follow-up psychiatric assessment essential. Moderate or severe hepatic impairment produces excessive exposure and is contraindicated.

Adverse Effects

  • Diarrhea, weight loss, nausea, headache, back pain, influenza, insomnia, dizziness, decreased appetite, or abdominal pain

  • Anxiety, depression, suicidal thoughts or behavior, severe GI intolerance, hypersensitivity, or clinically important underweight status

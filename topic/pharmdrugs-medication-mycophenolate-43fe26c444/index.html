Boxed Warning

Boxed warnings include embryo-fetal toxicity, malignancy, and serious infections.

Mechanism

Mycophenolate works by inhibiting inosine monophosphate dehydrogenase, blocking de novo guanine synthesis that activated T and B lymphocytes depend on. That protects transplants and treats immune disease but explains infection risk, cytopenias, GI toxicity, teratogenicity, and interaction-heavy immunosuppression plans.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify pregnancy status and contraception counseling.

  • Monitor infection, CBC, renal/liver function, and GI toxicity.

  • Do not stop transplant immunosuppression without prescriber direction.

Interactions

  • Live vaccines may be unsafe or less effective.

  • Antacids/cholestyramine can reduce exposure.

  • Other immunosuppressants increase infection risk.

Description

Mycophenolate is an antimetabolite immunosuppressant and IMPDH inhibitor: it works by inhibiting inosine monophosphate dehydrogenase, blocking de novo guanine synthesis that activated T and B lymphocytes depend on. It is used for cancer treatment or immune/autoimmune protocols depending on the agent, regimen, tumor biology, and dose schedule.

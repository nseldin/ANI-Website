Used To Treat

Symptomatic neurogenic orthostatic hypotension in autonomic failure disorders.

Mechanism

Droxidopa is converted to norepinephrine, increasing vascular tone and standing blood pressure. The nursing balance is fewer orthostatic symptoms versus supine hypertension, headache, falls, dosing timing, and cardiovascular risk.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for droxidopa in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Droxidopa is a norepinephrine precursor for neurogenic orthostatic hypotension: it is converted to norepinephrine, increasing vascular tone and standing blood pressure. It is used for symptomatic neurogenic orthostatic hypotension in autonomic failure disorders.

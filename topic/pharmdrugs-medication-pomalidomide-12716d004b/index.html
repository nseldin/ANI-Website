Class

Cereblon-binding immunomodulatory imide drug (IMiD)

Used To Treat

With dexamethasone for multiple myeloma progressing after at least two prior therapies including lenalidomide and a proteasome inhibitor, and selected AIDS-related or HIV-negative Kaposi sarcoma.

Boxed Warning

Embryo-fetal toxicity and venous/arterial thromboembolism: pregnancy is contraindicated, two-method contraception/pregnancy testing and PS-Pomalidomide REMS controls are mandatory, and drug in semen requires male precautions. DVT, PE, MI, and stroke occur; use risk-based antithrombotic prophylaxis.

Mechanism

Pomalidomide-bound cereblon retargets the CRL4 E3 ubiquitin ligase to ubiquitinate and degrade Ikaros and Aiolos. Altered transcription promotes malignant-cell apoptosis, increases T/NK immune activity, reduces TNF-alpha/IL-6 signaling, and contributes antiangiogenic effects; dexamethasone adds synergistic myeloma-cell death.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify REMS, reproductive precautions, blood/sperm donation restrictions, cycle day, dexamethasone partner, and renal/hepatic dose context.

  • Monitor CBC, fever/infection, bleeding, thrombosis, rash/DRESS/SJS-TEN, liver injury, neuropathy, confusion/dizziness, tumor lysis, and strong CYP1A2 inhibitors; smoking can lower exposure.

Key Labs

  • Pregnancy testing per REMS, CBC with differential/platelets, liver tests, renal function and dialysis timing, and tumor-lysis labs when burden is high.

Nclex Traps

  • Pomalidomide can work after lenalidomide resistance, but it does not lose the class's fetal and thrombosis hazards.

  • Tobacco smoke induces CYP1A2 and can reduce pomalidomide exposure.

Description

Pomalidomide is a later-generation thalidomide-derived IMiD that binds cereblon, drives Ikaros/Aiolos degradation, directly suppresses myeloma cells, and enhances T-cell/NK-cell immunity even in some lenalidomide-resistant disease. Severe teratogenicity and venous/arterial thrombosis are boxed risks; neutropenia, liver injury, severe cutaneous reactions, neuropathy, dizziness/confusion, TLS, and CYP1A2/smoking interactions add practical complexity.

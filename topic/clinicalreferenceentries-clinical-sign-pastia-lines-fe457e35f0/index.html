Definition

Pastia lines is an observed flexural skin sign. It is a clue to scarlet fever. A positive finding means linear petechiae or deeper erythema accentuate skin creases.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Pastia lines means linear petechiae or deeper erythema accentuate skin creases.

Why It Matters

The observed Pastia lines finding can signal scarlet fever. Streptococcal toxin-mediated capillary injury is intensified by friction in folds. Pastia lines cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • scarlet fever

Assessment Method

Inspect antecubital, axillary, and groin folds.

Positive Finding

Linear petechiae or deeper erythema accentuate skin creases.

Mechanism

Streptococcal toxin-mediated capillary injury is intensified by friction in folds.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Pastia lines finding raises concern for scarlet fever because streptococcal toxin-mediated capillary injury is intensified by friction in folds. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Pastia lines does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Pastia lines to focus evaluation of scarlet fever and the reviewed alternatives: contact dermatitis, heat rash, meningococcemia. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect antecubital, axillary, and groin folds. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Pastia lines as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Flexural irritation and other petechial illnesses can mimic it. Therefore, Pastia lines cannot confirm or exclude scarlet fever by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • contact dermatitis

  • heat rash

  • meningococcemia

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as contact dermatitis, heat rash, meningococcemia.

False Negative Situations

Pastia lines may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of scarlet fever.

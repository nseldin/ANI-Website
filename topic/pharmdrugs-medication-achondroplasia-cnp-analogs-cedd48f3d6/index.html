Class Example Names

  • Vosoritide

Used To Treat

Increase linear growth in children with achondroplasia and open epiphyses; age threshold, interval, preparation, renal restrictions, and accelerated-approval details are product specific.

Description

Achondroplasia CNP analogs counter excessive FGFR3 growth-inhibitory signaling at the growth plate. Daily vosoritide is an active CNP analog; once-weekly navepegritide is a long-acting prodrug that gradually releases active CNP. Neither changes the germline FGFR3 variant, and neither can lengthen bones after epiphyseal closure.

Mechanism

Gain-of-function FGFR3 overactivates RAS-RAF-MEK-ERK/MAPK signaling and suppresses chondrocyte proliferation and differentiation during endochondral ossification. CNP activates natriuretic peptide receptor B, raising cGMP and protein-kinase-G signaling, which inhibits the MAPK pathway near RAF-1 and releases part of that growth brake.

Boxed Warning

Neither product currently has a boxed warning. Transient hypotension is the key class safety signal: assess hydration, nutrition, dizziness, fatigue, nausea, syncope risk, and renal eligibility. Both are not recommended below an eGFR of 60 mL/min/1.73 m2 under current labeling.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify open growth plates, age, body weight, renal function, product, interval, reconstitution steps, injection technique, growth trajectory, blood-pressure symptoms, and readiness for daily versus weekly adherence.

  • Continue multidisciplinary achondroplasia care for foramen magnum, airway, hearing, neurologic, orthopedic, and psychosocial complications; greater growth velocity does not replace surveillance.

Key Labs

  • Serial height/length and annualized growth velocity, weight-based dose, epiphyseal status, renal function, blood pressure and orthostatic symptoms; no serum CNP target is used for routine titration.

Nclex Traps

  • Vosoritide is daily active analog; navepegritide is a weekly prodrug. They are not dose-for-dose substitutes.

  • Open epiphyses are essential because the mechanism acts at functioning growth plates.

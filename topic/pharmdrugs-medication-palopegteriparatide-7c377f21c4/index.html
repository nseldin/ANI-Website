Class

Sustained-release PTH(1-34) prodrug parathyroid-hormone replacement

Used To Treat

Chronic hypoparathyroidism in adults; it was not studied for acute postsurgical hypoparathyroidism and is initiated only after calcium is stabilized with calcium and active vitamin D.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current YORVIPATH labeling has no boxed warning. Severe hypercalcemia and hypocalcemia can occur during initiation, titration, interruption or supplement changes; symptoms may be cardiac or neurologic. Avoid use in patients at increased osteosarcoma risk. Orthostatic hypotension can occur. Hypercalcemia increases digoxin-toxicity risk, so calcium and digoxin require close monitoring when combined.

Mechanism

PTH(1-34) is transiently linked to an inert PEG carrier by a cleavable TransCon linker. Physiologic conditions slowly release active PTH, which stimulates PTH1 receptors on renal tubular cells, osteoblast-lineage cells and other tissues. Signaling raises distal renal calcium reabsorption, lowers proximal phosphate reabsorption, stimulates renal 1-alpha-hydroxylase and allows regulated bone turnover, thereby increasing serum calcium and decreasing phosphate.

Nursing Essentials

  • Correct vitamin-D status and magnesium, stabilize albumin-corrected calcium at the label threshold, document calcium/calcitriol doses, renal function, stone history, skeletal radiation/malignancy risk and digoxin before initiation.

  • Use one injection for the total daily dose, adjust gradually with frequent calcium checks, and give the written rescue plan for missed doses or interruption because abrupt PTH withdrawal can produce dangerous hypocalcemia.

Key Labs

  • Albumin-corrected or ionized calcium, phosphate, magnesium, 25-OH vitamin D, creatinine/eGFR, urine calcium, calcium-phosphate burden and digoxin concentration/ECG when applicable.

Nclex Traps

  • Palopegteriparatide releases the same PTH(1-34) sequence as teriparatide but uses sustained replacement physiology for hypoparathyroidism, not intermittent anabolic osteoporosis dosing.

  • Two injections should not be combined to construct one daily dose; the label warns that this can destabilize calcium.

Description

Palopegteriparatide is a once-daily subcutaneous prodrug that continuously releases bioactive PTH(1-34) for adult hypoparathyroidism. Unlike intermittent osteoporosis teriparatide pulses, its purpose is physiologic parathyroid-hormone replacement across the day, restoring renal calcium conservation, phosphate excretion and active-vitamin-D production while reducing reliance on high oral calcium and calcitriol.

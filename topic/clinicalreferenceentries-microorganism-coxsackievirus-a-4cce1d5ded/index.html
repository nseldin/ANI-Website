Definition

Coxsackievirus A is a clinically important virus associated with hand-foot-and-mouth disease, herpangina, aseptic meningitis, and acute hemorrhagic conjunctivitis. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

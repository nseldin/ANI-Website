Definition

Cotton-wool spot is an observed retinal sign. It is a clue to focal retinal nerve-fiber-layer ischemia. A positive finding means fluffy white superficial lesions obscure underlying vessels.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Cotton-wool spot means fluffy white superficial lesions obscure underlying vessels.

Why It Matters

The observed Cotton-wool spot finding can signal focal retinal nerve-fiber-layer ischemia. Interrupted axoplasmic flow causes swollen nerve-fiber axons after precapillary ischemia. Cotton-wool spot cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • focal retinal nerve-fiber-layer ischemia

Assessment Method

Inspect retina or retinal photographs.

Positive Finding

Fluffy white superficial lesions obscure underlying vessels.

Mechanism

Interrupted axoplasmic flow causes swollen nerve-fiber axons after precapillary ischemia.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Cotton-wool spot finding raises concern for focal retinal nerve-fiber-layer ischemia because interrupted axoplasmic flow causes swollen nerve-fiber axons after precapillary ischemia. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Cotton-wool spot does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Cotton-wool spot to focus evaluation of focal retinal nerve-fiber-layer ischemia and the reviewed alternatives: hard exudate, myelinated nerve fiber, retinitis. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect retina or retinal photographs. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Cotton-wool spot as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Small lesions may be transient and do not identify one systemic cause. Therefore, Cotton-wool spot cannot confirm or exclude focal retinal nerve-fiber-layer ischemia by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as hard exudate, myelinated nerve fiber, retinitis.

False Negative Situations

Cotton-wool spot may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of focal retinal nerve-fiber-layer ischemia.

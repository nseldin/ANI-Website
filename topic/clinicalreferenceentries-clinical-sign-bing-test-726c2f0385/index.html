Definition

Bing test is a bedside tuning-fork occlusion test. It is a clue to conductive hearing loss. A positive finding means loudness increases with occlusion normally but not with conductive loss.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Bing test means loudness increases with occlusion normally but not with conductive loss.

Why It Matters

A positive Bing test result can signal conductive hearing loss. Canal occlusion traps low-frequency bone-conducted energy when the conductive pathway is otherwise normal. Bing test cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • conductive hearing loss

Assessment Method

Place a vibrating fork on the mastoid while alternately occluding and opening the ear canal.

Positive Finding

Loudness increases with occlusion normally but not with conductive loss.

Mechanism

Canal occlusion traps low-frequency bone-conducted energy when the conductive pathway is otherwise normal.

Clinical Interpretation

A positive Bing test result raises concern for conductive hearing loss because canal occlusion traps low-frequency bone-conducted energy when the conductive pathway is otherwise normal. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Bing test does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Bing test to focus evaluation of conductive hearing loss and the reviewed alternatives: mixed hearing loss, cerumen, poor cooperation. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Place a vibrating fork on the mastoid while alternately occluding and opening the ear canal. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Bing test as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Subjective loudness and incomplete occlusion reduce reliability. Therefore, Bing test cannot confirm or exclude conductive hearing loss by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • mixed hearing loss

  • cerumen

  • poor cooperation

False Positive Situations

A false-positive test can result from method deviation, subjective interpretation, or an alternative cause such as mixed hearing loss, cerumen, poor cooperation.

False Negative Situations

Bing test may be missed when technique, timing, cooperation, or disease stage prevents the defined response.

Modern Status

Historical adjunct. Bing test remains searchable because learners still encounter the term, but it cannot confirm or exclude conductive hearing loss. Apply the reviewed limitation for Bing test: Subjective loudness and incomplete occlusion reduce reliability.

Safety Precautions

Before assessing Bing test, explain the method, obtain consent, preserve privacy, and use only the technique described here: Place a vibrating fork on the mastoid while alternately occluding and opening the ear canal. Stop if that technique becomes unsafe. The reviewed precaution for this maneuver is: Do not confuse with the neurologic Bing plantar sign.

Nursing Relevance

Record the exact Bing test observation, side or distribution, timing, technique, patient response, and change from baseline; the reviewed positive finding is: Loudness increases with occlusion normally but not with conductive loss. Continue the focused evaluation by following the reviewed method and its limits: Place a vibrating fork on the mastoid while alternately occluding and opening the ear canal. Communicate the finding with its suspected association, conductive hearing loss, when it is new, worsening, or clinically important.

Documentation Guidance

Document the exact finding, assessment method, side or distribution, timing, patient response, associated symptoms and vital signs, and change from baseline. Record the observed result instead of writing only "positive" or "negative."

Base escalation for Bing test on the patient's current condition, associated findings, and the cause under evaluation; this card does not assign a universal urgency level.

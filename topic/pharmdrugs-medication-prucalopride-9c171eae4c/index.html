Class

Selective serotonin 5-HT4 receptor agonist GI prokinetic

Used To Treat

Chronic idiopathic constipation in adults when increased colonic propulsive motility is needed.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for prucalopride in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Mechanism

5-HT4 receptor activation on enteric neurons facilitates acetylcholine release and coordinated peristalsis from proximal colon toward the rectum, increasing high-amplitude propagating contractions and bowel movement frequency. Selective 5-HT4 activation on enteric neurons increases acetylcholine release, which coordinates high-amplitude propagating contractions from proximal colon toward rectum and thereby improves transit. It does not draw water into stool, so labeling it an osmotic laxative would hide its true prokinetic action. Because stronger propulsion against a mechanical obstruction can worsen pain, distention, vomiting, or perforation risk, obstruction must be excluded. New depression or suicidal thinking requires prompt reassessment even though the mechanism linking that warning to 5-HT4 selectivity is not established.

Nursing Essentials

  • Exclude obstruction, perforation, ileus, toxic megacolon, and severe inflammatory bowel disease; adjust for severe renal impairment.

  • Monitor bowel response, diarrhea/dehydration, abdominal pain, headache, and new or worsening depression, self-harm, or suicidal thoughts; stop and urgently contact the prescriber for behavioral change.

Key Labs

  • Renal function for dose selection, hydration/electrolytes if diarrhea is substantial, and direct mood/suicide assessment.

Nclex Traps

  • A constipation drug can still carry a psychiatric warning: mood and suicidality changes on prucalopride require immediate action.

Description

Prucalopride is a highly selective 5-HT4 agonist that stimulates enteric acetylcholine release and high-amplitude propagating colonic contractions. It is a true prokinetic rather than an osmotic laxative; diarrhea and headache are common, while new depression, self-injury, or suicidal thinking is a distinctive serious warning.

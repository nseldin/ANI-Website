Definition

Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease prion is a clinically important protein-only infectious agent associated with Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease, rapidly progressive dementia, and myoclonus with cerebellar dysfunction. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

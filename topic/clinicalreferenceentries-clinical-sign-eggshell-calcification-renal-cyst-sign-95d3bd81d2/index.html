Definition

Eggshell calcification renal cyst sign is an imaging sign. It is a clue to calcified renal cyst or cystic neoplasm. A positive finding means a delicate curvilinear rim outlines the cyst.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Eggshell calcification renal cyst sign means a delicate curvilinear rim outlines the cyst.

Why It Matters

The Eggshell calcification renal cyst sign imaging pattern can signal calcified renal cyst or cystic neoplasm. Chronic cyst-wall mineralization produces a shell-like contour. Eggshell calcification renal cyst sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • calcified renal cyst or cystic neoplasm

Assessment Method

Review CT for thin peripheral calcification around a cystic renal lesion.

Positive Finding

A delicate curvilinear rim outlines the cyst.

Mechanism

Chronic cyst-wall mineralization produces a shell-like contour.

Clinical Interpretation

The Eggshell calcification renal cyst sign imaging pattern raises concern for calcified renal cyst or cystic neoplasm because chronic cyst-wall mineralization produces a shell-like contour. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Eggshell calcification renal cyst sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Evidence Limitations

Calcification alone does not establish benignity. Therefore, Eggshell calcification renal cyst sign cannot confirm or exclude calcified renal cyst or cystic neoplasm by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

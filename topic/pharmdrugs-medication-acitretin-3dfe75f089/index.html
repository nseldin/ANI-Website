Boxed Warning

Boxed warning: severe birth defects can occur. Pregnancy must be avoided during therapy and for a prolonged period after stopping.

Mechanism

Acitretin works by altering retinoic-acid receptor signaling, which changes epithelial differentiation, keratinization, sebaceous activity, and cell maturation. The same nuclear-receptor developmental signaling explains severe teratogenic risk and why pregnancy prevention, lipid/liver monitoring, skin/mucosal effects, and mood symptoms matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify pregnancy prevention and alcohol avoidance instructions.

  • Monitor liver enzymes, lipids, skin/mucous membrane dryness, and mood changes.

  • Teach that pregnancy precautions continue long after the last dose.

Interactions

  • Alcohol can convert acitretin to etretinate and prolong teratogenic risk.

  • Vitamin A products increase retinoid toxicity.

  • Methotrexate increases hepatotoxicity concern.

Nclex Traps

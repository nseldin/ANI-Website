Class

Selective pituitary corticotropin-releasing-factor type-1 receptor antagonist

Used To Treat

Adjunct to glucocorticoid replacement to control excess adrenal androgens in adults and children age 4 years and older with classic congenital adrenal hyperplasia.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current CRENESSITY labeling has no boxed warning. Inadequate concomitant glucocorticoid therapy can cause acute adrenal insufficiency or adrenal crisis; never reduce below physiologic replacement and increase stress dosing for illness, surgery or trauma as directed. Serious hypersensitivity may occur. Crinecerfont is a CYP3A4 substrate; strong and moderate inducers lower exposure and require label-specific morning/evening dose changes.

Mechanism

In classic 21-hydroxylase-deficient CAH, inadequate cortisol removes negative feedback from hypothalamic CRF and pituitary ACTH. Chronic ACTH stimulation enlarges the adrenal cortex and diverts steroid precursors into androgen synthesis. Crinecerfont blocks CRF binding at pituitary CRF1, lowers ACTH secretion and reduces downstream androstenedione and 17-hydroxyprogesterone. It does not activate glucocorticoid receptors or supply cortisol during illness.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify classic CAH diagnosis, current replacement and stress-dose plan, meal timing, age/weight dose, androgen control, CYP3A inducers and adrenal-crisis education before starting.

  • Assess androstenedione beginning about four weeks after initiation to guide supervised glucocorticoid reduction. Vomiting, dehydration, severe weakness, hypotension, hypoglycemia or altered mental status requires emergency glucocorticoid and crisis care, not extra crinecerfont.

Key Labs

  • Androstenedione, 17-hydroxyprogesterone, ACTH as directed, electrolytes, glucose, blood pressure, growth/puberty in children, glucocorticoid adverse effects and adrenal-crisis assessment.

Nclex Traps

  • Crinecerfont controls ACTH-driven androgen excess but does not replace cortisol.

  • A lower androgen level is not permission to stop glucocorticoid replacement or omit stress dosing.

Description

Crinecerfont is an oral selective CRF1 receptor antagonist that lowers the excessive ACTH drive responsible for adrenal androgen overproduction in classic congenital adrenal hyperplasia. It allows androgen control with less supraphysiologic glucocorticoid exposure, but it does not replace the cortisol activity patients need to survive and therefore must remain adjunctive to glucocorticoid replacement.

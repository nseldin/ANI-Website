Used To Treat

Target-specific neutralization of North American crotalid venom or severe/life-threatening digoxin toxicity; dosing and response endpoints are completely target specific.

Description

Fab antidotes are antigen-binding antibody fragments that remove a toxin from its biologically active pool. Crotalidae immune Fab binds multiple venom toxins and redistributes them away from tissues; digoxin immune Fab binds free cardiac glycoside and shifts it away from sodium-potassium ATPase. Shared antibody-fragment chemistry does not make dosing or monitoring interchangeable.

Mechanism

Fab binding lowers free toxin concentration, pulling toxin out of receptor or tissue compartments and allowing the toxin-Fab complex to remain inactive while it is cleared. Because Fab may clear faster than venom leaking from a bite depot, crotalid coagulopathy and swelling can recur. After digoxin Fab, standard total digoxin assays measure bound plus unbound drug and become misleading.

Boxed Warning

Neither currently has a boxed warning. CROFAB can cause immediate anaphylaxis, delayed serum sickness, and cross-reaction with papain/papaya, chymopapain, bromelain, latex, or dust-mite sensitivities; recurrent coagulopathy may last one to two weeks. Digoxin Fab can unmask heart failure, rapid atrial conduction, and hypokalemia as glycoside effect reverses.

Nursing Essentials

  • For snakebite, mark and trend swelling, pain, neurotoxicity, platelets, PT/INR and fibrinogen; dose to initial control and monitor recurrence after discharge. For digoxin, trend rhythm, perfusion and potassium rather than total digoxin level after Fab.

  • Have anaphylaxis treatment ready for either biologic and preserve target-specific poison-control/toxicology consultation.

Key Labs

  • CROFAB: serial platelets, PT/INR, fibrinogen, hemoglobin and local/systemic envenomation findings. Digoxin Fab: continuous ECG, potassium, magnesium, renal function and hemodynamics; post-Fab total digoxin is not interpretable as free toxicity.

Nclex Traps

  • CROFAB does not make recurrence impossible; Fab can disappear before depot venom is exhausted.

  • Do not chase a high total digoxin concentration after Fab when the assay includes inactive Fab-bound digoxin.

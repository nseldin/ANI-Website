Class

Medication class reference

Description

Beta-lactams are cell-wall antibiotics; subgroups differ by spectrum, beta-lactamase stability, and stewardship role.

Used To Treat

Susceptible bacterial infections according to site, organism, culture data, allergy history, and local resistance patterns.

Mechanism

They bind penicillin-binding proteins and block peptidoglycan cross-linking, weakening the bacterial cell wall during growth. Beta-lactamase inhibitors protect partner drugs from enzymatic destruction.

Nursing Essentials

  • Clarify allergy phenotype, culture/site fit, renal dosing, seizure risk for selected agents, sodium/electrolyte load, and C. difficile diarrhea.

Description

Monoclonal antibodies and biologics bind selected extracellular, immune, inflammatory, or cancer targets.

Used To Treat

Autoimmune/inflammatory disease, cancer, asthma/allergy, migraine prevention, osteoporosis, infection prevention, or transplant-related indications depending on the target.

Mechanism

They bind a specific protein/receptor/cell marker to block signaling, flag cells for immune destruction, deliver a payload, or modulate immune checkpoints/cytokines.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess infection screening, vaccines, infusion/injection reactions, target-specific toxicity, pregnancy/lactation questions, and delayed immune adverse events.

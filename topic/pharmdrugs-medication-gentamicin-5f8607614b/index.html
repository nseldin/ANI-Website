Used To Treat

Susceptible infections based on organism, infection site, cultures, allergies, renal function, and local resistance patterns.

Mechanism

Gentamicin works by irreversibly binding the 30S ribosomal subunit, causing mRNA misreading and defective bacterial proteins in an oxygen-dependent, concentration-dependent bactericidal process. Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, peak/trough or extended-interval monitoring, renal dosing, and poor anaerobe activity are the key ideas.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warnings commonly emphasize nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, and neuromuscular blockade/respiratory paralysis risk.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity to aminoglycosides.

  • Use extreme caution with myasthenia gravis, renal impairment, or pregnancy unless benefits outweigh risks.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor renal function, peak/trough or extended-interval levels per protocol.

  • Report hearing changes, vertigo, tinnitus, decreased urine output.

  • Hydration matters.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

  • Subclass specificity: Gentamicin is categorized here as Aminoglycoside 30S ribosomal inhibitor antibiotic. Pathway: Anti-infective medication > Protein synthesis inhibitor > Aminoglycoside 30S inhibitor. This subtype shapes the expected mechanism, contraindications, boxed/high-alert warnings, monitoring, interactions, and patient teaching.

Interactions

  • Loop diuretics, vancomycin, amphotericin B, cyclosporine, neuromuscular blockers increase toxicity risk.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • creatinine/eGFR

  • peak/trough

  • urine output

  • Allergies, cultures before first dose when ordered, renal function for renally cleared drugs, liver function for hepatotoxic drugs, CBC, diarrhea/C. difficile cues, and therapeutic levels for aminoglycosides/vancomycin when ordered.

Nclex Traps

  • Tinnitus or hearing change is not a minor side effect; it can signal ototoxicity.

  • Do not delay urgent antibiotics for sepsis, but obtain cultures first if ordered and it does not delay care.

Description

Gentamicin is an aminoglycoside 30S ribosomal inhibitor antibiotic: it works by irreversibly binding the 30S ribosomal subunit, causing mRNA misreading and defective bacterial proteins in an oxygen-dependent, concentration-dependent bactericidal process. It is used for antibiotic therapy only when the suspected or proven organism, infection site, severity, allergies, pregnancy/age context, renal/hepatic function, and local resistance pattern match the exact subclass.

Class

Beta-2 agonist bronchodilator

Used To Treat

Bronchospasm in asthma or COPD; short-acting agents are rescue therapy and long-acting agents support maintenance regimens.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; overuse/paradoxical bronchospasm and LABA safety in asthma without anti-inflammatory therapy are key concerns.

Mechanism

Olodaterol works by stimulating beta-2 receptors on bronchial smooth muscle for prolonged bronchodilation. LABAs are maintenance bronchodilators, not rescue monotherapy for acute bronchospasm, and tachycardia/tremor can still occur.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess work of breathing, lung sounds, SpO2, HR, tremor, potassium with high-dose therapy.

  • Rescue inhaler overuse signals poor control.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity

  • tachydysrhythmia risk may require clarification

Interactions

  • Beta blockers may blunt effect.

  • Other sympathomimetics increase tachycardia.

  • Diuretics/steroids can worsen hypokalemia.

Key Labs

  • heart rate

  • potassium with high-dose/nebulized therapy

  • SpO2/peak flow

Nclex Traps

  • Albuterol can lower potassium and increase HR; worsening breathing after inhaler can be paradoxical bronchospasm.

Description

Olodaterol is a long-acting beta-2 agonist bronchodilator: it works by stimulating beta-2 receptors on bronchial smooth muscle for prolonged bronchodilation. It is used for bronchospasm in asthma or COPD. Short-acting agents are rescue therapy and long-acting agents support maintenance regimens.

Definition

Sister Mary Joseph nodule is an observed and palpated skin sign. It is a clue to umbilical metastasis from intra-abdominal or pelvic malignancy. A positive finding means a firm irregular umbilical nodule, sometimes ulcerated, is present.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Sister Mary Joseph nodule means a firm irregular umbilical nodule, sometimes ulcerated, is present.

Why It Matters

The observed Sister Mary Joseph nodule finding can signal umbilical metastasis from intra-abdominal or pelvic malignancy. Tumor spreads through lymphatic, venous, peritoneal, or embryologic channels to the umbilicus. Sister Mary Joseph nodule cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • umbilical metastasis from intra-abdominal or pelvic malignancy

Assessment Method

Inspect and gently palpate the umbilicus.

A firm irregular umbilical nodule, sometimes ulcerated, is present.

Mechanism

Tumor spreads through lymphatic, venous, peritoneal, or embryologic channels to the umbilicus.

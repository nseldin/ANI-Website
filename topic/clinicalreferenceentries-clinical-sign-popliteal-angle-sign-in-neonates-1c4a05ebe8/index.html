Definition

Popliteal-angle sign in neonates is a neonatal tone maneuver. It is a clue to gestational maturity and lower-limb flexor tone. A positive finding means a smaller extension angle reflects greater flexor tone and maturity.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Popliteal-angle sign in neonates means a smaller extension angle reflects greater flexor tone and maturity.

Why It Matters

A positive Popliteal-angle sign in neonates response can signal gestational maturity and lower-limb flexor tone. Advancing gestation increases passive hamstring and lower-limb flexor tone, so the knee meets gentle resistance earlier and retains a smaller extension angle. Popliteal-angle sign in neonates cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

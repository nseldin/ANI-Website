Definition

Active vaginal bleeding finding on speculum examination is an observed pelvic finding. It is a clue to active uterine or cervical bleeding. A positive finding means fresh blood pools in the posterior fornix or emerges from the cervical os.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Active vaginal bleeding finding on speculum examination means fresh blood pools in the posterior fornix or emerges from the cervical os.

Why It Matters

The observed Active vaginal bleeding finding on speculum examination finding can signal active uterine or cervical bleeding. Gravity collects active genital-tract bleeding in the dependent vaginal fornix. Active vaginal bleeding finding on speculum examination cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Used To Treat

Uncomplicated UTI in females age 12 years and older weighing at least 40 kg; and susceptible uncomplicated urogenital gonorrhea in patients age 12 years and older weighing at least 45 kg who have limited or no alternatives.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current BLUJEPA labeling has no boxed warning. Gepotidacin prolongs QTc in a concentration-dependent fashion. Avoid it with long-QT history, relevant cardiac disease, QT-prolonging antiarrhythmics/drugs, strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, severe renal impairment or severe hepatic impairment; gonorrhea dosing has additional exposure-risk restrictions. Acetylcholinesterase inhibition can intensify cholinergic effects, and hypersensitivity can occur.

Mechanism

Bacterial type II topoisomerases cut and reseal DNA to remove torsional stress and separate replicated chromosomes. Gepotidacin stabilizes enzyme-DNA cleavage complexes involving DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV, preventing normal religation and replication. Its binding site differs from fluoroquinolones, so common quinolone-resistance substitutions do not automatically confer the same resistance pattern, although target mutations and efflux can still reduce activity.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm indication, sex/age/weight threshold, culture or NAAT and susceptibility context, renal/hepatic function, QT history, potassium/magnesium, complete medication list, pregnancy and STI-site testing before use.

  • For gonorrhea, ensure guideline-directed testing for coinfections, partner management, abstinence counseling and test-of-cure/follow-up when indicated; a new antibiotic does not replace public-health care.

Key Labs

  • Urinalysis and urine culture for UTI as indicated; gonorrhea NAAT/culture and susceptibility when available; creatinine/eGFR, liver function, potassium/magnesium, ECG when risk is present, and digoxin level when clinically appropriate.

Nclex Traps

  • Gepotidacin is not a fluoroquinolone even though both affect gyrase/topoisomerase IV.

  • The uUTI and gonorrhea regimens and eligibility thresholds differ; never transfer one schedule to the other.

Description

Gepotidacin is a first-in-class oral triazaacenaphthylene antibiotic that inhibits bacterial DNA gyrase and topoisomerase IV through binding geometry distinct from fluoroquinolones. It is used for narrowly defined susceptible uncomplicated urinary or urogenital infections, not pyelonephritis, sepsis, or empiric treatment of every dysuria syndrome.

Class

ALS therapy with free-radical-scavenging chemistry and unestablished clinical mechanism

Used To Treat

Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis using product-specific IV infusion or oral-suspension treatment cycles.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current RADICAVA/RADICAVA ORS labeling has no boxed warning. Both formulations can cause hypersensitivity and anaphylaxis and contain sodium bisulfite, which can trigger life-threatening allergic or asthmatic reactions, particularly in sulfite-sensitive patients with asthma. Stop and treat airway, hypotensive or generalized hypersensitivity symptoms immediately.

Mechanism

Edaravone can donate an electron to reactive radical species and form a resonance-stabilized metabolite, interrupting lipid-peroxidation chains in membranes. Oxidative stress is one contributor to motor-neuron injury, but ALS is biologically heterogeneous and no validated chain from radical scavenging to the observed human effect has been established; it is not accurate to call edaravone a direct motor-neuron repair drug. Edaravone can donate electrons to reactive radical species and thereby interrupt lipid-peroxidation chain reactions in experimental membranes. Oxidative stress participates in motor-neuron injury, but ALS has multiple interacting mechanisms and the exact target that produces clinical benefit is unknown. Because antioxidant chemistry is plausible rather than a proven complete disease pathway, the card must not claim that edaravone removes the cause of ALS; it modestly changes functional decline in selected treatment contexts while respiratory, nutritional, mobility, and communication care remain essential.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify IV versus ORS formulation, exact treatment-cycle day, prior interruption, sulfite/asthma and edaravone allergy history, oral fasting interval, shake/measure technique and feeding-tube instructions.

  • Trend gait, falls, contusions, headache, ALSFRS-R domains, weight, swallowing, secretions and respiratory function; establish whether a decline is disease progression, administration error or toxicity.

Key Labs

  • No routine drug level. Use clinical respiratory and functional measures, nutritional status, allergy assessment, and hepatic/renal review when clinically indicated.

Nclex Traps

  • Do not present free-radical scavenging as a fully proven ALS therapeutic mechanism; the label explicitly says the mechanism is unknown.

  • Oral suspension and IV treatment cycles require exact conversion instructions and are not simply daily continuous dosing.

Description

Edaravone is a free-radical-scavenging redox modulator and antioxidant that interrupts oxidative lipid-chain injury in experimental systems and is given as cyclic IV RADICAVA or oral RADICAVA ORS; however, the mechanism producing clinical ALS benefit remains unknown. A scientifically honest card must separate this proposed antioxidant chemistry from a proven disease mechanism.

Description

Benzodiazepine anxiolytic/sedative/anticonvulsants amplify GABA-A inhibition to calm overactive neural circuits, stop selected seizures, treat alcohol withdrawal, or support procedures. Their first-screen danger is CNS and respiratory depression, falls/delirium, dependence, withdrawal, and synergy with opioids or alcohol.

Mechanism

GABA-A positive allosteric modulation increases chloride-channel opening frequency, making neurons less likely to fire.

Definition

Tripe palms is an observed palmar sign. It is a clue to paraneoplastic acanthosis with internal malignancy. A positive finding means palms become thickened, velvety, and deeply ridged like bovine tripe.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Tripe palms means palms become thickened, velvety, and deeply ridged like bovine tripe.

Why It Matters

The observed Tripe palms finding can signal paraneoplastic acanthosis with internal malignancy. Growth-factor signaling causes marked palmar hyperkeratosis and papillomatosis. Tripe palms cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

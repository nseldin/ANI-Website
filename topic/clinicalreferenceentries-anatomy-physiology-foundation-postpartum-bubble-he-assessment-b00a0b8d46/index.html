Name

Postpartum BUBBLE-HE assessment

Nclex Essential

true

Quick Answer

Use the framework to organize care, but prioritize hemorrhage, hypertension, infection, cardiopulmonary symptoms, neurologic change, thromboembolism, and mental-health danger signs immediately.

Sections

  • Label

    Physical recovery

    Text

    Assess feeding goals and breast concerns, fundal tone and position, bladder emptying, bowel function, lochia amount and character, perineum or cesarean incision, pain, mobility, and vital-sign trends.

  • Label

    Modern H and E

    Text

    ANI uses H for hemorrhoids and a real lower-extremity thromboembolism assessment, and E for emotional status, bonding, support, and safety. Homan's sign is not recommended for diagnosing or excluding deep-vein thrombosis.

  • Label

    Urgent postpartum warnings

    Text

    Escalate heavy or increasing bleeding, boggy uterus with hemorrhage, syncope, severe headache or vision change, chest pain, dyspnea, unilateral leg swelling, fever, wound infection, seizure, confusion, psychosis, or suicidal thoughts.

  • Label

    Individualization

    Text

    Birth route, anesthesia, hemorrhage, hypertensive disease, infection, feeding plan, loss, trauma, language, disability, and social support change the assessment and teaching plan.

Summary

BUBBLE-HE is a postpartum assessment organizer covering breasts, uterus, bladder, bowel, lochia, episiotomy or incision, hemorrhoids and thromboembolism risk, and emotional status.

    Cue

    B-U-B-B-L-E-H-E

    Expansion
    • Cue

      B

      Meaning

      Breasts and Feeding. Feeding goals, engorgement, pain, skin injury, and infection affect recovery and infant care.

    • Cue

      U

      Meaning

      Uterus. Fundal tone and position help identify atony, distention effects, and involution.

    • Cue

      B2

      Meaning

      Bladder. Retention can displace the uterus, impair contraction, and increase discomfort or infection risk.

    • Cue

      B3

      Meaning

      Bowel. Motility, pain, hydration, medicines, and perineal trauma affect elimination.

    • Cue

      L

      Meaning

      Lochia. Amount, trend, color, clots, and odor help detect hemorrhage or infection.

    • Cue

      E

      Meaning

      Episiotomy, Perineum, or Incision. Wound appearance and pain can reveal hematoma, infection, or separation.

    • Cue

      H

      Meaning

      Hemorrhoids and Thromboembolism Risk. Perianal discomfort and true lower-extremity cardiopulmonary assessment address common recovery and high-risk complications without using Homan's sign.

    • Cue

      E2

      Meaning

      Emotional Status and Support. Mood, bonding, sleep, trauma, psychosis, safety, and support are essential postpartum health domains.

    Id

    mnemonic:bubble-he

    Limitations

    • Curricular expansions vary.

    • ANI deliberately excludes Homan's sign because it is unreliable for DVT.

    • The sequence cannot replace vital signs, pain assessment, medication review, or individualized risk screening.

Mechanism

Methotrexate ectopic pregnancy works by inhibiting folate-dependent DNA synthesis, especially through dihydrofolate reductase effects at higher antineoplastic doses and anti-inflammatory folate/adenosine pathway effects at lower autoimmune doses. Rapidly dividing tissue explains marrow suppression, mucositis, embryo-fetal toxicity, liver injury, and the need for folic acid or leucovorin strategies in selected protocols.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Methotrexate ectopic pregnancy in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Methotrexate ectopic pregnancy is an antifolate antimetabolite and DMARD/antineoplastic: it works by inhibiting folate-dependent DNA synthesis, especially through dihydrofolate reductase effects at higher antineoplastic doses and anti-inflammatory folate/adenosine pathway effects at lower autoimmune doses. It is used for autoimmune inflammatory disease control such as rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, lupus, or inflammatory bowel disease depending on agent.

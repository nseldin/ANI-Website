Summary

Endometrial biopsy is a high-yield Obstetrics/Gynecology diagnostic reference. Sampling of uterine lining to evaluate abnormal uterine bleeding, hyperplasia, malignancy, or hormonal effects.

Quick Answer

Endometrial biopsy: Sampling of uterine lining to evaluate abnormal uterine bleeding, hyperplasia, malignancy, or hormonal effects. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Sampling of uterine lining to evaluate abnormal uterine bleeding, hyperplasia, malignancy, or hormonal effects.

    • Before / preparation

    • Verify consent when required, allergies, NPO/sedation needs, anticoagulant/bleeding risks, baseline vitals, and what monitoring is needed after the procedure.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Monitor cramping, bleeding, dizziness, fever, foul drainage, or severe pain and teach expected mild cramping/spotting per protocol.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Bleeding, infection, severe pain, respiratory distress, neuro change, perforation signs, abnormal drainage, fever, or unstable vital signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Postmenopausal bleeding is never brushed off as normal.

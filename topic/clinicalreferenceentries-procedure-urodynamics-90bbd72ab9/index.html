Summary

Urodynamics is a high-yield Renal/Urologic diagnostic reference. Tests bladder storage/emptying pressures and flow for incontinence or retention problems.

Quick Answer

Urodynamics: Tests bladder storage/emptying pressures and flow for incontinence or retention problems. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

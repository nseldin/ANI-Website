Boxed Warning

No universal boxed warning; first-dose syncope/orthostatic hypotension and falls are high-yield.

Mechanism

Silodosin works by blocking alpha-1 adrenergic tone in smooth muscle. In the prostate and bladder neck this improves urine flow; in vessels it lowers vascular tone, which is why first-dose syncope, orthostatic hypotension, dizziness, and fall risk matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor orthostatic BP, dizziness/falls, and urinary symptoms.

  • Give first dose at bedtime when ordered.

Contraindications

  • severe hypotension

Interactions

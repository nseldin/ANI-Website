Used To Treat

Hypertensive emergency or controlled acute blood pressure reduction in closely monitored critical-care settings.

Mechanism

Nitroprusside releases nitric oxide, activating guanylate cyclase and increasing cGMP in vascular smooth muscle. Arterial and venous relaxation lowers afterload and preload; metabolism can generate cyanide, requiring dose/duration vigilance and toxicity monitoring.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for nitroprusside in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Nitroprusside is a potent IV vasodilator that releases nitric oxide and rapidly dilates arteries and veins. It can drop blood pressure minute-to-minute, so continuous BP monitoring is mandatory; cyanide/thiocyanate toxicity risk rises with high doses, prolonged infusion, renal impairment, or hepatic vulnerability.

Definition

Peau d'orange sign is an observed skin sign. It is a clue to dermal lymphatic obstruction, often inflammatory breast cancer. A positive finding means skin is thickened and dimpled like orange peel.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Peau d'orange sign means skin is thickened and dimpled like orange peel.

Why It Matters

The observed Peau d'orange sign finding can signal dermal lymphatic obstruction, often inflammatory breast cancer. Lymphatic blockage causes dermal edema while hair follicles and tether points remain fixed, creating pits. Peau d'orange sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • dermal lymphatic obstruction, often inflammatory breast cancer

Inspect edematous skin over breast or another region.

Lymphatic blockage causes dermal edema while hair follicles and tether points remain fixed, creating pits.

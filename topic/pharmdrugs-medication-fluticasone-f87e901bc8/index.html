Used To Treat

Inflammation, asthma/COPD exacerbations, allergic reactions, autoimmune disease, adrenal insufficiency replacement, cerebral edema, and transplant/oncology protocols.

Mechanism

Fluticasone works by diffusing into cells and activating cytosolic glucocorticoid receptors that move to the nucleus, change transcription, suppress phospholipase A2/COX-2/cytokine signaling, and reduce inflammatory cell trafficking. Benefit is less inflammation/immune activation; risks are hyperglycemia, infection masking, adrenal suppression, mood changes, myopathy, bone loss, skin thinning, and route-specific effects such as oral thrush or ocular pressure.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; major concerns include immunosuppression, adrenal suppression, hyperglycemia, GI bleeding, mood changes, osteoporosis, and infection masking.

Contraindications

  • Systemic fungal infection for many systemic steroids

  • live vaccines with immunosuppressive doses

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor glucose, infection, GI bleeding, mood, BP, fluid retention.

  • Do not stop chronic therapy abruptly.

  • Taper if ordered.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • NSAIDs increase GI bleeding.

  • Diabetes meds may need adjustment.

  • Warfarin effect can change.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Nclex Traps

  • Steroids can hide infection fever while driving glucose up.

  • Steroids can mask infection and raise glucose. Do not stop chronic systemic steroids abruptly.

Class

Oral neuroactive-steroid GABA-A receptor positive allosteric modulator

Used To Treat

Postpartum depression in adults as a 14-day evening course, alone or added to an oral antidepressant.

Boxed Warning

Impaired ability to drive or perform hazardous activities: do not drive, operate machinery or perform work requiring full alertness until at least 12 hours after every dose throughout the 14-day course. Patients may be unable to judge their own competence. Additive CNS and respiratory depression occurs with alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines and other depressants.

Mechanism

Zuranolone binds an allosteric neurosteroid site and amplifies GABA-mediated chloride-channel responses at synaptic and extrasynaptic GABA-A receptors. The exact pathway producing PPD benefit is not fully understood; the proposed model is restoration of inhibitory-network tone after peripartum neurosteroid fluctuation rather than direct serotonin reuptake inhibition.

Nursing Essentials

  • Administer once in the evening with fat-containing food for 14 days; verify hepatic/renal dose adjustment, CYP3A inhibitors/inducers, other depressants, falls, infant-care support, pregnancy/contraception for treatment and 1 week after, and substance-use risk because it is Schedule IV.

  • Assess worsening depression, suicidality, sedation, confusion, gait, breathing and actual driving arrangements; reduce or discontinue for clinically important CNS depression under the prescribed plan.

Key Labs

  • No routine drug level. Use depression and suicide-risk scales, sedation/fall/respiratory assessment, pregnancy counseling and hepatic/renal review.

Nclex Traps

  • The no-driving interval is at least 12 hours after each dose, not merely after the first dose or until the patient feels awake.

  • The course lasts 14 days; do not continue indefinitely without a new evidence-based plan.

Description

Zuranolone is an oral neuroactive steroid that enhances GABA-A receptor responses and is taken each evening for exactly 14 days to treat adult postpartum depression. It is not a chronic daily antidepressant or ordinary hypnotic. Its rapid inhibitory-network effects can reduce depressive symptoms but also cause somnolence, slowed thinking, confusion, gait impairment, falls and next-day driving impairment that patients may not recognize themselves.

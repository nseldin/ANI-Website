Sections

  • Content

    Contact Precautions are transmission-based measures added to Standard Precautions when direct or indirect contact is an important route and baseline measures alone are not sufficient for the organism, syndrome, or care setting.

    Id

    scope

    Title

    Scope

  • Content

    Indications include C. difficile infection, uncontrolled draining wounds, and conditions specified by current public-health or facility guidance. CDC currently recommends Contact Precautions for patients colonized or infected with MRSA, including in inpatient acute care. Other multidrug-resistant-organism decisions still depend on the organism, syndrome, drainage control, care setting, outbreak status, and current policy.

    Id

    indications

    Title

    Indications

  • Content

    Gown and glove barriers reduce transfer between the patient or environment and healthcare workers' hands or clothing. Patient placement, dedicated equipment, transport planning, and environmental cleaning reduce opportunities for contaminated surfaces and devices to carry organisms onward.

    Id

    mechanism

    Title

    Mechanism

  • Content

    Place the patient appropriately, post clear instructions, perform hand hygiene, don gown and gloves before the contact specified by policy, dedicate or disinfect equipment, contain secretions or drainage, limit transport to necessary care, remove PPE without self-contamination, and perform hand hygiene. Use organism-specific cleaning and hand-hygiene measures when spores or environmental persistence change the risk.

    Id

    procedure

    Title

    Procedure

  • Content

    Contact Precautions do not replace Standard Precautions, source control, cleaning, or antibiotic stewardship. Requirements differ across acute care, long-term care, and home care, and unnecessary isolation can impair mobility, communication, and timely care. Follow current local guidance rather than treating the card as a permanent universal order.

    Id

    limitations

    Title

    Limitations

  • Content

    Never delay emergency assessment or treatment to complete routine isolation setup. Ensure necessary monitoring and clinician contact continue, educate visitors clearly, and reassess the indication and discontinuation criteria under facility policy.

    Id

    precautions

    Title

    Precautions

  • Content

    Reviewed against CDC isolation-precautions, C. difficile acute-care prevention, and current MRSA infection-control guidance.

Used To Treat

Local or regional anesthesia, procedural numbing, nerve blocks, arrhythmia treatment for selected agents, or topical pain control depending on formulation.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; local anesthetic systemic toxicity, CNS toxicity, dysrhythmias, and methemoglobinemia for selected agents are key concerns.

Mechanism

Flucaine works by stabilizing neuronal firing through sodium channels, calcium channels, GABA, glutamate, or synaptic-vesicle targets depending on the agent. The nurse connects this to CNS toxicity, rash risk, pregnancy risk, and drug levels when ordered.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor CNS symptoms, numbness/tingling, seizures, cardiac rhythm, dose limits, and epinephrine-containing product cautions.

Contraindications

  • Serious hypersensitivity

  • heart block or severe shock may require clarification for the specific agent

Interactions

  • Other sodium-channel blockers/antiarrhythmics can increase toxicity risk.

Key Labs

  • ECG if toxicity

  • methemoglobin if cyanosis unresponsive to oxygen with risk agent

Nclex Traps

  • Tinnitus, metallic taste, circumoral numbness, seizures, or dysrhythmias after local anesthetic can be systemic toxicity.

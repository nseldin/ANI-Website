Definition

Livedo reticularis is an observed vascular skin sign. It is a clue to heterogeneous cutaneous perfusion. A positive finding means a regular lace-like reticular discoloration is visible.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Livedo reticularis means a regular lace-like reticular discoloration is visible.

Why It Matters

The observed Livedo reticularis finding can signal heterogeneous cutaneous perfusion. Sluggish deoxygenated blood in the superficial venous plexus outlines a vascular network. Livedo reticularis cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • heterogeneous cutaneous perfusion

Assessment Method

Inspect limbs and trunk for a netlike violaceous pattern and response to warming.

Positive Finding

A regular lace-like reticular discoloration is visible.

Mechanism

Sluggish deoxygenated blood in the superficial venous plexus outlines a vascular network.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Livedo reticularis finding raises concern for heterogeneous cutaneous perfusion because sluggish deoxygenated blood in the superficial venous plexus outlines a vascular network. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Livedo reticularis does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Livedo reticularis to focus evaluation of heterogeneous cutaneous perfusion and the reviewed alternatives: livedo racemosa, cutis marmorata, cholesterol emboli. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect limbs and trunk for a netlike violaceous pattern and response to warming. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Livedo reticularis as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Transient symmetric cold-related livedo is common. Therefore, Livedo reticularis cannot confirm or exclude heterogeneous cutaneous perfusion by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • livedo racemosa

  • cutis marmorata

  • cholesterol emboli

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as livedo racemosa, cutis marmorata, cholesterol emboli.

False Negative Situations

Livedo reticularis may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of heterogeneous cutaneous perfusion.

Class

Liver-directed thyroid-hormone-receptor-beta partial agonist

Used To Treat

Adults with noncirrhotic MASH/NASH and moderate-to-advanced F2-F3 fibrosis, together with diet and exercise, under accelerated U.S. approval; it is not labeled for incidental steatosis or decompensated cirrhosis.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Monitor for hepatotoxicity, gallstones/cholecystitis/gallstone pancreatitis and statin toxicity. Avoid Child-Pugh B or C hepatic impairment and decompensated cirrhosis. New jaundice, right-upper-quadrant pain, fever/rash/eosinophilia, myalgia, weakness or dark urine requires prompt interruption/evaluation according to the label.

Mechanism

THR-beta activation changes transcription of genes controlling mitochondrial fatty-acid oxidation, de novo lipogenesis, cholesterol conversion and lipoprotein clearance. Lower hepatocellular lipid burden reduces lipotoxic inflammatory and fibrogenic signaling. Resmetirom is more potent at THR-beta than THR-alpha but is not absolutely receptor-exclusive, so clinical monitoring remains necessary.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify fibrosis stage, cirrhosis/decompensation status, weight-based dose and whole-tablet administration.

  • Reconcile statin identity/dose and CYP2C8/OATP drugs, then teach immediate reporting of liver, gallbladder and muscle-injury symptoms.

Contraindications

  • No labeled absolute contraindication

  • Avoid moderate/severe hepatic impairment or decompensated cirrhosis

  • Clarify active hepatotoxicity, acute gallbladder disease and whether the patient actually meets the noncirrhotic F2-F3 MASH population

Interactions

  • Avoid strong CYP2C8 inhibitors such as gemfibrozil; reduce dose with moderate inhibitors such as clopidogrel.

  • Limit atorvastatin, pravastatin, rosuvastatin and simvastatin as labeled and monitor toxicity.

  • Avoid OATP1B1/1B3 inhibitors; monitor sensitive CYP2C8 substrates more closely.

Key Labs

  • AST, ALT, alkaline phosphatase and bilirubin

  • Fasting lipid panel, thyroid function when clinically appropriate, glucose/A1c and metabolic risk markers

  • Creatine kinase/renal function for muscle symptoms and gallbladder imaging for compatible pain

Nclex Traps

  • Steatosis alone is not the labeled indication; noncirrhotic F2-F3 steatohepatitis matters.

  • Resmetirom is not thyroid-replacement therapy for hypothyroidism.

  • A falling LDL can coexist with increased statin exposure and myopathy risk.

Description

Resmetirom is a liver-directed thyroid-hormone-receptor-beta partial agonist that changes hepatic gene transcription to increase lipid oxidation and improve lipoprotein handling, reducing intrahepatic triglyceride stress. Its treatment population is specifically noncirrhotic steatohepatitis with F2-F3 fibrosis, and treatment requires surveillance for hepatotoxicity and gallbladder events such as cholelithiasis or cholecystitis.

Adverse Effects

  • Diarrhea, nausea, pruritus, vomiting, constipation, abdominal pain and dizziness

  • Drug-induced liver injury

  • Cholelithiasis, cholecystitis and obstructive gallstone pancreatitis

  • Increased statin exposure with liver-test elevation, myopathy or rhabdomyolysis

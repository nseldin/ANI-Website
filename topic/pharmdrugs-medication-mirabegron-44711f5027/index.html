Contraindications

  • Serious mirabegron hypersensitivity

  • Avoid severe uncontrolled hypertension

  • Apply product-specific limits in severe renal impairment, moderate hepatic impairment and strong interaction contexts

Nursing Essentials

  • Check baseline and follow-up blood pressure, voiding diary and post-void residual when obstruction or incomplete emptying is plausible.

  • Review CYP2D6 substrates and digoxin before initiation or dose increase; symptom benefit is reassessed over weeks, not after one tablet.

Interactions

  • Moderate CYP2D6 inhibition raises exposure to metoprolol, desipramine and potentially flecainide, propafenone, thioridazine and other sensitive substrates.

  • Can increase digoxin concentration; use low initial digoxin dosing and monitor levels per labeling.

  • Antimuscarinics add urinary-retention risk.

Key Labs

  • Blood pressure and pulse

  • Renal and hepatic function

  • Post-void residual, symptom diary and digoxin concentration when used

Nclex Traps

  • Mirabegron relaxes detrusor during filling; it does not stimulate urination.

  • It avoids antimuscarinic blockade but still can cause retention.

  • Its CYP2D6 interaction distinguishes it from vibegron.

Used To Treat

Adult overactive bladder with urgency, frequency and urge urinary incontinence; selected Myrbetriq tablet or granule formulations also treat age- and weight-defined pediatric neurogenic detrusor overactivity and are not milligram-for-milligram interchangeable.

Mechanism

Beta-3 receptor activation couples to Gs, stimulates adenylyl cyclase, raises cyclic AMP and activates protein kinase A. Downstream calcium availability and contractile-protein activity fall, so the detrusor remains more compliant while urine accumulates. This targets storage, not outlet resistance or bladder-emptying strength. Moderate CYP2D6 inhibition is a separate hepatic interaction mechanism and can raise narrow-therapeutic-index substrate exposure.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Mirabegron can increase blood pressure and is not recommended in severe uncontrolled hypertension. Monitor for new retention, especially with bladder outlet obstruction or an antimuscarinic. Angioedema can threaten the airway. Extended-release tablets and pediatric granules are different products, not milligram-for-milligram substitutes.

Description

Mirabegron is a beta-3 adrenergic agonist that relaxes detrusor smooth muscle during bladder filling, increasing storage capacity without directly blocking muscarinic receptors. It is useful when anticholinergic dry mouth, constipation or cognitive burden is undesirable, but it can raise blood pressure, inhibit CYP2D6, and worsen urinary retention in outlet obstruction or when combined with an antimuscarinic.

Adverse Effects

  • Hypertension, headache, nasopharyngitis and urinary tract infection

  • Tachycardia, palpitations and rare atrial fibrillation

  • Urinary retention and increased post-void residual

  • Angioedema, constipation, diarrhea or dizziness

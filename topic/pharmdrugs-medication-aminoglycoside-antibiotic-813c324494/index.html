Class

Medication class reference

Description

Aminoglycosides are concentration-dependent bactericidal protein-synthesis inhibitors.

Used To Treat

Serious aerobic gram-negative infections, synergy regimens, and topical/otic/ophthalmic uses depending on formulation.

Mechanism

They bind the 30S ribosomal subunit and cause misreading of bacterial mRNA. Renal cortical and inner-ear accumulation explain nephrotoxicity and ototoxicity.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor creatinine, urine output, drug levels per protocol, hearing/tinnitus/vertigo, neuromuscular weakness, and nephrotoxin co-use.

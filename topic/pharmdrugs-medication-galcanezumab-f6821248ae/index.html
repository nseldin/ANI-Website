Used To Treat

Preventive treatment of migraine in adults and treatment of episodic cluster headache during an active cluster period in adults, using different loading and maintenance regimens.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current EMGALITY labeling has no boxed warning. Serious hypersensitivity, including anaphylaxis, angioedema, dyspnea, urticaria, and rash, can occur days after administration and may be prolonged. Monitor new or worsening hypertension and Raynaud phenomenon. Current U.S. EMGALITY labeling does not contain the dedicated severe-constipation warning present in current AIMOVIG, AJOVY, and VYEPTI labels; still assess clinically significant bowel symptoms rather than treating that difference as proof of zero risk.

Mechanism

Galcanezumab binds CGRP ligand before it can activate the CLR/RAMP1 receptor complex, reducing Gs-adenylyl-cyclase-cAMP signaling in trigeminovascular pathways. It does not broadly suppress immune function. Proteolytic catabolism and an approximately 27-day half-life explain monthly dosing, few conventional CYP interactions, and prolonged exposure after discontinuation.

Nursing Essentials

  • For migraine verify a 240-mg loading dose as two 120-mg injections, then 120 mg monthly. For episodic cluster headache verify 300 mg as three 100-mg injections at cluster onset and monthly until the cluster period ends.

  • Document headache or cluster pattern, blood pressure, digital perfusion, allergy history, pregnancy plans, product strength, storage, injection-site rotation, and sharps disposal. Maintain an acute rescue plan because preventive antibody dosing does not abort an attack already in progress.

Contraindications

  • Contraindicated after serious hypersensitivity to galcanezumab or an excipient. Delayed onset and prolonged symptoms are possible because antibody exposure persists.

Interactions

  • Conventional CYP interactions are unlikely because galcanezumab is catabolized as a protein rather than metabolized by hepatic CYP enzymes.

  • Do not interchange the migraine and episodic cluster-headache regimens: device strength, injection count, loading strategy, and treatment duration differ because they were studied for different clinical patterns.

Key Labs

  • No routine concentration, CBC, liver panel, TB screen, or hepatitis screen is required solely for galcanezumab. Monitor clinical response, injection sites, hypersensitivity, blood pressure, and digital perfusion.

Nclex Traps

  • Galcanezumab binds CGRP ligand, not the receptor.

  • Migraine uses 240 mg once then 120 mg monthly; episodic cluster headache uses 300 mg at onset and monthly during the cluster period.

  • The absence of a dedicated constipation warning in the current label does not make all bowel complaints irrelevant.

Description

Galcanezumab is a humanized IgG4 monoclonal antibody that binds the CGRP ligand and prevents receptor activation. For migraine, the first 240-mg loading dose is two 120-mg injections followed by 120 mg monthly. For episodic cluster headache, 300 mg is given as three 100-mg injections at cluster onset and monthly until the cluster period ends. It is not an acute abortive dose for a single migraine or cluster attack.

Adverse Effects

  • Injection-site pain and reactions are the most common adverse effects.

  • Serious or delayed hypersensitivity, postmarketing hypertension, and Raynaud phenomenon are the major high-consequence concerns.

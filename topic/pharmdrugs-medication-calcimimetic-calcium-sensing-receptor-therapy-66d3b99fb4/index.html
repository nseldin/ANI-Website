Class Example Names

  • Cinacalcet

  • Etelcalcetide

Used To Treat

Secondary hyperparathyroidism in adults with CKD on dialysis; cinacalcet also treats hypercalcemia in parathyroid carcinoma and selected severe primary hyperparathyroidism when surgery is not possible.

Description

Calcimimetics make parathyroid chief cells perceive extracellular calcium as higher than it is. Positive allosteric modulation of the calcium-sensing receptor suppresses PTH, which then lowers calcium release from bone and often lowers serum calcium and phosphorus. This is why the class can control secondary hyperparathyroidism without adding calcium, but can cause dangerous hypocalcemia, QT prolongation, seizures and low-turnover bone.

Mechanism

CaSR is a G-protein-coupled receptor using Gq and Gi pathways. Allosteric sensitization shifts its calcium-response curve left, so a given extracellular calcium concentration generates stronger intracellular signaling and less PTH secretion. Cinacalcet is oral and inhibits CYP2D6; etelcalcetide is an IV dialysis peptide that activates CaSR through a disulfide interaction. Suppression below physiologic PTH removes remodeling stimulus and can cause adynamic bone.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Do not initiate when corrected calcium is below the lower limit of normal. Hypocalcemia can prolong QT, cause ventricular arrhythmia, lower seizure threshold, worsen hypotension/heart failure and produce tetany. Monitor intact PTH to avoid adynamic bone disease.

Adverse Effects

  • Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea and decreased appetite

  • Hypocalcemia, paresthesia, muscle spasm and QT-related arrhythmia

  • Seizures or worsening heart failure/hypotension

  • Adynamic bone disease from over-suppressed PTH

Contraindications

  • Initiation with below-normal corrected calcium

  • Cinacalcet is not indicated for secondary HPT in CKD patients not on dialysis because hypocalcemia risk is high

  • Clarify hepatic impairment, seizure/QT history and interacting drugs

Nursing Essentials

  • Trend corrected/ionized calcium and PTH according to dialysis protocol; symptoms can precede a scheduled lab.

  • Differentiate secondary HPT treatment from emergency hyperkalemia or calcium replacement.

Interactions

  • Cinacalcet strongly inhibits CYP2D6 and can raise flecainide, propafenone, tricyclic and other substrate exposure.

  • Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors can increase cinacalcet exposure.

  • Other calcium-lowering or QT-prolonging drugs compound risk.

Key Labs

  • Corrected or ionized calcium

  • Phosphorus and intact PTH

  • ECG/electrolytes and hepatic function when risk warrants

Nclex Traps

  • A calcimimetic lowers PTH by mimicking a high-calcium signal; it does not supply calcium.

  • A lower PTH is not always better because very low PTH can mean adynamic bone.

  • Cinacalcet's CYP2D6 inhibition is a drug-specific interaction layer.

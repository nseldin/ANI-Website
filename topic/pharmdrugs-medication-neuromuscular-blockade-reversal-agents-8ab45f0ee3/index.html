Used To Treat

Reversal of selected nondepolarizing neuromuscular blockade after surgery, guided by the blocking agent, depth of blockade, quantitative train-of-four monitoring, airway readiness, and reversal-drug mechanism.

Description

Neuromuscular reversal can remove the blocker directly or increase acetylcholine competition. Sugammadex encapsulates rocuronium or vecuronium in plasma. Neostigmine inhibits acetylcholinesterase, raising acetylcholine at the neuromuscular junction and requires an antimuscarinic to control bradycardia and secretions. Neither replaces ventilation until objective recovery is adequate.

Mechanism

Sugammadex creates a concentration gradient by binding free aminosteroid blocker; drug leaves the nicotinic receptor and enters the sugammadex complex. Neostigmine prevents acetylcholine breakdown, so more transmitter competes with residual blocker. Profound blockade can overwhelm neostigmine's ceiling effect, whereas sugammadex dose is selected from post-tetanic count or train-of-four depth.

Boxed Warning

Neither currently has a boxed warning. Sugammadex can cause anaphylaxis and marked bradycardia or cardiac arrest within minutes, reduces hormonal-contraceptive exposure for 7 days, and is not recommended in severe renal impairment. Neostigmine can produce cholinergic bradycardia, bronchospasm, secretions, bowel activity, and paradoxical weakness if misused.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm the exact paralytic, actual body weight when dosing sugammadex, quantitative train-of-four depth, renal function, airway patency, ventilation, oxygenation, temperature, acid-base status and all other causes of weakness.

  • Continue respiratory monitoring after apparent reversal because obstruction, recurarization, opioid effect, residual anesthetic, or respiratory disease can still cause failure.

Key Labs

  • Quantitative train-of-four ratio and clinical respiratory assessment; renal function for sugammadex and ECG/hemodynamics for either agent. Laboratory tests do not replace neuromuscular monitoring.

Nclex Traps

  • Sugammadex reverses rocuronium and vecuronium, not succinylcholine or every nondepolarizing blocker.

  • A patient can move yet retain clinically important pharyngeal weakness; objective recovery and airway assessment matter.

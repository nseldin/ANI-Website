Quick Answer

Count pregnancies and outcomes using the institution's gestational-age definitions; multiple gestation is one pregnancy but may add more than one living child.

Sections

  • Label

    What the notation communicates

    Text

    Gravida counts pregnancies including the current pregnancy. Term and preterm count pregnancy outcomes within defined gestational ranges. The A field records losses before the local viability threshold, and L records living children.

  • Label

    Why conventions matter

    Text

    Gestational thresholds and whether a system separately identifies miscarriage, induced abortion, ectopic pregnancy, molar pregnancy, or multiples can vary. Confirm local documentation rules instead of inferring from the acronym alone.

  • Label

    Respectful history

    Text

    Ask neutrally and privately. Pregnancy loss, infertility, adoption, surrogacy, and infant death can be traumatic; shorthand must not erase the person's language or experience.

  • Label

    Clinical limitations

    Text

    GTPAL does not convey complications, cesarean history, hemorrhage, hypertensive disease, preterm mechanism, neonatal outcome, or current risk. Document those separately.

Summary

GTPAL is obstetric-history shorthand for total pregnancies, term births, preterm births, pregnancy losses, and living children.

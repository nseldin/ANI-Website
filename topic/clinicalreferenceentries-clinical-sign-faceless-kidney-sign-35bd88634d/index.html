Definition

Faceless kidney sign is an imaging sign. It is a clue to renal duplication or renal sinus displacement. A positive finding means the normal echogenic renal sinus appears absent or displaced, giving a faceless appearance.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Faceless kidney sign means the normal echogenic renal sinus appears absent or displaced, giving a faceless appearance.

Why It Matters

The Faceless kidney sign imaging pattern can signal renal duplication or renal sinus displacement. Duplicated collecting systems or mass effect separate or distort central sinus echoes. Faceless kidney sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • renal duplication or renal sinus displacement

Assessment Method

Review longitudinal renal ultrasound for central sinus anatomy.

Positive Finding

The normal echogenic renal sinus appears absent or displaced, giving a faceless appearance.

Mechanism

Duplicated collecting systems or mass effect separate or distort central sinus echoes.

Clinical Interpretation

The Faceless kidney sign imaging pattern raises concern for renal duplication or renal sinus displacement because duplicated collecting systems or mass effect separate or distort central sinus echoes. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Faceless kidney sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Faceless kidney sign to focus evaluation of renal duplication or renal sinus displacement and the reviewed alternatives: poor acoustic window, horseshoe kidney, sinus lipomatosis. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Review longitudinal renal ultrasound for central sinus anatomy. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Faceless kidney sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Operator angle and technical quality can simulate the appearance. Therefore, Faceless kidney sign cannot confirm or exclude renal duplication or renal sinus displacement by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • poor acoustic window

  • horseshoe kidney

  • sinus lipomatosis

False Positive Situations

A false-positive interpretation can result from acquisition artifact, an inappropriate imaging phase or plane, or overlap with poor acoustic window, horseshoe kidney, sinus lipomatosis.

False Negative Situations

Faceless kidney sign may be missed when the associated process is early or subtle, the relevant region is not included, or acquisition timing and resolution are inadequate.

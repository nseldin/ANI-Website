Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for lefamulin in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Mechanism

Lefamulin binds the peptidyl-transferase center of 23S rRNA in the 50S subunit and interferes with correct positioning of tRNA, preventing peptide transfer and bacterial protein synthesis. Its binding site differs from macrolides and many other 50S drugs. Binding in the 50S peptidyl-transferase center prevents correct A- and P-site tRNA positioning, thereby blocking peptide-bond formation and bacterial protein synthesis. Because lefamulin is both affected by and can affect CYP3A pathways, interacting drugs can raise toxicity or lower antimicrobial exposure depending on route. QT risk rises when exposure, bradycardia, low potassium/magnesium, congenital long-QT physiology, or other QT drugs converge. Oral fasting instructions matter because food substantially lowers exposure, which can turn an apparently correct dose into inadequate pneumonia treatment.

Nursing Essentials

  • Check QTc, potassium/magnesium, rhythm history, interacting QT/CYP3A drugs, pregnancy status, liver function, and IV infusion rate.

  • Give oral tablets according to fasting instructions and monitor diarrhea/C. difficile, nausea, liver injury, and pneumonia/oxygenation response.

Key Labs

  • ECG/QTc when risk is present, potassium, magnesium, liver tests, pregnancy testing, cultures, and respiratory response.

Nclex Traps

  • Lefamulin is a pleuromutilin, not a macrolide. QT and CYP3A interactions can convert a pneumonia treatment into a dysrhythmia problem.

Description

Lefamulin is a systemic pleuromutilin antibiotic that binds a distinct site in the 50S peptidyl-transferase center, blocking tRNA positioning and peptide-bond formation in susceptible community-acquired pneumonia organisms. QT prolongation, CYP3A interactions, embryo-fetal toxicity, and strict oral timing around food are its major teaching points.

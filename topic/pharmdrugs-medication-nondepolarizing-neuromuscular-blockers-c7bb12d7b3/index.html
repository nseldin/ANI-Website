Name

Nondepolarizing neuromuscular blockers

Aliases

  • competitive neuromuscular blockers

Description

Nondepolarizing neuromuscular blockers competitively block nicotinic acetylcholine receptors at the neuromuscular junction, preventing skeletal muscle contraction without depolarizing the endplate.

Used To Treat

Intubation, mechanical ventilation synchrony, surgery, and procedural paralysis when ordered with airway and sedation/analgesia support.

Mechanism

Competitive nicotinic receptor antagonism stops acetylcholine from triggering endplate depolarization. Paralysis continues until the drug redistributes/metabolizes or is reversed depending on the agent and protocol.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm sedation/analgesia, ventilator support, eye care, DVT/skin precautions, train-of-four or ordered paralysis monitoring, and reversal plan.

  • Know elimination differences: cisatracurium is useful in organ dysfunction because Hofmann elimination is less kidney/liver dependent.

Nclex Traps

  • Neuromuscular blockers do not treat pain, anxiety, or awareness.

Class

PARP1/2 inhibitor with potent PARP-DNA trapping

Used To Treat

Germline-BRCA-mutated HER2-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer, and with enzalutamide for HRR-gene-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: no boxed warning is listed. Do not start until marrow recovery from prior therapy; monitor CBC monthly and interrupt/reduce for significant cytopenia. Prolonged cytopenia requires evaluation for MDS/AML. Reduce dose in moderate or severe renal impairment and prevent fetal exposure.

Mechanism

Talazoparib inhibits PARP catalytic repair and tightly stabilizes PARP1/2 on damaged DNA. Trapped complexes stall replication forks and generate double-strand breaks that require homologous recombination; BRCA/HRR-deficient tumor cells accumulate unrecoverable damage and undergo apoptosis.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify germline BRCA or HRR eligibility and whether talazoparib is single-agent breast therapy or the lower-dose enzalutamide prostate regimen.

  • Screen P-gp and BCRP inhibitors, renal function, fatigue/dyspnea, bleeding, infection, pregnancy risk, and fracture/fall context in prostate regimens.

Key Labs

  • CBC baseline and monthly, creatinine/eGFR, pregnancy testing when applicable, and marrow/cytogenetics if cytopenias persist.

Nclex Traps

  • Talazoparib dose differs by indication and combination; do not transfer the breast-cancer dose into an enzalutamide regimen.

  • Potent PARP trapping explains both antitumor effect and marrow toxicity.

Description

Talazoparib is an oral PARP1/2 inhibitor notable for potent PARP-DNA trapping, which obstructs replication and creates synthetic lethality in BRCA/HRR-deficient tumors. The same potency produces prominent anemia, neutropenia, and thrombocytopenia; MDS/AML, fetal harm, renal accumulation, and P-gp/BCRP interactions are high-yield concerns.

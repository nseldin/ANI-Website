Used To Treat

Depression, neuropathic pain, migraine prophylaxis, insomnia, or selected bladder/pain syndromes depending on the medication.

Mechanism

Clomipramine works by blocking norepinephrine and serotonin reuptake while also blocking anticholinergic, antihistamine, and alpha receptors. That mixed receptor profile explains sedation, orthostasis, anticholinergic effects, and overdose danger.

Boxed Warning

Antidepressant boxed warning: increased risk of suicidal thoughts/behaviors in pediatric and young adult patients.

Contraindications

  • Recent MI

  • MAOI use

  • significant conduction disorders without specialist management

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor anticholinergic effects, orthostasis, sedation, cardiac conduction/QRS widening in overdose.

  • Overdose is dangerous.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • MAOIs, serotonergic drugs, CNS depressants, anticholinergics, QT-prolonging drugs.

  • MAOIs, linezolid, methylene blue, St. John's wort, triptans, other serotonergic drugs, alcohol/CNS depressants, anticoagulants/NSAIDs, and QT-prolonging drugs when relevant.

Key Labs

  • ECG if overdose/cardiac risk

  • mental status

  • fall risk

  • Mood/suicidality, serotonin syndrome cues, sodium in SIADH-risk clients, blood pressure for SNRIs, ECG/QT for risk agents, and overdose danger for TCAs.

Nclex Traps

  • TCA overdose can kill through dysrhythmias; sodium bicarbonate may be used for wide QRS per protocol.

  • Do not expect immediate benefit. Watch for early suicidality, serotonin syndrome, hyponatremia, bleeding risk, and abrupt discontinuation symptoms.

Description

Clomipramine is a tricyclic antidepressant (TCA): it works by blocking norepinephrine and serotonin reuptake while also blocking anticholinergic, antihistamine, and alpha receptors. It is used for depression, neuropathic pain, migraine prophylaxis, insomnia, or selected bladder/pain syndromes depending on the medication.

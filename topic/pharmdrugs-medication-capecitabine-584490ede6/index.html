Mechanism

Capecitabine is enzymatically converted through 5'-DFCR and 5'-DFUR to 5-fluorouracil. 5-FU metabolites inhibit thymidylate synthase, depleting dTMP for DNA synthesis, and can incorporate into RNA/DNA, injuring rapidly dividing cells.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for capecitabine in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Capecitabine is an oral prodrug designed to generate 5-fluorouracil inside tissues, especially tumor tissue. Its active fluoropyrimidine metabolites inhibit thymidylate synthase and disrupt DNA/RNA synthesis, so diarrhea, mucositis, hand-foot syndrome, myelosuppression, DPD deficiency, renal dosing, and warfarin interaction are high-yield.

Definition

Trolley-track sign is an imaging sign. It is a clue to posterior spinal ligament and facet-joint ossification in axial spondyloarthritis. A positive finding means three vertical lines resemble trolley tracks.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Trolley-track sign means three vertical lines resemble trolley tracks.

Why It Matters

The Trolley-track sign imaging pattern can signal posterior spinal ligament and facet-joint ossification in axial spondyloarthritis. Central interspinous ossification and paired facet-capsule ossification create parallel tracks. Trolley-track sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Review AP spinal radiograph.

Three vertical lines resemble trolley tracks.

Central interspinous ossification and paired facet-capsule ossification create parallel tracks.

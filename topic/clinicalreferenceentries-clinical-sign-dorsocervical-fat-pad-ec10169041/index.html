Definition

Dorsocervical fat pad is an observed body-composition sign. It is a clue to glucocorticoid excess or generalized adiposity. A positive finding means a localized fat pad is prominent over the cervicothoracic junction.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Dorsocervical fat pad means a localized fat pad is prominent over the cervicothoracic junction.

Why It Matters

The observed Dorsocervical fat pad finding can signal glucocorticoid excess or generalized adiposity. Hormonal and metabolic effects redistribute adipose tissue to the trunk and dorsocervical region. Dorsocervical fat pad cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • glucocorticoid excess or generalized adiposity

Assessment Method

Inspect and palpate the upper back and posterior neck.

Positive Finding

A localized fat pad is prominent over the cervicothoracic junction.

Mechanism

Hormonal and metabolic effects redistribute adipose tissue to the trunk and dorsocervical region.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Dorsocervical fat pad finding raises concern for glucocorticoid excess or generalized adiposity because hormonal and metabolic effects redistribute adipose tissue to the trunk and dorsocervical region. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Dorsocervical fat pad does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Dorsocervical fat pad to focus evaluation of glucocorticoid excess or generalized adiposity and the reviewed alternatives: kyphosis, lipoma, Madelung disease. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect and palpate the upper back and posterior neck. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Dorsocervical fat pad as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Posture, lipoma, and generalized obesity mimic it. Therefore, Dorsocervical fat pad cannot confirm or exclude glucocorticoid excess or generalized adiposity by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • kyphosis

  • lipoma

  • Madelung disease

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as kyphosis, lipoma, Madelung disease.

False Negative Situations

Dorsocervical fat pad may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of glucocorticoid excess or generalized adiposity.

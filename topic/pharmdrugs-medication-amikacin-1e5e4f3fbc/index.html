Used To Treat

Serious gram-negative bacterial infections, synergistic therapy for selected infections, and some topical/ophthalmic/otic infections depending on formulation.

Mechanism

Amikacin works by irreversibly binding the 30S ribosomal subunit, causing mRNA misreading and defective bacterial proteins in an oxygen-dependent, concentration-dependent bactericidal process. Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, peak/trough or extended-interval monitoring, renal dosing, and poor anaerobe activity are the key ideas.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warnings commonly emphasize nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, and neuromuscular blockade/respiratory paralysis risk.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity

  • caution/clarify myasthenia gravis or neuromuscular disorders

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor peak/trough or AUC per protocol, creatinine, urine output, hearing/balance.

  • Hydration matters.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Loop diuretics and vancomycin increase oto/nephrotoxicity risk.

  • Neuromuscular blockers increase paralysis risk.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • creatinine/eGFR

  • drug levels

  • urine output

  • Allergies, cultures before first dose when ordered, renal function for renally cleared drugs, liver function for hepatotoxic drugs, CBC, diarrhea/C. difficile cues, and therapeutic levels for aminoglycosides/vancomycin when ordered.

Nclex Traps

  • Hearing changes and rising creatinine are not minor side effects.

  • Do not delay urgent antibiotics for sepsis, but obtain cultures first if ordered and it does not delay care.

Description

Amikacin is an aminoglycoside antibiotic: it works by irreversibly binding the 30S ribosomal subunit, causing mRNA misreading and defective bacterial proteins in an oxygen-dependent, concentration-dependent bactericidal process. It is used for serious gram-negative bacterial infections, synergistic therapy for selected infections, and some topical/ophthalmic/otic infections depending on formulation.

    Validated physical or chemical processes inactivate organisms through heat, moisture, oxidation, alkylation, radiation, filtration, or another approved mechanism matched to the device.

  • Content

    Cleaning must occur before sterilization because soil can shield organisms. Trained sterile-processing personnel follow device instructions, load parameters, monitoring, packaging, storage, and traceability requirements.

    Title

    Procedure

  • Content

    A cycle label alone does not prove successful processing. Wrong cleaning, packaging, load configuration, device compatibility, storage, or monitoring can invalidate sterility. Prions are a special exception: routine reprocessing may be inadequate for instruments exposed to high-infectivity tissue in suspected or confirmed CJD.

  • Content

    Do not improvise sterilization or reuse single-use products outside an approved reprocessing system. Quarantine a load when required indicators or records are unacceptable. For possible prion contamination, preserve instrument traceability and immediately follow current CJD-specific infection-control and manufacturer guidance.

    CDC guidance distinguishes sterilization from lower levels of decontamination, requires validated reprocessing for critical equipment, and identifies prion-contaminated high-risk instruments as needing specialized CJD procedures.

Used To Treat

Susceptible infections based on organism, infection site, cultures, allergies, renal function, and local resistance patterns.

Mechanism

Chloramphenicol works by binding the 50S ribosomal subunit and inhibiting peptidyl-transferase activity, blocking peptide-bond formation. Bone-marrow suppression, rare aplastic anemia, gray baby syndrome, and drug interactions make it a high-caution antibiotic.

Contraindications

  • Known serious hypersensitivity to the medication/class.

  • Clarify before giving if pregnancy, severe renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vitals, or high-risk interactions are present.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

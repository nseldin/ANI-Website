Boxed Warning

High-alert warning focus: cycloserine can cause serious CNS toxicity, including seizures, psychosis, depression, confusion, and suicidal ideation. Renal impairment, alcohol, high doses, and other neurotoxic TB drugs increase danger.

Class

Second-line antitubercular; D-alanine analog cell-wall synthesis inhibitor

Used To Treat

Drug-resistant tuberculosis as part of a multidrug regimen when susceptibility and specialist guidance support use.

Description

Cycloserine is a D-alanine analog TB drug that inhibits alanine racemase and D-alanine-D-alanine ligase, blocking peptidoglycan cell-wall building. Its first-read danger is CNS toxicity: headache, tremor, depression, psychosis, seizures, suicidality, and toxicity accumulation with renal impairment.

Mechanism

Cycloserine interferes with the cytosolic steps that make D-alanine and D-Ala-D-Ala, preventing normal peptidoglycan cross-link substrate formation. It can also affect CNS neurotransmission, so renal clearance, drug levels when used, pyridoxine, and neuropsychiatric assessment matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess baseline mood, seizure history, alcohol use, renal function, and neurologic status.

  • Teach to report depression, behavior changes, hallucinations, tremor, severe headache, or seizure activity immediately.

  • Pyridoxine may be ordered to reduce neurotoxicity risk.

Contraindications

  • Clarify epilepsy/seizure disorder, severe anxiety/psychosis/depression, heavy alcohol use, severe renal impairment, and hypersensitivity.

Interactions

  • Alcohol increases seizure and psychiatric risk. Isoniazid, ethionamide, fluoroquinolones, and other neurotoxic drugs can compound CNS toxicity.

Key Labs

  • Renal function.

  • Drug levels if ordered by TB program.

  • Neurologic and mental-status trend.

Nclex Traps

  • Do not confuse cycloserine with cyclosporine. Cycloserine is an antitubercular cell-wall drug with major CNS toxicity; cyclosporine is a calcineurin immunosuppressant.

  • A new mood change on cycloserine is a medication safety issue, not just stress.

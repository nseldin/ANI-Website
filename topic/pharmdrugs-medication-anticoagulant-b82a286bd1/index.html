Description

Anticoagulants reduce clot formation by interfering with thrombin generation, thrombin activity, vitamin K-dependent factors, or antithrombin pathways.

Used To Treat

Prevention or treatment of venous/arterial thrombosis and stroke prevention in selected atrial fibrillation clients.

Mechanism

Each subclass blocks a different clotting step: warfarin lowers vitamin K-dependent factors, heparins enhance antithrombin, DOAC Xa inhibitors reduce thrombin generation, and dabigatran directly inhibits thrombin.

  • Assess bleeding, falls/trauma, renal/hepatic function, interacting drugs, procedure/neuraxial timing, adherence, and reversal plan.

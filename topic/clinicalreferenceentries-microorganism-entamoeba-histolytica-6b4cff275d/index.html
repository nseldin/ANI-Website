Definition

Entamoeba histolytica is a clinically important protozoan parasite associated with amebic colitis, amebic dysentery, and amebic liver abscess. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

    Entamoeba histolytica is associated with amebic colitis, amebic dysentery, and amebic liver abscess. Severity and presentation vary by age, immune status, pregnancy, anatomic site, inoculum, and comorbidity. Urgent escalation is based on the syndrome—such as shock, respiratory compromise, rapidly progressive neurologic findings, severe dehydration, or invasive infection—not merely on a positive organism label.

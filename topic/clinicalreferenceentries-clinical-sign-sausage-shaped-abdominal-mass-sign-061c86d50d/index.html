Definition

Sausage-shaped abdominal mass sign is a palpated clinical sign. It is a clue to intussusception. A positive finding means a tender elongated mass is felt, often in the right upper abdomen.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Sausage-shaped abdominal mass sign means a tender elongated mass is felt, often in the right upper abdomen.

Why It Matters

The observed Sausage-shaped abdominal mass sign finding can signal intussusception. Invaginated bowel and edematous mesentery form a tubular mass. Sausage-shaped abdominal mass sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • intussusception

Assessment Method

Gently palpate the abdomen between painful episodes when safe.

Positive Finding

A tender elongated mass is felt, often in the right upper abdomen.

Mechanism

Invaginated bowel and edematous mesentery form a tubular mass.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Sausage-shaped abdominal mass sign finding raises concern for intussusception because invaginated bowel and edematous mesentery form a tubular mass. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Sausage-shaped abdominal mass sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Sausage-shaped abdominal mass sign to focus evaluation of intussusception and the reviewed alternatives: constipation, Wilms tumor, duplication cyst. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Gently palpate the abdomen between painful episodes when safe. Evidence Limitations

Guarding and small lesions make it difficult to feel. Therefore, Sausage-shaped abdominal mass sign cannot confirm or exclude intussusception by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as constipation, Wilms tumor, duplication cyst.

False Negative Situations

Sausage-shaped abdominal mass sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of intussusception.

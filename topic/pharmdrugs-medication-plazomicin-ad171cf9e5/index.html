Class

Aminoglycoside antibiotic

Used To Treat

Serious gram-negative bacterial infections, synergistic therapy for selected infections, and some topical/ophthalmic/otic infections depending on formulation.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warnings commonly emphasize nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, and neuromuscular blockade/respiratory paralysis risk.

Mechanism

Plazomicin works by irreversibly binding the 30S ribosomal subunit, causing mRNA misreading and defective bacterial proteins in an oxygen-dependent, concentration-dependent bactericidal process. Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, peak/trough or extended-interval monitoring, renal dosing, and poor anaerobe activity are the key ideas.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor peak/trough or AUC per protocol, creatinine, urine output, hearing/balance.

  • Hydration matters.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity

  • caution/clarify myasthenia gravis or neuromuscular disorders

Interactions

  • Loop diuretics and vancomycin increase oto/nephrotoxicity risk.

  • Neuromuscular blockers increase paralysis risk.

Description

Plazomicin is an aminoglycoside antibiotic: it works by irreversibly binding the 30S ribosomal subunit, causing mRNA misreading and defective bacterial proteins in an oxygen-dependent, concentration-dependent bactericidal process. It is used for serious gram-negative bacterial infections, synergistic therapy for selected infections, and some topical/ophthalmic/otic infections depending on formulation.

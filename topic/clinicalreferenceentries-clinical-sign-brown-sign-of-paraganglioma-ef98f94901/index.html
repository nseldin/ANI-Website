Definition

Brown sign of paraganglioma is a pneumatic otoscopy sign. It is a clue to middle-ear paraganglioma. A positive finding means the mass blanches with positive pressure and may pulsate.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Brown sign of paraganglioma means the mass blanches with positive pressure and may pulsate.

Why It Matters

The observed Brown sign of paraganglioma finding can signal middle-ear paraganglioma. Compression temporarily empties blood from the highly vascular tumor. Brown sign of paraganglioma cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • middle-ear paraganglioma

Assessment Method

During specialist pneumatic otoscopy, observe a red retrotympanic mass with gentle pressure.

Positive Finding

The mass blanches with positive pressure and may pulsate.

Mechanism

Compression temporarily empties blood from the highly vascular tumor.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Brown sign of paraganglioma finding raises concern for middle-ear paraganglioma because compression temporarily empties blood from the highly vascular tumor. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Brown sign of paraganglioma does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Brown sign of paraganglioma to focus evaluation of middle-ear paraganglioma and the reviewed alternatives: high jugular bulb, cholesterol granuloma, hemotympanum. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: During specialist pneumatic otoscopy, observe a red retrotympanic mass with gentle pressure. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Brown sign of paraganglioma as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Not all tumors blanch and other vascular structures may. Therefore, Brown sign of paraganglioma cannot confirm or exclude middle-ear paraganglioma by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • high jugular bulb

  • cholesterol granuloma

  • hemotympanum

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as high jugular bulb, cholesterol granuloma, hemotympanum.

False Negative Situations

Brown sign of paraganglioma may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of middle-ear paraganglioma.

Apply the reviewed limitation for Brown sign of paraganglioma: Not all tumors blanch and other vascular structures may.

Safety Precautions

Observe Brown sign of paraganglioma with consent, privacy, adequate exposure, and only the manipulation required by this assessment: During specialist pneumatic otoscopy, observe a red retrotympanic mass with gentle pressure. The reviewed precaution for this finding is: Never biopsy or aggressively press a pulsatile middle-ear mass.

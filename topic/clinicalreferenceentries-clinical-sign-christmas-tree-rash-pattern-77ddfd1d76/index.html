Definition

Christmas-tree rash pattern is an observed distribution sign. It is a clue to pityriasis rosea. A positive finding means oval scaly lesions align obliquely along ribs like tree branches.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Christmas-tree rash pattern means oval scaly lesions align obliquely along ribs like tree branches.

Why It Matters

The observed Christmas-tree rash pattern finding can signal pityriasis rosea. Oval inflammatory plaques elongate along trunk cleavage lines, whose oblique orientation over the ribs creates the branching Christmas-tree distribution. Christmas-tree rash pattern cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

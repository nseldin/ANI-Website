Summary

HER2 testing (human epidermal growth factor receptor 2) is a high-yield Oncology diagnostic reference. Tumor marker testing for HER2 overexpression/amplification, important in breast and gastric cancer therapy selection.

Quick Answer

HER2 testing (human epidermal growth factor receptor 2): Tumor marker testing for HER2 overexpression/amplification, important in breast and gastric cancer therapy selection. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Negative

    Meaning

    HER2 overexpression/amplification is not demonstrated, so HER2-targeted therapy may not be indicated for that tumor context.

  • Label

    Positive

    Meaning

    HER2 overexpression or gene amplification is present and can guide HER2-targeted therapy choices.

  • Label

    Equivocal

    Meaning

    Borderline results need reflex or confirmatory testing according to tumor-specific protocols.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Tumor marker testing for HER2 overexpression/amplification, important in breast and gastric cancer therapy selection.

    • Before / preparation

    • Verify timing, specimen type, anticoagulant/medication effects, fasting status when relevant, and whether a trend is more important than one isolated value.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Trend the result with symptoms, vital signs, assessment findings, medications, and baseline history. Escalate critical or rapidly changing values.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Critical values, sharp trend changes, results that do not fit the client's condition, specimen contamination, or a result that requires immediate treatment.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • HER2 status guides targeted therapy; it does not by itself diagnose cancer.

Category

Thyroid labs

Name

Free T4

Range

Often about 0.8-1.8 ng/dL, facility dependent.

Why

Free T4 reflects circulating thyroid hormone and helps confirm hypo/hyperthyroidism.

Aliases

  • thyroxine

  • FT4

High Causes

  • Hyperthyroidism

  • Thyroid hormone overreplacement

  • Thyroiditis

Low Causes

  • Hypothyroidism

  • Pituitary disease

  • Severe illness can affect values

Critical Concerns

  • Very high thyroid hormone with fever, tachycardia, agitation, or heart failure suggests thyroid storm.

Nursing Considerations

  • Check pulse, temperature, weight, bowel pattern, tremor, and medication adherence.

Nclex Traps

  • Levothyroxine is taken on an empty stomach and separated from calcium/iron.

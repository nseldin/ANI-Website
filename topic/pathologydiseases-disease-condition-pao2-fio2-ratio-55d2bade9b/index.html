Pronunciation

P-F RAY-shee-oh

Plain Meaning

A severity ratio comparing arterial oxygen level to the fraction of inspired oxygen being delivered.

Word Parts

  • Root

    PaO2

    Meaning

    partial pressure of oxygen in arterial blood

  • Root

    FiO2

    Meaning

    fraction of inspired oxygen

Word Origin

The ratio asks: how much oxygen got into arterial blood compared with how much oxygen we supplied?

Pathology

The PaO2/FiO2 ratio falls when lungs cannot transfer oxygen effectively despite increased oxygen delivery. In ARDS, it helps grade oxygenation impairment: lower ratios mean more severe gas-exchange failure when interpreted with PEEP/CPAP and imaging criteria.

Etiology

ARDS, pneumonia, pulmonary edema, atelectasis, aspiration, pulmonary contusion, and other acute hypoxemic respiratory failure patterns.

Signs Symptoms

  • The ratio itself is calculated from ABG and FiO2; clinical cues include hypoxemia, dyspnea, tachypnea, cyanosis, confusion, and ventilator/high-flow oxygen need

Diagnostics

  • ABG PaO2 divided by FiO2 expressed as a decimal

  • Example: PaO2 80 on FiO2 0.50 gives P/F 160

  • ARDS categories commonly use mild 201-300, moderate 101-200, severe <=100 in the right clinical context

Treatments

  • Treatment targets the cause and oxygenation strategy: PEEP, lung-protective ventilation, proning, fluid strategy, antibiotics/diuresis/source control as appropriate

Nursing Priorities

  • Know the FiO2 when interpreting PaO2; PaO2 of 80 is very different on room air than on 100% oxygen

  • Trend the ratio with respiratory effort, ventilator settings, hemodynamics, and chest imaging

  • Escalate falling ratio or severe hypoxemia despite support

Complications

  • ARDS severity progression

  • Respiratory failure

  • Hypoxic organ injury

Patient Education

  • This is usually an ICU/acute-care measure; explain that it tracks how effectively oxygen is crossing from lungs into blood.

Nclex Traps

  • Do not interpret PaO2 without knowing FiO2.

  • A lower P/F ratio means worse oxygen transfer.

  • P/F ratio is an oxygenation measure, not a direct CO2 ventilation measure.

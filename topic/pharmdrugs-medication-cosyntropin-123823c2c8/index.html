Used To Treat

ACTH stimulation testing to assess adrenal cortisol reserve and help evaluate adrenal insufficiency.

Mechanism

Cosyntropin mimics adrenocorticotropic hormone at adrenal melanocortin-2 receptors, activating steroidogenesis and cortisol release from the adrenal cortex. It is a diagnostic challenge, not chronic adrenal replacement.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for cosyntropin in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Cosyntropin is synthetic ACTH that stimulates adrenal melanocortin-2 receptors so clinicians can test whether the adrenal cortex can produce cortisol on demand. A poor cortisol rise after dosing suggests impaired adrenal reserve, so timing, baseline cortisol, recent steroids, acute illness, and specimen handling matter.

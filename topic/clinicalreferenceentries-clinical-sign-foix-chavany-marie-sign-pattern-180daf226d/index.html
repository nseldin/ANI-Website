Definition

Foix-Chavany-Marie sign pattern is a neurologic syndrome-sign. It is a clue to bilateral anterior opercular dysfunction. A positive finding means voluntary control is lost while automatic smiling, yawning, or crying may remain.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Foix-Chavany-Marie sign pattern means voluntary control is lost while automatic smiling, yawning, or crying may remain.

Why It Matters

The Foix-Chavany-Marie sign pattern pattern can signal bilateral anterior opercular dysfunction. Bilateral opercular corticobulbar disruption disconnects commanded movement while limbic and extrapyramidal pathways persist. Foix-Chavany-Marie sign pattern cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Assessment Method

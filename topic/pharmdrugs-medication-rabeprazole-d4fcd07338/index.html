No universal boxed warning; long-term risks include C. difficile, hypomagnesemia, fractures, B12 deficiency, and kidney concerns.

Mechanism

Rabeprazole works by irreversibly inhibiting gastric proton pumps, strongly reducing acid secretion. Long-term safety cues include C. difficile risk, magnesium, B12, fractures, and pneumonia risk.

Nursing Essentials

  • Give before meals when ordered.

  • Monitor magnesium/B12/renal issues with long-term use.

  • Assess for GI bleeding alarm symptoms.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity

  • some agents interact with rilpivirine-containing products

Interactions

  • Clopidogrel activation may be reduced by omeprazole/esomeprazole.

  • pH-dependent drugs may be affected.

Key Labs

  • CBC if bleeding

  • B12 if long-term

Nclex Traps

  • Long-term PPI is not harmless; watch low magnesium and C. diff diarrhea.

Description

Rabeprazole is a proton pump inhibitor (PPI): it works by irreversibly inhibiting gastric proton pumps, strongly reducing acid secretion. It is used for GERD, erosive esophagitis, peptic ulcer disease, H. pylori regimens, GI bleed risk reduction, and hypersecretory conditions.

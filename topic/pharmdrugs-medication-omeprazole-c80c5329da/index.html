Used To Treat

Nausea and vomiting due to surgery/anesthesia, chemotherapy, migraine, vestibular disorders, pregnancy, gastroparesis, or GI illness depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Omeprazole works by irreversibly inhibiting gastric proton pumps, strongly reducing acid secretion. Long-term safety cues include C. difficile risk, magnesium, B12, fractures, and pneumonia risk.

Boxed Warning

No boxed warning commonly emphasized; C. difficile risk, hypomagnesemia, fractures, B12 deficiency, and kidney injury warnings matter with long-term use.

Contraindications

  • Known hypersensitivity to PPIs.

  • Avoid/clarify with clopidogrel when antiplatelet effect is critical.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Give before meals when scheduled.

  • Monitor long-term magnesium/B12 concerns and diarrhea.

  • Review need for ongoing therapy.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Clopidogrel activation can be reduced by omeprazole.

  • Warfarin, diazepam, phenytoin interactions may occur.

  • Drugs needing acidic pH may absorb differently.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • magnesium if long-term

  • B12 if long-term symptoms

  • creatinine if kidney concern

  • QT risk, sedation, EPS, anticholinergic effects, bowel obstruction concern, hydration, and serotonin syndrome risk when relevant.

Nclex Traps

  • Chronic watery diarrhea on acid suppression can be C. difficile.

  • Different antiemetics have different dangers: ondansetron/QT, metoclopramide/EPS-tardive dyskinesia, promethazine/sedation-tissue injury.

Description

Omeprazole is a proton pump inhibitor: it works by irreversibly inhibiting gastric proton pumps, strongly reducing acid secretion. It is used for bowel symptom control, constipation relief, bowel preparation, secretion thinning, or diarrhea management depending on the specific agent and contraindications.

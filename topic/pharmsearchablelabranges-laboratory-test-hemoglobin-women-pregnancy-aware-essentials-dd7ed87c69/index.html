Name

Hemoglobin

Range

Adult female about 12-16 g/dL; pregnant female anemia thresholds: <11 g/dL in 1st trimester, <10.5 g/dL in 2nd trimester, <11 g/dL in 3rd trimester

Why

Pregnancy hemodilution lowers hemoglobin. Example: Hgb 10.8 g/dL may be mild/expected in 2nd trimester, but Hgb 9.5 g/dL with dyspnea, tachycardia, bleeding, or poor fetal tolerance is not a shrug-off finding.

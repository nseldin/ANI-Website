    Interpretation

  • Content

    A staining pattern is rarely a species identification, and mixed flora, low burden, artifacts, or poor specimens can mislead. Culture, molecular, antigen, serologic, or histopathologic methods may still be required.

    Potentially infectious material must be prepared and discarded under the laboratory's containment procedures; stained appearance does not prove an organism is nonviable.

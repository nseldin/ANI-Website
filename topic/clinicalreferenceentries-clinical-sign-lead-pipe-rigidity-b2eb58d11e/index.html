Definition

Lead-pipe rigidity is an observed tone sign. It is a clue to parkinsonism. A positive finding means smooth, uniform resistance persists in both directions.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Lead-pipe rigidity means smooth, uniform resistance persists in both directions.

Why It Matters

The observed Lead-pipe rigidity finding can signal parkinsonism. Basal-ganglia dysfunction increases tonic muscle activity without the velocity dependence of spasticity. Lead-pipe rigidity cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • parkinsonism

Assessment Method

Passively move a relaxed limb slowly through its range.

Positive Finding

Smooth, uniform resistance persists in both directions.

Mechanism

Basal-ganglia dysfunction increases tonic muscle activity without the velocity dependence of spasticity.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Lead-pipe rigidity finding raises concern for parkinsonism because basal-ganglia dysfunction increases tonic muscle activity without the velocity dependence of spasticity. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Lead-pipe rigidity does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Lead-pipe rigidity to focus evaluation of parkinsonism and the reviewed alternatives: spasticity, contracture, gegenhalten. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Passively move a relaxed limb slowly through its range. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Lead-pipe rigidity as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Guarding and paratonia can resemble uniform resistance. Therefore, Lead-pipe rigidity cannot confirm or exclude parkinsonism by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • spasticity

  • contracture

  • gegenhalten

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as spasticity, contracture, gegenhalten.

False Negative Situations

Lead-pipe rigidity may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of parkinsonism.

Mechanism

Zolpidem binds the benzodiazepine site of GABA-A receptors with relative alpha-1 preference, increasing inhibitory signaling that promotes sleep. The same CNS depression explains amnesia, impaired driving, falls, and dangerous behaviors while not fully awake.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for zolpidem in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Zolpidem is a Z-drug hypnotic that promotes sleep through GABA-A receptor modulation. It can cause next-day impairment, falls, dependence, additive CNS depression, and complex sleep behaviors such as sleep-driving, so safe bedtime conditions and avoiding alcohol/CNS depressant stacking are central.

Definition

Adherent pharyngeal pseudomembrane is an observed mucosal sign. It is a clue to respiratory diphtheria or other necrotizing pharyngitis. A positive finding means a gray-white firmly adherent membrane bleeds when disturbed.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Adherent pharyngeal pseudomembrane means a gray-white firmly adherent membrane bleeds when disturbed.

Why It Matters

The observed Adherent pharyngeal pseudomembrane finding can signal respiratory diphtheria or other necrotizing pharyngitis. Toxin-induced epithelial necrosis combines with fibrin, inflammatory cells, and bacteria to form an adherent layer. Adherent pharyngeal pseudomembrane cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

  • respiratory diphtheria or other necrotizing pharyngitis

Definition

Vesicle means a small, circumscribed, fluid-filled blister. It differs from a solid papule and from a larger fluid-filled bulla.

Plain Language

A vesicle is a small blister containing clear or blood-tinged fluid.

Why It Matters

Vesicle distribution, pain, mucosal involvement, medication timing, and systemic findings help distinguish infection, contact reactions, autoimmune blistering, and injury. Widespread painful blistering or mouth, eye, or genital involvement can be an emergency.

Associated Conditions

  • a small fluid-filled space created within or beneath the epidermis by infection, inflammation, contact reactions, autoimmune injury, burns, friction, or other skin damage

Assessment Method

Inspect without unnecessary rupture, document size, number, grouping, distribution, surrounding redness, pain or itch, fluid appearance, crusting, dermatomal or mucosal involvement, recent medications and exposures, fever, and infection-control risk.

Positive Finding

A small, circumscribed, elevated blister containing clear, serous, or blood-tinged fluid is present; it differs from a solid papule and from the larger fluid-filled lesion called a bulla.

Mechanism

Injury or inflammation separates layers of epidermis or the epidermis from underlying tissue, and fluid accumulates in the newly formed space.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Vesicle finding raises concern for a small fluid-filled space created within or beneath the epidermis by infection, inflammation, contact reactions, autoimmune injury, burns, friction, or other skin damage because injury or inflammation separates layers of epidermis or the epidermis from underlying tissue, and fluid accumulates in the newly formed space. Evidence Limitations

Fragile vesicles may rupture before examination and resemble erosions or crusts, size terminology overlaps, and fluid appearance alone does not prove or exclude infection. A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as papule, pustule, bulla, friction blister, erosion.

False Negative Situations

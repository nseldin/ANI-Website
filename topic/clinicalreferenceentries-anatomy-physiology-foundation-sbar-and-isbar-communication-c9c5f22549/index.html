Quick Answer

Lead with identity and the immediate concern, then provide only decision-relevant background, state the assessment clearly, and close with an explicit recommendation, request, or read-back.

Sections

  • Label

    Why structure improves safety

    Text

    A predictable order reduces omissions and forces the speaker to separate the current problem from old history. It also makes the requested decision visible instead of burying it at the end of a long narrative.

  • Label

    Preparation

    Text

    Before calling, assess the patient, gather current vital signs and relevant trends, review allergies, medicines, recent results, orders, code status when relevant, and know what help is needed. Do not delay an emergency escalation to collect nonessential details.

  • Label

    Closed-loop communication

    Text

    Repeat back critical orders, clarify ambiguous language, document the notification and response, and escalate through the chain of command when deterioration continues or the response is unsafe.

  • Label

    What SBAR cannot do

    Text

    A template cannot replace clinical judgment or a complete transfer record. It must be adapted to the urgency, setting, patient, and local handoff policy.

Summary

SBAR and ISBAR organize urgent or routine clinical communication so the receiver quickly understands who is involved, what changed, the relevant context, the nurse's assessment, and the requested next step.

Class

Inhaled dual phosphodiesterase-3/phosphodiesterase-4 inhibitor

Used To Treat

Twice-daily maintenance treatment of COPD in adults by oral inhalation through a standard jet nebulizer; it is not a rescue bronchodilator for acute bronchospasm.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current OHTUVAYRE labeling has no boxed warning. It is not for acute symptoms; use a short-acting bronchodilator for rescue. Discontinue and treat paradoxical bronchospasm. Psychiatric adverse reactions, including suicidality, have occurred; weigh risks in patients with depression or suicidal history and promptly assess new mood, behavior or thought changes. Hypersensitivity is a contraindication.

Mechanism

PDE3 hydrolyzes cAMP and can hydrolyze cGMP; PDE4 selectively hydrolyzes cAMP. Inhibiting both enzymes allows intracellular cAMP/cGMP to accumulate. In airway smooth muscle, protein-kinase signaling lowers contractile tone; in inflammatory cells, higher cAMP suppresses mediator release and cellular activation. The label establishes dual enzyme inhibition but does not assign each clinical outcome to one isolated downstream pathway.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm maintenance versus rescue plan, COPD symptoms/exacerbations, inhaler and nebulizer technique, depression/suicide history, hepatic function and all other maintenance bronchodilators.

  • Shake the ampule vigorously, empty it into a standard jet nebulizer with mouthpiece, administer morning and evening, and discard residual product. A sudden breathing decline needs rescue therapy and assessment, not another maintenance dose.

Key Labs

  • Clinical dyspnea, wheeze, sputum and exacerbation frequency, rescue-inhaler use, spirometry when indicated, oxygenation, mood/suicide screening and hepatic assessment in moderate/severe impairment.

Nclex Traps

  • Ensifentrine's PDE3 component contributes bronchodilation and PDE4 contributes anti-inflammatory signaling; it is not an inhaled corticosteroid.

  • Maintenance nebulization does not make it an emergency bronchodilator.

Description

Ensifentrine is an inhaled dual phosphodiesterase-3/phosphodiesterase-4 inhibitor that allows cyclic nucleotides to accumulate, relaxing airway smooth muscle while reducing selected inflammatory-cell signaling during COPD maintenance therapy. PDE3 inhibition contributes bronchodilation, while PDE4 inhibition raises cAMP in inflammatory cells. The product is nebulized twice daily and is not a rescue bronchodilator, injection or oral drug.

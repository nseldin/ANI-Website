Mechanism

Isotretinoin works by altering retinoic-acid receptor signaling, which changes epithelial differentiation, keratinization, sebaceous activity, and cell maturation. The same nuclear-receptor developmental signaling explains severe teratogenic risk and why pregnancy prevention, lipid/liver monitoring, skin/mucosal effects, and mood symptoms matter.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warning: severe birth defects, pregnancy loss, premature birth, and infant death can occur if pregnancy happens during therapy.

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy.

  • Clients who can become pregnant must meet pregnancy-prevention requirements.

  • Hypersensitivity to isotretinoin or product components.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Pregnancy is a hard stop unless pregnancy-prevention requirements are met.

  • Verify required pregnancy testing and contraception documentation.

  • Monitor mood changes, severe headache/vision changes, lipids, and liver enzymes.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Vitamin A supplements increase toxicity risk.

  • Tetracyclines can increase intracranial hypertension risk.

  • Alcohol can worsen lipid/liver concerns.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • pregnancy test

  • AST/ALT

  • triglycerides

  • Use medication-specific labs when ordered. For high-risk drugs, connect renal function, liver function, electrolytes, CBC, coagulation studies, drug levels, ECG, and vital-sign trends to safety.

Nclex Traps

  • Do not treat isotretinoin as routine acne teaching. Pregnancy prevention is the safety priority.

  • The NCLEX trap is memorizing the name without connecting class, contraindication, assessment, and priority adverse effect.

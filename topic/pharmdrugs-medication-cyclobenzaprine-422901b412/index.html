Mechanism

Cyclobenzaprine acts in the CNS to reduce tonic somatic motor activity; it does not repair the muscle injury. TCA-like anticholinergic effects, sedation, falls, serotonin-risk combinations, and cardiac conduction risk are key.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for cyclobenzaprine in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Cyclobenzaprine is a centrally acting skeletal muscle relaxant and TCA-like structure: it acts in the CNS to reduce tonic somatic motor activity; it does not repair the muscle injury. It is used for short-term treatment of acute painful musculoskeletal spasm with rest and physical therapy.

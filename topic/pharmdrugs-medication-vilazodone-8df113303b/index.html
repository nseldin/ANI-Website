Class

SSRI and 5-HT1A partial agonist antidepressant

Used To Treat

Major depressive disorder in adults.

Boxed Warning

Suicidal thoughts and behaviors: antidepressants increase risk in pediatric and young adult patients. Monitor for clinical worsening or emergent suicidality, especially during initiation and dose changes; vilazodone is not approved for pediatric use.

Mechanism

Vilazodone inhibits the serotonin transporter and partially agonizes 5-HT1A receptors, increasing and modulating serotonergic transmission. The independent contribution of 5-HT1A activity to antidepressant benefit is unknown.

Nursing Essentials

  • Give with food and follow titration; assess suicidality, bipolar/mania history, serotonergic combinations, bleeding-risk drugs, sodium/fall risk, diarrhea, nausea, and sexual effects.

  • Observe MAOI washout and taper when possible; monitor clonus, hyperreflexia, fever, agitation, and autonomic instability for serotonin syndrome.

Key Labs

  • Sodium when at risk, pregnancy context, bleeding/CBC when clinically indicated, and symptom/suicide assessment.

Nclex Traps

  • Vilazodone exposure falls without food; a correctly swallowed fasting dose may still act like an underdose.

Description

Vilazodone combines selective serotonin-reuptake inhibition with 5-HT1A partial agonism. It must be taken with food for reliable absorption and titrated to reduce GI intolerance; diarrhea/nausea are common, while suicidality, serotonin syndrome, bleeding, hyponatremia, mania, and sexual dysfunction are serious teaching anchors.

Used To Treat

Schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, acute agitation/psychosis, treatment-resistant depression adjunct therapy, or selected nausea/tic indications depending on the medication.

Mechanism

Lurasidone works by modulating dopamine pathways, often through D2 blockade, with many agents also affecting serotonin, histamine, alpha, or muscarinic receptors. That is why EPS, NMS, QT, metabolic, and sedation monitoring differ by drug.

Boxed Warning

Class boxed warning: increased mortality in elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis.

Contraindications

  • Dementia-related psychosis unless benefits outweigh risks

  • clarify significant QT prolongation or severe CNS depression

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor EPS, tardive dyskinesia, neuroleptic malignant syndrome, metabolic effects, orthostasis, QT prolongation.

  • NMS: fever, rigidity, altered mental status, autonomic instability.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

  • Subclass signal: lurasidone is an SGA often remembered for bipolar depression use and comparatively lower metabolic burden, but EPS/akathisia can still occur.

Interactions

  • QT-prolonging drugs, CNS depressants, antihypertensives, anticholinergics.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • glucose/A1c

  • lipids

  • CBC for clozapine

  • ECG/QTc when indicated

  • EPS, neuroleptic malignant syndrome cues, QT risk, sedation, orthostasis, glucose/lipids/weight, ANC for clozapine, and suicidality/adherence.

Nclex Traps

  • NMS is not ordinary anxiety: high fever + rigidity + autonomic instability is emergency.

  • Fever, rigidity, altered mental status, autonomic instability, and elevated CK point toward neuroleptic malignant syndrome.

Description

Lurasidone is a second-generation atypical antipsychotic and lower metabolic-risk SGA: it works by modulating dopamine pathways, often through D2 blockade, with many agents also affecting serotonin, histamine, alpha, or muscarinic receptors. It is used for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, acute agitation/psychosis, and selected adjunct mood or nausea/tic indications depending on the specific medication.

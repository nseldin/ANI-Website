Used To Treat

Susceptible infections based on organism, infection site, cultures, allergies, renal function, and local resistance patterns.

Mechanism

Daptomycin works by inserting into gram-positive bacterial membranes in a calcium-dependent way, causing rapid membrane depolarization and cell death. It is not used for pneumonia because pulmonary surfactant inactivates it; CPK, myopathy, eosinophilic pneumonia symptoms, and renal dosing matter.

Description

Daptomycin is a lipopeptide membrane depolarizing antibiotic: it works by inserting into gram-positive bacterial membranes in a calcium-dependent way, causing rapid membrane depolarization and cell death. It is used for serious resistant gram-positive bloodstream, skin/soft tissue, or right-sided endocarditis contexts when susceptibility fits; not pneumonia because surfactant inactivates it.

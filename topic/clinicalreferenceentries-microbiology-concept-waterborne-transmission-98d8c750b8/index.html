    Waterborne transmission is exposure to an infectious agent through contaminated drinking, recreational, environmental, or healthcare water.

  • Content

    People may ingest water, inhale aerosols, aspirate it, expose eyes or damaged skin, or acquire organisms from moist devices and surfaces. Water systems can act as both route and reservoir.

    Cryptosporidium may follow ingestion, Legionella follows inhalation or aspiration of contaminated aerosols, and Pseudomonas can exploit moist healthcare reservoirs.

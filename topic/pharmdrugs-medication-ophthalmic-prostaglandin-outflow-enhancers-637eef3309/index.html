Used To Treat

Once-daily reduction of elevated intraocular pressure in open-angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension according to the exact product and patient.

Description

Ophthalmic prostaglandin-pathway drugs are common first-line pressure-lowering drops because they enhance aqueous outflow with little systemic cardiopulmonary effect. Latanoprost, travoprost and tafluprost are FP-receptor prodrugs; bimatoprost is a prostamide analog with FP activity; latanoprostene bunod also releases nitric oxide to relax trabecular outflow tissue.

Mechanism

FP-receptor signaling remodels extracellular matrix in the ciliary muscle and increases uveoscleral drainage. The class can also alter melanocyte melanin content and hair-follicle cycling, explaining permanent iris darkening and longer, thicker lashes. More frequent dosing can paradoxically reduce pressure-lowering efficacy, so one drop once daily is not an arbitrary convenience.

Boxed Warning

No class boxed warning. High-yield risks are permanent iris pigmentation, eyelid darkening, eyelash growth/misdirection, periorbital fat atrophy/deepened lid sulcus, conjunctival hyperemia, uveitis or herpetic-keratitis reactivation, and cystoid macular edema in susceptible aphakic/pseudophakic eyes.

Nursing Essentials

  • Document baseline iris color, lens/capsule status, macular-edema risk, uveitis or herpetic-keratitis history, and adherence. Avoid letting overflow repeatedly contact eyelid skin.

  • Verify the exact eye, formulation, concentration, frequency, contact-lens instructions, and whether other drops must be separated by at least 5 minutes. Never let the bottle tip touch the eye, lashes, fingers, or another surface.

  • After the drop, gently close the eyelid and press the nasolacrimal duct for 1 to 2 minutes when appropriate. This reduces drainage into the nose and systemic exposure; wiping excess from the skin also limits local irritation.

Key Labs

  • Intraocular pressure, optic nerve and visual field; ocular examination for pigmentation, lash direction, inflammation, corneal disease and macular edema when vision changes occur.

Nclex Traps

  • Iris darkening may be permanent even after the drug is stopped.

  • Using the drop more often than prescribed can reduce rather than improve the pressure response.

  • These drugs increase outflow; they do not primarily suppress aqueous production.

Class

Irreversible epoxyketone 20S proteasome inhibitor

Used To Treat

Adults with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma in approved single-agent or combination regimens after the required prior therapy.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: no boxed warning is listed. Cardiac arrest and fatal heart failure can occur within a day of dosing. Optimize BP and volume status, monitor for ischemia/failure and severe dyspnea, and hold for serious cardiac, pulmonary, renal, hypertensive, TMA, or PRES toxicity.

Mechanism

Carfilzomib covalently and irreversibly binds N-terminal threonine-containing active sites, strongly suppressing beta-5 chymotrypsin-like proteasome activity. Ubiquitinated proteins accumulate, survival signaling and protein homeostasis fail, and highly secretory plasma cells undergo apoptosis for at least 48 hours after inhibition. Irreversible beta-5 proteasome blockade makes ubiquitinated and misfolded proteins accumulate, thereby activating the unfolded-protein response, oxidative stress, and apoptosis in plasma cells that already produce large immunoglobulin loads. The same irreversible stress can injure endothelium and strain cardiac, renal, and pulmonary reserve, which explains why dyspnea, rapid weight gain, hypertension, chest symptoms, or creatinine rise requires immediate assessment rather than routine hydration. Because both underhydration and fluid overload can be dangerous, volume support must follow the regimen and the patient's current hemodynamics.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess weight, edema, JVD, lung sounds, BP, oxygenation, chest pain, rhythm, urine output, and renal function before and after dosing; hydration must balance TLS/renal protection against heart-failure risk.

  • Verify dexamethasone premedication and infusion timing; monitor infusion reactions, thrombosis prophylaxis in combinations, bleeding, platelet nadir, and neurologic symptoms.

Key Labs

  • BP, weight/volume assessment, CBC/platelets, creatinine/eGFR, electrolytes and TLS labs, liver tests, ECG and cardiac biomarkers/imaging when indicated.

Nclex Traps

  • Carfilzomib is the irreversible proteasome inhibitor with prominent cardiac and hypertensive toxicity; bortezomib is more strongly associated with neuropathy.

  • More IV fluid is not always safer when a proteasome regimen can precipitate heart failure.

Description

Carfilzomib is an IV epoxyketone proteasome inhibitor that irreversibly binds active-site threonine in the 20S core, producing sustained proteotoxic stress and apoptosis in myeloma cells. Its defining clinical hazard is cardiopulmonary and vascular toxicity: heart failure, ischemia, dysrhythmia, hypertension, pulmonary hypertension, dyspnea, renal failure, and fluid-sensitive decompensation can occur alongside TLS, thrombosis, TMA, PRES, and cytopenias.

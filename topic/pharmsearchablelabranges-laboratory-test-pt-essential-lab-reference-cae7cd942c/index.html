Category

Coagulation

Name

PT

Range

Often about 11-13.5 seconds. Facility ranges vary. INR standardizes warfarin monitoring.

Why

PT checks the extrinsic and common coagulation pathways. It rises with warfarin effect, vitamin K deficiency, liver dysfunction, DIC, and factor deficiencies.

Aliases

  • prothrombin time

High Causes

  • Warfarin therapy or toxicity

  • Vitamin K deficiency

  • Liver disease

  • DIC

  • Massive transfusion or clotting factor deficiency

Low Causes

  • Short PT is usually less clinically important than prolonged PT.

Critical Concerns

  • Bleeding, neuro changes after a fall, GI bleeding, or very high INR requires rapid escalation.

Nursing Considerations

  • Assess bleeding, bruising, stool/urine blood, medication interactions, diet consistency with warfarin, and fall risk.

Nclex Traps

  • PT is related to warfarin, but NCLEX usually asks about INR for warfarin safety. Heparin is commonly tied to aPTT or anti-Xa.

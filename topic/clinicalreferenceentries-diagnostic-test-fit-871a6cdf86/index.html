Summary

FIT (fecal immunochemical test) is a high-yield GI diagnostic reference. Immunochemical stool blood test used for colorectal cancer screening.

Quick Answer

FIT (fecal immunochemical test): Immunochemical stool blood test used for colorectal cancer screening. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Negative

    Meaning

    No human hemoglobin is detected above the assay cutoff in that stool sample.

  • Label

    Positive

    Meaning

    Human blood is detected in stool, requiring follow-up evaluation for colorectal cancer, polyps, or lower GI bleeding sources.

  • Label

    Clinical significance

    Meaning

    FIT is more specific to lower GI human blood than guaiac FOBT, but a positive result is not a cancer diagnosis.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Immunochemical stool blood test used for colorectal cancer screening.

    • Before / preparation

    • Confirm the purpose of screening, age/risk eligibility, prep requirements, and whether abnormal results require diagnostic confirmation.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Teach that screening estimates risk; it does not always diagnose. Promote follow-up and reduce fear with clear next steps.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Positive screening with symptoms, high-risk history, severe abnormality, or failure to follow up on a positive result.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Do not treat a screen as a final diagnosis unless the test is designed and interpreted that way.

Summary

Esophageal pH monitoring is a high-yield GI diagnostic reference. Measures acid exposure in the esophagus over time to evaluate GERD or reflux-related symptoms.

Quick Answer

Esophageal pH monitoring: Measures acid exposure in the esophagus over time to evaluate GERD or reflux-related symptoms. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

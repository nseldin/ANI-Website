Mechanism

Nizatidine works by blocking histamine H2 receptors on parietal cells, lowering gastric acid. Confusion risk in older adults and renal dosing are classic nursing angles.

Nursing Essentials

Description

Nizatidine is a H2 receptor blocker: it works by blocking histamine H2 receptors on parietal cells, lowering gastric acid. It is used for GERD, heartburn, peptic ulcer disease, stress-ulcer prophylaxis in selected clients, and histamine-mediated symptoms when clinically indicated.

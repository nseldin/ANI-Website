Used To Treat

Inflammation, asthma/COPD exacerbations, allergic reactions, autoimmune disease, adrenal replacement, cerebral edema, fetal lung maturity, or oncology/transplant protocols depending on the drug.

Mechanism

Mometasone works by diffusing into cells and activating cytosolic glucocorticoid receptors that move to the nucleus, change transcription, suppress phospholipase A2/COX-2/cytokine signaling, and reduce inflammatory cell trafficking. Benefit is less inflammation/immune activation; risks are hyperglycemia, infection masking, adrenal suppression, mood changes, myopathy, bone loss, skin thinning, and route-specific effects such as oral thrush or ocular pressure.

Contraindications

  • Known serious hypersensitivity to the medication/class.

Used To Treat

Adult insomnia characterized by difficulty with sleep onset, sleep maintenance, or both, using product-specific bedtime doses with at least 7 hours available before planned awakening.

Description

Dual orexin receptor antagonists reduce wake drive by competitively blocking orexin A and orexin B signaling at OX1R and OX2R. Unlike GABAergic sedative-hypnotics, they dampen a wake-promoting network rather than globally enhancing inhibitory GABA signaling, but they still cause CNS depression, next-day impairment, falls, and additive toxicity with alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines, and other sedatives.

Mechanism

Lateral hypothalamic orexin neurons stabilize wakefulness and suppress inappropriate transitions into REM sleep. Blocking both receptors permits sleep onset and maintenance; the same pathway explains sleep paralysis, hypnagogic or hypnopompic hallucinations, and cataplexy-like weakness. Because orexin signaling is deficient in narcolepsy, all three products are contraindicated in narcolepsy.

Boxed Warning

Current DORAs have no boxed warning. Product labels warn about CNS depression and driving impairment, worsening depression/suicidality, complex sleep behaviors such as sleep-driving, sleep paralysis/hallucinations/cataplexy-like symptoms, respiratory-risk uncertainty, CYP3A interactions, abuse potential, and use only when a full sleep period remains.

Nursing Essentials

  • Before the first dose assess narcolepsy, sleep apnea/COPD, depression/suicide risk, falls, substance use, other CNS depressants, CYP3A inhibitors/inducers, bedtime timing, and ability to remain in bed at least 7 hours.

  • Discontinue and contact the prescriber after any complex sleep behavior; the patient's lack of memory does not make the event benign.

Key Labs

  • No routine serum level. Monitor daytime alertness, driving safety, falls, respiratory symptoms, mood/suicidality, unusual REM-transition phenomena, adherence, and drug interactions.

Nclex Traps

  • These drugs are contraindicated in narcolepsy because they suppress an already deficient orexin wake system.

  • Seven hours available for sleep does not guarantee unimpaired driving the next day, especially at higher doses or with interacting depressants.

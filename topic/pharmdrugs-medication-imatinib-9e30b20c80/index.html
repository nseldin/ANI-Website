Used To Treat

Targeted cancer therapy or selected inflammatory/immune conditions when the medication matches the disease-driving kinase pathway.

Mechanism

Blocks the specific kinase pathway targeted by that medication, which can slow tumor growth or selected immune/inflammatory signaling.

Boxed Warning

Warnings are product-specific; hepatotoxicity, QT prolongation, myelosuppression, bleeding, thrombosis, hypertension, and embryo-fetal toxicity are common themes.

Contraindications

  • Pregnancy risk requires clarification

  • serious hypersensitivity

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor CBC, LFTs, BP, QT risk, bleeding/infection, pregnancy prevention.

  • Check interactions before giving.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

  • Subclass specificity: Imatinib is categorized here as BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase inhibitor. Pathway: Oncology/targeted therapy > Kinase inhibitor > BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase inhibitor. This subtype shapes the expected mechanism, contraindications, boxed/high-alert warnings, monitoring, interactions, and patient teaching.

Interactions

  • Strong CYP inhibitors/inducers, QT-prolonging drugs, and anticoagulants may matter for the specific agent.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • CBC

  • AST/ALT

  • bilirubin

  • electrolytes

  • ECG/QTc when indicated

  • Use medication-specific labs when ordered. For high-risk drugs, connect renal function, liver function, electrolytes, CBC, coagulation studies, drug levels, ECG, and vital-sign trends to safety.

Nclex Traps

  • Oral chemo still has chemo-level safety needs; it is not 'just a pill.'

  • The NCLEX trap is memorizing the name without connecting class, contraindication, assessment, and priority adverse effect.

Description

Imatinib is a BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase inhibitor: it blocks the specific kinase pathway targeted by that medication, which can slow tumor growth or selected immune/inflammatory signaling. It is used for targeted cancer therapy or selected inflammatory/immune conditions when the medication matches the disease-driving kinase pathway.

Class

Long-acting IM INSTI plus NNRTI complete HIV-1 maintenance regimen

Used To Treat

Replace a stable antiretroviral regimen in virologically suppressed adults and adolescents age 12 years or older weighing at least 35 kg who have no treatment-failure history and no known or suspected cabotegravir or rilpivirine resistance.

Description

Cabotegravir-rilpivirine is a complete long-acting HIV-1 maintenance regimen that combines an integrase strand-transfer inhibitor with a nonnucleoside reverse-transcriptase inhibitor, blocking viral DNA integration and new viral-DNA synthesis at two sequential replication steps. CABENUVA is given as two separate gluteal IM injections monthly or every two months. It is for carefully selected virologically suppressed patients, not routine treatment of uncontrolled viremia.

Mechanism

Cabotegravir chelates catalytic magnesium at the HIV integrase active site and prevents viral DNA strand transfer into the host genome. Rilpivirine binds an allosteric pocket in HIV-1 reverse transcriptase and distorts polymerase activity. Nanocrystal depots release both drugs for months, which supports infrequent dosing but creates a pharmacologic tail: subtherapeutic single-drug exposure can persist after missed or stopped injections and select resistance.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current CABENUVA labeling has no boxed warning. Serious hypersensitivity, hepatotoxicity, depressive disorders and rare severe post-injection reactions occur. Strong UGT1A1/UGT1A9 or CYP3A inducers are contraindicated. Long-acting concentrations can remain for 12 months or longer; missed doses require oral bridging or reinitiation, and a fully suppressive alternative regimen must begin no later than the next scheduled injection window after discontinuation to prevent virologic failure and resistance.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify HIV RNA below 50, resistance and failure history, HBV treatment needs, pregnancy, liver status, interacting drugs, oral lead-in choice and exact monthly/every-two-month schedule before injection.

  • Administer cabotegravir and rilpivirine as separate deep gluteal IM injections at the same visit; observe for post-injection distress and create a written missed-dose/bridging plan before the patient leaves.

Key Labs

  • HIV RNA, CD4 count, resistance history, HBV status, AST/ALT/bilirubin, pregnancy assessment and symptom screening for depression or hypersensitivity; check viral load after missed injections or suspected failure.

Nclex Traps

  • CABENUVA is a complete two-drug maintenance regimen, but it has no HBV activity; stopping tenofovir-containing therapy without an HBV plan can reactivate hepatitis B.

  • The long tail is a liability as well as a convenience: stopping injections without timely suppressive ART can create functional monotherapy and resistance.

Definition

Transillumination sign of scrotal swelling is a bedside light test. It is a clue to fluid-filled versus solid scrotal mass. A positive finding means a fluid-filled swelling glows while most solid masses do not.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Transillumination sign of scrotal swelling means a fluid-filled swelling glows while most solid masses do not.

Why It Matters

A positive Transillumination sign of scrotal swelling result can signal fluid-filled versus solid scrotal mass. Clear serous fluid transmits light more uniformly than tissue, blood, or bowel. Transillumination sign of scrotal swelling cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • fluid-filled versus solid scrotal mass

Assessment Method

In a dark room, place a cool light behind the swelling without pressure.

Positive Finding

A fluid-filled swelling glows while most solid masses do not.

Mechanism

Clear serous fluid transmits light more uniformly than tissue, blood, or bowel.

Definition

Cannon a waves is an observed jugular waveform sign. It is a clue to atrioventricular dissociation. A positive finding means intermittent giant a waves appear when the atrium contracts against a closed tricuspid valve.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Cannon a waves means intermittent giant a waves appear when the atrium contracts against a closed tricuspid valve.

Why It Matters

The observed Cannon a waves finding can signal atrioventricular dissociation. Independent atrial contraction occasionally coincides with ventricular systole, sharply raising right-atrial pressure. Cannon a waves cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

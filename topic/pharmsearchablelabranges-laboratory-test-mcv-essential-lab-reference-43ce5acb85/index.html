Category

Hematology

Name

MCV

Range

80-100 fL

Why

MCV sorts anemia by red-cell size: microcytic, normocytic, or macrocytic.

Aliases

  • mean corpuscular volume

High Causes

  • Vitamin B12 deficiency

  • Folate deficiency

  • Alcohol use disorder

  • Liver disease

  • Some medications

Low Causes

  • Iron deficiency

  • Thalassemia

  • Anemia of chronic disease can be low or normal

Critical Concerns

  • Macrocytosis with neuro symptoms points toward B12 deficiency rather than folate alone.

Nursing Considerations

  • Link MCV to iron studies, B12, folate, reticulocyte count, and bleeding history.

Nclex Traps

  • Folate may improve anemia in B12 deficiency but will not fix neurologic injury.

Used To Treat

Nausea/vomiting, motion sickness prevention, allergy symptoms, and selected sedation adjunct uses when ordered.

Mechanism

Blocks H1 histamine receptors and has anticholinergic/phenothiazine effects in vestibular and vomiting pathways, reducing nausea while producing strong sedation and anticholinergic adverse effects.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess respiratory status, sedation, fall risk, anticholinergic burden, tissue injury risk with injectable use, pediatric age, and concurrent CNS depressants.

  • Use extreme caution with route/concentration/site per policy because severe tissue injury can occur with injection exposure.

Contraindications

  • Contraindicated in children under 2 years due to fatal respiratory depression risk. Clarify severe CNS/respiratory depression, coma, lower-respiratory symptoms in children, or phenothiazine hypersensitivity.

Interactions

  • Opioids, benzodiazepines, alcohol, sleep medicines, and other anticholinergics increase sedation, respiratory depression, and confusion risk.

Key Labs

  • No routine lab; monitor respiratory rate, oxygenation, sedation scale, BP, and injection site when applicable.

Nclex Traps

  • Promethazine is sedating and anticholinergic. Pediatric respiratory warning and tissue injury with injectable exposure are bigger safety hooks than simply remembering it treats nausea.

Description

Promethazine is a phenothiazine H1 antihistamine antiemetic with anticholinergic and sedating effects: it blocks H1 histamine receptors and has anticholinergic/phenothiazine effects in vestibular and vomiting pathways, reducing nausea while producing strong sedation and anticholinergic adverse effects. It is used for nausea/vomiting, motion sickness prevention, allergy symptoms, and selected sedation adjunct uses when ordered.

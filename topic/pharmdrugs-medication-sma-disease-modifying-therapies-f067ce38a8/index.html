Used To Treat

5q spinal muscular atrophy caused by biallelic SMN1 dysfunction, using age-, route-, genotype-, prior-treatment-, and product-specific regimens.

Description

SMA disease-modifying therapies increase functional survival motor neuron (SMN) protein by three different strategies. Intrathecal nusinersen alters SMN2 pre-mRNA splicing, oral risdiplam alters SMN2 splicing systemically, and onasemnogene abeparvovec delivers a functional SMN transgene through an AAV9 vector. Earlier treatment preserves more motor units because none of these approaches resurrects motor neurons already lost.

Mechanism

SMN1 loss leaves SMN2 as an imperfect backup because exon 7 is usually skipped, producing unstable protein. Nusinersen binds an intronic splice-silencing region and risdiplam stabilizes splice-complex recognition so exon 7 is retained and full-length SMN rises. Onasemnogene supplies episomal DNA encoding SMN; it does not edit SMN1, integrate predictably into the genome, or guarantee lifelong expression in every tissue.

Boxed Warning

Risk is modality specific. Onasemnogene products carry boxed serious-liver-injury warnings and require corticosteroids plus intensive hepatic monitoring. Nusinersen has no boxed warning but requires platelet/coagulation and renal testing around intrathecal doses. Risdiplam has no boxed warning but requires embryo-fetal, male-fertility, administration, and MATE-transporter interaction counseling.

Nursing Essentials

  • Record genotype, age, weight, respiratory/bulbar status, scoliosis or spinal hardware, prior SMA therapy, product-specific route, vaccination plan, and baseline hepatic, hematologic, renal, and cardiac data before treatment.

  • Continue respiratory support, nutrition, aspiration prevention, rehabilitation, and motor-function trending; a molecular therapy does not erase established weakness or supportive-care needs.

Key Labs

  • Product-specific: platelets/coagulation and urine protein for nusinersen; hepatic tests, platelets and troponin for onasemnogene; pregnancy/fertility and interacting MATE-substrate review for risdiplam.

Nclex Traps

  • SMN2 copy number modifies severity but does not replace molecular diagnosis or determine one universal regimen.

  • ZOLGENSMA is IV for selected children under 2; ITVISMA is intrathecal for patients age 2 and older, and prior ZOLGENSMA excludes ITVISMA under current labeling.

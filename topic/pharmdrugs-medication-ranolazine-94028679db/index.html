Used To Treat

Chronic angina symptom reduction, usually as add-on therapy when heart-rate or blood-pressure-lowering options are limited or insufficient.

Mechanism

Ranolazine inhibits late inward sodium current in ischemic cardiac myocytes, reducing intracellular sodium overload and secondary calcium overload. Lower diastolic wall tension can improve oxygen balance during ischemia; QT prolongation, dizziness, constipation, renal/hepatic context, and CYP3A interactions matter.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for ranolazine in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Ranolazine is an antianginal drug that improves myocardial relaxation/efficiency by inhibiting late inward sodium current. It does not work like nitrates or beta blockers; the nursing identity is chronic angina control with QT prolongation and CYP interaction vigilance.

Definition

Page kidney sign pattern is a clinical constellation. It is a clue to external renal compression causing secondary hypertension. A positive finding means hypertension develops with evidence of a compressive perirenal or subcapsular process.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Page kidney sign pattern means hypertension develops with evidence of a compressive perirenal or subcapsular process.

Why It Matters

The Page kidney sign pattern pattern can signal external renal compression causing secondary hypertension. Renal compression lowers local perfusion and activates renin-angiotensin signaling. Page kidney sign pattern cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Mechanism

Renal compression lowers local perfusion and activates renin-angiotensin signaling.

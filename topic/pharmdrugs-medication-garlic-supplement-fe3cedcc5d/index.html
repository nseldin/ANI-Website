Used To Treat

Mechanism

Garlic supplement works by producing organosulfur compounds with antiplatelet, blood-pressure, and lipid/glucose effects. Bleeding risk rises with anticoagulants, antiplatelets, NSAIDs, and surgery.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Garlic supplement in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Class Example Names

Used To Treat

Signs and/or symptoms of inflammatory dry-eye disease according to the selected product, while also addressing exposure, eyelid, medication and systemic causes.

Description

Cyclosporine ophthalmic and lifitegrast both target T-cell-driven ocular-surface inflammation but at different steps. Cyclosporine inhibits calcineurin-dependent cytokine transcription; lifitegrast blocks LFA-1 binding to ICAM-1, reducing T-cell adhesion, migration and activation. Neither is a rapid anesthetic or simple artificial tear.

Mechanism

Chronic tear-film instability stresses epithelium and amplifies inflammatory cytokines, damaging goblet cells and lacrimal functional units. Suppressing T-cell signaling can interrupt this cycle and improve tear production or symptoms over time. Because structural recovery is gradual, early burning does not measure the final response and adherence must be judged over weeks to months.

Boxed Warning

Neither drug currently has a boxed warning. Hypersensitivity, instillation burning, blurred vision and contamination remain relevant. Lifitegrast commonly causes dysgeusia through nasolacrimal drainage; cyclosporine can sting and should not be placed while contact lenses are in the eye. Evaluate unexplained pain, photophobia, discharge or vision loss for infection or corneal disease.

Nursing Essentials

  • Teach that benefit is delayed and regular dosing matters. Continue prescribed lubricants with appropriate spacing, and assess eyelid disease, autoimmune disease, anticholinergic burden, screen exposure and environmental dryness rather than treating every dry-eye complaint as identical.

  • Verify the exact eye, formulation, concentration, frequency, contact-lens instructions, and whether other drops must be separated by at least 5 minutes. Never let the bottle tip touch the eye, lashes, fingers, or another surface.

  • After the drop, gently close the eyelid and press the nasolacrimal duct for 1 to 2 minutes when appropriate. This reduces drainage into the nose and systemic exposure; wiping excess from the skin also limits local irritation.

Key Labs

  • Symptom and ocular-surface examination, tear production/staining when ordered, visual acuity and assessment for infection or corneal epithelial disease; routine serum drug levels are not used.

Nclex Traps

  • These are immune-modulating maintenance drops, not immediate lubricants.

  • Lifitegrast's unusual taste is from drainage/systemic exposure and reinforces why punctal occlusion can help.

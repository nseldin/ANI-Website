Description

Class I antiarrhythmics are medications grouped by a shared clinical action: block cardiac sodium channels and slow impulse conduction. Subclasses differ by how they affect repolarization and QRS/QT patterns. Common uses include selected atrial or ventricular dysrhythmias depending on subclass and client risk.

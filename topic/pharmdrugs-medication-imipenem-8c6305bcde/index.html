Carbapenem beta-lactam antibiotic

Used To Treat

Susceptible bacterial infections such as respiratory, urinary, skin/soft tissue, intra-abdominal, bloodstream, surgical prophylaxis, or CNS infections when the chosen beta-lactam covers the likely organism.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; serious hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis and C. difficile-associated diarrhea are major class concerns.

Mechanism

Imipenem works by binding multiple penicillin-binding proteins and broadly blocking cell-wall synthesis. Carbapenems are high-power beta-lactams for severe resistant or polymicrobial infections; seizure risk, renal dosing, allergy history, culture de-escalation, and ertapenem's lack of Pseudomonas coverage matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Check allergy history and reaction type.

  • Obtain cultures before antibiotics when possible without delaying emergency treatment.

  • Monitor diarrhea, rash, renal function for selected agents.

  • Subclass specificity: Imipenem is categorized here as Carbapenem beta-lactam antibiotic. Pathway: Anti-infective medication > Beta-lactam pathway > Carbapenem subclass. This subtype shapes the expected mechanism, contraindications, boxed/high-alert warnings, monitoring, interactions, and patient teaching.

Description

Imipenem is a carbapenem beta-lactam antibiotic: it works by binding multiple penicillin-binding proteins and broadly blocking cell-wall synthesis. It is used for seizure prevention/treatment; selected agents also treat bipolar disorder, neuropathic pain, migraine prevention, or tremor.

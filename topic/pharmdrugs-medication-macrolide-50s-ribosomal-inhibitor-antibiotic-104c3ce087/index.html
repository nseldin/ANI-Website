Description

Macrolides are 50S ribosomal inhibitors often used for respiratory and atypical organisms.

Used To Treat

Atypical pneumonia, respiratory infections, pertussis, selected STIs, MAC regimens, and susceptible infections depending on the agent.

Mechanism

They bind the 50S ribosomal subunit and inhibit translocation during bacterial protein synthesis. CYP3A4 inhibition varies by agent; QT risk remains a class issue.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess QT risk, potassium/magnesium, liver disease, warfarin/digoxin/statin interactions, diarrhea, and palpitations/syncope.

Name

Hematocrit

Range

Adult female about 36-46%; pregnant female reference anchors: 1st trimester 31-41%, 2nd trimester 30-39%, 3rd trimester 28-40%

Why

Plasma volume expansion lowers Hct, but numbers matter. Example: Hct 33% can fit pregnancy physiology; Hct 26% with dizziness, tachycardia, bleeding, or low Hgb needs anemia/bleeding thinking.

Group Note

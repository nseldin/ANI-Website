Definition

Pseudo-Darier sign is a bedside lesion-rubbing sign. It is a clue to smooth-muscle contraction in congenital smooth-muscle hamartoma. A positive finding means transient induration, piloerection, or rippling occurs without a true wheal.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Pseudo-Darier sign means transient induration, piloerection, or rippling occurs without a true wheal.

Why It Matters

The observed Pseudo-Darier sign finding can signal smooth-muscle contraction in congenital smooth-muscle hamartoma. Mechanical stimulation contracts excess arrector-pili smooth muscle within the lesion. Pseudo-Darier sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

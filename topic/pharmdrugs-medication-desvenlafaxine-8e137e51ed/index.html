Used To Treat

Depression, anxiety disorders, neuropathic pain, fibromyalgia, chronic musculoskeletal pain, or vasomotor symptoms depending on the medication.

Mechanism

Desvenlafaxine works by blocking serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake. That can improve mood/pain pathways but may raise blood pressure or worsen serotonin-toxicity risk.

Boxed Warning

Antidepressant boxed warning: increased risk of suicidal thoughts/behaviors in pediatric and young adult patients.

Contraindications

  • MAOI use within required washout period

  • uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma risk for selected agents

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor BP, mood/suicidality, serotonin syndrome, withdrawal if abruptly stopped.

  • May help neuropathic pain depending medication.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Other serotonergic drugs increase serotonin syndrome risk.

  • NSAIDs/anticoagulants increase bleeding risk.

  • MAOIs, linezolid, methylene blue, St. John's wort, triptans, other serotonergic drugs, alcohol/CNS depressants, anticoagulants/NSAIDs, and QT-prolonging drugs when relevant.

Key Labs

  • blood pressure

  • sodium in SIADH-risk clients

  • liver function for selected agents

  • Mood/suicidality, serotonin syndrome cues, sodium in SIADH-risk clients, blood pressure for SNRIs, ECG/QT for risk agents, and overdose danger for TCAs.

Nclex Traps

  • Do not stop abruptly; discontinuation symptoms can be intense.

  • Do not expect immediate benefit. Watch for early suicidality, serotonin syndrome, hyponatremia, bleeding risk, and abrupt discontinuation symptoms.

Description

Desvenlafaxine is a serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI) antidepressant: it works by blocking serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake. It is used for depression, anxiety disorders, neuropathic pain, fibromyalgia, chronic musculoskeletal pain, or vasomotor symptoms depending on the medication.

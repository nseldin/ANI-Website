Used To Treat

Constipation and bowel cleansing before selected procedures when ordered.

Mechanism

Polyethylene glycol works by holding water in the intestinal lumen by osmotic effect, softening stool and increasing motility. Lactulose also traps ammonia as ammonium in the gut for hepatic encephalopathy. Dehydration, electrolytes, renal function, and aspiration/bowel prep instructions matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess bowel obstruction symptoms, hydration, electrolyte risk, renal/cardiac status, and procedure instructions.

  • For bowel prep, teach clear timing, fluid intake, and when to stop oral intake.

Contraindications

  • Clarify suspected bowel obstruction, perforation, severe colitis/toxic megacolon, or significant dehydration.

Interactions

  • Rapid GI transit during bowel prep can reduce absorption of oral medications.

Key Labs

  • Electrolytes and renal function if high-risk, frail, or large-volume bowel prep.

Nclex Traps

  • Bowel prep safety is hydration, electrolytes, falls, and knowing when obstruction/perforation symptoms make it unsafe.

Description

Polyethylene glycol is an osmotic laxative / bowel preparation agent: it works by holding water in the intestinal lumen by osmotic effect, softening stool and increasing motility. It is used for constipation and bowel cleansing before selected procedures when ordered.

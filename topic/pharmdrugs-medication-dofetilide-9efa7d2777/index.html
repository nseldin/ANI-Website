Class

Class III antiarrhythmic; potassium-channel blocker

Used To Treat

Maintenance of normal sinus rhythm in atrial fibrillation or atrial flutter in selected clients.

Boxed Warning

Drug-specific boxed warning: can cause life-threatening ventricular dysrhythmias. Initiation or re-initiation requires inpatient monitoring with ECG and renal-based dosing.

Mechanism

Dofetilide works by blocking cardiac potassium currents so repolarization and the effective refractory period last longer. That can interrupt reentry rhythms, but it also lengthens QT and can provoke torsades when bradycardia, hypokalemia, hypomagnesemia, renal accumulation, or other QT-prolonging drugs are present.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor QTc, creatinine clearance, potassium, magnesium, telemetry, and medication interactions.

  • Keep potassium and magnesium normal to reduce torsades risk.

Interactions

  • Contraindicated or unsafe with several drugs that raise dofetilide levels or prolong QT.

  • Diuretics that lower potassium/magnesium increase torsades risk.

  • Do not stack with other QT-prolonging antiarrhythmics unless specifically directed by cardiology.

Description

Dofetilide is a class III antiarrhythmic and potassium-channel blocker: it works by blocking cardiac potassium currents so repolarization and the effective refractory period last longer. It is used for maintenance of normal sinus rhythm in atrial fibrillation or atrial flutter in selected clients.

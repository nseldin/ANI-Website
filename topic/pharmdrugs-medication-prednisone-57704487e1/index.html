Used To Treat

Inflammation, asthma/COPD exacerbations, allergic reactions, autoimmune disease, adrenal replacement, cerebral edema, fetal lung maturity, or oncology/transplant protocols depending on the drug.

Mechanism

Prednisone works by diffusing into cells and activating cytosolic glucocorticoid receptors that move to the nucleus, change transcription, suppress phospholipase A2/COX-2/cytokine signaling, and reduce inflammatory cell trafficking. Benefit is less inflammation/immune activation; risks are hyperglycemia, infection masking, adrenal suppression, mood changes, myopathy, bone loss, skin thinning, and route-specific effects such as oral thrush or ocular pressure.

Boxed Warning

No single universal boxed warning for all prednisone products; immunosuppression, adrenal suppression, hyperglycemia, and GI bleeding risk are major concerns.

Contraindications

  • Systemic fungal infection unless specifically treated/ordered.

  • Live vaccines during high-dose immunosuppression require clarification.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Do not stop long-term therapy abruptly.

  • Monitor glucose, infection signs, mood changes, GI bleeding.

  • Take with food when appropriate.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • NSAIDs increase GI bleeding risk.

  • Vaccines/live vaccines need caution.

  • Diuretics may worsen hypokalemia.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • glucose

  • WBC interpretation

  • potassium

  • occult blood if GI symptoms

  • Glucose, infection signs, blood pressure, weight/edema, mood, GI bleeding risk, potassium for mineralocorticoid effects, and adrenal suppression with long-term therapy.

Nclex Traps

  • Steroids can hide infection signs while increasing infection risk.

  • Steroids can mask infection and raise glucose. Do not stop chronic systemic steroids abruptly.

Description

Prednisone is a systemic corticosteroid: it works by diffusing into cells and activating cytosolic glucocorticoid receptors that move to the nucleus, change transcription, suppress phospholipase A2/COX-2/cytokine signaling, and reduce inflammatory cell trafficking. It is used for inflammation, asthma/COPD exacerbations, allergic reactions, autoimmune disease, adrenal replacement, cerebral edema, fetal lung maturity, or oncology/transplant protocols depending on the drug.

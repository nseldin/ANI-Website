Definition

Argyll Robertson pupil is an observed pupillary sign. It is a clue to dorsal midbrain pupillary pathway damage, classically neurosyphilis. A positive finding means small irregular pupils constrict for near effort but respond poorly to light.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Argyll Robertson pupil means small irregular pupils constrict for near effort but respond poorly to light.

Why It Matters

The observed Argyll Robertson pupil finding can signal dorsal midbrain pupillary pathway damage, classically neurosyphilis. Pretectal light-reflex pathways are interrupted while the cortical near-response pathway is preserved. Argyll Robertson pupil cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Compare pupil size and reaction to bright light with constriction during near focus.

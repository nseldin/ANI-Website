Used To Treat

Secondary hyperparathyroidism in adults with chronic kidney disease receiving hemodialysis, administered IV at the end of dialysis.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for etelcalcetide in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Mechanism

Etelcalcetide allosterically binds and sensitizes CaSR to extracellular calcium. Increased CaSR signaling suppresses PTH release, which secondarily lowers calcium and attenuates phosphate elevation in dialysis clients.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify corrected calcium is acceptable before initiation/dose increase and administer after hemodialysis through the venous line per protocol.

  • Monitor calcium within one week after changes and at least monthly, PTH/phosphorus, numbness/tingling, cramps, seizures, QT-risk drugs, BP, and heart-failure symptoms; do not overlap casually with cinacalcet.

Key Labs

  • Corrected/ionized calcium, phosphorus, intact PTH, magnesium, ECG/QTc when at risk, and dialysis/heart-failure status.

Nclex Traps

  • Calcimimetic means the receptor senses more calcium; the actual blood calcium usually falls.

Description

Etelcalcetide is an IV calcimimetic that allosterically binds and sensitizes calcium-sensing receptors on parathyroid chief cells, suppressing PTH after hemodialysis as though extracellular calcium were higher. Clinically important hypocalcemia can cause paresthesias, cramps, seizures, QT prolongation, ventricular dysrhythmias, and possibly worsening heart failure.

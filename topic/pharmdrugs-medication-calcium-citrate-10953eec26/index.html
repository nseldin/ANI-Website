Used To Treat

Calcium supplementation when dietary intake is inadequate or bone-mineral support is needed, especially when lower stomach acid makes calcium carbonate less reliable.

Mechanism

Calcium citrate dissociates in the GI tract to provide calcium ions that can be absorbed and used for bone mineralization, neuromuscular excitability, coagulation, and cardiac conduction. Citrate improves solubility when gastric acid is low.

Class

Oral calcium salt supplement; acid-independent calcium replacement

Description

Calcium citrate is an oral calcium salt supplement and acid-independent calcium replacement: it dissociates in the GI tract to provide calcium ions that can be absorbed and used for bone mineralization, neuromuscular excitability, coagulation, and cardiac conduction. It is used for calcium supplementation when dietary intake is inadequate or bone-mineral support is needed, especially when lower stomach acid makes calcium carbonate less reliable.

Definition

Bag-of-worms sign is a palpated scrotal sign. It is a clue to varicocele. A positive finding means soft tortuous veins feel like a bag of worms and may decompress supine.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Bag-of-worms sign means soft tortuous veins feel like a bag of worms and may decompress supine.

Why It Matters

The observed Bag-of-worms sign finding can signal varicocele. Incompetent gonadal venous drainage dilates the pampiniform plexus. Bag-of-worms sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • varicocele

Assessment Method

Palpate the spermatic cord standing and supine, with gentle Valsalva if appropriate.

Positive Finding

Soft tortuous veins feel like a bag of worms and may decompress supine.

Mechanism

Incompetent gonadal venous drainage dilates the pampiniform plexus.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Bag-of-worms sign finding raises concern for varicocele because incompetent gonadal venous drainage dilates the pampiniform plexus. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Bag-of-worms sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Bag-of-worms sign to focus evaluation of varicocele and the reviewed alternatives: hydrocele, cord lipoma, epididymal cyst. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Palpate the spermatic cord standing and supine, with gentle Valsalva if appropriate. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Bag-of-worms sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Small varicoceles require ultrasound and persistent right-sided or supine disease raises secondary concern. Therefore, Bag-of-worms sign cannot confirm or exclude varicocele by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • hydrocele

  • cord lipoma

  • epididymal cyst

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as hydrocele, cord lipoma, epididymal cyst.

False Negative Situations

Bag-of-worms sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of varicocele.

Class

Long-acting beta-2 agonist bronchodilator

Used To Treat

Maintenance bronchodilation in COPD and asthma control only in appropriate combination inhalers, usually with an inhaled corticosteroid for asthma or with LAMA/ICS partners depending on product.

Description

Vilanterol is a long-acting beta-2 agonist bronchodilator that stimulates airway beta-2 receptors to raise cAMP and relax bronchial smooth muscle for maintenance airflow control. It is a controller bronchodilator, not a rescue medication for sudden bronchospasm.

Mechanism

Vilanterol stimulates beta-2 receptors on bronchial smooth muscle, activating Gs signaling and adenylyl cyclase to raise intracellular cAMP. Higher cAMP relaxes airway smooth muscle and improves airflow for many hours. Asthma products pair vilanterol with an inhaled corticosteroid because LABA monotherapy can increase asthma-related risk.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm the inhaler is for maintenance, not acute rescue. Assess breath sounds, dyspnea, exacerbation frequency, tremor, palpitations, heart rate, potassium risk, and inhaler technique.

Interactions

  • Other sympathomimetics, beta blockers, potassium-lowering diuretics, MAOIs/TCAs, and QT-risk drugs can increase adverse-effect concerns.

Nclex Traps

  • Vilanterol is LABA maintenance therapy, not fast rescue.

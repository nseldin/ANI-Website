Mechanism

Atropine ophthalmic works by blocking muscarinic vagal effects on the heart, increasing SA node firing and AV conduction.

Contraindications

Description

Atropine ophthalmic is an antidote or reversal agent: it works by blocking muscarinic vagal effects on the heart, increasing SA node firing and AV conduction. It is used for toxicity, overdose, reversal, or rescue situations for a specific medication or poison exposure.

Class

Dual CFTR corrector plus CFTR potentiator combination

Used To Treat

Cystic fibrosis in adults and children meeting the current age and CFTR-variant responsiveness criteria, including many clients with at least one F508del or another responsive/CFTR-producing variant.

Description

Elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor is a dual-CFTR-corrector plus CFTR-potentiator combination: elexacaftor and tezacaftor stabilize misfolded protein so more reaches the epithelial surface, while ivacaftor increases opening of the rescued channels. This can transform mucus hydration and organ function, but current labeling emphasizes potentially fatal liver injury, CYP3A interactions, cataracts, intracranial hypertension, and neuropsychiatric events including suicidal thoughts/behaviors.

Mechanism

Elexacaftor and tezacaftor bind different sites on CFTR and improve folding, processing, trafficking, and surface stability, producing more functional channels. Ivacaftor potentiates those channels by increasing open probability; the combination raises chloride/bicarbonate transport more than either corrector alone.

Boxed Warning

Drug-induced liver injury and liver failure: fatal cases and transplantation have occurred with or without prior liver disease. Obtain ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase, and bilirubin before therapy; monitor monthly for six months, every three months for the next 12 months, then at least annually, and interrupt promptly for significant abnormalities or symptoms.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify genotype/eligibility and exact age/weight dose; give each component with fat-containing food and screen CYP3A inducers/inhibitors plus grapefruit/Seville orange exposure.

  • Monitor liver injury, respiratory/nutritional response, rash, pediatric cataracts, severe headache/visual symptoms for intracranial hypertension, and new depression, anxiety, sleep disturbance, or suicidal thoughts/behaviors.

Key Labs

  • ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase, bilirubin on the boxed-warning schedule; pulmonary function/sweat chloride as ordered, nutrition/weight, and pediatric eye exams.

Nclex Traps

  • Correctors increase surface CFTR; the potentiator opens it. The regimen's success does not make liver or neuropsychiatric surveillance optional.

Used To Treat

Mechanism

Rasburicase converts uric acid to allantoin, which is much more water soluble. It rapidly lowers uric acid but can cause hemolysis/methemoglobinemia in G6PD deficiency and requires special specimen handling for uric acid levels.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for rasburicase in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Rasburicase is a recombinant urate oxidase enzyme: it converts uric acid to allantoin, which is much more water soluble. It is used for prevention or treatment of hyperuricemia in tumor lysis syndrome risk or treatment protocols.

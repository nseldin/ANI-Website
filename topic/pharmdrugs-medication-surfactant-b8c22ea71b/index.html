Used To Treat

Neonatal respiratory distress syndrome from surfactant deficiency and selected neonatal rescue protocols.

Mechanism

Surfactant lowers alveolar surface tension, improving alveolar stability, compliance, oxygenation, and reducing atelectasis. Nursing priorities are airway/ET tube position, oxygenation, bradycardia/desaturation during administration, ventilator changes, and thermoregulation.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Surfactant in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Surfactant is a pulmonary surfactant replacement: it lowers alveolar surface tension, improving alveolar stability, compliance, oxygenation, and reducing atelectasis. It is used for neonatal respiratory distress syndrome from surfactant deficiency and selected neonatal rescue protocols.

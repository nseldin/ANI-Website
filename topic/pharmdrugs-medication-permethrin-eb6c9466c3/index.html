Used To Treat

Scabies with 5% cream and head lice with lower-strength products; formulation and contact time are not interchangeable.

Mechanism

Permethrin binds voltage-sensitive sodium channels in parasite nerve membranes and slows channel closing after depolarization. Persistent sodium influx causes after-discharges, incoordination and paralysis. Esterases rapidly metabolize absorbed drug in humans. It has activity against mites and some ova, but surviving eggs, resistance, missed skin folds or untreated contacts can require repeat protocol-directed treatment. Host hypersensitivity can keep itching active after mites are dead.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity to permethrin, pyrethrins, pyrethroids or formulation components

  • Avoid ocular/mucosal exposure and ingestion

  • Clarify severe inflamed/infected skin, infancy, pregnancy/lactation and prior treatment failure

Nursing Essentials

  • For scabies, massage 5% cream over the prescribed entire surface and wash off after the labeled interval; include folds, under nails and special head/neck sites when directed.

  • Treat close contacts and wash/dry or isolate clothing, towels and bedding according to infection-control guidance.

Interactions

  • No major systemic CYP interaction is expected

  • Multiple simultaneous scabicides/pediculicides increase neurocutaneous irritation

  • Topical corticosteroids before eradication can mask ongoing infestation

Key Labs

  • No routine laboratory monitoring

  • Skin scraping/dermoscopy when diagnosis or failure is uncertain

  • Serial burrows/lesions, contact treatment and infection assessment

Nclex Traps

  • Permethrin 5% scabies cream is not the same regimen as 1% lice treatment.

  • Itching after treatment can persist despite eradication.

  • Retreating one patient without treating contacts invites reinfestation.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: For external use only; avoid eyes and mucosa. Burning, stinging and pruritus may transiently worsen. Persistent itch for up to several weeks can be post-scabetic inflammation, but new burrows or untreated contacts suggest failure/reinfestation. In infants and older adults, scalp, hairline, temples and forehead may require inclusion when directed.

Description

Permethrin is a synthetic pyrethroid that kills mites and lice by keeping arthropod voltage-gated sodium channels open longer than normal. Delayed repolarization produces repetitive nerve firing, paralysis and death. Human sodium channels recover more rapidly and skin absorption is low, creating useful selectivity. For scabies, correct whole-skin coverage, adequate contact time, simultaneous contact treatment and environmental decontamination matter as much as the molecule.

Adverse Effects

  • Burning, stinging, tingling, erythema, pruritus and rash

  • Contact dermatitis or hypersensitivity

  • Rare dizziness, headache or paresthesia

  • Treatment failure from resistance, missed application or reinfestation

Class

Magnesium antacid / osmotic laxative

Used To Treat

Occasional constipation or acid indigestion when appropriate.

Mechanism

Magnesium hydroxide works by holding water in the intestinal lumen by osmotic effect, softening stool and increasing motility. Lactulose also traps ammonia as ammonium in the gut for hepatic encephalopathy. Dehydration, electrolytes, renal function, and aspiration/bowel prep instructions matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess kidney function before repeated use because magnesium can accumulate in renal impairment.

  • Monitor diarrhea, dehydration, electrolyte changes, and bowel response.

Contraindications

  • Clarify severe renal impairment, bowel obstruction symptoms, severe abdominal pain, or unexplained nausea/vomiting.

Interactions

  • Can reduce absorption of tetracyclines, fluoroquinolones, levothyroxine, iron, and other oral drugs if given too close together.

Key Labs

  • Magnesium and renal function if repeated/high-risk use.

Nclex Traps

  • Magnesium-containing laxatives are not benign in kidney disease. Watch for hypermagnesemia, lethargy, hypotension, and decreased reflexes.

Description

Magnesium hydroxide is a magnesium antacid / osmotic laxative: it works by holding water in the intestinal lumen by osmotic effect, softening stool and increasing motility. It is used for occasional constipation or acid indigestion when appropriate.

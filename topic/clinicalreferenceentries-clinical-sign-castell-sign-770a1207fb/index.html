Definition

Castell sign is a percussion maneuver. It is a clue to splenomegaly. A positive finding means the note changes from resonant to dull with inspiration.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Castell sign means the note changes from resonant to dull with inspiration.

Why It Matters

A positive Castell sign response can signal splenomegaly. An enlarged spleen descends into the percussion area during inspiration. Castell sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • splenomegaly

Assessment Method

Percuss the lowest left anterior interspace in expiration and deep inspiration.

Positive Finding

The note changes from resonant to dull with inspiration.

Mechanism

An enlarged spleen descends into the percussion area during inspiration.

Clinical Interpretation

A positive Castell sign response raises concern for splenomegaly because an enlarged spleen descends into the percussion area during inspiration. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Castell sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Castell sign to focus evaluation of splenomegaly and the reviewed alternatives: pleural fluid, fecal loading, gastric mass. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Percuss the lowest left anterior interspace in expiration and deep inspiration. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Castell sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Obesity, gastric contents, and lung disease affect percussion. Therefore, Castell sign cannot confirm or exclude splenomegaly by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • pleural fluid

  • fecal loading

  • gastric mass

False Positive Situations

A false-positive maneuver can result from incorrect position or force, pain from pleural fluid, fecal loading, gastric mass, guarding, or calling an incompletely defined response positive.

False Negative Situations

Castell sign may be missed with incomplete technique, guarding, early or partial disease, limited cooperation, or a response too subtle for the examiner.

Modern Status

Current adjunct with modest reliability. When performed as described, Castell sign can adjust concern for splenomegaly, but it is not a stand-alone diagnosis. Apply the reviewed limitation for Castell sign: Obesity, gastric contents, and lung disease affect percussion.

Safety Precautions

Before assessing Castell sign, explain the method, obtain consent, preserve privacy, and use only the technique described here: Percuss the lowest left anterior interspace in expiration and deep inspiration. Stop if that technique becomes unsafe. The reviewed precaution for this maneuver is: Confirm suspected splenomegaly with imaging and avoid vigorous palpation when rupture risk exists.

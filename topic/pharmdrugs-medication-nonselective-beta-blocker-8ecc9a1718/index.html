Class

Nonselective beta-1/beta-2 adrenergic receptor antagonist class

Description

Nonselective beta blockers block both beta-1 cardiac receptors and beta-2 receptors in bronchi, vessels, liver, and skeletal muscle. That means rate/contractility reduction comes with extra asthma/COPD caution and stronger masking of adrenergic hypoglycemia warning signs.

Mechanism

Beta-1 blockade slows heart rate, AV conduction, contractility, and renin release; beta-2 blockade can reduce bronchodilation and alter metabolic compensation during hypoglycemia.

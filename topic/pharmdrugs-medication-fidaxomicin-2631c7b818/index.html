Class

Narrow-spectrum macrocyclic antibiotic for C. difficile

Used To Treat

Respiratory infections, atypical pneumonia, pertussis, selected STIs, MAC prophylaxis/treatment, and some GI motility uses depending on the agent.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; major concerns include QT prolongation, hepatotoxicity, and CYP interactions for selected agents.

Mechanism

Fidaxomicin binds bacterial RNA polymerase and blocks early transcription initiation. Because systemic absorption is minimal, the therapeutic action is local in the intestinal lumen; monitor diarrhea response, recurrence, hydration, and severe colitis cues.

Nursing Essentials

  • Assess QT risk, liver disease, interactions.

  • Teach to report palpitations/syncope or severe diarrhea.

  • Subclass specificity: Fidaxomicin is categorized here as Narrow-spectrum macrocyclic antibiotic for C. difficile. Pathway: Anti-infective medication > C. difficile therapy > Macrocyclic RNA-polymerase inhibitor. This subtype shapes the expected mechanism, contraindications, boxed/high-alert warnings, monitoring, interactions, and patient teaching.

Contraindications

  • Significant QT prolongation or interacting QT drugs may require clarification

  • serious prior macrolide reaction

Interactions

  • Warfarin, statins, QT-prolonging drugs; erythromycin/clarithromycin have more CYP interactions.

Key Labs

  • LFTs if indicated

  • ECG/QT risk

  • INR if on warfarin

Nclex Traps

  • Macrolide + other QT drugs can create torsades risk.

Description

Fidaxomicin is a minimally absorbed macrocyclic antibiotic that inhibits C. difficile RNA polymerase/transcription inside the gut lumen. It reduces C. difficile replication while sparing more normal flora than broader antibiotics.

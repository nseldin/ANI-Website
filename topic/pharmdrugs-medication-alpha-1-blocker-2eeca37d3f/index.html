Description

Alpha-1 blockers reduce sympathetic alpha-1 tone in vascular smooth muscle and, for uroselective agents, the prostate/bladder neck. That explains the useful effect on urine flow and blood pressure as well as first-dose syncope and orthostatic fall risk.

Used To Treat

Benign prostatic hyperplasia and hypertension for selected agents.

Mechanism

Blocking alpha-1 receptors in the bladder neck/prostate improves urine flow; blocking vascular alpha-1 receptors lowers vascular tone and can cause orthostatic hypotension.

Nursing Essentials

  • Teach first-dose dizziness/syncope precautions, slow position changes, fall risk, and cataract-surgery/IFIS disclosure for tamsulosin-like agents.

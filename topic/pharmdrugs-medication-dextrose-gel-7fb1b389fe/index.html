Class

Diabetes medication

Used To Treat

blood glucose management or hypoglycemia rescue depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Dextrose gel supplies absorbable glucose directly. Glucose enters circulation and becomes available to the brain and other glucose-dependent tissues; response should be checked with repeat blood glucose and clinical assessment, especially in neonates or clients on insulin/sulfonylureas.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Dextrose gel in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Dextrose gel is concentrated glucose used to raise blood glucose through buccal/oral absorption. It treats the immediate low-glucose state but does not explain why hypoglycemia occurred, so reassessment, feeding plan, repeat glucose checks, and escalation for persistent/severe symptoms matter.

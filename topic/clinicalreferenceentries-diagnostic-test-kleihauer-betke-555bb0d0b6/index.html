Summary

Kleihauer-Betke is a high-yield Obstetrics diagnostic reference. Estimates fetal-maternal hemorrhage, often tied to Rh immune globulin dosing after trauma/bleeding.

Quick Answer

Kleihauer-Betke: Estimates fetal-maternal hemorrhage, often tied to Rh immune globulin dosing after trauma/bleeding. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    Negative / low fetal cells

    Meaning

    Little or no fetal blood is detected in maternal circulation.

  • Label

    Positive / elevated fetal cells

    Meaning

    Fetomaternal hemorrhage is detected/quantified and can guide Rh immune globulin dosing or fetal-maternal monitoring.

  • Label

    Clinical significance

    Meaning

    Use after trauma, bleeding, stillbirth evaluation, or Rh-negative pregnancy contexts; it is not routine blood typing.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Estimates fetal-maternal hemorrhage, often tied to Rh immune globulin dosing after trauma/bleeding.

    • Before / preparation

    • Confirm gestational age, fetal/maternal baseline status, Rh status when invasive testing is involved, and whether the client is having bleeding, contractions, or ruptured membranes.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Monitor maternal vital signs, fetal status when indicated, uterine activity, bleeding, fluid leakage, infection cues, and decreased fetal movement.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Decreased fetal movement, nonreassuring fetal tracing, bleeding, leaking fluid, fever, severe abdominal pain, contractions after an invasive procedure, or hypertensive warning signs.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • This test helps estimate fetal blood in maternal circulation; it is not the same as routine blood typing.

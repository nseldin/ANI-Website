Used To Treat

Parkinson disease motor symptoms such as bradykinesia, rigidity, and tremor.

Mechanism

Levodopa works by entering the brain and being converted to dopamine, restoring dopamine signaling in Parkinson motor circuits. Carbidopa limits peripheral conversion, reducing nausea and allowing more levodopa to reach the CNS. Wearing off, dyskinesias, hallucinations, orthostasis, and protein timing matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor for improved mobility, dyskinesias, hallucinations, orthostatic hypotension, nausea, and wearing-off before the next dose.

  • Teach fall precautions and consistent timing with meals. Protein can interfere with absorption for some clients.

Contraindications

  • Clarify narrow-angle glaucoma, suspicious skin lesions/melanoma history, severe psychosis, or recent MAOI use according to product labeling.

Interactions

  • Antipsychotics and metoclopramide can reduce benefit. MAOIs increase hypertensive risk. Iron can reduce absorption.

Key Labs

  • No routine serum drug level. Track symptom pattern, orthostatic vitals, falls, and mental status.

Nclex Traps

  • Do not stop abruptly. Watch for dyskinesia, hallucinations, orthostatic hypotension, and end-of-dose wearing off.

Description

Levodopa is a dopamine precursor Parkinson medication: it works by entering the brain and being converted to dopamine, restoring dopamine signaling in Parkinson motor circuits. It is used for parkinson disease motor symptoms such as bradykinesia, rigidity, and tremor.

Definition

Anticholinergic dry-flushed sign pattern is a clinical toxidrome constellation. It is a clue to central and peripheral muscarinic blockade. A positive finding means hot dry flushed skin, mydriasis, tachycardia, urinary retention, ileus, and delirium cluster.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Anticholinergic dry-flushed sign pattern means hot dry flushed skin, mydriasis, tachycardia, urinary retention, ileus, and delirium cluster.

Why It Matters

The Anticholinergic dry-flushed sign pattern pattern can signal central and peripheral muscarinic blockade. Muscarinic blockade prevents sweating, accommodation, gland secretion, gut motility, and parasympathetic restraint. Anticholinergic dry-flushed sign pattern cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Definition

Violaceous striae is an observed skin sign. It is a clue to cortisol excess with dermal atrophy. A positive finding means broad purple-red striae, often wider than ordinary stretch marks, are present.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Violaceous striae means broad purple-red striae, often wider than ordinary stretch marks, are present.

Why It Matters

The observed Violaceous striae finding can signal cortisol excess with dermal atrophy. Cortisol suppresses collagen and thins dermis, so stretching exposes underlying vasculature. Violaceous striae cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • cortisol excess with dermal atrophy

Assessment Method

Inspect abdomen, thighs, breasts, and arms.

Positive Finding

Broad purple-red striae, often wider than ordinary stretch marks, are present.

Mechanism

Cortisol suppresses collagen and thins dermis, so stretching exposes underlying vasculature.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Violaceous striae finding raises concern for cortisol excess with dermal atrophy because cortisol suppresses collagen and thins dermis, so stretching exposes underlying vasculature. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Violaceous striae does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Violaceous striae to focus evaluation of cortisol excess with dermal atrophy and the reviewed alternatives: pregnancy striae, growth spurt, weight gain. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect abdomen, thighs, breasts, and arms. Evidence Limitations

Color and width overlap, especially early in ordinary striae. Therefore, Violaceous striae cannot confirm or exclude cortisol excess with dermal atrophy by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as pregnancy striae, growth spurt, weight gain.

False Negative Situations

Violaceous striae may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of cortisol excess with dermal atrophy.

Definition

Clutton joints is an observed joint sign. It is a clue to late congenital syphilis. A positive finding means bilateral painless synovial effusions develop.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Clutton joints means bilateral painless synovial effusions develop.

Why It Matters

The observed Clutton joints finding can signal late congenital syphilis. Chronic immune-mediated synovitis related to congenital treponemal infection causes effusion without destructive pain. Clutton joints cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • late congenital syphilis

Assessment Method

Assess painless symmetric swelling of knees or other large joints in an older child.

Positive Finding

Bilateral painless synovial effusions develop.

Mechanism

Chronic immune-mediated synovitis related to congenital treponemal infection causes effusion without destructive pain.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Clutton joints finding raises concern for late congenital syphilis because chronic immune-mediated synovitis related to congenital treponemal infection causes effusion without destructive pain.

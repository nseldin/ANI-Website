Description

ACE inhibitors reduce renin-angiotensin-aldosterone signaling and protect vascular, cardiac, and kidney physiology in selected clients.

Used To Treat

Hypertension, heart failure, post-MI cardiac protection, diabetic/proteinuric kidney protection, and other ordered cardiovascular indications.

Mechanism

They inhibit angiotensin-converting enzyme, lowering angiotensin II and aldosterone while increasing bradykinin. That reduces vasoconstriction and sodium retention but explains cough and angioedema.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor blood pressure, creatinine/eGFR, potassium, cough, angioedema, pregnancy status, dehydration, and renal-artery stenosis risk.

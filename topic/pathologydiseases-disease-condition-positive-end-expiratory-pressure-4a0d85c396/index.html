Plain Meaning

Pressure left in the lungs at the end of exhalation to keep alveoli from collapsing.

Word Parts

  • Root

    positive

    Meaning

    pressure above atmospheric pressure

  • Root

    end-expiratory

    Meaning

    at the end of exhalation

Word Origin

PEEP literally names the pressure intentionally present at end-expiration.

Pathology

PEEP raises end-expiratory alveolar pressure so recruitable alveoli stay open instead of repeatedly collapsing and reopening. In ARDS and other hypoxemic respiratory failure, that can improve oxygenation by increasing functional residual capacity, reducing atelectasis, and decreasing shunt physiology.

Etiology

Applied PEEP is a ventilator or noninvasive-pressure setting. Auto-PEEP is unintended trapped pressure from incomplete exhalation, commonly with obstructive physiology, high respiratory rate, short expiratory time, or air trapping.

Signs Symptoms

  • PEEP itself is a setting, not a symptom

  • Improved oxygenation may occur when alveoli recruit

  • Excessive pressure can reduce venous return, lower preload/cardiac output, worsen hypotension, raise plateau pressures, or contribute to barotrauma

Diagnostics

  • Ventilator PEEP setting

  • Oxygenation response: SpO2, ABG, PaO2/FiO2 ratio

  • Hemodynamic response: BP/MAP, urine output, lactate, perfusion

  • Airway pressures, plateau pressure, driving pressure, and auto-PEEP assessment when indicated

Treatments

  • Use ordered PEEP to improve oxygenation/recruitment

  • Pair with lung-protective ventilation in ARDS

  • Treat auto-PEEP by correcting air trapping and expiratory time problems

  • Balance oxygenation benefit against hypotension/barotrauma risk

Nursing Priorities

  • Trend oxygenation and hemodynamics after PEEP changes

  • Watch BP/MAP, urine output, mental status, lung sounds, ventilator pressures, synchrony, and pneumothorax signs

  • Know that more PEEP is not automatically better if perfusion collapses

Complications

  • Hypotension from reduced venous return

  • Barotrauma

  • Pneumothorax

  • Worsened RV strain in susceptible clients

  • Auto-PEEP with air trapping

Patient Education

  • In ICU teaching, explain that PEEP helps keep air sacs open between breaths so oxygen can cross into blood more effectively.

Nclex Traps

  • PEEP can improve oxygenation but can also drop preload and blood pressure.

  • Auto-PEEP is trapped pressure from incomplete exhalation, not a helpful ordered setting.

  • Do not interpret PaO2/FiO2 or ventilator response without knowing FiO2 and PEEP context.

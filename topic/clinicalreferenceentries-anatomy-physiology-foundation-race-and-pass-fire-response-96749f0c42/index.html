RACE and PASS fire response

Nclex Essential

true

Quick Answer

Protect people and activate the facility response first. Use an extinguisher only if trained, the fire is small, the correct extinguisher is available, and a safe exit remains behind you.

Sections

  • Label

    Facility response

    Text

    Healthcare fire plans prioritize removing people in immediate danger, activating the alarm or emergency system, containing smoke and fire when safe, and extinguishing or evacuating according to policy. Exact wording and order can vary by facility.

  • Label

    Extinguisher boundaries

    Text

    Choose the correct extinguisher, maintain an exit path, stay outside smoke and toxic products, and abandon the attempt if the fire grows, visibility falls, or the extinguisher is ineffective.

  • Label

    Healthcare-specific concerns

    Text

    Oxygen-enriched environments, immobile patients, locked units, electrical equipment, hazardous materials, and smoke compartments require trained facility procedures rather than improvisation.

  • Label

    After activation

    Text

    Close doors when instructed, account for patients and staff, follow horizontal or vertical evacuation plans, protect life-support continuity, and do not re-enter until cleared.

Summary

RACE organizes initial fire response and PASS recalls basic portable-extinguisher operation for trained people when evacuation remains safe.

    • Cue

      R

      Meaning

      Rescue or Remove from Immediate Danger. Life safety comes before property protection.

      A

      Meaning

      Alarm or Activate the Response. Early activation brings trained help and starts the facility plan.

    • Cue

      C

      Meaning

      Confine or Contain. Closing doors when safe can limit smoke and fire spread.

    • Cue

      E

      Meaning

      Extinguish or Evacuate. The choice depends on training, fire size, equipment, and a protected exit.

    • Cue

      P

      Meaning

      Pull the Pin. This releases the extinguisher operating mechanism.

    • Cue

      A2

      Meaning

      Aim at the Base. The extinguishing agent must reach the burning material rather than only the flame.

    • Cue

      S

      Meaning

      Squeeze the Handle. This discharges the agent.

    • Cue

      S2

      Meaning

      Sweep Side to Side. A controlled sweep covers the base while the user maintains an exit route.

    Limitations

Used To Treat

Growth hormone deficiency, selected pediatric growth disorders, adult GH deficiency, and other labeled endocrine/growth indications.

Mechanism

Somatropin activates growth hormone receptors, especially in liver and growth-plate tissues, increasing IGF-1 production and anabolic/growth signaling. It also changes glucose and lipid metabolism, which explains hyperglycemia and edema monitoring.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for somatropin in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Somatropin is recombinant human growth hormone that activates GH receptors and IGF-1-mediated growth/metabolic signaling. The opening safety lens is not just height: glucose intolerance, intracranial hypertension symptoms, slipped capital femoral epiphysis hip/knee pain, edema, scoliosis progression, and malignancy-context screening matter.

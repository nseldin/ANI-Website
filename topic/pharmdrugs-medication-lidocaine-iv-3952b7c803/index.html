Mechanism

Lidocaine IV works by blocking fast sodium channels preferentially in ischemic or depolarized ventricular tissue, shortening repolarization in many settings. CNS toxicity, seizures, paresthesias, tremor, and rhythm monitoring are the bedside anchors.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Lidocaine IV in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Lidocaine IV is a class IB antiarrhythmic and sodium-channel blocker: it works by blocking fast sodium channels preferentially in ischemic or depolarized ventricular tissue, shortening repolarization in many settings. It is used for selected rhythm disturbances, with nursing focus on ECG rhythm, heart rate, blood pressure, electrolytes, and drug-specific toxicity.

Category

Inflammation labs

Name

ESR

Range

Female often 0-20 mm/hr. Male often 0-15 mm/hr. Rises with age and varies by lab.

Why

ESR is a nonspecific inflammation trend marker. It moves slowly and is less acute than CRP.

Aliases

  • erythrocyte sedimentation rate

  • sed rate

High Causes

  • Inflammation

  • Infection

  • Autoimmune disease

  • Temporal arteritis

  • Malignancy

  • Pregnancy/older age can increase

Low Causes

  • Usually less clinically urgent.

Critical Concerns

  • Headache, jaw claudication, vision changes, and high ESR can suggest temporal arteritis.

Nursing Considerations

  • Interpret with symptoms and CRP; do not diagnose from ESR alone.

Nclex Traps

  • ESR is nonspecific and slow-moving.

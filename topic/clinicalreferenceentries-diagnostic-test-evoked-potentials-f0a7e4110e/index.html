Summary

Evoked potentials is a high-yield Neurology diagnostic reference. Measures nervous-system electrical response to sensory stimulation.

Quick Answer

Evoked potentials: Measures nervous-system electrical response to sensory stimulation. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Measures nervous-system electrical response to sensory stimulation.

    • Before / preparation

    • Assess baseline neurologic status, seizure precautions when needed, bleeding/anticoagulant risk for invasive procedures, and MRI metal/implant restrictions.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Trend neuro checks, level of consciousness, pupils, motor/sensory findings, headache, seizure activity, and procedure-site complications.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Acute neuro deficit, seizure, decreased level of consciousness, sudden severe headache, signs of increased ICP, or CSF leak/meningitis symptoms.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • A small neurologic change can be the whole emergency.

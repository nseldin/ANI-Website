Class

Intrathecal SMN2-directed splice-switching antisense oligonucleotide

Used To Treat

Spinal muscular atrophy in pediatric and adult patients through product-specific intrathecal loading and maintenance regimens.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current SPINRAZA labeling has no boxed warning. Antisense-class thrombocytopenia and coagulation abnormalities can increase lumbar-puncture bleeding risk, and renal toxicity can present with proteinuria. Obtain platelets, prothrombin time, activated partial thromboplastin time, and quantitative spot urine protein at baseline and before every dose as labeled.

Mechanism

Nusinersen binds the intronic splice silencing site ISS-N1 downstream of exon 7 in SMN2 pre-mRNA. This displaces inhibitory splicing factors and permits spliceosome inclusion of exon 7, converting more SMN2 transcripts from an unstable truncated product into full-length SMN protein needed for motor-neuron RNA handling, axonal transport, neuromuscular-junction integrity, and survival.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify current loading or maintenance point, last dose, platelet/coagulation and urine-protein results, infection, anticoagulants, spinal anatomy/hardware, sedation plan, CSF volume removal, and intrathecal-only route before administration.

  • After lumbar puncture monitor headache, leak, back/radicular pain, bleeding, infection, respiratory status, and new neurologic deficit; continue longitudinal motor, bulbar, respiratory, growth and nutritional assessment.

Key Labs

  • Platelet count, PT/INR, aPTT, and quantitative spot urine protein at baseline and before each dose; renal function and clinical motor/respiratory outcome measures as indicated.

Nclex Traps

  • Nusinersen changes SMN2 splicing; it does not edit or replace the defective SMN1 gene.

  • A normal platelet count alone does not clear an intrathecal dose when coagulation, urine protein, infection, or procedural safety is abnormal.

Description

Nusinersen is an intrathecal RNA-splicing modulator and antisense oligonucleotide that corrects SMN2 pre-mRNA splicing, increases exon 7 retention, and raises production of full-length survival motor neuron protein in 5q spinal muscular atrophy. It acts within the central nervous system and is given by lumbar puncture; it does not replace respiratory, nutritional, mobility, or aspiration support and cannot restore motor neurons already lost.

Used To Treat

Toxicity, overdose, reversal, or rescue situations for a specific medication or poison exposure.

Mechanism

Dalteparin works by binding antithrombin through a heparin pentasaccharide sequence and accelerating factor Xa inhibition more than thrombin inhibition. It prevents clot extension but creates bleeding and HIT concerns; renal function, weight, platelet trend, and neuraxial timing matter.

Boxed Warning

Thrombolytics can cause severe or fatal bleeding, including intracranial hemorrhage.

Contraindications

  • Known serious hypersensitivity to the medication/class.

  • Clarify before giving if pregnancy, severe renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vitals, or high-risk interactions are present.

  • Active bleeding, suspected intracranial hemorrhage, recent major surgery/trauma, severe uncontrolled hypertension, and other protocol-specific exclusions.

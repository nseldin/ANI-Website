Mechanism

Pseudoephedrine indirectly and directly increases adrenergic signaling, especially alpha-mediated vasoconstriction in nasal mucosa. Systemic sympathetic stimulation explains tachycardia, hypertension, palpitations, urinary retention, insomnia, and caution in uncontrolled hypertension or stimulant stacking.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for pseudoephedrine in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Pseudoephedrine is a systemic sympathomimetic nasal decongestant: it indirectly and directly increases adrenergic signaling, especially alpha-mediated vasoconstriction in nasal mucosa. It is used for temporary nasal/sinus congestion relief when systemic adrenergic stimulation is safe for the client.

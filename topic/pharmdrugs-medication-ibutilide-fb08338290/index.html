Class

Class III antiarrhythmic; potassium-channel blocker

Used To Treat

Acute conversion of atrial fibrillation or atrial flutter in selected monitored settings.

Boxed Warning

Drug-specific boxed warning: can cause life-threatening dysrhythmias, especially sustained polymorphic ventricular tachycardia.

Mechanism

Ibutilide works by blocking cardiac potassium currents so repolarization and the effective refractory period last longer. That can interrupt reentry rhythms, but it also lengthens QT and can provoke torsades when bradycardia, hypokalemia, hypomagnesemia, renal accumulation, or other QT-prolonging drugs are present.

Nursing Essentials

  • Continuous ECG monitoring is required. Correct potassium and magnesium before administration.

  • Have resuscitation equipment available.

Interactions

  • Other QT-prolonging drugs increase torsades risk.

  • Low potassium or magnesium from diuretics, vomiting, diarrhea, or poor intake increases risk.

  • Other antiarrhythmics require careful timing and specialist direction.

Name

WBC

Range

0-1 mo 9,000-30,000/mm3; 1-3 mo 5,000-19,500; 1-2 yr 6,000-17,000; 2-4 yr 5,500-15,500; 5-11 yr 5,000-14,500

Why

Younger children can run higher WBC ranges; pair with low-grade fever or higher fever, appearance, perfusion, and source.

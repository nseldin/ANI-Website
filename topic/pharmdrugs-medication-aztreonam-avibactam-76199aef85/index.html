Class

Monobactam plus diazabicyclooctane serine-beta-lactamase inhibitor for MBL-producing Enterobacterales

Used To Treat

In combination with metronidazole, complicated intra-abdominal infection in adults with limited or no alternatives caused by susceptible gram-negative organisms; anaerobic coverage comes from metronidazole, not EMBLAVEO.

Description

Aztreonam-avibactam is an IV resistance-targeted combination designed for difficult Enterobacterales, including isolates that produce a class B metallo-beta-lactamase plus one or more serine beta-lactamases. Aztreonam survives NDM, VIM, or IMP hydrolysis but can be destroyed by co-produced ESBL, AmpC, KPC, or OXA-48-like enzymes; avibactam blocks many of those serine enzymes and protects the monobactam.

Mechanism

Aztreonam binds bacterial PBPs and prevents peptidoglycan cross-linking. Its monobactam structure is stable to class B zinc-dependent metallo-beta-lactamases. Avibactam forms a reversible covalent adduct with class A, class C, and some class D serine beta-lactamases, including ESBLs, KPC, AmpC, and OXA-48-like enzymes. The combination therefore closes the common loophole in which an MBL-producing organism also makes a serine enzyme that would otherwise hydrolyze aztreonam.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current EMBLAVEO labeling has no boxed warning. Stop immediately for hypersensitivity or bronchospasm; aztreonam-associated toxic epidermal necrolysis has been reported in heavily predisposed transplant patients. Monitor aminotransferase elevations and consider discontinuation when clinically indicated. Clostridioides difficile-associated diarrhea, anemia, hypokalemia, fever and renal-dose requirements are important.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm the organism, infection site, susceptibility and suspected beta-lactamase pattern; add metronidazole for the labeled complicated intra-abdominal indication because EMBLAVEO itself does not provide dependable anaerobic coverage.

  • Use the renal-function-specific loading, maintenance and infusion schedule. Trend abdominal source control, cultures, fever, WBC, liver tests, potassium, hemoglobin, allergy and diarrhea.

Key Labs

  • Intra-abdominal/blood cultures and susceptibility, creatinine clearance, CBC, potassium, AST/ALT/bilirubin, temperature and source-control response.

Nclex Traps

  • Avibactam does not inhibit NDM, VIM, or IMP. The combination works because aztreonam resists those MBLs while avibactam blocks accompanying serine enzymes.

  • For labeled cIAI use, metronidazole supplies anaerobic activity and cannot be omitted by assuming the combination covers everything.

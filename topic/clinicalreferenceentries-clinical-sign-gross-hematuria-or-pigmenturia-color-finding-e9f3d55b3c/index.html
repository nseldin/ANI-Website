Definition

Gross hematuria or pigmenturia color finding is an observed specimen sign. It is a clue to gross hematuria or pigmenturia. A positive finding means urine appears red, pink, tea, or cola colored.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Gross hematuria or pigmenturia color finding means urine appears red, pink, tea, or cola colored.

Why It Matters

The observed Gross hematuria or pigmenturia color finding finding can signal gross hematuria or pigmenturia. Intact blood, lysed hemoglobin, myoglobin, pigments, or concentrated metabolites alter urine color by different mechanisms. Gross hematuria or pigmenturia color finding cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • gross hematuria or pigmenturia

Assessment Method

Inspect freshly voided urine under appropriate conditions and confirm with urinalysis.

Positive Finding

Urine appears red, pink, tea, or cola colored.

Mechanism

Intact blood, lysed hemoglobin, myoglobin, pigments, or concentrated metabolites alter urine color by different mechanisms.

Use Gross hematuria or pigmenturia color finding to focus evaluation of gross hematuria or pigmenturia and the reviewed alternatives: beeturia, rifampin, porphyria. Evidence Limitations

Foods and medicines frequently mimic blood and visible color misses microscopic hematuria. Therefore, Gross hematuria or pigmenturia color finding cannot confirm or exclude gross hematuria or pigmenturia by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

False Negative Situations

Gross hematuria or pigmenturia color finding may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of gross hematuria or pigmenturia.

Definition

Half-and-half nails is an observed nail sign. It is a clue to chronic kidney disease and other systemic illness. A positive finding means the proximal nail is pale or white and the distal portion is red-brown.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Half-and-half nails means the proximal nail is pale or white and the distal portion is red-brown.

Why It Matters

The observed Half-and-half nails finding can signal chronic kidney disease and other systemic illness. Nail-bed vascular and melanin changes associated with chronic uremia create a biphasic appearance. Half-and-half nails cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • chronic kidney disease and other systemic illness

Assessment Method

Inspect multiple nails for proximal and distal color zones.

Positive Finding

The proximal nail is pale or white and the distal portion is red-brown.

Mechanism

Nail-bed vascular and melanin changes associated with chronic uremia create a biphasic appearance.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Half-and-half nails finding raises concern for chronic kidney disease and other systemic illness because nail-bed vascular and melanin changes associated with chronic uremia create a biphasic appearance. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Half-and-half nails does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Half-and-half nails to focus evaluation of chronic kidney disease and other systemic illness and the reviewed alternatives: Terry nails, onychomycosis, traumatic discoloration. Evidence Limitations

It is absent in many CKD patients and occurs without kidney disease. Therefore, Half-and-half nails cannot confirm or exclude chronic kidney disease and other systemic illness by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as Terry nails, onychomycosis, traumatic discoloration.

False Negative Situations

Half-and-half nails may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of chronic kidney disease and other systemic illness.

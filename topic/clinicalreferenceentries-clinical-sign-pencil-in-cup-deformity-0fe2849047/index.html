Definition

Pencil-in-cup deformity is an imaging sign. It is a clue to erosive psoriatic arthritis. A positive finding means a tapered eroded bone end sits within an expanded concave adjacent surface.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Pencil-in-cup deformity means a tapered eroded bone end sits within an expanded concave adjacent surface.

Why It Matters

The Pencil-in-cup deformity imaging pattern can signal erosive psoriatic arthritis. Aggressive marginal erosion and remodeling create reciprocal pencil and cup shapes. Pencil-in-cup deformity cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • erosive psoriatic arthritis

Assessment Method

Review hand or foot radiographs.

Positive Finding

A tapered eroded bone end sits within an expanded concave adjacent surface.

Mechanism

Aggressive marginal erosion and remodeling create reciprocal pencil and cup shapes.

Clinical Interpretation

The Pencil-in-cup deformity imaging pattern raises concern for erosive psoriatic arthritis because aggressive marginal erosion and remodeling create reciprocal pencil and cup shapes. Evidence Limitations

It reflects established structural damage and is not sensitive early. Therefore, Pencil-in-cup deformity cannot confirm or exclude erosive psoriatic arthritis by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

A false-positive interpretation can result from acquisition artifact, an inappropriate imaging phase or plane, or overlap with rheumatoid arthritis, gout, neuropathic arthropathy.

False Negative Situations

Definition

Dupuytren contracture sign is an observed and palpated hand sign. It is a clue to palmar fibromatosis associated with varied conditions. A positive finding means nodules and cords flex one or more fingers and prevent full extension.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Dupuytren contracture sign means nodules and cords flex one or more fingers and prevent full extension.

Why It Matters

The observed Dupuytren contracture sign finding can signal palmar fibromatosis associated with varied conditions. Fibroblast proliferation and collagen contraction shorten the palmar fascia. Dupuytren contracture sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

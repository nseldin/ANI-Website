Description

NSAIDs are medications grouped by a shared clinical action: inhibit cyclooxygenase pathways, reducing prostaglandin-mediated inflammation, pain, and fever. Common uses include pain, fever, inflammation, and selected platelet inhibition with aspirin. Use the linked examples to compare drug-specific warnings, contraindications, labs, and nursing care.

Definition

Sporothrix schenckii is a clinically important fungus associated with lymphocutaneous sporotrichosis, pulmonary sporotrichosis, and disseminated sporotrichosis. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

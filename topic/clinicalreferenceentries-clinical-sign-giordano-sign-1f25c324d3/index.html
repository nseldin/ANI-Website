Definition

Giordano sign is a bedside percussion sign. It is a clue to renal or perirenal inflammation. A positive finding means percussion produces marked unilateral flank pain.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Giordano sign means percussion produces marked unilateral flank pain.

Why It Matters

A positive Giordano sign response can signal renal or perirenal inflammation. Mechanical energy moves an inflamed renal capsule or perirenal tissue and activates somatic pain fibers. Giordano sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • renal or perirenal inflammation

Assessment Method

Place one hand over the costovertebral angle and gently strike it with the ulnar surface of the other fist.

Positive Finding

Percussion produces marked unilateral flank pain.

Mechanism

Mechanical energy moves an inflamed renal capsule or perirenal tissue and activates somatic pain fibers.

Clinical Interpretation

A positive Giordano sign response raises concern for renal or perirenal inflammation because mechanical energy moves an inflamed renal capsule or perirenal tissue and activates somatic pain fibers. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Giordano sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Giordano sign to focus evaluation of renal or perirenal inflammation and the reviewed alternatives: rib injury, paraspinal strain, herpes zoster. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Place one hand over the costovertebral angle and gently strike it with the ulnar surface of the other fist. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Giordano sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Musculoskeletal tenderness and variable force limit specificity; absence does not exclude infection. Therefore, Giordano sign cannot confirm or exclude renal or perirenal inflammation by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • rib injury

  • paraspinal strain

  • herpes zoster

False Positive Situations

A false-positive maneuver can result from incorrect position or force, pain from rib injury, paraspinal strain, herpes zoster, guarding, or calling an incompletely defined response positive.

False Negative Situations

Giordano sign may be missed with incomplete technique, guarding, early or partial disease, limited cooperation, or a response too subtle for the examiner.

Modern Status

Current adjunct; nonspecific. When performed as described, Giordano sign can adjust concern for renal or perirenal inflammation, but it is not a stand-alone diagnosis. Apply the reviewed limitation for Giordano sign: Musculoskeletal tenderness and variable force limit specificity; absence does not exclude infection.

Safety Precautions

Before assessing Giordano sign, explain the method, obtain consent, preserve privacy, and use only the technique described here: Place one hand over the costovertebral angle and gently strike it with the ulnar surface of the other fist. Stop if that technique becomes unsafe. The reviewed precaution for this maneuver is: Use gentle force and do not repeatedly strike a painful or traumatized flank.

Nursing Relevance

Continue the focused evaluation by following the reviewed method and its limits: Place one hand over the costovertebral angle and gently strike it with the ulnar surface of the other fist. Communicate the finding with its suspected association, renal or perirenal inflammation, when it is new, worsening, or clinically important.

Document the exact finding, assessment method, side or distribution, timing, patient response, associated symptoms and vital signs, and change from baseline. Record the observed result instead of writing only "positive" or "negative."

Base escalation for Giordano sign on the patient's current condition, associated findings, and the cause under evaluation; this card does not assign a universal urgency level.

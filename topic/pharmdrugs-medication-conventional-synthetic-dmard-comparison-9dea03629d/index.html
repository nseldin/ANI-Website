Used To Treat

Long-term suppression of inflammatory arthritis and selected autoimmune disease. Methotrexate is a common anchor for rheumatoid arthritis; hydroxychloroquine is useful in milder rheumatoid disease and lupus; leflunomide suppresses activated lymphocyte pyrimidine synthesis; sulfasalazine is used in rheumatoid arthritis and inflammatory bowel disease. These drugs modify disease over weeks to months and do not provide immediate analgesia.

Description

Conventional synthetic DMARDs slow immune-mediated tissue injury through different intracellular routes. Low-dose methotrexate increases anti-inflammatory adenosine signaling after intracellular polyglutamation. Hydroxychloroquine accumulates in acidic endosomes and lysosomes, altering antigen processing and nucleic-acid sensing. Leflunomide becomes teriflunomide, which blocks de novo pyrimidine production needed by rapidly activated lymphocytes. Colonic bacteria split sulfasalazine into sulfapyridine and 5-aminosalicylic acid; systemic sulfapyridine is important to rheumatoid benefit while 5-ASA acts mainly in the colon. Because the mechanisms, time courses, organ toxicities, and pregnancy implications differ, the drugs are not interchangeable entries under a generic DMARD label.

Mechanism

Methotrexate polyglutamates persist inside cells and inhibit folate-dependent enzymes including ATIC; AICAR accumulates, extracellular adenosine rises, and A2A-receptor signaling suppresses neutrophil and macrophage inflammation. Hydroxychloroquine is a lysosomotropic weak base that raises endosomal pH and reduces TLR7/TLR9 nucleic-acid signaling, antigen processing, and downstream interferon/cytokine activation. Teriflunomide reversibly inhibits mitochondrial dihydroorotate dehydrogenase, limiting de novo uridine synthesis and slowing activated T- and B-cell proliferation. Sulfasalazine is azo-cleaved in the colon; its exact rheumatoid mechanism is not singular, but sulfapyridine exposure alters prostaglandin, cytokine, NF-kappaB, and immune-cell signaling. These distinct pathways explain why toxicity monitoring must follow the molecule rather than the word DMARD.

Boxed Warning

Methotrexate has boxed warnings for embryo-fetal toxicity, severe reactions, hypersensitivity, and fatal medication errors from daily instead of weekly dosing. Leflunomide has boxed warnings for embryo-fetal toxicity and hepatotoxicity. Hydroxychloroquine and sulfasalazine have no boxed warning but can still cause irreversible retinal injury or cardiomyopathy, and severe blood, liver, kidney, lung, or hypersensitivity reactions. A class comparison never substitutes for the exact product warning.

Adverse Effects

  • Methotrexate and leflunomide: liver injury, cytopenias, infection, pulmonary toxicity, gastrointestinal injury, and fetal harm

  • Hydroxychloroquine and sulfasalazine: retinal/cardiac toxicity or severe hypersensitivity, cytopenias, hemolysis, hepatotoxicity, nephrotoxicity, and gastrointestinal intolerance

Contraindications

  • Clarify pregnancy, liver and kidney function, cytopenias, active infection, vaccination status, lung disease, alcohol exposure, and interacting drugs before selection.

  • Do not collapse sulfonamide sensitivity, retinal risk, folate antagonism, or a teriflunomide washout need into one generic checklist.

Nursing Essentials

  • Record the exact drug, dose frequency, indication, baseline screening, and monitoring cadence; methotrexate for inflammatory disease is commonly weekly, never automatically daily.

  • Teach that benefit is delayed, adherence and laboratory surveillance prevent avoidable injury, and symptom relief does not prove inflammatory damage is controlled.

Interactions

  • Methotrexate toxicity rises when renal elimination is impaired or interacting antifolates, trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole, NSAID contexts, or other hepatotoxins are added.

  • Hydroxychloroquine adds QT and hypoglycemia risk; leflunomide has transporter interactions and prolonged persistence; sulfasalazine can reduce folate or digoxin absorption.

Key Labs

  • CBC, liver tests, renal function, pregnancy assessment, infection screening, and disease-activity trend selected for the exact drug

  • Eye examination for hydroxychloroquine; blood pressure and washout verification for leflunomide; urinalysis and hemolysis assessment for sulfasalazine when indicated

Nclex Traps

  • DMARD means disease-modifying, not rapid pain relief. Improvement can take weeks while structural protection requires sustained control.

  • Folic acid reduces several methotrexate toxicities but does not make a daily dosing error safe or reverse embryo-fetal risk.

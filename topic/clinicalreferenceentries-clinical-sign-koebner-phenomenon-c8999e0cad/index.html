Definition

Koebner phenomenon is an observed lesion-distribution sign. It is a clue to isomorphic appearance of disease at trauma sites. A positive finding means typical lesions of the underlying dermatosis develop along injured skin.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Koebner phenomenon means typical lesions of the underlying dermatosis develop along injured skin.

Why It Matters

The observed Koebner phenomenon finding can signal isomorphic appearance of disease at trauma sites. Trauma releases inflammatory signals that recruit disease-specific immune responses into previously uninvolved skin. Koebner phenomenon cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

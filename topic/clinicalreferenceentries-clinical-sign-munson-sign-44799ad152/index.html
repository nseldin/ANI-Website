Definition

Munson sign is an observed eyelid-contour sign. It is a clue to advanced keratoconus. A positive finding means the conical cornea creates a V-shaped bulge in the lower lid.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Munson sign means the conical cornea creates a V-shaped bulge in the lower lid.

Why It Matters

The observed Munson sign finding can signal advanced keratoconus. Progressive corneal thinning and ectasia push the lid outward. Munson sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • advanced keratoconus

Assessment Method

Ask the patient to look downward and observe the lower lid.

Positive Finding

The conical cornea creates a V-shaped bulge in the lower lid.

Mechanism

Progressive corneal thinning and ectasia push the lid outward.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Munson sign finding raises concern for advanced keratoconus because progressive corneal thinning and ectasia push the lid outward. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Munson sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Munson sign to focus evaluation of advanced keratoconus and the reviewed alternatives: globe proptosis, lid mass, pellucid degeneration. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Ask the patient to look downward and observe the lower lid. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Munson sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

It appears only in advanced disease and is not a screening sign. Therefore, Munson sign cannot confirm or exclude advanced keratoconus by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • globe proptosis

  • lid mass

  • pellucid degeneration

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as globe proptosis, lid mass, pellucid degeneration.

False Negative Situations

Munson sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of advanced keratoconus.

Modern Status

Current late keratoconus sign. Interpret Munson sign in the assessment context defined for this card because its reviewed limitation is topic-specific: It appears only in advanced disease and is not a screening sign.

Safety Precautions

Observe Munson sign with consent, privacy, adequate exposure, and only the manipulation required by this assessment: Ask the patient to look downward and observe the lower lid. The reviewed precaution for this finding is: Use corneal topography for diagnosis and advise against eye rubbing.

Nursing Relevance

Document the exact finding, assessment method, side or distribution, timing, patient response, associated symptoms and vital signs, and change from baseline. Record the observed result instead of writing only "positive" or "negative."

Used To Treat

Depression and selected anxiety, pain, sleep, OCD, PTSD, or panic indications depending on the medication.

Mechanism

Modulates monoamine neurotransmission. SSRIs increase serotonin, SNRIs increase serotonin/norepinephrine, TCAs block reuptake plus anticholinergic effects, and MAOIs block monoamine breakdown.

Boxed Warning

Antidepressant boxed warning: increased risk of suicidal thoughts and behaviors in children, adolescents, and young adults. Monitor closely after starting or changing dose.

Contraindications

  • Known serious hypersensitivity to the medication/class.

  • Clarify before giving if pregnancy, severe renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vitals, or high-risk interactions are present.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

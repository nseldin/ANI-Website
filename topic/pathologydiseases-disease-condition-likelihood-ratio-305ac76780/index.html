Plain Meaning

A likelihood ratio tells how much a test result shifts the probability of disease.

Pathology

LR+ compares how likely a positive result is in disease versus no disease; higher LR+ values rule in more strongly. LR- compares how likely a negative result is in disease versus no disease; lower LR- values rule out more strongly. Likelihood ratios help move from pretest probability to post-test probability.

Pathophysiology

LRs combine sensitivity and specificity into a result-specific probability shift. They are especially useful because they can be applied to the client's pretest probability instead of pretending every client starts with the same risk.

Signs Symptoms

  • Strong positive LR meaningfully increases disease probability

  • Strong low LR- meaningfully decreases disease probability

  • Weak LRs barely move probability and should not drive major decisions alone

Diagnostics

  • Use with pretest probability, clinical stability, result timing, and whether the test was performed correctly

  • Positive and negative likelihood ratios are different; do not use one number for both directions

Treatments

  • Use strong probability shifts to support rule-in/rule-out decisions when aligned with clinical judgment

  • Pursue additional testing when the LR leaves probability in an uncertain zone

Nursing Priorities

  • Think of LR as the test's probability lever

  • Do not let a weak test result overrule a dangerous clinical picture

  • Communicate whether a result strongly changes concern or only nudges it

Complications

  • Overconfidence in weak tests

  • Delayed diagnosis

  • Unnecessary treatment

  • Failure to escalate unstable clients

Patient Education

  • Some test results strongly change the plan; others only guide the next test.

  • Follow-up may still be needed when probability remains uncertain.

Nclex Traps

  • Likelihood ratio is not the same as sensitivity or specificity alone.

  • A test result changes probability; it does not replace assessment.

  • High-risk symptoms can require action while testing continues.

Definition

S3 gallop is an auscultatory cardiac sign. It is a clue to rapid ventricular filling with volume overload or reduced compliance. A positive finding means a low-frequency sound follows S2 early in diastole.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive S3 gallop means a low-frequency sound follows S2 early in diastole.

Why It Matters

A positive S3 gallop response can signal rapid ventricular filling with volume overload or reduced compliance. Rapid inflow abruptly decelerates against a dilated or volume-loaded ventricle, vibrating the ventricular wall and blood column. S3 gallop cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • rapid ventricular filling with volume overload or reduced compliance

Assessment Method

Listen at the apex with the bell in left lateral position and at the lower sternal border for right-sided origin.

Positive Finding

A low-frequency sound follows S2 early in diastole.

Mechanism

Rapid inflow abruptly decelerates against a dilated or volume-loaded ventricle, vibrating the ventricular wall and blood column.

Clinical Interpretation

A positive S3 gallop response raises concern for rapid ventricular filling with volume overload or reduced compliance because rapid inflow abruptly decelerates against a dilated or volume-loaded ventricle, vibrating the ventricular wall and blood column. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; S3 gallop does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Differential Diagnoses

  • physiologic youthful S3

False Positive Situations

A false-positive maneuver can result from incorrect position or force, pain from physiologic youthful S3, split S2, pericardial knock, guarding, or calling an incompletely defined response positive.

False Negative Situations

S3 gallop may be missed with incomplete technique, guarding, early or partial disease, limited cooperation, or a response too subtle for the examiner.

Modern Status

Current important sign; can be physiologic in youth and pregnancy. Interpret S3 gallop in the assessment context defined for this card because its reviewed limitation is topic-specific: It is difficult to hear and age changes interpretation.

Safety Precautions

Before assessing S3 gallop, explain the method, obtain consent, preserve privacy, and use only the technique described here: Listen at the apex with the bell in left lateral position and at the lower sternal border for right-sided origin. Stop if that technique becomes unsafe. The reviewed precaution for this maneuver is: Time the sound with the carotid pulse and distinguish it from an opening snap.

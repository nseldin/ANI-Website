Summary

Carotid duplex ultrasound is a high-yield Cardiovascular diagnostic reference. Uses ultrasound and Doppler to evaluate carotid stenosis, plaque, and stroke/TIA risk.

Quick Answer

Carotid duplex ultrasound: Uses ultrasound and Doppler to evaluate carotid stenosis, plaque, and stroke/TIA risk. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Uses ultrasound and Doppler to evaluate carotid stenosis, plaque, and stroke/TIA risk.

    • Before / preparation

    • Screen for pregnancy when applicable, contrast allergy, kidney risk if contrast may be used, metal/implant restrictions for MRI, and ability to lie still.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Explain the procedure, protect safety during transport, monitor after contrast or sedation if used, and act on urgent findings instead of waiting passively.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Airway symptoms after contrast, new neuro change, unstable vital signs, severe pain, bleeding, or imaging results that suggest hemorrhage, obstruction, embolus, or ischemia.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • The test does not replace assessment. A stable-looking image order can still become urgent if the client deteriorates.

Definition

Giant a waves is an observed jugular waveform sign. It is a clue to increased resistance to right-atrial emptying. A positive finding means regularly enlarged a waves precede each ventricular systole.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Giant a waves means regularly enlarged a waves precede each ventricular systole.

Why It Matters

The observed Giant a waves finding can signal increased resistance to right-atrial emptying. Right-atrial contraction encounters a narrowed tricuspid valve or stiff hypertensive right ventricle. Giant a waves cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • increased resistance to right-atrial emptying

Regularly enlarged a waves precede each ventricular systole.

Right-atrial contraction encounters a narrowed tricuspid valve or stiff hypertensive right ventricle.

The observed Giant a waves finding raises concern for increased resistance to right-atrial emptying because right-atrial contraction encounters a narrowed tricuspid valve or stiff hypertensive right ventricle.

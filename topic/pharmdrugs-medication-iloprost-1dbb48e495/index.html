Class

Prostacyclin (PGI2) analog vasodilator

Used To Treat

Inhaled pulmonary arterial hypertension therapy and, by IV formulation where approved, severe frostbite to reduce digit-amputation risk.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current VENTAVIS and AURLUMYN labels have no boxed warning. Correct hypotension before IV AURLUMYN and monitor vital signs with initiation and every dose increase because systemic vasodilation can cause symptomatic hypotension. Inhaled VENTAVIS can cause syncope, hypotension, bronchospasm, and pulmonary edema; worsening edema or oxygenation may indicate PVOD and requires reassessment rather than another inhalation.

Mechanism

Iloprost activates Gs-coupled IP receptors on vascular smooth muscle and platelets. Adenylyl cyclase raises cAMP and protein kinase A signaling, reducing calcium-dependent contraction and platelet activation. In PAH this lowers pulmonary vascular resistance and right-ventricular afterload; in frostbite it improves flow through constricted, injured microvasculature. Systemic vasodilation explains hypotension, dizziness, flushing, tachycardia, and headache, while platelet effects create bleeding concern.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm brand, formulation, indication, and route before preparation. For VENTAVIS assess blood pressure, syncope, airway reactivity, oxygenation, device technique, and prescribed waking-day schedule. For AURLUMYN verify adult severe frostbite, weight-based rate, baseline pressure, 30-minute titration steps, 6-hour daily infusion, and maximum 8-day course.

  • Why the 8-day limit? Current frostbite efficacy and dosing evidence supports up to 8 consecutive days; it is an evidence boundary tied to the studied regimen, not proof that toxicity begins automatically on day 9. Continue only under a new specialist benefit-risk decision.

  • Monitor blood pressure with each IV increase, limb color/warmth/capillary refill/pain and demarcation for frostbite, or oxygenation, syncope, cough, bronchospasm, edema, and PAH response for inhaled therapy.

Contraindications

  • AURLUMYN lists no formal contraindication, but preexisting symptomatic hypotension must be corrected before infusion.

  • VENTAVIS requires caution with low blood pressure, bleeding risk, reactive airway disease, and pulmonary edema; exact product instructions govern use.

Interactions

  • Anticoagulants and antiplatelet drugs can add bleeding risk, and other vasodilators or antihypertensives can add hypotension.

  • VENTAVIS inhalation devices and AURLUMYN infusion preparation are product specific; never inject inhalation solution or nebulize the IV product.

Key Labs

  • Blood pressure and hemodynamics, oxygenation, CBC and bleeding assessment, renal/hepatic context for IV titration, and indication-specific limb perfusion or PAH functional/right-heart response.

Nclex Traps

  • VENTAVIS inhalation treats PAH; AURLUMYN IV treats severe frostbite. Same molecule does not mean same route, schedule, or indication.

  • The IV maximum of 8 consecutive days is a studied-regimen boundary, not a universal prostacyclin rule.

  • Pulmonary edema after inhaled vasodilation can signal PVOD.

Class Pathway

Prostacyclin analog > IP receptor-Gs-cAMP > Inhaled PAH or IV severe-frostbite formulation

Description

Iloprost is a prostacyclin analog that activates IP receptors, raises cAMP, dilates arteries, and inhibits platelet activation in two route-specific products. Inhaled VENTAVIS treats adult WHO Group 1 PAH, while intravenous AURLUMYN treats severe adult frostbite to reduce digit-amputation risk; their devices, doses, schedules, and routes are not interchangeable. Inhalation produces repeated pulmonary vasodilation during waking hours and can provoke cough or bronchospasm; frostbite therapy is a monitored 6-hour daily infusion for up to 8 consecutive days, using microvascular dilation to improve threatened tissue perfusion.

Adverse Effects

  • Headache, flushing, jaw pain, nausea, vomiting, dizziness, hypotension, palpitations or tachycardia, and bleeding are prostacyclin-related effects.

  • Inhaled use adds cough, bronchospasm, and throat discomfort. IV frostbite infusion can be dose limited by vasodilator symptoms.

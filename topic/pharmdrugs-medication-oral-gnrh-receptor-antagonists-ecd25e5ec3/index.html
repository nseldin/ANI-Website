Class Example Names

Used To Treat

Elagolix treats moderate-to-severe endometriosis-associated pain; single-agent relugolix treats advanced prostate cancer, while relugolix also appears in a separate estrogen/progestin combination product for selected gynecologic indications.

Description

Oral GnRH receptor antagonists competitively block GnRH receptors on anterior-pituitary gonadotrophs, rapidly lowering LH and FSH without the initial testosterone flare produced by GnRH agonists. The downstream hormone differs by patient context: elagolix suppresses ovarian estradiol and progesterone, whereas relugolix monotherapy suppresses testicular testosterone. They share a receptor but are not interchangeable indications or dosing regimens.

Mechanism

GnRH normally arrives in pulses and activates pituitary Gq/11 signaling, driving gonadotropin secretion. Competitive blockade lowers LH/FSH within hours to days, reducing gonadal steroid synthesis. Suppression is reversible after discontinuation, but hypoestrogenism can reduce bone density and androgen deprivation can alter metabolism, hemoglobin, sexual function, cardiovascular physiology, and QT risk.

Boxed Warning

Single-agent elagolix and relugolix currently have no boxed warning. Elagolix is dose- and duration-limited by potentially incomplete bone-density recovery and also carries pregnancy-loss, mood/suicide, hepatic, and contraceptive issues. Relugolix requires QT/electrolyte assessment, P-gp/CYP3A interaction management, testosterone/PSA monitoring, and embryo-fetal contraception counseling.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm exact molecule and product: ORILISSA is elagolix alone; ORGOVYX is relugolix alone; MYFEMBREE adds estradiol and norethindrone and has a different boxed-warning framework.

  • Track symptoms and target hormones while monitoring bone, mood, pregnancy, metabolic, cardiovascular, hepatic, and interaction risks appropriate to the indication.

Key Labs

  • Product-specific pregnancy testing, BMD when risk warrants, liver tests, mood assessment, testosterone and PSA for prostate cancer, glucose/lipids/hemoglobin, ECG and electrolytes when QT risk is present.

Nclex Traps

  • Antagonists suppress immediately and do not cause the initial agonist flare.

  • Reduced menstrual bleeding can hide pregnancy during elagolix treatment; it is not reliable contraception.

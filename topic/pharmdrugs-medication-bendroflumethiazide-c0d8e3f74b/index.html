Description

Bendroflumethiazide is a thiazide/thiazide-like diuretic: it works by blocking sodium-chloride reabsorption in the distal convoluted tubule, increasing diuresis while raising hypokalemia, hyponatremia, uric acid, glucose, and photosensitivity concerns. It is used for hypertension and edema management. Thiazides are also useful study anchors for kidney sodium handling, potassium loss, and calcium retention.

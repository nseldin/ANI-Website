Definition

Eruptive xanthomas is an observed skin sign. It is a clue to severe hypertriglyceridemia. A positive finding means crops of small yellow-red papules appear abruptly.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Eruptive xanthomas means crops of small yellow-red papules appear abruptly.

Why It Matters

The observed Eruptive xanthomas finding can signal severe hypertriglyceridemia. Triglyceride-rich lipoproteins are taken up by dermal macrophages, forming lipid-laden foam cells. Eruptive xanthomas cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • severe hypertriglyceridemia

Assessment Method

Inspect extensor surfaces and buttocks.

Positive Finding

Crops of small yellow-red papules appear abruptly.

Mechanism

Triglyceride-rich lipoproteins are taken up by dermal macrophages, forming lipid-laden foam cells.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Eruptive xanthomas finding raises concern for severe hypertriglyceridemia because triglyceride-rich lipoproteins are taken up by dermal macrophages, forming lipid-laden foam cells. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Eruptive xanthomas does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Eruptive xanthomas to focus evaluation of severe hypertriglyceridemia and the reviewed alternatives: folliculitis, molluscum contagiosum, disseminated histiocytosis. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect extensor surfaces and buttocks. Evidence Limitations

Lesions resemble acne or molluscum and may disappear as lipids fall. Therefore, Eruptive xanthomas cannot confirm or exclude severe hypertriglyceridemia by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as folliculitis, molluscum contagiosum, disseminated histiocytosis.

False Negative Situations

Eruptive xanthomas may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of severe hypertriglyceridemia.

Description

Divalproex is a broad-spectrum antiseizure medication and increases GABA and modulates sodium/calcium channels: it works by stabilizing neuronal firing through sodium channels, calcium channels, GABA, glutamate, or synaptic-vesicle targets depending on the agent. It is used for parkinson disease motor symptom treatment, wearing-off management, or levodopa support depending on component.

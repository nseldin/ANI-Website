Class

Calcium channel blocker

Used To Treat

Hypertension and angina. Diltiazem and verapamil are also used for selected supraventricular rate-control problems.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary by agent; major concerns include hypotension, bradycardia/heart block for non-DHP agents, and heart failure worsening.

Mechanism

Levamlodipine works by blocking L-type calcium channels. Dihydropyridines mainly vasodilate arteries; diltiazem/verapamil also slow AV nodal conduction, so heart rate and heart block matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Check BP; check HR for diltiazem/verapamil.

  • Monitor edema, constipation, dizziness, bradycardia.

Contraindications

  • Severe hypotension

  • advanced AV block/sick sinus without pacemaker for non-DHP agents

  • severe LV dysfunction for selected agents

Interactions

  • Beta blockers/digoxin with diltiazem/verapamil increase bradycardia/heart block.

  • Grapefruit may increase levels for some agents.

Key Labs

  • blood pressure

  • heart rate

  • ECG when indicated

Nclex Traps

  • Verapamil/diltiazem are not the same nursing check as amlodipine; HR matters for AV-nodal blockers.

Description

Levamlodipine is a calcium channel blocker: it works by blocking L-type calcium channels. It is used for hypertension and angina. Diltiazem and verapamil are also used for selected supraventricular rate-control problems.

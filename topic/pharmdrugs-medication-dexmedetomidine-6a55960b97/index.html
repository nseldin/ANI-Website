Class

Highly selective central alpha-2 adrenergic agonist sedative-sympatholytic

Used To Treat

IV sedation of initially intubated/mechanically ventilated patients in intensive care and sedation of nonintubated patients before or during procedures; formulation-specific indications and pediatric ages must be checked.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current PRECEDEX labeling has no boxed warning. Continuous monitoring is required for hypotension, bradycardia and sinus arrest; fatalities have occurred in susceptible patients. Rapid loading can cause transient hypertension. Prolonged exposure can produce tolerance, tachyphylaxis and withdrawal agitation, hypertension or tachycardia. Fever/hyperthermia may be drug related. Reduce dose with hepatic impairment and use caution with heart block or severe ventricular dysfunction.

Mechanism

Alpha-2A receptor activation in the locus coeruleus couples to Gi proteins, lowers cAMP, opens potassium channels and suppresses calcium-dependent norepinephrine release, producing a sleep-like sedative state. Similar presynaptic action in medullary sympathetic pathways reduces heart rate and vascular tone; spinal alpha-2 signaling contributes some analgesia. Rapid/high exposure also stimulates peripheral alpha receptors, explaining transient hypertension during loading.

Nursing Essentials

  • Establish a sedation target and baseline rhythm, blood pressure, volume status, hepatic function, neurologic exam, airway risk and concurrent opioids/negative chronotropes; titrate rather than chasing motionlessness.

  • Assess pain separately, maintain airway monitoring despite relative respiratory preservation, and taper after prolonged therapy while watching for rebound sympathetic activation.

Key Labs

  • Continuous ECG, heart rate, blood pressure, respiratory rate, oxygenation and sedation score; temperature, liver function, perfusion and withdrawal signs during prolonged use.

Nclex Traps

  • Arousable sedation is expected and does not necessarily mean under-sedation; set a target before escalating.

  • Minimal direct respiratory depression does not protect against airway obstruction, opioid synergy or respiratory failure from underlying disease.

Description

Dexmedetomidine is a titratable central alpha-2 agonist that produces cooperative, arousable sedation and sympatholysis with relatively little direct respiratory depression at labeled IV rates. It is not an analgesic substitute and can cause profound bradycardia, sinus arrest and hypotension, especially with hypovolemia, conduction disease, rapid loading or other negative chronotropes.

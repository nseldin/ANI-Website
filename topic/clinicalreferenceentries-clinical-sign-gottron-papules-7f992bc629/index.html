Definition

Gottron papules is an observed skin sign. It is a clue to dermatomyositis. A positive finding means violaceous or erythematous scaly papules overlie the knuckles.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Gottron papules means violaceous or erythematous scaly papules overlie the knuckles.

Why It Matters

The observed Gottron papules finding can signal dermatomyositis. Interface dermatitis and immune microvascular injury target extensor joint skin. Gottron papules cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • dermatomyositis

Assessment Method

Inspect extensor MCP and interphalangeal joints.

Positive Finding

Violaceous or erythematous scaly papules overlie the knuckles.

Mechanism

Interface dermatitis and immune microvascular injury target extensor joint skin.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Gottron papules finding raises concern for dermatomyositis because interface dermatitis and immune microvascular injury target extensor joint skin. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Gottron papules does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Evidence Limitations

Skin-tone variation and hand dermatitis can obscure them. Therefore, Gottron papules cannot confirm or exclude dermatomyositis by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as eczema, knuckle pads, lupus.

False Negative Situations

Gottron papules may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of dermatomyositis.

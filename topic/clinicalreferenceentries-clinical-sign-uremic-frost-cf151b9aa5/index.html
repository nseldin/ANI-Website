Definition

Uremic frost is an observed skin sign. It is a clue to profound untreated azotemia. A positive finding means white powder-like crystals coat the skin, often after sweat dries.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Uremic frost means white powder-like crystals coat the skin, often after sweat dries.

Why It Matters

The observed Uremic frost finding can signal profound untreated azotemia. Very high urea diffuses into sweat and crystallizes on evaporation. Uremic frost cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • profound untreated azotemia

Assessment Method

Inspect the face and upper body for a fine white crystalline coating.

Positive Finding

White powder-like crystals coat the skin, often after sweat dries.

Mechanism

Very high urea diffuses into sweat and crystallizes on evaporation.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Uremic frost finding raises concern for profound untreated azotemia because very high urea diffuses into sweat and crystallizes on evaporation. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Uremic frost does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Uremic frost to focus evaluation of profound untreated azotemia and the reviewed alternatives: dried sweat salt, xerosity, powder contamination. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Inspect the face and upper body for a fine white crystalline coating. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Uremic frost as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Appearance is nonspecific without severe biochemical uremia. Therefore, Uremic frost cannot confirm or exclude profound untreated azotemia by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • dried sweat salt

  • xerosity

  • powder contamination

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as dried sweat salt, xerosity, powder contamination.

False Negative Situations

Uremic frost may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of profound untreated azotemia.

Modern Status

Historical but real sign; now rare because dialysis is accessible. Uremic frost remains searchable because learners still encounter the term, but it cannot confirm or exclude profound untreated azotemia. Apply the reviewed limitation for Uremic frost: Appearance is nonspecific without severe biochemical uremia.

Observe Uremic frost with consent, privacy, adequate exposure, and only the manipulation required by this assessment: Inspect the face and upper body for a fine white crystalline coating. The reviewed precaution for this finding is: Do not scrape irritated skin; severe uremic symptoms need urgent renal evaluation.

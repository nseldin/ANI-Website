Class

Soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator

Used To Treat

Adults with symptomatic chronic heart failure and ejection fraction below 45% after a recent worsening event requiring hospitalization or outpatient IV diuretics, to reduce cardiovascular death and heart-failure hospitalization risk.

Boxed Warning

Embryo-fetal toxicity: do not use during pregnancy. Exclude pregnancy before treatment and use effective contraception during therapy and for one month after the last dose.

Mechanism

Vericiguat directly stimulates soluble guanylate cyclase and sensitizes it to endogenous nitric oxide. Increased cyclic GMP promotes vascular smooth-muscle relaxation and may improve myocardial/vascular function; unlike PDE5 inhibitors, it increases cGMP production rather than preventing cGMP breakdown.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor blood pressure, orthostasis, dizziness/syncope, hemoglobin, heart-failure symptoms, weight, perfusion, and adherence with food.

  • Avoid another soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator; concomitant PDE5-inhibitor use is not recommended because of hypotension risk.

Key Labs

  • Blood pressure, hemoglobin/hematocrit, renal/hepatic context, pregnancy testing when applicable, and heart-failure congestion/perfusion trends.

Nclex Traps

  • Vericiguat stimulates sGC; sildenafil inhibits PDE5. Both raise cGMP, so combining them can produce dangerous hypotension.

Description

Vericiguat is a soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator that directly stimulates sGC and sensitizes it to nitric oxide, increasing cGMP signaling in adults with recently worsening heart failure. It is not a substitute for foundational HFrEF therapy, and its pathway creates clinically important hypotension and embryo-fetal risk.

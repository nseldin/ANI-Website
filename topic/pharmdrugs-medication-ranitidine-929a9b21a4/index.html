Mechanism

Ranitidine works by blocking histamine H2 receptors on parietal cells, lowering gastric acid. Confusion risk in older adults and renal dosing are classic nursing angles.

Description

Ranitidine is a histamine-2 receptor antagonist: it works by blocking histamine H2 receptors on parietal cells, lowering gastric acid. It is used for GERD, heartburn, peptic ulcer disease, stress-ulcer prophylaxis in selected clients, and histamine-mediated symptoms when clinically indicated.

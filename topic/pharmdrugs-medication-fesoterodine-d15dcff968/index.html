Class

Antimuscarinic prodrug converted by esterases to 5-hydroxymethyl tolterodine

Used To Treat

Overactive bladder with urgency, frequency and urge incontinence in adults; current labeling also includes neurogenic detrusor overactivity in pediatric patients age 6 years and older with weight-based dosing.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Contraindicated with urinary retention, gastric retention or uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma. Angioedema can threaten the airway and has occurred after the first dose. Severe renal impairment, strong CYP3A4 inhibition and selected pediatric weights restrict maximum dosing.

Mechanism

Esterases convert fesoterodine to 5-HMT before it competitively blocks muscarinic receptors. M3 blockade reduces IP3-calcium-driven detrusor contraction and increases storage. The prodrug design bypasses tolterodine's CYP2D6-dependent activation step, but 5-HMT elimination still depends on CYP2D6/CYP3A4 metabolism and renal excretion. This explains both more predictable activation and persistent interaction/organ-function limits.

Nursing Essentials

  • Teach that ER tablets are swallowed whole and that new lip/tongue swelling is an airway emergency.

  • Check residual volume, renal function, bowel pattern and the complete anticholinergic medication list.

Contraindications

  • Urinary retention, gastric retention and uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma

  • Hypersensitivity to fesoterodine or tolterodine

  • Apply renal, hepatic, age/weight and strong-CYP3A4 dose restrictions

Interactions

  • Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors increase 5-HMT exposure and limit dose; inducers can reduce exposure.

  • Other anticholinergics add retention, constipation and cognitive toxicity.

  • CYP2D6 inhibition changes clearance even though esterase activation bypasses CYP2D6.

Key Labs

  • Renal and hepatic function

  • Post-void residual and symptom diary

  • Cognition and bowel function

Nclex Traps

  • Fesoterodine is not tolterodine under another name; it is a prodrug delivered directly to the shared 5-HMT pathway.

  • Esterase activation does not eliminate CYP interaction during metabolite clearance.

  • Angioedema is a drug-specific emergency beyond routine dry mouth.

Description

Fesoterodine is an antimuscarinic prodrug rapidly hydrolyzed by nonspecific esterases to 5-hydroxymethyl tolterodine, the same active metabolite produced from tolterodine. Because activation does not require CYP2D6, CYP2D6 genotype has less effect on formation of the active moiety; CYP2D6 and CYP3A4 still clear that metabolite, so renal function, strong CYP3A4 inhibitors and exposure-related anticholinergic effects remain important.

Adverse Effects

  • Dry mouth and constipation

  • Urinary retention and urinary tract infection

  • Dizziness, somnolence, confusion or hallucinations

  • Angioedema, blurred vision and dyspepsia

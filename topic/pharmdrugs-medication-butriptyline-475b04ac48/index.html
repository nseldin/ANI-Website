Boxed Warning

Antidepressant boxed warning: increased risk of suicidal thoughts/behaviors in pediatric and young adult patients.

Mechanism

Butriptyline works by blocking norepinephrine and serotonin reuptake while also blocking anticholinergic, antihistamine, and alpha receptors. That mixed receptor profile explains sedation, orthostasis, anticholinergic effects, and overdose danger.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor anticholinergic effects, orthostasis, sedation, cardiac conduction/QRS widening in overdose.

  • Overdose is dangerous.

Contraindications

  • Recent MI

  • MAOI use

  • significant conduction disorders without specialist management

Interactions

Nclex Traps

Definition

Acanthamoeba species is a clinically important protozoan parasite associated with Acanthamoeba keratitis, granulomatous amebic encephalitis, and disseminated acanthamebiasis. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

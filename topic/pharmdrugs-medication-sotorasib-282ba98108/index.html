Irreversible mutant-selective KRAS G12C inhibitor

Used To Treat

Adults with biomarker-confirmed KRAS G12C-mutated locally advanced or metastatic non-small-cell lung cancer after prior systemic therapy, and with panitumumab for selected previously treated metastatic colorectal cancer.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: no boxed warning is listed. Monitor ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase, and bilirubin before therapy, every 3 weeks for 3 months, then monthly or as indicated; hold or discontinue for severe hepatotoxicity. Hold immediately for suspected ILD/pneumonitis and permanently discontinue when confirmed.

Mechanism

Sotorasib binds the switch-II pocket and forms an irreversible bond with the mutant cysteine at codon 12 when KRAS G12C is GDP-bound. The trapped protein cannot reactivate RAF-MEK-ERK and PI3K-AKT signaling, reducing proliferation and promoting apoptosis. In colorectal cancer, EGFR feedback can bypass isolated KRAS blockade, supporting combination EGFR inhibition.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm an FDA-approved KRAS G12C result and the correct tumor-specific monotherapy or panitumumab regimen.

  • Avoid PPIs and H2 blockers; if acid reduction cannot be avoided, follow labeled antacid separation. Screen strong CYP3A inducers and sensitive CYP3A, P-gp, or BCRP substrates.

Key Labs

  • KRAS G12C result, ALT/AST/alkaline phosphatase/bilirubin on the labeled schedule, CBC/CMP, and prompt oxygenation/chest evaluation for new cough, dyspnea, or fever.

Nclex Traps

  • Sotorasib does not treat every KRAS mutation; codon and amino-acid substitution matter.

  • Do not attribute a new cough solely to lung cancer progression before evaluating drug-induced pneumonitis.

Description

Sotorasib is an oral mutant-selective KRAS G12C inhibitor that covalently locks the G12C protein in its inactive GDP-bound state, shutting down oncogenic RAS signaling while largely sparing wild-type KRAS. Hepatotoxicity, especially after prior immune-checkpoint therapy, and ILD/pneumonitis are the key organ risks; acid suppression can sharply reduce absorption.

Used To Treat

Rate control and rhythm support in selected tachydysrhythmias.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary; abrupt withdrawal can worsen angina/MI risk for selected beta blockers. Major safety risks include bradycardia, hypotension, heart block, bronchospasm in susceptible clients, and masking hypoglycemia symptoms.

Mechanism

Block beta-adrenergic stimulation, slowing SA node firing and AV node conduction.

Generic

Class

Antiviral

Used To Treat

Viral infections such as herpes viruses, influenza, HIV, hepatitis, COVID-19, CMV, or prophylaxis in high-risk clients depending on the agent.

Boxed Warning

Warnings vary widely by antiviral; major nursing concerns include renal dosing, hepatic toxicity, bone marrow suppression for selected agents, and resistance/adherence issues.

Mechanism

Trizivir works by interfering with viral entry, replication, or release depending on the virus and agent. Renal dosing, timing from symptom onset, and organ-specific toxicity matter.

Nursing Essentials

  • Start time-sensitive antivirals promptly when ordered.

  • Assess renal/hepatic function and immune status.

  • Teach adherence and infection-control precautions.

Contraindications

  • Serious hypersensitivity

  • renal/hepatic impairment may require dose adjustment or clarification

Interactions

  • Many antivirals have CYP/P-gp interactions; antiretrovirals especially require interaction checks.

Key Labs

  • renal function

  • LFTs

  • CBC for marrow-suppressing agents

  • viral load/CD4 when relevant

Nclex Traps

  • Antivirals often need renal dosing; confusion in older adults on renally cleared antivirals can signal toxicity.

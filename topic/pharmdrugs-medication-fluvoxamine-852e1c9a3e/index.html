Used To Treat

Depression, anxiety disorders, OCD, PTSD, panic disorder, PMDD, and selected eating-disorder indications depending on the medication.

Mechanism

Fluvoxamine works by blocking serotonin reuptake in the CNS, increasing serotonin signaling over time. Teach that benefit is delayed, but serotonin syndrome and suicidal ideation monitoring start early.

Boxed Warning

Antidepressant boxed warning: increased risk of suicidal thoughts/behaviors in pediatric and young adult patients.

Contraindications

  • MAOI use within required washout period

  • linezolid or methylene blue unless specifically managed

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Therapeutic effect takes weeks.

  • Monitor suicidality early and after dose changes.

  • Watch for serotonin syndrome and SIADH/hyponatremia, especially older adults.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • MAOIs, linezolid, methylene blue, triptans, tramadol, lithium, St. John's wort increase serotonin syndrome risk.

  • NSAIDs/anticoagulants/antiplatelets increase bleeding risk.

  • MAOIs, linezolid, methylene blue, St. John's wort, triptans, other serotonergic drugs, alcohol/CNS depressants, anticoagulants/NSAIDs, and QT-prolonging drugs when relevant.

Key Labs

  • sodium if older adult/SIADH risk

  • bleeding labs if anticoagulated

  • Mood/suicidality, serotonin syndrome cues, sodium in SIADH-risk clients, blood pressure for SNRIs, ECG/QT for risk agents, and overdose danger for TCAs.

Nclex Traps

  • Serotonin syndrome is hyperreflexia/clonus/fever/agitation/diarrhea, not just anxiety.

  • Do not expect immediate benefit. Watch for early suicidality, serotonin syndrome, hyponatremia, bleeding risk, and abrupt discontinuation symptoms.

Description

Fluvoxamine is a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI) antidepressant: it works by blocking serotonin reuptake in the CNS, increasing serotonin signaling over time. It is used for depression, anxiety disorders, OCD, PTSD, panic disorder, PMDD, and selected eating-disorder indications depending on the medication.

Summary

Diffusion capacity (DLCO) (diffusing capacity of the lungs for carbon monoxide) is a high-yield Respiratory diagnostic reference. Pulmonary function measure of gas transfer across the alveolar-capillary membrane, useful in interstitial lung disease, emphysema, and pulmonary vascular disease.

Quick Answer

Diffusion capacity (DLCO) (diffusing capacity of the lungs for carbon monoxide): Pulmonary function measure of gas transfer across the alveolar-capillary membrane, useful in interstitial lung disease, emphysema, and pulmonary vascular disease. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

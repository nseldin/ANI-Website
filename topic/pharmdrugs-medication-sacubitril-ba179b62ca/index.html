Class

Neprilysin inhibitor component of angiotensin receptor-neprilysin inhibitor therapy

Used To Treat

Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction and selected heart-failure populations when prescribed as the sacubitril/valsartan combination product.

Description

Sacubitril is the neprilysin-inhibitor half of sacubitril/valsartan, the ARNI used in heart failure care. Its value comes from increasing beneficial natriuretic-peptide signaling while valsartan blocks angiotensin II receptor effects.

Mechanism

Sacubitril is converted to an active metabolite that inhibits neprilysin, an enzyme that breaks down natriuretic peptides, bradykinin, adrenomedullin, and related vasoactive peptides. Higher natriuretic-peptide activity promotes vasodilation, natriuresis, diuresis, lower sympathetic tone, and less maladaptive remodeling. Increased bradykinin explains angioedema risk and the required ACE-inhibitor washout.

Contraindications

  • Do not use with an ACE inhibitor or within the unsafe ACE-inhibitor washout window. Clarify history of angioedema, pregnancy, severe hyperkalemia, hypotension, or renal artery stenosis.

Key Labs

  • Potassium, creatinine/eGFR, blood pressure trend, weight, edema, and heart-failure symptom response.

Nclex Traps

  • ACE-inhibitor washout and angioedema risk are the high-yield safety details.

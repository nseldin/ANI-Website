Class

Liver-activated vitamin D2 prohormone; VDR agonist for CKD secondary hyperparathyroidism

Used To Treat

Capsules treat secondary hyperparathyroidism in adults with stage 3 or 4 CKD and adults on dialysis; injection is used in adults with CKD on dialysis.

Boxed Warning

No current U.S. boxed warning. High-yield warning context: Contraindicated with hypercalcemia or vitamin-D toxicity. Hypercalcemia can trigger arrhythmia, seizures and digitalis toxicity; chronic excess causes soft-tissue/vascular calcification. Fatal anaphylaxis has occurred after injection in hemodialysis patients. Monitor calcium, phosphorus and intact PTH closely after initiation or titration.

Mechanism

Hepatic CYP27-family metabolism converts doxercalciferol to an active VDR ligand. The VDR-RXR complex changes gene transcription in parathyroid, intestine and bone: PTH synthesis/proliferation falls and mineral absorption rises. Oversuppressed PTH reduces remodeling stimulus and produces low-turnover bone; excessive calcium-phosphate exposure promotes vascular and soft-tissue calcification.

Nursing Essentials

  • Tie dose changes to calcium, phosphorus and intact PTH rather than treating it as a fixed vitamin supplement.

  • Monitor patients during initial IV exposures for anaphylaxis and have emergency treatment available.

Contraindications

  • Hypercalcemia, vitamin-D toxicity and serious hypersensitivity

  • Do not combine duplicate vitamin-D compounds or excessive calcium

  • Hold/reduce when PTH is persistently abnormally low

Interactions

  • Calcium products, thiazides and other vitamin-D compounds increase hypercalcemia.

  • Hypercalcemia potentiates digitalis toxicity.

  • CYP inhibitors/inducers and magnesium-containing products require review.

Key Labs

  • Calcium, phosphorus and intact PTH

  • Magnesium and alkaline phosphatase

  • Hepatic function and ECG/digoxin monitoring when relevant

Nclex Traps

  • Doxercalciferol is activated in liver, not by the failing kidney.

  • Lower PTH is therapeutic only until turnover becomes too low.

  • IV anaphylaxis is a distinct product risk beyond hypercalcemia.

Description

Doxercalciferol is a synthetic 1-alpha-hydroxyvitamin-D2 prohormone that is activated in the liver to a VDR agonist, suppressing excess PTH in CKD. Hepatic conversion produces active 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin-D2 and bypasses the kidney's failed 1-alpha-hydroxylation step. VDR activation also raises calcium and phosphate absorption, so dosing is titrated to intact PTH without allowing hypercalcemia, hyperphosphatemia or adynamic bone.

Adverse Effects

  • Hypercalcemia and hyperphosphatemia

  • Nausea, vomiting, edema, headache, malaise and pruritus

  • Arrhythmia/seizure or digitalis toxicity secondary to hypercalcemia

  • Anaphylaxis with injection and adynamic bone from low PTH

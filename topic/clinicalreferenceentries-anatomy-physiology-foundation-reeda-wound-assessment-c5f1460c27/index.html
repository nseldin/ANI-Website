Quick Answer

Describe each finding and its trend rather than charting only the acronym; pain, systemic illness, dehiscence, hematoma, perfusion, and function also matter.

Sections

  • Label

    Why each feature matters

    Text

    Redness and edema may reflect expected inflammation or infection; ecchymosis can reveal tissue trauma or hematoma; discharge may represent blood, serous fluid, or infection; approximation shows whether wound edges remain aligned.

  • Label

    Complete assessment

    Text

    Assess location, size, temperature, tenderness, odor, drainage amount and character, bleeding, fluctuance, wound-edge separation, surrounding perfusion, pain trend, fever, and effect on movement, voiding, bowel function, or feeding.

  • Label

    Urgent findings

    Text

    Escalate rapidly expanding swelling, severe pressure pain, uncontrolled bleeding, wound separation with deeper tissue exposure, crepitus, necrosis, systemic toxicity, or suspected deep infection.

  • Label

    Documentation

    Text

    Use objective measurements and standardized wound terms. Photographs require consent and policy-compliant secure handling.

Summary

REEDA organizes observation of redness, edema, ecchymosis, discharge, and approximation in a healing incision, laceration, or perineal wound.

Definition

Traube space dullness is a percussion sign. It is a clue to left pleural effusion or splenic enlargement. A positive finding means normally tympanitic Traube space becomes dull.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Traube space dullness means normally tympanitic Traube space becomes dull.

Why It Matters

A positive Traube space dullness response can signal left pleural effusion or splenic enlargement. Fluid, enlarged spleen, or adjacent solid tissue replaces or displaces the underlying gastric air resonance. Traube space dullness cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • left pleural effusion or splenic enlargement

Assessment Method

Percuss the left lower anterior chest over the normal gastric bubble region.

Positive Finding

Normally tympanitic Traube space becomes dull.

Mechanism

Fluid, enlarged spleen, or adjacent solid tissue replaces or displaces the underlying gastric air resonance.

Clinical Interpretation

A positive Traube space dullness response raises concern for left pleural effusion or splenic enlargement because fluid, enlarged spleen, or adjacent solid tissue replaces or displaces the underlying gastric air resonance. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Traube space dullness does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Traube space dullness to focus evaluation of left pleural effusion or splenic enlargement and the reviewed alternatives: splenomegaly, cardiomegaly, gastric distention. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Percuss the left lower anterior chest over the normal gastric bubble region. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Traube space dullness as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Recent food, obesity, and gastric position cause false positives. Therefore, Traube space dullness cannot confirm or exclude left pleural effusion or splenic enlargement by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • splenomegaly

  • cardiomegaly

  • gastric distention

False Positive Situations

A false-positive maneuver can result from incorrect position or force, pain from splenomegaly, cardiomegaly, gastric distention, guarding, or calling an incompletely defined response positive.

False Negative Situations

Traube space dullness may be missed with incomplete technique, guarding, early or partial disease, limited cooperation, or a response too subtle for the examiner.

Modern Status

Limited adjunct with multiple causes. When performed as described, Traube space dullness can adjust concern for left pleural effusion or splenic enlargement, but it is not a stand-alone diagnosis. Apply the reviewed limitation for Traube space dullness: Recent food, obesity, and gastric position cause false positives.

Safety Precautions

Before assessing Traube space dullness, explain the method, obtain consent, preserve privacy, and use only the technique described here: Percuss the left lower anterior chest over the normal gastric bubble region. Stop if that technique becomes unsafe. The reviewed precaution for this maneuver is: Do not confuse this with cardiovascular Traube pistol-shot sound.

Class

Daily C-type natriuretic-peptide analog/NPR-B agonist

Used To Treat

Increase linear growth in pediatric patients with achondroplasia and open epiphyses, using daily weight-based subcutaneous dosing under accelerated approval.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current VOXZOGO labeling has no boxed warning. Transient blood-pressure decreases can cause dizziness, fatigue, nausea or syncope; ensure adequate food and hydration before dosing and monitor symptomatic patients. It is not recommended when eGFR is below 60 mL/min/1.73 m2 under current labeling. Injection reactions and vomiting are common.

Mechanism

Gain-of-function FGFR3 suppresses endochondral ossification through RAS-RAF-MEK-ERK/MAPK signaling. Vosoritide binds natriuretic peptide receptor B, activates guanylyl cyclase, raises cGMP and protein kinase G activity, and inhibits MAPK signaling at RAF-1. Chondrocytes can then proliferate and mature more effectively, widening the growth plate and supporting longitudinal bone growth.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify open epiphyses, daily weight-based dose, hydration/nutrition, renal function, blood-pressure symptoms and caregiver injection technique; periodically recalculate for growth.

  • Trend annualized growth velocity and continue achondroplasia-specific neurologic, airway, hearing and orthopedic surveillance because increased height does not address every complication.

Key Labs

  • Standing and recumbent blood pressure when symptomatic, renal function, serial height/length and weight, growth velocity and epiphyseal status; no serum drug level guides dosing.

Nclex Traps

  • Vosoritide acts only while growth plates are open and is discontinued when growth potential ends.

  • It antagonizes downstream FGFR3 signaling but does not genetically correct FGFR3.

Description

Vosoritide is a daily subcutaneous C-type natriuretic peptide analog that partially releases the FGFR3 growth brake in achondroplasia. It activates NPR-B/cGMP signaling in growth-plate chondrocytes, promotes proliferation and differentiation, and increases annualized growth velocity while epiphyses remain open. It does not alter the FGFR3 variant or remove established skeletal anatomy.

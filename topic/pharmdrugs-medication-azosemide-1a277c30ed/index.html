Class

Loop diuretic

Used To Treat

Fluid overload/edema from heart failure, kidney disease, or liver disease; also used for hypertension in selected clients.

Boxed Warning

Potent diuresis warning: excessive water/electrolyte loss can cause dehydration and electrolyte depletion.

Mechanism

Azosemide works by blocking sodium-potassium-chloride reabsorption in the loop of Henle, causing strong diuresis and electrolyte loss.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor potassium, magnesium, sodium, creatinine, BP, daily weight, I&O.

  • Assess hearing with high doses/rapid IV use.

  • Replace electrolytes as ordered.

Contraindications

  • Anuria

  • severe electrolyte depletion until corrected

Interactions

  • Digoxin toxicity risk rises with hypokalemia.

  • Aminoglycosides increase ototoxicity.

  • Lithium levels can rise.

Nclex Traps

Description

Azosemide is a diuretic: it works by blocking sodium-potassium-chloride reabsorption in the loop of Henle, causing strong diuresis and electrolyte loss. It is used for fluid overload/edema from heart failure, kidney disease, or liver disease. Also used for hypertension in selected clients.

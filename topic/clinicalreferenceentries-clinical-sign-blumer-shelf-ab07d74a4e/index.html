Definition

Blumer shelf is a digital pelvic examination sign. It is a clue to peritoneal metastasis in the pouch of Douglas. A positive finding means a hard shelf-like mass is felt.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Blumer shelf means a hard shelf-like mass is felt.

Why It Matters

The observed Blumer shelf finding can signal peritoneal metastasis in the pouch of Douglas. Tumor implants accumulate in the dependent rectouterine or rectovesical peritoneal pouch. Blumer shelf cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • peritoneal metastasis in the pouch of Douglas

Assessment Method

During indicated rectal or pelvic examination, palpate the anterior rectal wall with consent and chaperone.

Positive Finding

A hard shelf-like mass is felt.

Mechanism

Tumor implants accumulate in the dependent rectouterine or rectovesical peritoneal pouch.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Blumer shelf finding raises concern for peritoneal metastasis in the pouch of Douglas because tumor implants accumulate in the dependent rectouterine or rectovesical peritoneal pouch. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Blumer shelf does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Blumer shelf to focus evaluation of peritoneal metastasis in the pouch of Douglas and the reviewed alternatives: endometriosis, fecal impaction, pelvic abscess. Evidence Limitations

Stool, endometriosis, and pelvic fibrosis can mimic a mass. Therefore, Blumer shelf cannot confirm or exclude peritoneal metastasis in the pouch of Douglas by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • endometriosis

  • pelvic abscess

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as endometriosis, fecal impaction, pelvic abscess.

False Negative Situations

Blumer shelf may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of peritoneal metastasis in the pouch of Douglas.

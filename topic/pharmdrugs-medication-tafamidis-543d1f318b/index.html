Class

Transthyretin thyroxine-pocket binder and tetramer kinetic stabilizer

Used To Treat

Cardiomyopathy of wild-type or hereditary transthyretin-mediated amyloidosis in adults to reduce cardiovascular mortality and cardiovascular-related hospitalization.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current VYNDAQEL/VYNDAMAX labeling has no boxed warning. Verify product and total daily capsule count because 61 mg tafamidis free acid and four 20-mg tafamidis-meglumine capsules are exposure-equivalent regimens, not milligram-equivalent products. Tafamidis inhibits BCRP and may increase substrates such as rosuvastatin.

Mechanism

TTR normally circulates as a tetramer. Wild-type aging effects or destabilizing variants permit the tetramer to dissociate; monomers then misfold, oligomerize and form extracellular amyloid. Tafamidis binds the two thyroxine pockets at the dimer-dimer interface and raises the kinetic barrier to dissociation, the rate-limiting step in amyloidogenesis.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm ATTR-CM diagnosis and wild-type/variant evaluation, exact product, swallowing, adherence, BCRP substrates, pregnancy context, volume status, orthostasis, conduction disease, arrhythmia and concurrent heart-failure care.

  • Teach that benefit is preventive over time; acute dyspnea, edema, syncope or rapid weight gain needs heart-failure evaluation rather than an extra capsule.

Key Labs

  • Weight, blood pressure, renal/liver function, ECG/rhythm, NT-proBNP/troponin and echocardiographic or functional measures as clinically indicated; no routine tafamidis level.

Nclex Traps

  • VYNDAQEL 80 mg and VYNDAMAX 61 mg are alternative exposure-matched regimens but cannot be substituted per milligram.

  • Tafamidis stabilizes TTR tetramers; it is not a TTR gene silencer.

Description

Tafamidis is an oral transthyretin thyroxine-pocket binder and kinetic stabilizer that slows tetramer dissociation into amyloid-prone monomers and reduces further ATTR cardiomyopathy progression. It does not lower TTR synthesis, remove existing cardiac deposits immediately, or act as an acute heart-failure drug. VYNDAQEL 80 mg and VYNDAMAX 61 mg provide comparable exposure but are not substitutable milligram for milligram.

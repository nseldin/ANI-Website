Class

IV CD4-domain-2-directed post-attachment HIV-1 inhibitor monoclonal antibody

Used To Treat

With other antiretrovirals, multidrug-resistant HIV-1 in heavily treatment-experienced adults failing their current regimen.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current TROGARZO labeling has no boxed warning. Infusion-related hypersensitivity and anaphylaxis can cause dyspnea, angioedema, wheeze, chest tightness, cough, flushing, nausea or vomiting; stop and treat immediately. Monitor immune-reconstitution inflammatory syndrome and fetal/infant immune risk. If maintenance is at least 3 days late, the 2,000-mg loading dose is required again before resuming every-14-day dosing.

Mechanism

HIV gp120 first binds CD4 domain 1. Ibalizumab binds a conformational epitope primarily in CD4 domain 2 on the opposite face from the MHC-II interaction site. It does not prevent gp120 attachment; instead it prevents post-attachment rearrangement and coreceptor/fusion progression. This preserves ordinary CD4-mediated immune function while inhibiting CCR5- and CXCR4-tropic virus.

Nursing Essentials

  • Give the 2,000-mg loading dose then 800 mg every 2 weeks by the labeled IV infusion or push method; track exact dates and reload after a delay of 3 days or more.

  • Use an optimized active background regimen, monitor HIV RNA/CD4, inspect for infusion reaction and IRIS, and never interpret CD4 binding as proof of CD4-cell depletion.

Key Labs

  • HIV RNA, CD4 count, resistance history, infection assessment and infusion vital signs; no routine serum ibalizumab concentration or conventional CYP interaction monitoring is required.

Nclex Traps

  • Ibalizumab is post-attachment: gp120 can attach, but the virus cannot complete entry-related conformational change.

  • Binding CD4 domain 2 does not block the domain-1 MHC-II interface and is not conventional CD4 immunosuppression.

Description

Ibalizumab-uiyk is a humanized IgG4 monoclonal antibody given IV that binds domain 2 of host CD4 after HIV has attached and blocks the conformational steps required for entry and cell-cell fusion. It is a salvage antiretroviral, not a general immune suppressant and not adequate alone.

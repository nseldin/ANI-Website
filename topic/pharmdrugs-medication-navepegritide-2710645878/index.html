Class

Once-weekly sustained-release C-type natriuretic-peptide prodrug/NPR-B agonist

Used To Treat

Increase linear growth in children age 2 years and older with achondroplasia and open epiphyses using once-weekly weight-based subcutaneous dosing under accelerated approval.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current YUVIWEL labeling has no boxed warning. Transient hypotension is the key warning; assess dizziness, fatigue and nausea and ensure an appropriate response plan. YUVIWEL is not recommended with eGFR below 60 mL/min/1.73 m2. Vomiting, injection-site reactions, limb pain and nausea are common. Reconstitution and weight-based vial selection create medication-error risk.

Mechanism

Active CNP is transiently attached to branched PEG carriers through cleavable TransCon linkers. Physiologic cleavage provides sustained CNP exposure. CNP activates NPR-B guanylyl cyclase, raises cGMP and protein kinase G signaling, and inhibits FGFR3-driven MAPK activity. Chondrocyte proliferation and differentiation increase, supporting endochondral bone growth.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify age, open epiphyses, weight, renal function, weekly day, vial strength, reconstitution volume and trained caregiver technique; recalculate as weight changes.

  • A dose may be given within the labeled two-day window around the scheduled day; otherwise follow missed-dose rules rather than bunching injections. Continue full achondroplasia surveillance.

Key Labs

  • Renal function, blood pressure and hypotension symptoms, serial height/length, weight, annualized growth velocity and epiphyseal status; no routine CNP or cGMP target is used for titration.

Nclex Traps

  • Navepegritide is a weekly CNP prodrug; vosoritide is a daily active analog. The preparation and missed-dose rules are not interchangeable.

  • Accelerated approval is based on growth velocity and does not imply correction of every achondroplasia complication.

Description

Navepegritide is a once-weekly prodrug that gradually releases active CNP(89-126) for children with achondroplasia. The released peptide activates NPR-B and counters excessive FGFR3-MAPK growth suppression at the growth plate. Its weekly interval distinguishes it from daily vosoritide, but it still requires open epiphyses and does not correct the causative FGFR3 variant.

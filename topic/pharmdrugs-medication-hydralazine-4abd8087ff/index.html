Mechanism

Hydralazine works by directly relaxing arteriolar smooth muscle, lowering systemic vascular resistance and afterload. Reflex sympathetic activation can cause tachycardia, headache, fluid retention, and angina worsening; immune-like lupus syndrome can occur with exposure.

Boxed Warning

No boxed warning commonly emphasized; reflex tachycardia, hypotension, lupus-like syndrome, and fluid retention are key concerns.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity.

  • Coronary artery disease or rheumatic mitral valve disease requires caution.

  • Lupus-like syndrome history requires review.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor blood pressure and heart rate.

  • Assess headache, dizziness, edema, chest pain.

  • Often used for severe hypertension including pregnancy contexts per protocol.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Other antihypertensives increase hypotension risk.

  • NSAIDs may blunt antihypertensive effect.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • blood pressure trend

  • heart rate

  • ANA/CBC if lupus-like symptoms per provider

  • Use medication-specific labs when ordered. For high-risk drugs, connect renal function, liver function, electrolytes, CBC, coagulation studies, drug levels, ECG, and vital-sign trends to safety.

Nclex Traps

  • After giving a vasodilator, watch for reflex tachycardia and symptomatic hypotension.

  • The NCLEX trap is memorizing the name without connecting class, contraindication, assessment, and priority adverse effect.

Infection labs

Name

Procalcitonin

Range

Often <0.1-0.25 ng/mL is low, but protocols vary.

Why

Procalcitonin can support bacterial infection/sepsis assessment and antibiotic stewardship.

Aliases

  • PCT

High Causes

  • Bacterial infection

  • Sepsis

  • Major trauma/surgery can also raise it

Low Causes

  • Viral illness or noninfectious inflammation may have lower values, but exceptions occur.

Critical Concerns

  • Do not ignore clinical sepsis because procalcitonin is low early.

Nursing Considerations

  • Use with cultures, lactate, vitals, source assessment, and provider orders.

Nclex Traps

  • Never let one biomarker override an unstable client.

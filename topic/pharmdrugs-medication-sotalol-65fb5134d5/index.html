Used To Treat

Selected atrial and ventricular dysrhythmias, including atrial fibrillation/flutter maintenance in selected clients and serious ventricular dysrhythmias.

Mechanism

Sotalol works by blocking beta-adrenergic stimulation, lowering heart rate, contractility, conduction, renin release, and myocardial oxygen demand. Hold/clarify cues are bradycardia, hypotension, heart block, and selected acute decompensation.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warning: can cause life-threatening ventricular dysrhythmias and QT prolongation. Initiation or dose increases require ECG/QTc and renal-based monitoring per label/protocol.

Contraindications

  • selected rhythm disturbances, with nursing focus on ECG rhythm, heart rate, blood pressure, electrolytes, and drug-specific toxicity.

  • Clarify severe bradycardia, symptomatic hypotension, high-grade heart block without pacing, acute decompensated heart failure, and bronchospasm risk for nonselective agents.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor QTc, heart rate, blood pressure, potassium, magnesium, and renal function.

  • Clarify low potassium, low magnesium, significant bradycardia, high-grade AV block, or prolonged QT before giving.

  • Renal impairment increases accumulation and torsades risk.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Other QT-prolonging drugs increase torsades risk.

  • Diuretics can lower potassium/magnesium and increase dysrhythmia risk.

  • Other rate-slowing drugs can worsen bradycardia or heart block.

  • Review duplicate therapy, additive sedation, additive bleeding risk, QT-prolonging combinations, renal/hepatic clearance, and CYP/P-gp interactions when relevant.

Key Labs

  • QTc

  • blood pressure

  • ECG/QT interval, potassium, magnesium, heart rate, blood pressure. For amiodarone also monitor thyroid, liver, pulmonary symptoms, and eye changes.

Nclex Traps

  • Sotalol is both Class III and beta-blocking. Do not miss bradycardia, hypotension, renal dosing, and QT/torsades risk.

  • Do not ignore new cough, dyspnea, bradycardia, syncope, or QT prolongation in a client taking a Class III antiarrhythmic.

Description

Sotalol is a class III antiarrhythmic and potassium-channel blocker with beta-blocking effects: it works by blocking beta-adrenergic stimulation, lowering heart rate, contractility, conduction, renin release, and myocardial oxygen demand. It is used for hypertension, angina, rate control, post-MI protection, dysrhythmia control, migraine/tremor uses for selected agents, heart failure with selected evidence-based beta blockers, and pregnancy hypertension with labetalol when ordered.

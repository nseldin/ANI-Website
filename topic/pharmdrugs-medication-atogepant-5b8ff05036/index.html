Class

Once-daily oral CGRP receptor antagonist gepant for migraine prevention

Used To Treat

Preventive treatment of episodic or chronic migraine in adults; it does not treat an individual attack as a rescue indication.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current QULIPTA labeling has no boxed warning. Serious hypersensitivity can occur hours to days after administration. Monitor new/worsening hypertension and Raynaud phenomenon. Strong CYP3A inhibitors require lower dosing; CYP3A inducers and OATP inhibitors require indication-specific adjustment, and severe hepatic impairment should be avoided.

Mechanism

Atogepant competitively occupies the CLR/RAMP1 CGRP receptor complex, reducing Gs-adenylyl-cyclase-cAMP signaling in trigeminovascular nociceptive pathways. Daily blockade reduces the probability and burden of future migraine activation rather than reversing an attack on demand. The label does not claim that CGRP blockade corrects every generator of episodic or chronic migraine.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm prevention rather than rescue, episodic versus chronic migraine, daily dose, constipation/bowel-obstruction risk, hepatic function, renal status, pregnancy and CYP3A/OATP interactions.

  • Trend monthly migraine days, disability and acute-medication use; counsel hydration/fiber as appropriate but promptly assess severe abdominal pain, distention, vomiting or obstipation.

Contraindications

  • Contraindicated after serious hypersensitivity to atogepant or an excipient.

  • Avoid severe hepatic impairment. Severe renal impairment and end-stage renal disease require indication-specific limits; treatment of chronic migraine is not recommended in that setting because the studied regimen and exposure do not support safe extrapolation.

Interactions

  • Strong CYP3A inhibitors require dose reduction. CYP3A inducers can lower exposure and OATP inhibitors can raise it, with different recommendations for episodic versus chronic migraine.

  • The interaction changes how much active drug reaches the receptor; do not transfer a dose table from rimegepant or ubrogepant simply because all are gepants.

Key Labs

  • No routine serum level. Monitor headache-day response, bowel function, weight/appetite, blood pressure, Raynaud symptoms, hepatic/renal suitability and interaction changes.

Nclex Traps

  • Atogepant prevents migraine and is not labeled to abort an attack already underway.

  • Daily dosing maintains preventive receptor blockade; taking extra tablets during an attack adds exposure without creating a labeled rescue regimen.

  • Dose selection is not interchangeable across episodic/chronic migraine and interacting-drug scenarios.

Description

Atogepant is a once-daily oral CGRP receptor antagonist designed for adult migraine prevention, including episodic and chronic migraine. It reduces future headache burden rather than aborting an attack on demand. Constipation, nausea and fatigue/somnolence are common, while hypersensitivity, hypertension, Raynaud phenomenon and CYP3A/OATP interaction rules can change whether 10, 30 or 60 mg is appropriate.

Adverse Effects

  • Nausea, constipation, fatigue or somnolence, decreased appetite, and weight loss are the characteristic common effects.

  • Serious or delayed hypersensitivity, postmarketing hypertension, and Raynaud phenomenon require discontinuation or evaluation according to severity.

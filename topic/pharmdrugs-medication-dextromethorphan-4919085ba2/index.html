Mechanism

Dextromethorphan suppresses the medullary cough center through NMDA receptor antagonism and sigma-related CNS effects. It also has serotonergic activity, which explains serotonin syndrome risk with serotonergic drugs and dissociation/misuse risk at high doses.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for dextromethorphan in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Dextromethorphan is a non-opioid cough suppressant that acts centrally on cough-control pathways. It is not an expectorant; the major nuance is serotonergic/CNS toxicity risk with MAOIs, SSRIs/SNRIs, linezolid, misuse, or high doses.

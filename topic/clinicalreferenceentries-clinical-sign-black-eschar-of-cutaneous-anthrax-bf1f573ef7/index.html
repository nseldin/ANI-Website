Definition

Black eschar of cutaneous anthrax is an observed skin sign. It is a clue to cutaneous anthrax. A positive finding means a painless depressed black eschar forms with disproportionate edema.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Black eschar of cutaneous anthrax means a painless depressed black eschar forms with disproportionate edema.

Why It Matters

The observed Black eschar of cutaneous anthrax finding can signal cutaneous anthrax. Bacillus anthracis toxins cause local necrosis and vascular leakage while pain may remain limited. Black eschar of cutaneous anthrax cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

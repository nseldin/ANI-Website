Definition

Leser-Trélat sign is an observed eruption sign. It is a clue to possible internal malignancy associated with abrupt seborrheic keratoses. A positive finding means a sudden eruption or rapid increase of numerous lesions occurs.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Leser-Trélat sign means a sudden eruption or rapid increase of numerous lesions occurs.

Why It Matters

The observed Leser-Trélat sign finding can signal possible internal malignancy associated with abrupt seborrheic keratoses. Tumor-derived growth factors are proposed to stimulate epidermal proliferation, though the association is debated. Leser-Trélat sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Assessment Method

A sudden eruption or rapid increase of numerous lesions occurs.

Evidence Limitations

Seborrheic keratoses are common and no validated lesion threshold exists. Therefore, Leser-Trélat sign cannot confirm or exclude possible internal malignancy associated with abrupt seborrheic keratoses by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Summary

Six-minute walk test is a high-yield Respiratory diagnostic reference. Measures distance, symptoms, oxygen saturation, and endurance over six minutes for cardiopulmonary functional status.

Quick Answer

Six-minute walk test: Measures distance, symptoms, oxygen saturation, and endurance over six minutes for cardiopulmonary functional status. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Measures distance, symptoms, oxygen saturation, and endurance over six minutes for cardiopulmonary functional status.

    • Before / preparation

    • Assess oxygenation, respiratory effort, bronchodilator/steroid use, anticoagulants if invasive, and whether the client can cooperate with the test.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Monitor airway, breathing, oxygenation, cough, bleeding risk, and tolerance before and after respiratory testing.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Hypoxemia, respiratory distress, hemoptysis, absent breath sounds, pneumothorax signs, or severe bronchospasm.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Airway and oxygenation come before completing a diagnostic plan.

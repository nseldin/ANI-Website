Summary

Sputum culture is a high-yield Respiratory diagnostic reference. Identifies respiratory organisms and antibiotic susceptibility when lower respiratory infection is suspected.

Quick Answer

Sputum culture: Identifies respiratory organisms and antibiotic susceptibility when lower respiratory infection is suspected. Nursing focus: prepare correctly, protect safety, trend results with assessment, and escalate red-flag findings.

Result Meanings

  • Label

    No growth / normal flora

    Meaning

    No dominant bacterial pathogen is isolated, or the sample reflects expected airway flora.

  • Label

    Positive growth

    Meaning

    A respiratory organism is isolated and susceptibility results can guide antibiotics when the specimen quality and symptoms fit infection.

  • Label

    Poor-quality specimen

    Meaning

    Many epithelial cells or heavy oral contamination can make the result misleading and may require recollection.

Sections

    • What it tells you

    • Identifies respiratory organisms and antibiotic susceptibility when lower respiratory infection is suspected.

    • Before / preparation

    • Collect the correct specimen from the correct source, use sterile technique when required, label site/time, and obtain cultures before antimicrobials when it will not dangerously delay treatment.

    • Priority nursing actions

    • Prevent contamination, send the specimen promptly, and connect results to isolation, antibiotic stewardship, and clinical deterioration.

    • Concerning results / reportable cues

    • Positive blood or CSF culture, sepsis cues, worsening fever, hypotension, mental status change, or a contaminated specimen that could mislead therapy.

    • Common NCLEX trap / nuance

    • Do not delay life-saving antibiotics too long for a perfect specimen in an unstable client.

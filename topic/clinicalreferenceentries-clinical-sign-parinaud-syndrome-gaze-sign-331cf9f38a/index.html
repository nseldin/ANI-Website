Definition

Parinaud syndrome gaze sign is an ocular motor constellation. It is a clue to dorsal midbrain dysfunction. A positive finding means upgaze is impaired, often with convergence-retraction nystagmus and light-near dissociation.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Parinaud syndrome gaze sign means upgaze is impaired, often with convergence-retraction nystagmus and light-near dissociation.

Why It Matters

The Parinaud syndrome gaze sign pattern can signal dorsal midbrain dysfunction. Dorsal midbrain compression disrupts vertical gaze centers and pretectal pupillary pathways. Parinaud syndrome gaze sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • dorsal midbrain dysfunction

Assessment Method

Assess vertical saccades, convergence, pupils, and lid position.

Positive Finding

Upgaze is impaired, often with convergence-retraction nystagmus and light-near dissociation.

Mechanism

Dorsal midbrain compression disrupts vertical gaze centers and pretectal pupillary pathways.

Clinical Interpretation

The Parinaud syndrome gaze sign pattern raises concern for dorsal midbrain dysfunction because dorsal midbrain compression disrupts vertical gaze centers and pretectal pupillary pathways. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Parinaud syndrome gaze sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Parinaud syndrome gaze sign to focus evaluation of dorsal midbrain dysfunction and the reviewed alternatives: progressive supranuclear palsy, third nerve palsy, orbital restriction. Evidence Limitations

Incomplete patterns occur and medication or age can limit upgaze. Therefore, Parinaud syndrome gaze sign cannot confirm or exclude dorsal midbrain dysfunction by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Negative Situations

Parinaud syndrome gaze sign may be absent even when dorsal midbrain dysfunction is present because the complete named pattern need not occur.

Used To Treat

Boxed Warning

Abrupt withdrawal warning: sudden discontinuation can worsen angina, MI risk, or dysrhythmias in coronary disease.

Mechanism

Talinolol works by blocking beta-adrenergic stimulation, lowering heart rate, contractility, conduction, renin release, and myocardial oxygen demand. Hold/clarify cues are bradycardia, hypotension, heart block, and selected acute decompensation.

Nursing Essentials

  • Check HR and BP before giving.

  • Use caution with asthma/COPD depending selectivity.

  • Masks hypoglycemia tachycardia.

Contraindications

  • Severe bradycardia

  • heart block greater than first degree without pacemaker

  • cardiogenic shock/decompensated HF unless directed

Interactions

Description

Talinolol is a beta blocker: it works by blocking beta-adrenergic stimulation, lowering heart rate, contractility, conduction, renin release, and myocardial oxygen demand. It is used for hypertension, angina, rate control, dysrhythmias, post-MI protection, heart failure with selected agents, migraine prevention, tremor, or hyperthyroid symptom control.

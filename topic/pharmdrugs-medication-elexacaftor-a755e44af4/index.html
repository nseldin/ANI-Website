Class

CFTR corrector

Used To Treat

As one component of elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor for cystic fibrosis with an eligible CFTR genotype; elexacaftor is not administered as stand-alone therapy.

Boxed Warning

The elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor regimen carries a boxed warning for drug-induced liver injury and liver failure, including fatal cases and transplantation. Baseline and scheduled ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase, and bilirubin monitoring belong to the complete Trikafta regimen rather than to unsupervised ingredient use.

Mechanism

Elexacaftor binds CFTR and stabilizes a folding/processing state that increases mature protein delivery and persistence at the cell membrane. More surface CFTR creates substrate for ivacaftor potentiation, increasing chloride and bicarbonate transport, airway-surface hydration, mucociliary clearance, and multi-organ CFTR function.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify genotype, age/weight dose, complete combination product, fat-containing-food administration, and strong or moderate CYP3A inhibitors/inducers; avoid grapefruit and Seville orange exposure according to product instructions.

  • Monitor the regimen for liver injury, respiratory and nutritional response, rash, cataracts in pediatric clients, intracranial-hypertension symptoms, and new depression, anxiety, sleep disturbance, or suicidal thoughts/behaviors.

Key Labs

  • ALT, AST, alkaline phosphatase, bilirubin on the combination label schedule; pulmonary function or sweat chloride when ordered; weight/nutrition and pediatric eye examinations.

Nclex Traps

  • Elexacaftor is a corrector that increases surface CFTR; it is not the potentiator and is not used alone.

Description

Elexacaftor is a CFTR corrector that binds and stabilizes selected misfolded CFTR protein, especially F508del-containing protein, increasing folding, trafficking, and survival at the epithelial surface instead of intracellular destruction. It binds at a site different from tezacaftor, so the two correctors increase surface CFTR more than either alone. Ivacaftor then makes those rescued channels open more often.

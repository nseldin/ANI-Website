Definition

Pillow sign is an observed postural sign. It is a clue to catatonia. A positive finding means the head remains elevated above the bed for a prolonged period.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Pillow sign means the head remains elevated above the bed for a prolonged period.

Why It Matters

The observed Pillow sign finding can signal catatonia. Sustained catatonic posturing fixes neck muscles in an antigravity position. Pillow sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • catatonia

Assessment Method

Observe the supine patient's head without a pillow.

Positive Finding

The head remains elevated above the bed for a prolonged period.

Mechanism

Sustained catatonic posturing fixes neck muscles in an antigravity position.

Clinical Interpretation

The observed Pillow sign finding raises concern for catatonia because sustained catatonic posturing fixes neck muscles in an antigravity position. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Pillow sign does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Pillow sign to focus evaluation of catatonia and the reviewed alternatives: opisthotonus, anxiety, seizure. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Observe the supine patient's head without a pillow. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Pillow sign as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Voluntary posture and neck disease can mimic it. Therefore, Pillow sign cannot confirm or exclude catatonia by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • opisthotonus

  • anxiety

  • seizure

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as opisthotonus, anxiety, seizure.

False Negative Situations

Pillow sign may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of catatonia.

Modern Status

Historical but recognized catatonia sign. Pillow sign remains searchable because learners still encounter the term, but it cannot confirm or exclude catatonia. Apply the reviewed limitation for Pillow sign: Voluntary posture and neck disease can mimic it.

Safety Precautions

Observe Pillow sign with consent, privacy, adequate exposure, and only the manipulation required by this assessment: Observe the supine patient's head without a pillow. The reviewed precaution for this finding is: Do not leave the neck strained; assess comfort, rigidity, and medical causes.

Nursing Relevance

Record the exact Pillow sign observation, side or distribution, timing, technique, patient response, and change from baseline; the reviewed positive finding is: The head remains elevated above the bed for a prolonged period. Document the exact finding, assessment method, side or distribution, timing, patient response, associated symptoms and vital signs, and change from baseline. Record the observed result instead of writing only "positive" or "negative."

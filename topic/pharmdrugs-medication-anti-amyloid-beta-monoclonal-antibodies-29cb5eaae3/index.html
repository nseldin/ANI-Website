Used To Treat

Alzheimer disease in patients with mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia stage and confirmed amyloid pathology; benefit, eligibility, stopping rules, and MRI surveillance are product specific.

Description

Anti-amyloid beta monoclonal antibodies are disease-modifying Alzheimer therapies that bind aggregated amyloid-beta species and promote their clearance from brain tissue. Lecanemab preferentially recognizes soluble protofibrils and other aggregated forms, whereas donanemab targets an N-terminal pyroglutamate epitope concentrated in deposited plaque. Neither reverses established neuronal loss, and neither is a general treatment for moderate or advanced dementia.

Mechanism

Antibody binding marks extracellular amyloid aggregates for microglial phagocytosis and other clearance pathways, reducing amyloid burden over time. Removing vascular and parenchymal amyloid can also destabilize small vessels, producing amyloid-related imaging abnormalities: ARIA-E is vasogenic edema or sulcal effusion, while ARIA-H is microhemorrhage or superficial siderosis. ApoE epsilon4 homozygosity markedly raises symptomatic and severe ARIA risk.

Boxed Warning

Both current products carry boxed ARIA warnings. Confirm amyloid pathology and baseline MRI, discuss ApoE epsilon4 testing and genotype-specific risk, and obtain each product's scheduled surveillance MRIs before specified early infusions. New headache, confusion, visual change, dizziness, gait change, focal deficit, nausea, or seizure requires urgent evaluation; thrombolysis and antithrombotic decisions need special caution because fatal intracerebral hemorrhage has occurred.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify exact antibody, disease stage, amyloid confirmation, ApoE counseling, baseline microhemorrhage/siderosis burden, antithrombotic or thrombolytic exposure, infusion schedule, and required MRI dates before treatment.

  • Do not normalize neurologic symptoms as dementia fluctuation. Hold the infusion and escalate suspected ARIA for neurologic assessment and MRI according to product severity rules.

Key Labs

  • No serum level guides dosing; the safety program centers on neurologic assessment, amyloid confirmation, baseline and surveillance brain MRI, ApoE epsilon4 status when obtained, and infusion-reaction monitoring.

Nclex Traps

  • ARIA can be asymptomatic on surveillance MRI or symptomatic; absence of symptoms does not replace scheduled imaging.

  • Lecanemab and donanemab share a class but differ in epitope, infusion schedule, MRI timing, and treatment-stopping strategy.

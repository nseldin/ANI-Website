Name

Description

Long-acting basal insulin analogs provide background insulin coverage between meals and overnight. They are not designed to correct a single meal spike.

Used To Treat

Basal insulin replacement in type 1 diabetes and basal support in type 2 diabetes or inpatient insulin regimens when ordered.

Mechanism

These analogs slow absorption or prolong receptor exposure so insulin signaling is flatter and longer. The goal is steady suppression of hepatic glucose output and background glucose uptake support.

Nursing Essentials

  • Give consistently as ordered even when NPO unless the prescriber/protocol changes the plan, because basal insulin prevents ketosis and fasting hyperglycemia.

  • Monitor fasting glucose trends and hypoglycemia, especially with renal impairment, poor intake, or dose changes.

Nclex Traps

  • Basal insulin is not the same job as rapid mealtime insulin. Do not use basal insulin as a quick correction tool.

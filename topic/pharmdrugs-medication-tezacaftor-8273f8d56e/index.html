Used To Treat

With ivacaftor, or with elexacaftor plus ivacaftor, for cystic fibrosis in clients whose age and CFTR genotype meet the current combination-product criteria; tezacaftor is not used alone.

Boxed Warning

When tezacaftor is given in elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor, the complete regimen carries a boxed warning for drug-induced liver injury and liver failure. Follow the exact liver-test schedule and precautions for the dispensed combination product; warning status is not interchangeable across every tezacaftor-containing regimen.

Mechanism

Tezacaftor binds CFTR during protein processing and stabilizes conformations that escape premature intracellular degradation, increasing mature CFTR at the epithelial surface. The resulting rise in chloride and bicarbonate transport improves mucus hydration only when responsive CFTR protein can be produced and the full regimen is taken correctly.

Nursing Essentials

  • Nursing focus for tezacaftor: verify indication, dose, route, allergies, contraindications, pregnancy status, renal/hepatic function, baseline vital signs, and drug-specific monitoring before giving.

  • Monitor liver tests according to the product, pulmonary/nutritional response, rash, pediatric cataracts, adherence, and product-specific neuropsychiatric or intracranial-hypertension warnings.

Key Labs

  • Product-specific ALT, AST and bilirubin monitoring; pulmonary function or sweat chloride when ordered; weight/nutrition and pediatric eye examinations.

Nclex Traps

  • Correctors move more CFTR to the surface; ivacaftor is the potentiator that increases channel opening. Tezacaftor does not replace the other prescribed components.

Description

Tezacaftor is a CFTR corrector that binds and stabilizes selected mutant CFTR protein during processing, increasing folding, trafficking, and persistence at the epithelial surface. In triple therapy it works at a complementary binding site with elexacaftor to place more channels on the cell membrane, while ivacaftor potentiates those channels once they arrive.

Description

Selpercatinib is a targeted antineoplastic and kinase inhibitor class: it blocks the specific kinase pathway targeted by that medication, which can slow tumor growth or selected immune/inflammatory signaling. It is used for targeted cancer therapy or selected inflammatory/immune conditions when the medication matches the disease-driving kinase pathway.

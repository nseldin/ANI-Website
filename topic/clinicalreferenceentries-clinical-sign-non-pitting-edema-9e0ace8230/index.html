Definition

Non-pitting edema is an observed and palpated edema subtype. It is a clue to protein-rich lymphatic swelling with tissue fibrosis, myxedema from hypothyroidism, lipedema, chronic inflammatory or infiltrative swelling, and some advanced local edema states. A positive finding means the area is visibly or palpably swollen but resists displacement and does not retain a clear indentation after pressure.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Non-pitting edema means the area is visibly or palpably swollen but resists displacement and does not retain a clear indentation after pressure.

Why It Matters

The observed Non-pitting edema finding can signal protein-rich lymphatic swelling with tissue fibrosis, myxedema from hypothyroidism, lipedema, chronic inflammatory or infiltrative swelling, and some advanced local edema states. Protein-rich lymph stasis can cause chronic inflammation and fibrosis that stiffen tissue, while glycosaminoglycan accumulation in myxedema binds water within firm tissue rather than leaving freely mobile fluid. Non-pitting edema cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • protein-rich lymphatic swelling with tissue fibrosis, myxedema from hypothyroidism, lipedema, chronic inflammatory or infiltrative swelling, and some advanced local edema states

Assessment Method

Inspect the swollen area and compare sides, distribution, foot or hand involvement, skin thickening, texture, folds, color, temperature, tenderness, scars, lymph-node surgery or radiation history, thyroid findings, medication exposures, and systemic symptoms; press gently over firm tissue and assess a Stemmer sign only when appropriate.

Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Non-pitting edema as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

Examiner pressure, obesity, lipedema, chronic venous change, early lymphedema, and mixed venous-lymphatic disease can blur the pitting distinction; the finding does not show whether total-body volume is high. Therefore, Non-pitting edema cannot confirm or exclude protein-rich lymphatic swelling with tissue fibrosis, myxedema from hypothyroidism, lipedema, chronic inflammatory or infiltrative swelling, and some advanced local edema states by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Positive Situations

A false-positive observation can result from lighting, baseline anatomy, transient physiology, or a mimic such as Pitting edema, lipedema, obesity, localized mass, chronic venous edema.

False Negative Situations

Non-pitting edema may be missed when it is intermittent, subtle, concealed by skin or body habitus, or absent at the observed stage of protein-rich lymphatic swelling with tissue fibrosis, myxedema from hypothyroidism, lipedema, chronic inflammatory or infiltrative swelling, and some advanced local edema states.

Modern Status

Current descriptive edema subtype; non-pitting texture supports selected lymphatic, thyroid, and fibrotic causes but is not diagnostic, and lymphedema can pit before fibrosis develops.

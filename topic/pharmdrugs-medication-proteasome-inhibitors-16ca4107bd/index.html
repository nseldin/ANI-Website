  • Bortezomib

Used To Treat

Multiple myeloma and selected other plasma-cell or lymphoid malignancies under agent-specific regimens; bortezomib also has a mantle-cell lymphoma indication.

Description

Proteasome inhibitors block the 20S proteolytic core of the 26S proteasome, causing ubiquitinated and misfolded proteins to accumulate until the unfolded-protein response and cellular stress become lethal. Plasma cells are unusually vulnerable because immunoglobulin production creates a heavy protein-folding burden, but each drug has a distinct clinical fingerprint: bortezomib is reversible and neuropathy-prone, carfilzomib is irreversible and cardiopulmonary-toxic, and ixazomib is an oral weekly reversible inhibitor.

Mechanism

Inhibiting the beta-5 chymotrypsin-like proteasome activity prevents controlled degradation of regulatory and damaged proteins, disrupts NF-kappa-B and other survival pathways, produces endoplasmic-reticulum stress, and activates apoptosis. Bortezomib and ixazomib bind reversibly; carfilzomib covalently and irreversibly binds active-site threonine.

Boxed Warning

High-yield class safety context: no shared boxed warning applies, but cytopenias, infection, herpes-zoster reactivation, GI toxicity, neuropathy, tumor lysis, and embryo-fetal harm require monitoring. Carfilzomib adds major cardiac, renal, pulmonary, hypertensive, and thrombotic risk.

Nursing Essentials

  • Identify route and agent before applying a class plan: subcutaneous/IV bortezomib, IV carfilzomib, and oral ixazomib are not administration variants of one product.

  • Trend CBC, neuropathy and functional status, infection/zoster prevention, renal/hepatic function, tumor-lysis risk, and agent-specific cardiac/volume or oral-adherence hazards.

Key Labs

  • CBC with platelets, CMP including renal/liver function and electrolytes, tumor-lysis labs when burden is high, and BP/cardiac assessment for carfilzomib.

Nclex Traps

  • Proteasome inhibition exploits protein stress, not DNA cross-linking.

  • Aggressive hydration for tumor-lysis prevention can worsen carfilzomib-associated heart failure; volume strategy must be individualized.

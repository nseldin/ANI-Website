Description

Fluoroquinolones are DNA gyrase/topoisomerase antibiotics with important boxed-warning toxicity.

Used To Treat

Selected complicated urinary, respiratory, GI, bone/joint, prostatitis, exposure, or resistant infections when benefits outweigh risks.

Mechanism

They inhibit bacterial topoisomerase II/IV, impairing DNA supercoiling and replication. Human tissue toxicity links to tendon rupture, neuropathy, CNS effects, dysglycemia, QT risk, and aortic cautions.

Nursing Essentials

  • Teach tendon pain, neuropathy, mood/CNS changes, severe diarrhea, palpitations, glucose symptoms, cation separation, and myasthenia/aortic-risk cautions.

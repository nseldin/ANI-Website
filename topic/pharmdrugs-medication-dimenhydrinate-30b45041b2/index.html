Used To Treat

Nausea and vomiting due to surgery/anesthesia, chemotherapy, migraine, vestibular disorders, pregnancy, gastroparesis, or GI illness depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Dimenhydrinate works by antagonizing adenosine receptors, especially the adenosine brake on CNS and respiratory-center activity. In apnea of prematurity, less adenosine inhibition increases medullary respiratory drive, improves CO2 responsiveness, increases minute ventilation, and can improve diaphragmatic contractility. The citrate salt is the neonatal formulation, so dose conversions differ from caffeine base.

Description

Dimenhydrinate is a methylxanthine respiratory stimulant and nonselective adenosine receptor antagonist: it works by antagonizing adenosine receptors, especially the adenosine brake on CNS and respiratory-center activity. It is used for bowel symptom control, constipation relief, bowel preparation, secretion thinning, or diarrhea management depending on the specific agent and contraindications.

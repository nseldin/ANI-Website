Used To Treat

Anxiety, acute agitation, alcohol withdrawal, procedural sedation, muscle spasm, seizure rescue/status epilepticus, or insomnia depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Clonazepam works by enhancing GABA-A activity, increasing inhibitory CNS tone. That is why sedation, falls, respiratory depression with other depressants, and withdrawal seizures matter.

Boxed Warning

Class boxed warning: concomitant opioid use can cause profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death; dependence/withdrawal risks also matter.

Contraindications

  • Severe respiratory insufficiency unless monitored

  • myasthenia gravis or sleep apnea risk may require clarification

  • acute narrow-angle glaucoma for selected agents

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor sedation, respiratory status, fall risk, paradoxical agitation, and withdrawal risk.

  • Use seizure precautions when given for seizures/withdrawal.

  • Flumazenil can reverse but may precipitate seizures in dependent clients.

  • Verify the exact medication, dose, route, allergies, indication, baseline assessment, and hold parameters.

  • Trend the client's response rather than treating one isolated number.

  • Question orders when the medication effect could worsen the client's current priority problem.

Interactions

  • Opioids, alcohol, sedatives, antipsychotics, antihistamines increase CNS/respiratory depression.

  • Opioids, alcohol, sleep medications, antipsychotics, antihistamines, and other CNS depressants.

Key Labs

  • respiratory assessment

  • liver function for longer-acting agents

  • CIWA/seizure assessment when relevant

  • Respiratory rate, oxygenation, sedation level, blood pressure, fall risk, alcohol/opioid use, and baseline mental status.

Nclex Traps

  • Older adults are high-risk for delirium and falls. Do not mix with opioids casually.

  • Older adults can develop confusion, falls, paradoxical agitation, and respiratory compromise. Flumazenil may trigger seizures in dependent clients.

Description

Clonazepam is a benzodiazepine anxiolytic/sedative/anticonvulsant: it works by enhancing GABA-A activity, increasing inhibitory CNS tone. It is used for anxiety, acute agitation, alcohol withdrawal, seizure rescue/status epilepticus support, procedural sedation, muscle spasm, or insomnia depending on the specific medication and order.

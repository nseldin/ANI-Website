Used To Treat

Mechanism

Potassium phosphate replaces phosphate needed for ATP, 2,3-BPG, bone/mineral balance, and cellular metabolism while also adding potassium. IV use can rapidly cause hyperkalemia, hypocalcemia, dysrhythmias, kidney injury, or infusion complications.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Potassium phosphate in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Potassium phosphate is an electrolyte replacement and potassium and phosphate salt: it replaces phosphate needed for ATP, 2,3-BPG, bone/mineral balance, and cellular metabolism while also adding potassium. It is used for treatment or prevention of hypophosphatemia with potassium replacement when both electrolytes fit the client situation.

Definition

Ancylostoma duodenale is a clinically important nematode (roundworm) associated with hookworm disease, iron-deficiency anemia, and ground itch with pulmonary migration. The organism or agent name identifies the cause or association; it is not itself the disease diagnosis, and detection must be interpreted with the specimen, host, and syndrome.

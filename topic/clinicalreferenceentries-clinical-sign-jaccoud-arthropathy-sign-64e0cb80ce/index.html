Definition

Jaccoud arthropathy sign is an observed hand-deformity pattern. It is a clue to nonerosive ligamentous arthropathy. A positive finding means deformities are often passively correctable.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Jaccoud arthropathy sign means deformities are often passively correctable.

Why It Matters

The Jaccoud arthropathy sign pattern can signal nonerosive ligamentous arthropathy. Repeated inflammation stretches periarticular soft tissues without the fixed erosive destruction typical of rheumatoid arthritis. Jaccoud arthropathy sign cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • nonerosive ligamentous arthropathy

Assessment Method

Inspect ulnar deviation and swan-neck-type changes, then assess gentle reducibility.

Evidence Limitations

Late deformities can become fixed and imaging is needed to assess erosion. Therefore, Jaccoud arthropathy sign cannot confirm or exclude nonerosive ligamentous arthropathy by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

False Negative Situations

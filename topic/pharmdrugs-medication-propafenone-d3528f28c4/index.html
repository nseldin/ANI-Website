Boxed Warning

Drug-specific boxed warning/class IC warning: increased mortality and serious proarrhythmia risk in clients with structural heart disease or post-MI ventricular ectopy. Reserve for appropriate rhythm indications and monitored clients.

Brand Examples

  • Rythmol

  • Rythmol SR

Contraindications

  • Heart failure, cardiogenic shock, severe bradycardia, significant AV block or conduction disease without a pacemaker.

  • Bronchospastic disease or marked hypotension requires clarification because of beta-blocking effects.

  • Correct significant potassium or magnesium abnormalities before therapy.

Nursing Essentials

  • Monitor ECG, PR/QRS intervals, heart rate, blood pressure, dizziness, syncope, wheezing, dyspnea, chest pain, and new/worse dysrhythmias.

  • Assess for heart failure symptoms and bronchospasm because propafenone is not just a pure sodium-channel blocker.

  • Teach clients to report fainting, palpitations, shortness of breath, or new swelling promptly.

Nclex Traps

  • Propafenone has beta-blocking activity, so wheezing, bradycardia, AV block, or worsening heart failure are not random side effects.

  • Like flecainide, structural heart disease and post-MI history are major safety red flags.

Class

Class IC antiarrhythmic; sodium-channel blocker with beta-blocking activity

Used To Treat

Rhythm control for selected atrial fibrillation/flutter or paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia in clients without significant structural heart disease, and selected ventricular dysrhythmias when specialist-directed.

Mechanism

Blocks fast cardiac sodium channels to slow conduction and widen QRS. It also has beta-blocking effects that can cause bradycardia, AV block, bronchospasm, or worsened heart failure in vulnerable clients.

Interactions

  • Warfarin and digoxin levels/effects can rise; monitor for bleeding or digoxin toxicity when combined.

  • CYP2D6/CYP3A4 inhibitors, other antiarrhythmics, beta blockers, and AV-node blockers can increase toxicity or conduction slowing.

  • Grapefruit may increase exposure for some clients and should be clarified with the prescriber/pharmacist.

Key Labs

  • ECG/QRS and PR interval.

  • Potassium and magnesium.

  • Liver function when clinically indicated because metabolism is hepatic.

  • INR with warfarin and digoxin level/assessment when combined.

Description

Propafenone is a class IC antiarrhythmic and sodium-channel blocker with beta-blocking activity: it blocks fast cardiac sodium channels to slow conduction and widen QRS. It is used for rhythm control for selected atrial fibrillation/flutter or paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia in clients without significant structural heart disease, and selected ventricular dysrhythmias when specialist-directed.

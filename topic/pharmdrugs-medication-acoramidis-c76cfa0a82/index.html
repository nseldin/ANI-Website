Class

Transthyretin thyroxine-pocket binder and tetramer kinetic stabilizer

Used To Treat

Cardiomyopathy of wild-type or variant transthyretin-mediated amyloidosis in adults to reduce cardiovascular death and cardiovascular-related hospitalization.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current ATTRUBY labeling has no boxed warning and lists no contraindication. The most common adverse reactions are diarrhea and upper abdominal pain. Continue heart-failure and rhythm surveillance because slowing additional amyloid formation does not immediately reverse established deposits, restrictive physiology or conduction disease. Interpret a low free T4 with TSH and clinical context rather than reflexively diagnosing hypothyroidism.

Mechanism

Acoramidis binds the two thyroxine-binding sites at native TTR's dimer-dimer interface and raises the kinetic barrier to tetramer dissociation, thereby reducing formation of misfolding-prone monomers. It works downstream of gene transcription and therefore stabilizes circulating TTR rather than lowering hepatic TTR synthesis as siRNA or antisense silencers do. TTR stabilization can lower measured free T4 without changing TSH, probably because thyroxine binding to or displacement from TTR changes; this pattern must be interpreted with TSH, symptoms and the broader thyroid evaluation rather than treated as proof of hypothyroidism by itself.

Nursing Essentials

  • Verify the 712-mg twice-daily regimen as two 356-mg tablets per dose, adherence, GI tolerance, thyroid-test context, diagnosis, volume status, rhythm/conduction disease and concurrent heart-failure therapy.

  • Escalate worsening edema, dyspnea, syncope, hypotension or arrhythmia because a TTR stabilizer is neither an acute diuretic nor an inotrope and progression can still occur despite treatment.

Key Labs

  • Weight, blood pressure, renal/liver function, ECG/rhythm, NT-proBNP/troponin and functional or echocardiographic measures as indicated; pair free T4 with TSH and symptoms.

Nclex Traps

  • A pharmacodynamic claim of near-complete stabilization is not the same as a cure or immediate plaque removal.

  • Acoramidis stabilizes TTR and does not silence mutant or wild-type TTR mRNA.

Description

Acoramidis is an oral transthyretin thyroxine-pocket binder and kinetic stabilizer for wild-type or variant ATTR cardiomyopathy. Because tetramer dissociation is the rate-limiting first committed step in ATTR amyloid formation, keeping TTR tetramers assembled reduces the supply of monomers that can misfold into toxic oligomers and fibrils. The 712-mg twice-daily regimen produced near-complete in-vitro TTR stabilization by Day 28 in the labeled pharmacodynamic assay; that finding confirms target engagement, but it does not mean established myocardial amyloid has disappeared or that congestion will improve immediately.

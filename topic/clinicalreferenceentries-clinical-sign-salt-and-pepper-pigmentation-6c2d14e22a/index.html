Definition

Salt-and-pepper pigmentation is an observed skin sign. It is a clue to systemic sclerosis. A positive finding means mottled depigmentation retains pigment around hair follicles.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Salt-and-pepper pigmentation means mottled depigmentation retains pigment around hair follicles.

Why It Matters

The observed Salt-and-pepper pigmentation finding can signal systemic sclerosis. Immune melanocyte injury with follicular pigment sparing creates punctate dark islands in pale skin. Salt-and-pepper pigmentation cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • systemic sclerosis

False Positive Situations

False Negative Situations

Used To Treat

Parkinson disease motor symptom control, wearing-off management, restless legs syndrome for selected dopamine agonists, or related neurologic indications depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Cabergoline works by stimulating dopamine receptors. In Parkinson disease this supports motor signaling; in hyperprolactinemia selected agents suppress prolactin. Orthostasis, hallucinations, sleep attacks, impulse-control behaviors, and nausea are high-yield risks.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for cabergoline in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Cabergoline is a dopamine agonist and prolactin-lowering medication: it works by stimulating dopamine receptors. It is used for parkinson disease motor symptom control, wearing-off management, restless legs syndrome for selected dopamine agonists, or related neurologic indications depending on the agent.

Long-half-life once-weekly IV echinocandin beta-1,3-D-glucan synthase inhibitor

Used To Treat

Candidemia and invasive candidiasis in adults who have limited or no alternative options; it has not been studied for Candida endocarditis, osteomyelitis, or meningitis.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current REZZAYO labeling has no boxed warning. Serious hypersensitivity and anaphylaxis can occur. Infusion flushing, warmth, urticaria, nausea or chest tightness may improve when the infusion is paused or slowed. Photosensitivity requires UV protection, and abnormal liver tests require monitoring and a continued-treatment risk-benefit decision. Safety beyond four weekly doses is not established in the label.

Mechanism

Rezafungin noncompetitively inhibits the Fks-containing beta-1,3-D-glucan synthase complex, reducing a structural polysaccharide in the Candida cell wall. The wall becomes mechanically weak and osmotically unstable. Human cells lack beta-glucan walls, creating selectivity. FKS hot-spot mutations can reduce susceptibility, so persistent fungemia requires species, source-control and resistance reassessment.

Nursing Essentials

  • Obtain blood and sterile-site cultures, identify Candida species, repeat cultures until clearance, examine for metastatic infection and remove infected intravascular devices when appropriate.

  • Give by labeled IV infusion, inspect for hypersensitivity, counsel sun protection, and document the exact weekly schedule so a missed dose is not treated like a daily echinocandin omission.

Key Labs

  • Serial blood cultures, species/susceptibility, CBC, AST/ALT/alkaline phosphatase/bilirubin, potassium, magnesium, phosphate, renal function and clinical source-control response.

Nclex Traps

  • Once-weekly dosing comes from pharmacokinetics; it does not make rezafungin an oral drug or establish treatment for Candida meningitis, endocarditis or osteomyelitis.

  • The mechanism is fungal cell-wall glucan inhibition, not ergosterol synthesis or membrane binding.

Description

Rezafungin damages susceptible Candida cell walls by inhibiting the Fks-containing beta-1,3-D-glucan synthase complex, depleting structural glucan until the wall becomes mechanically weak and osmotically unstable. It is a long-acting IV echinocandin whose extended half-life permits a loading dose followed by therapy once weekly for adult candidemia or invasive candidiasis when alternatives are limited. The convenient interval does not prove penetration or efficacy at every deep Candida site.

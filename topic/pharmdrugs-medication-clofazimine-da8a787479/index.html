Boxed Warning

High-alert warning focus: clofazimine can cause persistent red-brown skin and body-fluid discoloration, severe abdominal pain or obstruction from GI crystal deposition, QT prolongation, hepatotoxicity, and major psychosocial distress from visible pigmentation.

Class

Riminophenazine antimycobacterial; antileprosy and MDR-TB companion drug

Used To Treat

Multibacillary leprosy as part of multidrug therapy and selected drug-resistant TB regimens when specialist guidance supports use.

Description

Clofazimine is a riminophenazine antimycobacterial that accumulates in tissues and disrupts mycobacterial DNA/membrane redox biology, with skin discoloration and GI crystal deposition as defining safety lessons. It is high-yield because the drug can visibly turn skin/body fluids red-brown and can add QT risk in MDR-TB regimens.

Mechanism

Clofazimine is lipophilic and concentrates inside macrophages and mycobacteria, where it binds DNA and promotes membrane/redox stress that impairs mycobacterial growth. Slow tissue release explains the long half-life, prolonged discoloration, and delayed toxicity concerns.

Nursing Essentials

  • Warn before therapy that skin, sweat, tears, sputum, urine, and stool discoloration can occur and may last months after stopping.

  • Monitor mood/body-image distress, abdominal pain, diarrhea, nausea, liver symptoms, QT-risk partners, and adherence.

  • Escalate severe abdominal pain, black/bloody stool, syncope/palpitations, jaundice, or severe depression.

Contraindications

  • Clarify severe hepatic disease, significant baseline QT prolongation, severe GI disease/obstruction risk, and hypersensitivity.

Interactions

  • Other QT-prolonging drugs in TB regimens, including bedaquiline, fluoroquinolones, macrolides, or azoles, can increase ECG monitoring needs.

Key Labs

  • ECG/QTc when QT-risk regimen is used.

  • Liver function tests when ordered or symptomatic.

  • Clinical GI and skin/mood assessment.

Nclex Traps

  • Clofazimine discoloration is expected but not trivial; it can affect adherence and mental health.

  • Severe abdominal pain on clofazimine is not just a routine upset stomach.

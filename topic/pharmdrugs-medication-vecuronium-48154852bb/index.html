Used To Treat

Skeletal-muscle paralysis to facilitate intubation, mechanical ventilation, or surgery in fully supported settings; these drugs provide paralysis only, not sedation, amnesia, or pain relief.

Mechanism

Vecuronium works by competitively blocking nicotinic acetylcholine receptors at the neuromuscular junction without depolarizing the motor end plate. Paralysis occurs without analgesia or sedation, so airway/ventilation, sedation awareness, train-of-four monitoring, and reversal planning matter.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Vecuronium in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Vecuronium is a nondepolarizing neuromuscular blocker: it works by competitively blocking nicotinic acetylcholine receptors at the neuromuscular junction without depolarizing the motor end plate. It is used for skeletal-muscle paralysis to facilitate intubation, mechanical ventilation, or surgery in fully supported settings; these drugs provide paralysis only, not sedation, amnesia, or pain relief.

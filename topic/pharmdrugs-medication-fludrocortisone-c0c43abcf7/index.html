Mechanism

Fludrocortisone works by replacing an essential trace element used in antioxidant selenoproteins and thyroid hormone metabolism. Excess can cause GI upset, hair/nail changes, neuropathy, and garlic-like breath.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for fludrocortisone in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Fludrocortisone is a mineralocorticoid replacement: it works by replacing an essential trace element used in antioxidant selenoproteins and thyroid hormone metabolism. It is used for deficiency prevention/treatment, pregnancy nutrition support, anemia support, wound/healing context, or parenteral nutrition depending on the nutrient.

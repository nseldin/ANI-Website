Class

Antiemetic drug class; receptor-pathway nausea and vomiting blockers

Description

Antiemetics reduce nausea/vomiting by interrupting specific emetic pathways: serotonin from gut/vagal signaling, dopamine in the chemoreceptor trigger zone, histamine/muscarinic vestibular pathways, neurokinin substance P, cannabinoids, or motility circuits. The target predicts the adverse effect, such as QT risk, EPS, sedation, anticholinergic burden, or constipation.

Mechanism

Different subclasses block 5-HT3, D2, H1, M1, NK1, cannabinoid, or GI motility pathways, so matching cause of nausea to receptor pathway matters.

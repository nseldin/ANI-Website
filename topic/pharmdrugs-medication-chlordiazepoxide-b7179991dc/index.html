Used To Treat

Anxiety, acute agitation, alcohol withdrawal, procedural sedation, seizure rescue/status epilepticus, muscle spasm, or insomnia depending on the agent.

Mechanism

Chlordiazepoxide works by enhancing GABA-A activity, increasing inhibitory CNS tone. That is why sedation, falls, respiratory depression with other depressants, and withdrawal seizures matter.

Boxed Warning

Boxed warning: combined use with opioids can cause profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death. Many benzodiazepines also carry dependence/withdrawal warnings.

Contraindications

  • Known serious hypersensitivity to the medication/class.

  • Clarify before giving if pregnancy, severe renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vitals, or high-risk interactions are present.

  • Clarify serious allergy or prior severe reaction before giving.

  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Interactions

  • Check for duplicate therapy, CNS depression, QT prolongation, bleeding risk, serotonin syndrome, renal/hepatic toxicity, and CYP/P-gp interactions.

  • Opioids, alcohol, sleep medications, antipsychotics, antihistamines, and other CNS depressants.

Nclex Traps

  • Do not assume a medication is safe because the name is familiar; match the med to the client's allergies, labs, vitals, pregnancy status, and diagnosis.

  • Older adults can develop confusion, falls, paradoxical agitation, and respiratory compromise. Flumazenil may trigger seizures in dependent clients.

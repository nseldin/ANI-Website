Definition

Rinne test is a bedside tuning-fork test. It is a clue to conductive versus sensorineural hearing pattern. A positive finding means air conduction is normally longer; bone conduction equal to or longer than air suggests conductive loss.

Plain Language

In plain language, a positive Rinne test means air conduction is normally longer; bone conduction equal to or longer than air suggests conductive loss.

Why It Matters

A positive Rinne test result can signal conductive versus sensorineural hearing pattern. Middle and external ear amplify air sound, while bone vibration bypasses them. Rinne test cannot confirm that association by itself; assess it using the method and limits defined for this finding.

Associated Conditions

  • conductive versus sensorineural hearing pattern

Assessment Method

Place a vibrating tuning fork on the mastoid, then beside the ear canal when bone sound stops.

Positive Finding

Air conduction is normally longer; bone conduction equal to or longer than air suggests conductive loss.

Mechanism

Middle and external ear amplify air sound, while bone vibration bypasses them.

Clinical Interpretation

A positive Rinne test result raises concern for conductive versus sensorineural hearing pattern because middle and external ear amplify air sound, while bone vibration bypasses them. Compare the response with the reviewed assessment and differentials; Rinne test does not establish the diagnosis alone.

Diagnostic Usefulness

Use Rinne test to focus evaluation of conductive versus sensorineural hearing pattern and the reviewed alternatives: cerumen, otosclerosis, poor technique. Follow this card's assessment method and limits: Place a vibrating tuning fork on the mastoid, then beside the ear canal when bone sound stops. Then anticipate or support cause-directed testing selected by the responsible clinician for the suspected condition rather than treating Rinne test as a diagnosis.

Evidence Limitations

False-negative Rinne occurs when bone sound crosses to the better ear. Therefore, Rinne test cannot confirm or exclude conductive versus sensorineural hearing pattern by itself; interpret the result in the population and clinical setting where it was assessed.

Differential Diagnoses

  • cerumen

  • otosclerosis

  • poor technique

False Positive Situations

A false-positive test can result from method deviation, subjective interpretation, or an alternative cause such as cerumen, otosclerosis, poor technique.

False Negative Situations

Rinne test may be missed when technique, timing, cooperation, or disease stage prevents the defined response.

Modern Status

Current adjunct; bedside tuning forks do not quantify hearing. When performed as described, Rinne test can adjust concern for conductive versus sensorineural hearing pattern, but it is not a stand-alone diagnosis. Apply the reviewed limitation for Rinne test: False-negative Rinne occurs when bone sound crosses to the better ear.

Safety Precautions

Before assessing Rinne test, explain the method, obtain consent, preserve privacy, and use only the technique described here: Place a vibrating tuning fork on the mastoid, then beside the ear canal when bone sound stops. Stop if that technique becomes unsafe. The reviewed precaution for this maneuver is: Use a proper 512-Hz fork and pair with Weber and audiometry.

Continue the focused evaluation by following the reviewed method and its limits: Place a vibrating tuning fork on the mastoid, then beside the ear canal when bone sound stops. Communicate the finding with its suspected association, conductive versus sensorineural hearing pattern, when it is new, worsening, or clinically important.

Documentation Guidance

Document the exact finding, assessment method, side or distribution, timing, patient response, associated symptoms and vital signs, and change from baseline. Record the observed result instead of writing only "positive" or "negative."

Base escalation for Rinne test on the patient's current condition, associated findings, and the cause under evaluation; this card does not assign a universal urgency level.

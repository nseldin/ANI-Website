  • Clarify pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic impairment, unstable vital signs, and high-risk interactions when clinically relevant.

Nursing Essentials

Nclex Traps

  • The NCLEX trap is memorizing the name without connecting class, contraindication, assessment, and priority adverse effect.

Used To Treat

Short-term tocolysis for preterm labor in selected clients when delaying birth allows corticosteroids, magnesium for neuroprotection, transfer, or additional evaluation.

Mechanism

Nifedipine for preterm labor works by blocking L-type calcium channels. Dihydropyridines mainly vasodilate arteries; diltiazem/verapamil also slow AV nodal conduction, so heart rate and heart block matter.

Boxed Warning

No drug-specific boxed warning is listed for Nifedipine for preterm labor in this nursing reference. Major safety review centers on hypersensitivity, organ toxicity, route-specific hazards, high-alert dosing, population risk, and serious class warnings when present.

Description

Nifedipine for preterm labor is a dihydropyridine calcium-channel blocker: it works by blocking L-type calcium channels. It is used for short-term tocolysis for preterm labor in selected clients when delaying birth allows corticosteroids, magnesium for neuroprotection, transfer, or additional evaluation.

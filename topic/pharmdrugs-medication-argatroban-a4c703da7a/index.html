Description

Argatroban is a direct thrombin inhibitor anticoagulant: it directly and reversibly inhibits thrombin, reducing fibrin formation, thrombin-mediated platelet activation, and clot propagation. It is used for anticoagulation for heparin-induced thrombocytopenia and selected PCI contexts when a non-heparin anticoagulant is needed.

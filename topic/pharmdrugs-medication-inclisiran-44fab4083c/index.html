Class

GalNAc-conjugated hepatic PCSK9-mRNA small-interfering RNA

Used To Treat

Adjunct to diet and exercise to reduce LDL-C in adults with hypercholesterolemia, including heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia, using health-professional-administered subcutaneous dosing on day 1, month 3, then every 6 months.

Boxed Warning

High-yield warning context: current LEQVIO labeling has no boxed warning. Serious hypersensitivity, including angioedema, may require discontinuation. Injection-site pain, erythema or rash is the most characteristic adverse effect. It is administered by a healthcare professional; a missed dose within 3 months can be given and the original schedule maintained, while a longer delay restarts the day-1/month-3 sequence.

Mechanism

After receptor-mediated hepatocyte uptake, the antisense strand enters RISC and guides it to complementary PCSK9 mRNA. Argonaute cleaves the transcript, the guide strand is recycled, and less PCSK9 protein is secreted. More LDL receptors avoid lysosomal degradation, return to the hepatocyte surface and clear circulating LDL. This catalytic RNA mechanism supports day-1, day-90 and six-month maintenance dosing. The GalNAc ligand concentrates inclisiran in hepatocytes through asialoglycoprotein-receptor uptake. Its guide strand remains in the RNA-induced silencing complex and can direct repeated PCSK9-mRNA cleavage, so hepatic PCSK9 production stays suppressed long after plasma drug falls. Less PCSK9 allows more LDL receptors to recycle, thereby increasing LDL clearance. This intracellular catalytic recycling explains the day-1, month-3, then six-month schedule; the interval is a pharmacodynamic consequence, not evidence that LDL no longer needs follow-up or that missed injections are harmless.

Nursing Essentials

  • Confirm baseline LDL, familial phenotype, concurrent lipid therapy, pregnancy/lactation context, injection schedule and ability to return at three and six-month intervals.

  • Record the exact administration date and next due date; twice-yearly maintenance can fail operationally if ownership of recall and follow-up is unclear.

Key Labs

  • Lipid panel and percentage LDL-C response, adherence to other therapy, injection-site and hypersensitivity assessment; no routine PCSK9 concentration or coagulation monitoring is required.

Nclex Traps

  • Inclisiran silences PCSK9 mRNA inside hepatocytes; evolocumab and alirocumab bind extracellular PCSK9 protein.

  • The long interval is not a one-time vaccine; the month-3 dose and ongoing six-month doses are required.

Description

Inclisiran is a long-acting double-stranded siRNA that reduces hepatic production of PCSK9 rather than binding circulating PCSK9 protein. A GalNAc ligand targets hepatocyte asialoglycoprotein receptors, allowing RNA-induced silencing complexes to cleave PCSK9 mRNA repeatedly and preserve LDL receptors for months.

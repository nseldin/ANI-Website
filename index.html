ANI Privacy & Data Use Notice

Version 2026-08-17.1. Effective August 17, 2026. The controller/operator identification and pending-company disclosure in the Terms above also apply to this notice. A medical search or report can reveal sensitive health interests even when it does not include a name. This notice explains what the current public website and Android app process, why, where it goes, and the choices available.

What stays on your device

In the current public build, ordinary chat history and memory, favorites, settings, study progress, and offline application files are stored locally in your browser or app. They are not automatically attached to feedback. Local information remains until you delete it through ANI where available, clear browser/app data, uninstall the app, or the device removes it. The Android feedback outbox is encrypted with Android Keystore-backed protection and excluded from backup.

Manual reports

If you choose to report an issue, ANI may collect the report type, subject, description after pattern-based redaction, optional public route/card and app/catalog/platform context, time, random event and integrity identifiers, redaction flags, privacy-minimized security metadata, and—only if selected—one screenshot. The report is used to reproduce problems, identify missing or incorrect content, improve search and interface behavior, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. Reports never automatically edit or publish medical content, and the production feedback system does not automatically send reports to Codex or use AI to review them.

Optional missing-topic telemetry

This feature is off until you separately opt in. For an explicit encyclopedia search that returns no plausible result, ANI may send the exact phrase, normalized/corrected/phonetic forms, unmatched clues, zero-result counts and trigger, app/platform/catalog version, time, random event IDs, and privacy-minimized security metadata. ANI uses this only to identify potentially missing topics, understand failed retrieval, and improve ANI's encyclopedia and search. It does not include chat history, an account profile, cookies, or URL query strings. You may turn it off at any time in Settings without losing access to the encyclopedia.

Screenshots

A screenshot is never uploaded automatically. If you select one, ANI re-encodes and downsizes it to remove ordinary file metadata. You must still remove visible names, faces, record numbers, messages, and other identifiers. Screenshot files are stored privately in Cloudflare R2, are not included in ANI's report JSON, ZIP archives, administrative exports, daily/overflow PDFs, or email, and are scheduled to expire after 30 days.

Network, security, analytics, and voice

Cloudflare hosts and protects ANI and may process request IP addresses, browser/device and HTTP information, Turnstile signals, security events, and service logs. ANI's report database stores daily rotating one-way HMAC network/client hashes for abuse prevention, quotas, deduplication, and integrity—not the raw IP in report records or exports. Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled on the website and Cloudflare describes it as privacy-first analytics that does not collect or use visitors' personal data. If you use voice input, your browser or operating system may process speech under its own terms; ANI's feedback system does not attach audio or ordinary chat history, and server transcription is disabled in the current public build.

Recipients and disclosures

Feedback is available only to the ANI operator and authorized reviewers; Cloudflare processes hosting, security, Workers, D1, private R2, Turnstile, logging, and Email Service functions. Sanitized report/query text and private PDF or ZIP summaries may be sent through Cloudflare Email Service to ANI's restricted operator inbox at seldin.nathan8@gmail.com, where Google/Gmail processes and stores the email. Screenshot files are not emailed. ANI does not sell feedback, use it for targeted advertising, or automatically provide it to Codex or an AI model. Information may be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users, rights, and service security.

Consent and choices

Agreement to the Terms is separate from optional health-search telemetry. The telemetry controls ask separately whether ANI may collect the data and whether Cloudflare/Gmail may process and receive the described private summaries. Both are off by default, and saying no does not block ANI. Manual reporting has its own unchecked collection and sharing consents at the time of submission. Strictly necessary network security may operate to deliver and protect a service you request.

Retention

Unsent web search-miss items expire after up to 30 days. Pending manual web and Android reports remain locally until a durable receipt, user deletion, or bounded-capacity handling. Server screenshot files expire after 30 days. Sanitized server report records remain in the regular collection until administrative review or capacity archiving; archived ZIPs in private trash remain until the operator explicitly performs a guarded purge. Daily PDFs, integrity/audit records, Cloudflare service logs, and Gmail copies follow the operator's and provider's operational retention and currently have no single automatic deletion period. An email already delivered cannot be retracted by ANI. This policy will be updated before any retention practice changes.
